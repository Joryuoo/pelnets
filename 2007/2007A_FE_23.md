### Explanation
To find the processing performance in MIPS (Million Instructions Per Second), we first calculate the average execution time in clocks per instruction based on their execution frequencies:

- **Command 1:** 10 clocks * 60% = 6 clocks
- **Command 2:** 5 clocks * 40% = 2 clocks

**Average clocks per instruction** = 6 + 2 = 8 clocks/instruction.

The processor has a clock frequency of 1 GHz, which means it runs at $1 \times 10^9$ clocks per second.

**Instructions per second** = Clock frequency / Average clocks per instruction 
$= 1,000,000,000 / 8 = 125,000,000$ instructions/second.

In MIPS, this is exactly **125 MIPS**.
