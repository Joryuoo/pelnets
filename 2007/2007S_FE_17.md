# 2007S_FE_17

Q17. Which of the following is a re-writable, erasable memory, using electrical signals, which is widely used for various devices such as digital cameras and digital music players and can maintain the data even after the power is turned off? 

a) DRAM 
b) Flash memory 
c) Mask ROM 
d) SRAM
?
b) Flash memory

### Explanation
- **Flash memory** is a type of non-volatile memory that can be electrically erased and reprogrammed. It retains its data even when power is removed, making it the standard storage medium for portable devices like digital cameras, USB drives, and smartphones.
- **DRAM** (Dynamic RAM) and **SRAM** (Static RAM) are volatile types of memory; they completely lose their stored data when power is turned off.
- **Mask ROM** is non-volatile but strictly read-only; its contents are programmed during manufacturing and cannot be re-written or erased.
