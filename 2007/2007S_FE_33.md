# 2007S_FE_33

Q33. Which of the following is a display, with low-voltage operation and low-power consumption, which does not need backlighting because it emits light when voltage is applied?

a) CRT
b) OLED
c) PDP
d) TFT LCD
?
b) OLED

### Explanation
OLED (Organic Light-Emitting Diode) displays use organic compounds that emit light when an electric current is applied. Because they emit their own light, they do not require a backlight, which allows them to be very thin, have low power consumption, and operate at low voltages.
- a) CRT: Cathode Ray Tube, bulky and uses high voltage.
- c) PDP: Plasma Display Panel, uses high voltage and consumes more power.
- d) TFT LCD: Thin-Film Transistor Liquid Crystal Display, requires a backlight to be visible.
