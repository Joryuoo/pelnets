# 2007S_FE_20

Q20. A computer is designed to enable pipeline control so that each instruction can be completed in five cycles.  How many cycles are needed to execute 20 instructions?  Here, all instructions can be executed without being stopped halfway. 

a) 20 
b) 21 
c) 24 
d) 25
?
c) 24

### Explanation
In an ideal linear pipeline with $k$ stages, the total time required to execute $n$ consecutive instructions is given by the formula:
$$\text{Total Cycles} = k + (n - 1)$$

- The first instruction takes all $k = 5$ cycles to pass through every stage and complete. This initially fills up the pipeline.
- Once the pipeline is completely full, one instruction finishes executing every subsequent cycle. So the remaining $(n - 1) = 19$ instructions will take exactly 19 cycles to complete.

Therefore, the total number of cycles needed is $5 + 19 = 24$ cycles.
