### Explanation
A break-even point chart plots sales against total costs (fixed + variable). For Company A to have larger profits than Company B once sales exceed the break-even point, its profit margin per additional unit must be higher. This occurs when Company A has lower variable costs per unit of product, resulting in a steeper profit curve. Therefore, choice c is correct.
