# 2007S_FE_72

Against what kind of attacks does SSL (Secure Socket Layer) protect users on the Internet? 

a) Bruce force attack 
b) DoS (Denial of Service) attack 
c) IP Spoofing 
d) Tapping and exploitation of data
?
d) Tapping and exploitation of data

### Explanation
SSL (Secure Socket Layer) encrypts network communications to provide confidentiality and data integrity, protecting against eavesdropping (tapping) and tampering (exploitation of data). Choice d is correct.

- **Choice a** (brute force attack) is generally mitigated by authentication policies (e.g., account lockouts) rather than SSL.
- **Choice b** (DoS attack) is mitigated by rate limiting, load balancing, and firewall rules.
- **Choice c** (IP spoofing) is handled at the network layer, typically by firewalls and routers, whereas SSL operates higher up the stack.
