### Explanation
The binary number $x$ is given as $1010\dots10_2$ consisting of $2n$ digits.
When $x$ is shifted to the right by 1 bit, we divide it by 2, resulting in $x/2 = 0101\dots01_2$ with $2n$ digits.
If we add $x$ and $x/2$, the corresponding bits align perfectly:
  $1010\dots10_2$
+ $0101\dots01_2$
------------------
  $1111\dots11_2$
The result is a $2n$-digit binary number consisting of all 1s. A binary number with $k$ ones is equal to $2^k - 1$. Here, with $2n$ ones, the sum is $2^{2n} - 1$.
Therefore, the equation is $x + x/2 = 2^{2n} - 1$.
