# 2007S_FE_42

A company uses 1,000 PCs. The mean failure in 20 days is to be restricted to 2 PCs. How many hours of MTBF are at least required for these PCs? Here, the mean usage time of the PCs is assumed to be 8 hours per day.

a) 8,000
b) 20,000
c) 80,000
d) 160,000
?
c) 80,000

### Explanation
### Explanation
We want the mean number of failures in a 20-day period for 1,000 PCs to be 2. 

First, find the total operating hours for all 1,000 PCs over the 20 days:
Total hours = 1,000 PCs × 20 days × 8 hours/day = 160,000 hours.

Since we expect 2 failures during this total operating time, the MTBF (Mean Time Between Failures) is:
MTBF = Total operating hours / Number of failures = 160,000 hours / 2 = 80,000 hours.

Therefore, at least 80,000 hours of MTBF is required.
