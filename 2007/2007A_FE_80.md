### Explanation
In banking systems, transactions that require immediate updates and verifications (like deposits and withdrawals) must be processed in **Online (real-time) mode** so that the customer's balance is updated immediately and overdrawing is prevented. 

Conversely, calculations that can be delayed and processed on a schedule (like interest calculation, or bulk postings that don't need real-time reflection to individual customers) are handled in **Batch mode**. Thus, the most appropriate combination is:
- Deposit: Online
- Interest calculation: Batch
- Withdrawal: Online
- Posting to account: Batch (often ledger postings are done at end of day, although user balances are updated online)
