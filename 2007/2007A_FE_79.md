### Explanation
The Three-Point Estimate (PERT expected duration) is calculated as: 
**Expected Duration = (Optimistic + 4 × Most Likely + Pessimistic) / 6**

Calculating for each activity:
1. **Requirement understanding:** (1.0 + 4(1.5) + 2.0) / 6 = (1.0 + 6.0 + 2.0) / 6 = 9.0 / 6 = 1.5 hours
2. **Design:** (2.0 + 4(2.5) + 3.0) / 6 = (2.0 + 10.0 + 3.0) / 6 = 15.0 / 6 = 2.5 hours
3. **Coding:** (4.5 + 4(6.5) + 7.0) / 6 = (4.5 + 26.0 + 7.0) / 6 = 37.5 / 6 = 6.25 hours
4. **Testing:** (2.5 + 4(4.5) + 5.0) / 6 = (2.5 + 18.0 + 5.0) / 6 = 25.5 / 6 = 4.25 hours

Total duration = 1.5 + 2.5 + 6.25 + 4.25 = 14.5 hours.
