### Explanation
To obtain exactly 2 liters of water in the 4L jug, we can trace the correct sequence of states `(4L, 3L)`:
1. `(0, 0)`: Both jugs are empty.
2. `(0, 3)`: Fill the 3L jug.
3. `(3, 0)`: Pour all 3 liters from the 3L jug into the 4L jug.
4. `(3, 3)`: Fill the 3L jug again.
5. `(4, 2)`: Pour from the 3L jug into the 4L jug until it is full. Since the 4L jug already has 3 liters, it can only take 1 liter. This leaves exactly 2 liters in the 3L jug.
6. `(0, 2)`: Empty the 4L jug.
7. `(2, 0)`: Pour the remaining 2 liters from the 3L jug into the 4L jug.

This step-by-step logic exactly matches the sequence in choice b.
