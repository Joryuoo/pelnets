# 2007S_FE_7

When you flip a coin four times, what is the probability that it will come up heads exactly twice?

a) 0.2
b) 0.375
c) 0.5
d) 0.625
?
b) 0.375

### Explanation
This is a binomial probability problem.
The probability of getting exactly $k$ successes in $n$ independent Bernoulli trials is given by the formula:
$P(X = k) = \binom{n}{k} \cdot p^k \cdot (1-p)^{n-k}$

Here:
- $n = 4$ (number of flips)
- $k = 2$ (number of heads)
- $p = 0.5$ (probability of getting heads on a fair coin)

Calculate $\binom{4}{2}$:
$\binom{4}{2} = \frac{4!}{2!(4-2)!} = \frac{4 \times 3}{2 \times 1} = 6$

Now, substitute into the formula:
$P(X = 2) = 6 \cdot (0.5)^2 \cdot (0.5)^2 = 6 \cdot (0.5)^4 = 6 \cdot 0.0625 = 0.375$

Thus, the probability is 0.375.
