# 2007A_FE_78

Q78. In a production process of 1,000 units of product X, “material cost” incurred is $10,000, and “direct labor cost” is $20,000.  “Production overhead” is 100% of direct labor cost. “Selling and distribution cost” is $10,000.  How much in dollars is the unit cost of product X? 

a) 10 
b) 30 
c) 50 
d) 60
?
c) 50

### Explanation
The unit product cost in manufacturing includes Direct Materials, Direct Labor, and Production Overhead. Selling and distribution costs are period costs, not product costs.
1. Material cost = $10,000
2. Direct labor cost = $20,000
3. Production overhead = 100% of direct labor cost = $20,000
4. Total production cost = $10,000 + $20,000 + $20,000 = $50,000.
5. Unit cost = Total production cost / Number of units = $50,000 / 1,000 = $50.
