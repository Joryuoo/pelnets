# 2007S_FE_27

What is an advantage of DVD-RAM in comparison with DVD-RW and DVD+RW?

a) Higher access speed
b) Higher capacity
c) More durable rewritable disc
d) Support of dual-layer DVD format
?
c) More durable rewritable disc

### Explanation
DVD-RAM (Digital Versatile Disc - Random Access Memory) is a rewritable optical disc specification known for its high durability and data integrity. It has hardware-based defect management and supports an estimated 100,000 rewrites, making it significantly more robust for repeated data storage compared to DVD-RW and DVD+RW, which generally support only around 1,000 rewrites.
