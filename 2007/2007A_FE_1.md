# 2007A_FE_1

Q1.
What range of decimal numbers can be represented with an 11-bit two's-complement 
number? 
 
a) -2048 to 2047 
b) -2048 to 2048 
c) -1024 to 1023 
d) -1024 to 1024
?
c) -1024 to 1023

### Explanation
An $n$-bit two's-complement number can represent decimal values from $-2^{n-1}$ to $2^{n-1}-1$. 
For an 11-bit number, $n = 11$, so the range is from $-2^{10}$ to $2^{10}-1$.
Since $2^{10} = 1024$, the range is $-1024$ to $1023$.
