# 2007A_FE_8

Q8.
Two players, X and Y play a dice game. A pair of dice is tossed. If the sum of the dice 
is 2, 4, or 6, X wins the game. Otherwise, Y wins. What is the probability that Y wins 
the game? 
 
a) 1/4 
b) 1/3 
c) 1/2 
d) 3/4
?
d) 3/4

### Explanation
When a pair of six-sided dice is tossed, there are $6 \times 6 = 36$ possible outcomes.
Let's find the number of ways X can win (where the sum is 2, 4, or 6):
- Sum = 2: (1,1) $\rightarrow$ 1 way
- Sum = 4: (1,3), (2,2), (3,1) $\rightarrow$ 3 ways
- Sum = 6: (1,5), (2,4), (3,3), (4,2), (5,1) $\rightarrow$ 5 ways
Total ways for X to win = $1 + 3 + 5 = 9$ ways.
The probability that X wins is $P(X) = 9/36 = 1/4$.
The probability that Y wins is the complement: $P(Y) = 1 - P(X) = 1 - 1/4 = 3/4$.
