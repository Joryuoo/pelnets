### Explanation
The problem requires determining the correct Boolean expression that represents the logical OR (union) of the shaded regions in the three Venn diagrams.
Each region in a Venn diagram corresponds to a specific Boolean minterm:
- A region entirely within $x$ but outside $y$ and $z$ is $x \cdot \overline{y} \cdot \overline{z}$.
- A region where multiple sets overlap corresponds to the logical AND of those sets.
By identifying the shaded regions in diagrams 1, 2, and 3 and combining them with the logical OR (+) operator, the resulting expression simplifies to the one given in choice c).
