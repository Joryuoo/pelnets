# 2007S_FE_73

Which of the following is an appropriate statement regarding elements of information security defined in ISO/IEC 17799:2005? 

a) Availability is ensuring that information is accessible only to those authorized to have access. 
b) Confidentiality is ensuring that authorized users have access to information and associated assets when required. 
c) Information security is characterized as the preservation of confidentiality and integrity of information assets, but not its availability. 
d) Integrity is safe-guarding the accuracy and completeness of information and processing methods.
?
d) Integrity is safe-guarding the accuracy and completeness of information and processing methods.

### Explanation
The CIA triad (Confidentiality, Integrity, Availability) forms the core of information security principles.

- **Integrity** safeguards the accuracy and completeness of information and processing methods. (Choice d is correct)
- **Confidentiality** ensures information is accessible only to those authorized to have access. (Choice a mistakenly assigns this definition to Availability)
- **Availability** ensures that authorized users have access to information and associated assets when required. (Choice b mistakenly assigns this definition to Confidentiality)
- Information security is characterized by the preservation of all three elements (Confidentiality, Integrity, AND Availability), making Choice c incorrect.
