# 2007S_FE_3

In a floating-point number format, which of the following is the correct operation for adjusting the radix point and the exponent so that the most significant digit of the mantissa can be a non-zero value? Here, an absolute value is used for the mantissa.

a) Carry
b) Normalize
c) Round down
d) Round up
?
b) Normalize

### Explanation
In floating-point representation, normalization is the process of adjusting the mantissa (or significand) and the exponent so that the most significant digit of the mantissa is non-zero (typically 1 in binary). This ensures a unique representation for each floating-point number and maximizes the precision of the stored value.
- **Normalize:** The correct operation described.
- **Carry:** Related to arithmetic addition overflows, not format adjustment.
- **Round down / Round up:** Operations used to reduce precision to fit the format size, which happen after normalization.
