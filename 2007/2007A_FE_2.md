# 2007A_FE_2

Q2.
Which of the following decimal fractions becomes a finite fraction in an octal 
representation? 
 
a) 0.3 
b) 0.4 
c) 0.5 
d) 0.8
?
c) 0.5

### Explanation
A decimal fraction converts to a finite fraction in a base-$b$ representation if its simplified fraction form has a denominator whose prime factors are only the prime factors of $b$. For octal ($b = 8$), the only prime factor is 2.
- a) $0.3 = 3/10$, denominator has prime factor 5.
- b) $0.4 = 2/5$, denominator has prime factor 5.
- c) $0.5 = 1/2$, denominator has only prime factor 2. Thus, $0.5_{10} = 0.4_8$, which is finite.
- d) $0.8 = 4/5$, denominator has prime factor 5.
