### Explanation
Let's analyze the required overall system availability: $(1-(1-A)^2)^2$.

First, consider a parallel subsystem with two components, each with availability $A$. The unavailability of one component is $(1-A)$. The unavailability of both failing simultaneously is $(1-A)^2$. Thus, the availability of this parallel subsystem is $1 - (1-A)^2$.

The overall formula squares this value: $(1 - (1-A)^2) \times (1 - (1-A)^2)$.

When you multiply the availabilities of two subsystems, it means they are connected in series. Therefore, the required system configuration consists of two identical subsystems connected in series, where each subsystem is a pair of components connected in parallel.

Looking at the options, choice d represents exactly this configuration: two parallel pairs that are connected in series.
