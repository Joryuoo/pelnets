# 2007A_FE_70

Which of the following is the major impact of a Denial of Service (DoS) attack?

a) An increased volume of spam e-mails
b) Degradation of service due to network traffic congestion
c) Falsification of database and/or website
d) Leakage of user ID and password
?
b) Degradation of service due to network traffic congestion

### Explanation
A Denial of Service (DoS) attack aims to disrupt the normal functioning of a targeted server, service, or network by overwhelming the target or its surrounding infrastructure with a flood of Internet traffic. This leads to a degradation or complete loss of service due to resource exhaustion and network congestion.

**Incorrect choices:**
* **a)** Spam emails are a nuisance but typically not the result of a DoS attack.
* **c)** Falsification of data points to integrity attacks (like SQL injection or defacement), not DoS.
* **d)** Leakage of credentials points to confidentiality attacks (like phishing or data breaches), not DoS.
