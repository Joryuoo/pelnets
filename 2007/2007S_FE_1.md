### Explanation
Hexadecimal fraction $0.248_{16}$ can be converted to decimal by multiplying each digit by its corresponding positional weight:
$2 \times 16^{-1} + 4 \times 16^{-2} + 8 \times 16^{-3}$
$= \frac{2}{16} + \frac{4}{256} + \frac{8}{4096}$
$= \frac{1}{8} + \frac{1}{64} + \frac{1}{512}$
Finding a common denominator of 512:
$= \frac{64}{512} + \frac{8}{512} + \frac{1}{512} = \frac{73}{512}$
Therefore, the correct answer is d).
