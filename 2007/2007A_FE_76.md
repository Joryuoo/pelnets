# 2007A_FE_76

Q76. The selling price per unit of product Z is set to $250, and sales for the coming year are expected to be 500 units.  If the company requires a return of 15% in the coming year on its investment of $250,000 in product Z, what is the target variable cost per unit for the coming year?  Here, the fixed cost is $10,000 per annum. 

a) $145 
b) $155 
c) $165 
d) $175
?
b) $155

### Explanation
1. Required return = 15% of $250,000 = $37,500.
2. Total revenue = 500 units * $250 = $125,000.
3. Profit equation: Profit = Total Revenue - Total Fixed Cost - Total Variable Cost.
   $37,500 = $125,000 - $10,000 - Total Variable Cost.
4. Total Variable Cost = $125,000 - $10,000 - $37,500 = $77,500.
5. Target variable cost per unit = Total Variable Cost / Number of units = $77,500 / 500 = $155.
