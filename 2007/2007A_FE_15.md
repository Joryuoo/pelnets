### Explanation
A perfect binary tree of height $h$ (where a tree consisting of just the root node has height 0) has all internal nodes with exactly two children, and all leaves at the exact same depth $h$.

At each depth level $d$, there are $2^d$ nodes.
The total number of nodes $n$ in the tree is the sum of a geometric series:
$n = 2^0 + 2^1 + 2^2 + \dots + 2^h = 2^{h+1} - 1$

Thus, choice c) is the correct relation.
- Choice a is incorrect because rearranging the correct formula gives $h = \log_2(n+1) - 1$.
- Choice b is incorrect because a binary tree can have perfect proper subtrees (e.g., a left leaf and a right subtree of height 1) but not be perfect overall since leaves would be at different depths.
- Choice d is incorrect because the number of internal nodes is $2^h - 1$, while $2^h$ is the number of leaf nodes.
