# 2007S_FE_28

Which of the following is the appropriate combination of 4 color inks or toners for a full-color printer?

a) Aqua, Orange, Brown, and Black
b) Cyan, Magenta, Yellow, and Black
c) Red, Green, Blue, and Black
d) Red, Green, Blue, and Gray
?
b) Cyan, Magenta, Yellow, and Black

### Explanation
Printers use the **CMYK** color model to produce full-color output. The four colors are:
- **C**yan
- **M**agenta
- **Y**ellow
- **K**ey (Black)

This is a subtractive color model used in color printing. In contrast, RGB (Red, Green, Blue) is an additive color model used for light-emitting displays like computer monitors.
