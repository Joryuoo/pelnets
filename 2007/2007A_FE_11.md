# 2007A_FE_11

There are three 7-bit character codes: 30, 3F, and 7A.  When an even parity bit is added to the front of each character code, which of the following shows the correct result?  Here, the character codes are expressed in hexadecimal.

a) 30, 3F, 7A
b) 30, 3F, FA
c) B0, 3F, FA
d) B0, BF, 7A
?
b) 30, 3F, FA

### Explanation
Even parity means the total number of 1s in the binary representation, including the parity bit, must be an even number.
Convert each hexadecimal code to its 7-bit binary representation:
- **30** = `0110000`. It has two 1s (an even number). To maintain even parity, the parity bit is `0`. Result: `00110000` = `30` in hex.
- **3F** = `0111111`. It has six 1s (an even number). To maintain even parity, the parity bit is `0`. Result: `00111111` = `3F` in hex.
- **7A** = `1111010`. It has five 1s (an odd number). To achieve even parity, the parity bit must be `1`. Result: `11111010` = `FA` in hex.

Therefore, the correct sequence is 30, 3F, FA.
