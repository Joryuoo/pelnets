### Explanation
To find the MIPS (Millions of Instructions Per Second), we first need to calculate the average Clock Cycles per Instruction (CPI):
- Data transfer: 5 cycles $\times$ 0.70 = 3.5
- Calculation: 10 cycles $\times$ 0.15 = 1.5
- Decision: 5 cycles $\times$ 0.10 = 0.5
- Jump: 1 cycle $\times$ 0.05 = 0.05

Total Average CPI = $3.5 + 1.5 + 0.5 + 0.05 = 5.55$ clock cycles/instruction.

The clock cycle time is given as 0.01 microseconds ($10^{-8}$ seconds), which means the clock frequency is:
$1 / (10^{-8}\text{ s}) = 100,000,000\text{ Hz} = 100\text{ MHz}$.

The instruction execution rate (Instructions Per Second) is:
$100,000,000 / 5.55 \approx 18,018,018$ instructions per second.

Converting to MIPS (Millions of Instructions Per Second), we get approximately **18.02**.
