# 2007A_FE_9

Q9.
There are a total of 1,900 students in a school, 553 of whom are taking a course in 
computer science, 667 of whom are in mathematics, and 290 of whom are in both 
computer science and mathematics. How many students are not taking a course in 
either computer science or in mathematics? 
 
a) 680 
b) 930 
c) 970 
d) 1260
?
c) 970

### Explanation
By the Principle of Inclusion-Exclusion, the number of students taking at least one of the courses (Computer Science or Mathematics) is:
$|C \cup M| = |C| + |M| - |C \cap M|$
$|C \cup M| = 553 + 667 - 290 = 1220 - 290 = 930$.
The number of students taking neither course is the total number of students minus those taking at least one of the courses:
$1900 - 930 = 970$.
