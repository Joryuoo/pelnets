### Explanation
**MTBF** (Mean Time Between Failures) is the average time a system is operating normally before a failure occurs. It is calculated as the sum of all operating times divided by the total number of failures. Mathematically, this is the sum of $t_i$ from $i=1$ to $n$, divided by $n$.

**MTTR** (Mean Time To Repair) is the average time required to repair a failed system. It is calculated as the sum of all repair times divided by the total number of failures. Mathematically, this is the sum of $r_i$ from $i=1$ to $n$, divided by $n$.

Therefore, choice b correctly represents both formulas:
- MTBF: $\frac{1}{n} \sum_{i=1}^n t_i$
- MTTR: $\frac{1}{n} \sum_{i=1}^n r_i$
