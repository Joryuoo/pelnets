# 2007A_FE_4

Q4.
An integer m is stored in a register as a binary value. If this value is shifted to the left 
by three bits and m is added to the shifted value, how many times as large as m is the 
resulting number? Here, no overflow occurs. 
 
a) 4 
b) 7 
c) 8 
d) 9
?
d) 9

### Explanation
Shifting a binary integer to the left by $k$ bits is mathematically equivalent to multiplying the number by $2^k$.
Shifting $m$ left by 3 bits results in $m \times 2^3 = 8m$.
Adding $m$ to this shifted value gives $8m + m = 9m$.
Thus, the resulting number is 9 times as large as $m$.
