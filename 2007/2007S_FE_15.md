### Explanation
Let $T_b(n)$ and $T_q(n)$ be the execution times for the bubble sort and quick sort, respectively. We are given their time complexities are proportional to $n^2$ and $n \log_2 n$, so $T_b(n) = k_1 n^2$ and $T_q(n) = k_2 n \log_2 n$ for some constants $k_1, k_2$.

For an array of $n = 200$, the times are equal ($T_b(200) = T_q(200)$):
$$k_1 \cdot 200^2 = k_2 \cdot 200 \log_2 200$$
This gives the ratio of the constants: $\frac{k_1}{k_2} = \frac{\log_2 200}{200}$

For $n = 40,000$, we want to find the performance ratio $\frac{T_b(40000)}{T_q(40000)}$:
$$\frac{T_b(40000)}{T_q(40000)} = \frac{k_1 \cdot 40000^2}{k_2 \cdot 40000 \log_2 40000} = \frac{k_1}{k_2} \cdot \frac{40000}{\log_2 40000}$$

Substituting the ratio of constants we found:
$$= \frac{\log_2 200}{200} \cdot \frac{40000}{\log_2 40000} = 200 \cdot \frac{\log_2 200}{\log_2 (200^2)}$$

Using the logarithmic property $\log_2(x^2) = 2 \log_2 x$, we know $\log_2(200^2) = 2 \log_2 200$, so:
$$= 200 \cdot \frac{\log_2 200}{2 \log_2 200} = \frac{200}{2} = 100$$
Quick sort is 100 times faster.
