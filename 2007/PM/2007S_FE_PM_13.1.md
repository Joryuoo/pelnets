**Blank A (b):**
The program needs to generate a mask pattern to isolate the bits to be compared. `GR4` is initialized to `-1` (all 1s in two's complement, i.e., `#FFFF`). The length of the bit pattern is `n`. To compare this pattern starting from the most significant bit, we need `n` 1s at the leftmost bits of the mask and 0s elsewhere. `GR7` contains `16 - n`. By logically shifting `GR4` left by `GR7` (`SLL GR4,0,GR7`), the `#FFFF` mask will have its rightmost `16 - n` bits filled with 0s and its leftmost `n` bits remaining 1s, exactly matching the required mask pattern.

**Blank B (c):**
The search proceeds bit by bit. `GR5` holds a copy of the target word. To check the next window of bits against the left-justified mask and pattern, the word itself must be shifted left by 1 bit in each iteration. `SLL GR5,1` correctly shifts the word to the left so that the next bit is aligned to the most significant bit for the subsequent loop comparison.
