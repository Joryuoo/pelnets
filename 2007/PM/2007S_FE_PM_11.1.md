### Explanation

- **Blank A:** `INITIALIZE VIEW-COUNT-TABLE` (b). Before calculating the view counts and inserting them into `VIEW-COUNT-TABLE`, we must initialize this two-dimensional table to zero.
- **Blank B:** `MOVE "N" TO END-OF-FILE` (c). We need to reset `END-OF-FILE` to 'N' before starting the `READ` loop for `PROGRAM-FILE`.
- **Blank C:** `ADD 1 TO COUNT-OF-MINUTE(VIEW-CHANNEL M)` (a). For every minute in the range of `START-MMMM` to `END-MMMM`, we increment the audience count for the channel viewed by one.
- **Blank D:** `MOVE ZERO TO SUMMATION` (f). Before calculating the rating for a specific program, we must initialize the variable `SUMMATION` to 0, which accumulates the total sum of audience counts during the program's broadcast time.
- **Blank E:** `ADD COUNT-OF-MINUTE(PROG-CHANNEL M) TO SUMMATION` (d). Inside the loop, for each minute `M` in the program's broadcast time, we add the number of viewers watching that channel to the total `SUMMATION`.
- **Blank F:** `100 * SUMMATION / ((END-MMMM - START-MMMM + 1) * SAMPLE-SIZE)` (b). This maps the average audience rating formula into COBOL. The total broadcast length in minutes is inclusive of both the start and end minute, meaning it is `(END-MMMM - START-MMMM + 1)`. The result is then divided by the sample size to get the percentage.
