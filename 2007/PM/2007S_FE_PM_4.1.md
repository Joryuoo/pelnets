The `TowerHanoiGame(n, FROM, INTERMEDIATE, TO)` function is defined to move `n` disks from the `FROM` peg to the `TO` peg, using the `INTERMEDIATE` peg as auxiliary.

According to the rules of Towers of Hanoi, moving `n` disks involves three main steps when `n > 1`:
1. **Blank A (Part 1)**: Move `n-1` disks from `FROM` to `INTERMEDIATE`.
   To achieve this using our function signature, we swap the roles of the `TO` and `INTERMEDIATE` pegs: `TowerHanoiGame(n-1, FROM, TO, INTERMEDIATE)`. This matches choice **d**.
2. **Blank B (Part 2)**: Move the largest disk (1 disk) from `FROM` to `TO`.
   Since this is just moving 1 disk directly, we call `TowerHanoiGame(1, FROM, INTERMEDIATE, TO)`. This matches choice **a**.
3. **Blank C (Part 3)**: Move the `n-1` disks that are now on `INTERMEDIATE` to their final destination `TO`.
   Here, the starting peg is `INTERMEDIATE` and the auxiliary peg is `FROM`. The function call is `TowerHanoiGame(n-1, INTERMEDIATE, FROM, TO)`. This matches choice **e**.
