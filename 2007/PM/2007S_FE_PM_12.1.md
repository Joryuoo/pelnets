### Explanation

- **Blank A:** In `Gate.enter`, the current `Gate` instance (represented by the keyword `this`) needs to be recorded on the ticket as the origin station. Therefore, `ticket.setOrigin(this)` is correct.
  - *Incorrect choices:* `Line.A` or `Line.D` would hardcode the origin to a specific station. `null` would erase the origin. `ticket` is a `Ticket` object, not a `Gate` object.
- **Blank B:** When exiting, the gate must verify if the ticket has enough value to cover the calculated fare. The ticket's balance must be greater than or equal to the fare, so `ticket.getValue() >= fare` is correct.
- **Blank C:** The classes `OneWayTicket` and `PrepaidCard` both inherit from the abstract class `Ticket`. The `Ticket` superclass has a constructor that takes an `int value`. To invoke this superclass constructor and initialize the value, `super(value)` must be used.
