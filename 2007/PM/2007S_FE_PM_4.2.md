- **Move FROM to TO** -> **Blank F (choice b)**

- `H(1, INTERMEDIATE, TO, FROM)` -> **Move INTERMEDIATE to FROM** -> **Blank G (choice c)**
- `Move INTERMEDIATE to TO` -> **Blank H (choice d)**
- `H(1, FROM, INTERMEDIATE, TO)` -> **Move FROM to TO** -> **Blank I (choice b)**
