Merging a sublist into an existing linked list requires updating multiple pointers to "splice" the new elements into their correct sorted positions based on their values. The sublist occupies addresses `0118` through `011D`, which consists of three elements: at `0118` (pointer at `0119`), `011A` (pointer at `011B`), and `011C` (pointer at `011D`).

To insert these three elements into the correct positions in the original list:
- The pointer at address **C** (`0111`) is updated to point to the new element at `011A`.
- The pointer of that new element at address **D** (`011B`) is updated to point to `0102` to continue the original list.
- Another insertion happens where the pointer at address **E** (`0115`) is updated to point to the new element at `011C`.
- Finally, the pointer of the new element at address **F** (`0119`) is updated to point to `0108`.

These specific pointer assignments ensure that all elements from both the original list and the sublist are properly interleaved in ascending order.
