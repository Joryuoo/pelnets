### Explanation

According to the notes preceding Flowchart 2, the distribution process replaces the `*` symbol with a `1` for each delivered issue.

This means a column containing `1` indicates an issue that has already been delivered, whereas a column still containing `*` indicates a future, undelivered issue. If a subscriber discontinues their subscription, they should be reimbursed only for the issues they paid for but have not yet received.

Thus, the reimbursed amount is calculated by multiplying the `UnitPrice` by the number of columns that still contain `*`. Therefore, choice **b** is correct.
