# 2007A_FE_48

Which of the following is the appropriate characteristic of “data oriented design” as compared with “process oriented design?”

a) Data modeling is performed prior to the modeling of business operations.
b) Data oriented design is effective for building a specific business application system in a short period of time.
c) It is possible to consider data as shared resources and to manage it in a unified manner.
d) It is possible to create data structures combined with business processes.
?
c) It is possible to consider data as shared resources and to manage it in a unified manner.

### Explanation
**Data-oriented design** (or data-centric design) focuses on the structure and relationships of the data that an organization uses, treating data as a valuable, independent, and shared corporate resource. Because data structures tend to be more stable over time than business processes (which change frequently), systems designed around data models can manage information in a unified manner and support various, evolving applications.

- **Process-oriented design**, by contrast, focuses primarily on the flow of tasks and functions. It typically results in applications that are highly specialized for a single process, often leading to data silos where data is not easily shared across different systems.
