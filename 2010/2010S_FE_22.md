### Explanation
MTBF (Mean Time Between Failures) is the average time the system is operational between breakdowns. It is calculated as the sum of all operational times ($t_i$) divided by the number of operational periods ($n$): $\frac{1}{n} \sum_{i=1}^n t_i$.

MTTR (Mean Time To Repair) is the average time taken to repair the system. It is calculated as the sum of all repair times ($r_i$) divided by the number of repair periods ($n$): $\frac{1}{n} \sum_{i=1}^n r_i$.

Choice b correctly identifies these two formulas.
