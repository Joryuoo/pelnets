### Explanation
Let's analyze the conditions for the bulb to turn on (Bulb = `1`):
1. **No sunlight:** The light sensor S1 outputs `1` when there is no light, and `0` when there is light. So, we need $S_1 = 1$.
2. **Movement detected:** The infrared sensors S2 and S3 output `0` when movement is detected, and `1` otherwise. The bulb should turn on if *either* detects movement. This means the condition is met when $S_2 = 0$ OR $S_3 = 0$.

A logic gate that outputs `1` when either or both of its inputs are `0` is the **NAND gate** (Not AND). Thus, the movement condition can be represented as $S_2 \text{ NAND } S_3$.

Finally, both conditions (no sunlight AND movement detected) must be met simultaneously, so we pass $S_1$ and the NAND output through an **AND gate**.
The overall boolean logic is: $\text{Bulb} = S_1 \text{ AND } (S_2 \text{ NAND } S_3)$. This matches the circuit diagram in choice d.
