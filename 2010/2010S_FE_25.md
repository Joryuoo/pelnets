### Explanation
We track the 4 page frames for the reference string: 2, 3, 6, 4, 6, 3, 1, 2, 4, 6.

**FIFO (First-In, First-Out):**
- `2, 3, 6, 4`: 4 initial page faults (frames: 2, 3, 6, 4)
- `6, 3`: Both hit.
- `1`: Fault (replaces oldest `2`, frames: 1, 3, 6, 4)
- `2`: Fault (replaces oldest `3`, frames: 1, 2, 6, 4)
- `4, 6`: Both hit.
**Total FIFO faults: 6.**

**LRU (Least Recently Used):**
- `2, 3, 6, 4`: 4 initial page faults (frames: 2, 3, 6, 4)
- `6, 3`: Both hit. (Access order from most to least recent: 3, 6, 4, 2)
- `1`: Fault (replaces least recent `2`, frames: 3, 6, 4, 1)
- `2`: Fault (replaces least recent `4`, frames: 3, 6, 2, 1)
- `4`: Fault (replaces least recent `6`, frames: 3, 4, 2, 1)
- `6`: Fault (replaces least recent `3`, frames: 6, 4, 2, 1)
**Total LRU faults: 4 + 4 = 8.**

Thus, FIFO has 6 faults and LRU has 8 faults.
