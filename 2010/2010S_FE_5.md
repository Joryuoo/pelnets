# 2010S_FE_5

![[2010S_FE_AM_Q5_full.png]]
?
c) w=0, x=1, y=0, z=1

### Explanation
Given the Boolean equations:
1) $x \cdot y = 0$
2) $x \cdot z + w = 1$
3) $x \cdot y + w = 0$

Substitute the value of $x \cdot y$ from equation (1) into equation (3):
$0 + w = 0 \implies w = 0$

Substitute $w = 0$ into equation (2):
$x \cdot z + 0 = 1 \implies x \cdot z = 1$
For a logical product (AND) to be 1, both variables must be 1. Therefore, $x = 1$ and $z = 1$.

Substitute $x = 1$ into equation (1):
$1 \cdot y = 0 \implies y = 0$

Therefore, the correct solution is $w = 0$, $x = 1$, $y = 0$, $z = 1$.
