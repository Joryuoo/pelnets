# 2010S_FE_17

Q17. Which of the following is an optical disc that uses organic dye for the recording layer of 
the storage media and makes the traces of burning by laser light called pits to record the 
data?  
 
a) CD-R 
b) CD-RW 
c) DVD-RAM 
d) DVD-ROM
?
a) CD-R

### Explanation
### Explanation
- **CD-R (Compact Disc-Recordable):** This is a write-once optical disc format. It uses an organic dye as its recording layer. A laser burns the dye to create non-reflective "pits" (leaving reflective "lands"), permanently recording the data.
- **CD-RW (Compact Disc-ReWritable):** Uses a phase-change metal alloy (not an organic dye) that can switch between crystalline and amorphous states to allow rewriting.
- **DVD-RAM:** Also uses a phase-change recording layer for rewriting data.
- **DVD-ROM:** A read-only disc that is pressed with pits at the factory, not burned with a laser by the user.
