In software engineering, good module design aims for High Cohesion (module strength) and Low Coupling. 

* **High Cohesion (Strength):** Ensures that the elements within a module are strongly related and focused on a single task.
* **Low Coupling:** Ensures that modules are as independent as possible, minimizing the impact of changes in one module on others.
