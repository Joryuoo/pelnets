# 2010A_FE_19

Backup sites, which are used during system downtime, are classified into three types: warm, cold, and hot sites. In general, which of the following is the list arranged in order from shortest to longest recovery time?

a) Cold site, warm site, hot site
b) Hot site, warm site, cold site
c) Warm site, cold site, hot site
d) Warm site, hot site, cold site
?
b) Hot site, warm site, cold site

### Explanation
Backup sites provide alternate facilities for disaster recovery, arranged here from the shortest recovery time to the longest:

1. **Hot site (Shortest recovery):** A fully equipped facility with hardware, software, and up-to-date data replicas mirroring the primary site. It can take over operations almost instantly.
2. **Warm site (Medium recovery):** A partially equipped facility with power and hardware, but without fully updated data or running applications. Data must be restored before operations can resume, taking hours to days.
3. **Cold site (Longest recovery):** A basic facility with power and physical space, but no active computing equipment or data. Hardware must be installed and data restored from scratch, which can take days to weeks.

Thus, the correct order is Hot site, Warm site, Cold site.
