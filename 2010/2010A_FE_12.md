### Explanation
To calculate the CPU's performance in MIPS (Million Instructions Per Second), we first determine the average CPI (Clocks Per Instruction).

Based on the table:
- Instruction 1 takes 10 clocks and appears 60% of the time.
- Instruction 2 takes 5 clocks and appears 40% of the time.

$\text{Average CPI} = (10 \times 0.60) + (5 \times 0.40) = 6 + 2 = 8 \text{ clocks per instruction}$.

The CPU clock frequency is 1 GHz ($1 \times 10^9$ cycles per second).

The number of instructions executed per second is:
$\frac{1 \times 10^9 \text{ cycles/second}}{8 \text{ cycles/instruction}} = 125,000,000 \text{ instructions/second}$.

Converting to MIPS (dividing by $10^6$):
$125,000,000 / 10^6 = 125 \text{ MIPS}$.
