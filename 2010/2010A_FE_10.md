### Explanation
To calculate `f(5)`, we trace the recursive calls:
- `f(5)` calls `f(4)` and `f(3)` (1 call to `f(5)`)
  - `f(4)` calls `f(3)` and `f(2)` (1 call to `f(4)`)
    - `f(3)` calls `f(2)` and `f(1)` (1 call to `f(3)`)
      - `f(2)` calls `f(1)` and `f(0)` (1 call to `f(2)`)
        - `f(1)` is a base case (1 call to `f(1)`)
        - `f(0)` is a base case (1 call to `f(0)`)
      - `f(1)` is a base case (1 call to `f(1)`)
    - `f(2)` calls `f(1)` and `f(0)` (1 call to `f(2)`)
      - `f(1)` is a base case (1 call to `f(1)`)
      - `f(0)` is a base case (1 call to `f(0)`)
  - `f(3)` calls `f(2)` and `f(1)` (1 call to `f(3)`)
    - `f(2)` calls `f(1)` and `f(0)` (1 call to `f(2)`)
      - `f(1)` is a base case (1 call to `f(1)`)
      - `f(0)` is a base case (1 call to `f(0)`)
    - `f(1)` is a base case (1 call to `f(1)`)

Counting the calls:
- `f(5)`: 1 time
- `f(4)`: 1 time
- `f(3)`: 2 times
- `f(2)`: 3 times
- `f(1)`: 5 times
- `f(0)`: 3 times
Total calls = 1 + 1 + 2 + 3 + 5 + 3 = 15 times.
