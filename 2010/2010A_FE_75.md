### Explanation
ABC analysis categorizes items based on their total annual sales amount. The total sales amount for each product is obtained by multiplying the annual sales quantity by the unit price.

Based on the provided table:
- Product 2: $60 \times 40 = 2,400$
- Product 5: $50 \times 12 = 600$
- Product 8: $150 \times 2 = 300$
- Product 1: $110 \times 2 = 220$
- Product 4: $130 \times 1 = 130$
...and so on.

The overall total annual sales amount is $3,825$.

In ABC analysis, Group A typically consists of the top items that account for the largest portion (around 70-80%) of the total cumulative sales amount.
If we sum the top two products:
Product 2 ($2,400$) + Product 5 ($600$) = $3,000$.
This accounts for $\frac{3,000}{3,825} \approx 78.4\%$ of the total sales amount.

Therefore, Products 2 and 5 belong in Group A.
