# 2010A_FE_17

Which of the following is an appropriate explanation of TCO?

a) Total cost required for system development
b) Total cost required for system installation
c) Total cost required for the initial investment of a system
d) Total cost required throughout the life cycle of a system
?
d) Total cost required throughout the life cycle of a system

### Explanation
TCO stands for **Total Cost of Ownership**.

It is a comprehensive financial estimate that calculates the direct and indirect costs of a system or product over its entire life cycle. This includes not only the initial purchase or development cost (Choices a, b, c) but also the ongoing costs such as operation, maintenance, training, upgrades, and eventual disposal.

Therefore, Choice (d) correctly explains that it encompasses all costs throughout the system's life cycle.
