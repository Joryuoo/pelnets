# 2010A_FE_59

Q59. Which of the following is the risk response which accepts the cost of damages because risk impact is assumed to be small when the risk becomes exposed?

a) Risk avoidance
b) Risk reduction
c) Risk retention
d) Risk transfer
?
c) Risk retention

### Explanation
### Explanation
Risk retention (or risk acceptance) is a strategy where an organization decides to accept the potential loss from a risk, usually because the cost or effort to mitigate it is greater than the impact of the risk itself.
- **Risk avoidance:** Changing plans to completely eliminate the risk.
- **Risk reduction:** Taking steps to lower the probability or impact of the risk.
- **Risk transfer:** Shifting the impact of the risk to a third party (e.g., insurance).
