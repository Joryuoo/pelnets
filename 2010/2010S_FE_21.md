### Explanation
To find the availability of the system, we calculate the probability that the system is operating normally. The system requires the Server, the LAN, at least one Client, and at least one Printer to operate.

- The availability of the server is $a$.
- The availability of the LAN is $1$.
- The availability of each client is $b$. The probability that all 3 clients fail is $(1 - b)^3$. Therefore, the probability that at least one client operates is $1 - (1 - b)^3$.
- The availability of each printer is $c$. The probability that both printers fail is $(1 - c)^2$. Therefore, the probability that at least one printer operates is $1 - (1 - c)^2$.

Multiplying these probabilities together gives the overall availability:
$a \times (1 - (1 - b)^3) \times (1 - (1 - c)^2) \times 1$. This matches choice d.
