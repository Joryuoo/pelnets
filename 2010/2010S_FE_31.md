### Explanation
A half adder produces sum (z) and carry (c) from two binary inputs x and y. The sum digit is computed using an XOR (Exclusive OR) gate, meaning it is the Exclusive logical sum of x and y. The carry digit is computed using an AND gate, meaning it is the Logical product of x and y. Therefore, element A represents Exclusive logical sum and element B represents Logical product, corresponding to choice a.
