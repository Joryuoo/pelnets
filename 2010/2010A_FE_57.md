# 2010A_FE_57

Q57. Which of the following is the appropriate element that is used to calculate slack time in the PERT (Program Evaluation and Review Technique)?

a) The earliest of the earliest start times of the successor activities
b) The earliest of the latest start times of the successor activities
c) The latest of the earliest start times of the successor activities
d) The latest of the latest start times of the successor activities
?
b) The earliest of the latest start times of the successor activities

### Explanation
### Explanation
Slack time (or float) is the amount of time a task can be delayed without causing a delay to subsequent tasks or the overall project. It is calculated as Late Finish (LF) minus Early Finish (EF). The Late Finish time of an activity is determined by looking at all of its immediate successor activities and taking the earliest (minimum) of their Latest Start (LS) times. This ensures that delaying the activity does not delay any of its dependent successors.
