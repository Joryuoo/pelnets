# 2010S_FE_44

Q44. Which of the following can be achieved by receiving mail text and its hash value from the sender and comparing the hash value with the one calculated from the mail text by the receiver? Here, the hash value from the sender is protected.

a) Confirmation of e-mail delivery
b) Detection of presence or absence of falsification
c) Prevention of spoofing
d) Prevention of tapping
?
b) Detection of presence or absence of falsification

### Explanation
### Explanation
Hashing is primarily used to ensure data integrity. By calculating the hash value of a received email and comparing it to the protected hash value provided by the sender, the receiver can verify if the email text has been altered (falsified) during transit. If the text changes even slightly, its hash value will drastically differ.

*   **a) Confirmation of e-mail delivery:** This would require a delivery receipt mechanism, not hashing.
*   **c) Prevention of spoofing:** This relies on digital signatures (using asymmetric encryption) and proper authentication protocols (like DKIM/SPF) to verify sender identity, not just hashing the text.
*   **d) Prevention of tapping:** This requires encrypting the email contents in transit, whereas hashing only detects if tapping resulted in modification.
