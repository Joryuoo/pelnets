### Explanation
A half adder adds two single binary digits x1 and x2. It has two outputs: sum (s) and carry (c).
The carry output is only 1 when both inputs are 1. This corresponds to the logical AND operation: `c = x1 ⋅ x2`.
The sum output is 1 when exactly one of the inputs is 1. This corresponds to the exclusive OR (XOR) operation. The XOR operation can be represented using AND, OR, and NOT operations as: `s = (x1 ⋅ NOT x2) + (NOT x1 ⋅ x2)`.
Therefore, the correct combination of logical expressions is shown in option c.
