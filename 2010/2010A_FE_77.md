# 2010A_FE_77

Q77. Which of the following is an appropriate description concerning financial indicators?

a) The current ratio is the ratio of current assets to current liabilities. As the value is smaller, safety and soundness are higher.
b) The equity to total asset is the ratio of equity capital to fixed assets. As the value is larger, steadiness and soundness are higher.
c) The fixed ratio is the ratio of fixed assets to fixed liabilities. As the value is smaller, safety and soundness are higher.
d) The total capital profit ratio is the ratio of profit to gross capital. As the value is larger, profitability is higher.
?
d) The total capital profit ratio is the ratio of profit to gross capital.  As the value is larger, profitability is higher.

### Explanation
- Choice a is incorrect because a larger current ratio (Current Assets / Current Liabilities) indicates higher short-term liquidity and soundness, not a smaller value.
- Choice b is incorrect because the equity to total asset ratio (Equity / Total Assets) uses total assets, not fixed assets.
- Choice c is incorrect because the fixed ratio is the ratio of fixed assets to equity capital, not fixed liabilities.
- Choice d is correct. The total capital profit ratio (Return on Assets or Return on Capital) is calculated by dividing profit by gross capital (total assets). A larger value indicates better profitability.
