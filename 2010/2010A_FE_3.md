# 2010A_FE_3

Which of the following prefix expressions is equivalent to the infix expression “(A+B) *C–(D–E)”?

a) – * + A B C – D E
b) – + A B * C – D E
c) A B + C * – D E –
d) A B + C * D E – –
?
a) – * + A B C – D E

### Explanation
To convert the infix expression `(A+B) * C - (D-E)` to prefix notation:
1. First, convert the addition inside the parentheses: `(A+B)` becomes `+ A B`.
2. Next, convert the subtraction inside the parentheses: `(D-E)` becomes `- D E`.
3. The expression is now: `(+ A B) * C - (- D E)`.
4. Apply the multiplication to `(+ A B)` and `C`: `* + A B C`.
5. The expression is now: `(* + A B C) - (- D E)`.
6. Apply the subtraction to the two remaining terms: `- * + A B C - D E`.
This matches option a.
