# 2010S_FE_71

Q71. Which of the following is a document that asks to provide the information necessary for the preparation of a document that asks for proposals?

a) IFB
b) RFI
c) RFP
d) RFQ
?
b) RFI

### Explanation
### Explanation
RFI (Request For Information) is a standard business process whose purpose is to collect written information about the capabilities of various suppliers. It is often used prior to issuing a Request for Proposal (RFP) to gather necessary background information.
- **IFB (Invitation for Bid)**: A call to contractors to submit a proposal on a project for a specific product or service.
- **RFP (Request for Proposal)**: A document that solicits a proposal, often made through a bidding process, by an agency or company interested in procurement of a commodity, service, or valuable asset.
- **RFQ (Request for Quotation)**: A standard business process whose purpose is to invite suppliers into a bidding process to bid on specific products or services.
