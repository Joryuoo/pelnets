# 2010S_FE_77

Q77. When the fixed order quantity model can be used for controlling inventory under the following conditions, what is the Economic Order Quantity (EOQ)? Here, EOQ is the optimal order quantity that theoretically minimizes the total of the cost of ordering and holding inventory. The demand is constant, and does not vary over time.

[Conditions]
Annual demand: 10,000 units
Ordering cost per order: $10
Annual holding cost per unit: $0.2

a) 250
b) 500
c) 1,000
d) 2,000
?
c) 1,000

### Explanation
### Explanation
The Economic Order Quantity (EOQ) formula is given by:
EOQ = sqrt( (2 * D * S) / H )
Where:
- D = Annual demand = 10,000
- S = Ordering cost per order = $10
- H = Annual holding cost per unit = $0.2

Calculation:
EOQ = sqrt( (2 * 10,000 * 10) / 0.2 )
EOQ = sqrt( 200,000 / 0.2 )
EOQ = sqrt( 1,000,000 )
EOQ = 1,000
