# 2010S_FE_4

When the resulting value of the expression $13 \times 16^3 + 11 \times 16^2 + 9 \times 16 + 3$ is represented in binary, how many "1" bits are included in the binary value?

a) 6
b) 10
c) 13
d) 16
?
b) 10

### Explanation
The expression $13 \times 16^3 + 11 \times 16^2 + 9 \times 16^1 + 3 \times 16^0$ is the polynomial expansion of a base-16 (hexadecimal) number.
The coefficients correspond to hexadecimal digits:
- 13 = D
- 11 = B
- 9 = 9
- 3 = 3
So the hexadecimal representation is `DB93`.

Next, convert each hexadecimal digit to its 4-bit binary equivalent and count the "1" bits:
- D (13) = `1101` (three 1s)
- B (11) = `1011` (three 1s)
- 9 = `1001` (two 1s)
- 3 = `0011` (two 1s)

Total number of "1" bits = 3 + 3 + 2 + 2 = 10.
