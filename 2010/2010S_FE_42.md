# 2010S_FE_42

Q42. Which of the following is the signaling protocol that is widely used to initiate, manage, and terminate multimedia communication sessions, such as voice and video calls over the Internet?

a) RTP
b) SIP
c) SMTP
d) SNMP
?
b) SIP

### Explanation
### Explanation
SIP (Session Initiation Protocol) is an application-layer signaling protocol widely used for controlling multimedia communication sessions such as voice and video calls over IP networks.

*   **a) RTP:** Real-time Transport Protocol is used for delivering audio and video over IP networks, but it handles the actual media stream transfer, not the signaling/session management.
*   **c) SMTP:** Simple Mail Transfer Protocol is used for email routing and delivery.
*   **d) SNMP:** Simple Network Management Protocol is used for managing and monitoring network devices.
