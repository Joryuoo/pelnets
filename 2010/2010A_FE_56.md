# 2010A_FE_56

Q56. A painter has 20 houses and 4 shops to paint. In comparison with the shop, the house usually takes half the time to paint. The painter has finished painting 10 houses in 3 days. If the painter continues to work at the same pace, how many days does it take to complete the remaining work?

a) 3.0
b) 3.6
c) 5.4
d) 8.4
?
c) 5.4

### Explanation
### Explanation
1. The painter finished 10 houses in 3 days. The time to paint 1 house is $3 \div 10 = 0.3$ days.
2. A house takes half the time of a shop, so a shop takes $0.3 \times 2 = 0.6$ days to paint.
3. The remaining work consists of 10 houses (since 10 out of 20 are done) and 4 shops.
4. Time to paint remaining 10 houses = $10 \times 0.3 = 3.0$ days.
5. Time to paint 4 shops = $4 \times 0.6 = 2.4$ days.
6. Total remaining time = $3.0 + 2.4 = 5.4$ days.
