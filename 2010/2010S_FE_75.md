# 2010S_FE_75

Q75. Which of the following is “B to C” in electronic commerce?

a) CALS
b) Virtual company
c) Virtual mall
d) Web-EDI
?
c) Virtual mall

### Explanation
### Explanation
B to C (Business-to-Consumer) electronic commerce involves transactions conducted directly between a company and consumers who are the end-users of its products or services. A "Virtual mall" (an online shopping center) is a prime example of B2C.
- **CALS (Continuous Acquisition and Life-cycle Support)** and **Web-EDI (Electronic Data Interchange)** are examples of B2B (Business-to-Business) commerce.
- **Virtual company** refers to an organization that uses technology to link people, assets, and ideas, often partnering with other companies, but it is not inherently a B2C classification.
