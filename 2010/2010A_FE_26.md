### Explanation
The output Z must be 1 only when input lines X and Y have the same value (both 0 or both 1). This describes the behavior of an Exclusive-NOR (XNOR) gate. An XNOR gate is typically depicted as an XOR gate followed by a NOT gate (represented by a small circle at the output).
