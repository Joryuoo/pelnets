### Explanation
A positive correlation coefficient indicates that as the value of the independent variable $x$ increases, the value of the dependent variable $y$ also tends to increase.

Looking at the options:
- Chart a) shows scattered points with no clear correlation.
- Chart b) shows an upward trend where increasing $x$ results in increasing $y$, indicating a positive correlation.
- Chart c) shows a downward trend indicating a negative correlation.
- Chart d) shows a parabolic or non-linear relationship.

Thus, chart b) is the correct answer.
