c) 9!

### Explanation
The game is played on a 3x3 grid, meaning there are 9 spaces in total.
To find the number of possible combinations of moves to fill all nine spaces (regardless of wins and losses), we calculate the number of permutations of all 9 moves.
- The first move can be placed in any of the 9 spaces.
- The second move can be placed in any of the 8 remaining spaces.
- The third move can be placed in any of the 7 remaining spaces.
- This continues down to the last move, which has exactly 1 remaining space.

Therefore, the total number of combinations is $9 \times 8 \times 7 \times \dots \times 1 = 9!$.
