# 2010S_FE_1

When the equation $100_n - 34_n = 44_n$ holds, which of the following represents $26_n$?
Here, each number is written in radix notation; that is, each subscript "n" indicates the radix. In addition, when there is no subscript, the radix 10 is implied.

a) 18
b) 20
c) 22
d) 38
?
c) 22

### Explanation
To solve for the radix $n$, we first expand the equation $100_n - 34_n = 44_n$ into base-10 terms:
- $100_n = 1 \cdot n^2 + 0 \cdot n^1 + 0 \cdot n^0 = n^2$
- $34_n = 3 \cdot n^1 + 4 \cdot n^0 = 3n + 4$
- $44_n = 4 \cdot n^1 + 4 \cdot n^0 = 4n + 4$

Substitute these into the equation:
$n^2 - (3n + 4) = 4n + 4$
$n^2 - 3n - 4 = 4n + 4$
$n^2 - 7n - 8 = 0$

Factor the quadratic equation:
$(n - 8)(n + 1) = 0$

Since a radix must be a positive integer greater than the digits used (the highest digit is 4, so $n > 4$), $n = 8$.
Now we convert $26_8$ to base 10:
$26_8 = 2 \cdot 8^1 + 6 \cdot 8^0 = 16 + 6 = 22$.

The correct answer is 22.
