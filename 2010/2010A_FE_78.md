### Explanation
Under the first-in first-out (FIFO) method, the oldest inventory items are recorded as sold or withdrawn first.
- The initial inventory on April 1 is 2,000 units at $100/unit.
- On April 5, 3,000 units are purchased at $130/unit.
- On April 10, 3,000 units are withdrawn.

According to FIFO, we first withdraw the 2,000 units from the April 1 batch at $100/unit.
The remaining 1,000 units needed for the withdrawal must come from the April 5 batch at $130/unit.

The total cost of the withdrawn units is:
$(2,000 \text{ units} \times \$100) + (1,000 \text{ units} \times \$130) = \$200,000 + \$130,000 = \$330,000$.

The average unit price of the withdrawal is the total cost divided by the total quantity:
$\$330,000 / 3,000 \text{ units} = \$110$.

Thus, the unit price of the withdrawal is 110 dollars.
