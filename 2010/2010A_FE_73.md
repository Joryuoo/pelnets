# 2010A_FE_73

Q73. Which of the following is the most appropriate position that is primarily responsible for centralized oversight of enterprise IT strategic planning?

a) CEO (Chief Executive Officer)
b) CFO (Chief Financial Officer)
c) CIO (Chief Information Officer)
d) COO (Chief Operating Officer)
?
c) CIO (Chief Information Officer)

### Explanation
The Chief Information Officer (CIO) is the executive responsible for the management, implementation, and usability of information and computer technologies. They primarily oversee enterprise IT strategic planning and ensure that IT aligns with business goals.

- The CEO is the highest-ranking executive overall.
- The CFO is responsible for managing the company's finances.
- The COO oversees the day-to-day administrative and operational functions of a business.
