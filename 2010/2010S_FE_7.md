# 2010S_FE_7

When three cards are randomly selected at a time from a standard deck of 52 playing cards, what is the probability that all of these three cards are in the same suit (heart, diamond, spade, or club)?

a) 11/850
b) 11/425
c) 22/425
d) 44/425
?
c) 22/425

### Explanation
A standard deck has 52 cards evenly divided into 4 suits (13 cards per suit).

Step-by-step probability approach:
1. The first card drawn can be any card in the deck, so its probability is 1 (or 52/52).
2. For the second card to be the same suit as the first, there are 12 remaining cards of that suit out of the 51 remaining cards in the deck. Probability = 12/51.
3. For the third card to match, there are 11 remaining cards of that suit out of 50 remaining cards in the deck. Probability = 11/50.

Multiply the probabilities together:
$P = 1 \times \frac{12}{51} \times \frac{11}{50}$
$P = \frac{12}{51} \times \frac{11}{50} = \frac{4}{17} \times \frac{11}{50} = \frac{44}{850} = \frac{22}{425}$.
