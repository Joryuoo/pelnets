### Explanation
A full adder computes the sum of three binary inputs (two significant bits and a carry-in bit) and outputs a sum bit (s) and a carry-out bit (c). The inputs given are x=1, y=0, z=1. The sum is 1 + 0 + 1 = 2 in decimal, which is 10 in binary. Therefore, the sum bit `s` is 0 and the carry-out bit `c` is 1.
