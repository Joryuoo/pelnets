# 2010S_FE_79

Q79. Which of the following appropriately explains “patent right” according to the WTO’s Agreement on Trade-Related Aspects of Intellectual Property Rights (TRIPS)?

a) It is a right of an author of a literary, scientific, artistic, or musical work to exclusively control this work and to receive benefits from it.
b) It is a right to exclusively utilize a new invention suitable for industrial use, which is granted upon application to and examination by the competent authority.
c) It is a right to exclusively utilize an indication which enables a company to distinguish its own products from those of other companies.
d) It is a right to exclusively utilize an ornamental innovation that is applied to the shape, color, pattern, or layout of a new art, craft, or industrial product.
?
b) It is a right to exclusively utilize a new invention suitable for industrial use, which is granted upon application to and examination by the competent authority.

### Explanation
### Explanation
A patent right protects inventions that are new, involve an inventive step, and are capable of industrial application.
- **Choice a** describes a **copyright**.
- **Choice c** describes a **trademark**.
- **Choice d** describes a **design right** (industrial design).
