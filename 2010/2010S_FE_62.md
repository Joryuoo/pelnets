# 2010S_FE_62

![[2010S_FE_AM_Q62_full.png]]
?
c)

### Explanation
The ID format XXNNN gives a total possible combinations of:
26 letters × 26 letters × 10 numbers × 10 numbers × 10 numbers = 676,000 IDs.

The number of issued IDs is increasing linearly:
- Fiscal year 2006 to 2007: 383,000 - 317,000 = 66,000
- Fiscal year 2007 to 2008: 447,000 - 383,000 = 64,000
- Fiscal year 2008 to 2009: 512,000 - 447,000 = 65,000

The average increase is approximately 65,000 per year.
The difference between the maximum capacity and the current usage (2009) is:
676,000 - 512,000 = 164,000.

Time until exhaustion = 164,000 / 65,000 ≈ 2.52 years.

So they will be exhausted roughly 2.52 years after 2009, which falls in fiscal year 2012. Thus, the correct answer is c.
