### Explanation

- **Blank D:** The problem description specifically states: *"In this calculation, any decimal parts are rounded off, and the rounding errors are corrected by the required amount of milk from dairy farm L."* To ensure that the sum of the required amounts from farms J, K, and L perfectly equals the total required amount (despite truncating the amounts for J and K), the amount for farm L (`wl`) must be calculated by subtracting the already calculated amounts for J (`wj`) and K (`wk`) from the total required amount. Therefore, the correct formula is `required amount of milk - wj - wk`, which corresponds to **b)**.

*Why others are wrong:*
- **d) and e)** would simply calculate the theoretical amount for L using standard multiplication and division (which could introduce its own rounding error), rather than absorbing the rounding errors of J and K.
