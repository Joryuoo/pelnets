To find the record with the second largest sales quantity, we must first identify the largest quantity, exclude it, and then find the largest quantity among the remaining records.

- The innermost subquery `(Select max(Qty) From TripSales)` finds the absolute maximum sales quantity.
- By adding the condition `Where Qty not in (Select max(Qty) From TripSales)`, we filter out the highest value from consideration.
- The subquery `Select max(Qty) From TripSales Where Qty not in (...)` then correctly evaluates to the maximum of the *remaining* values, which is the second largest quantity overall.
- The main query uses this value in the `WHERE Qty in (...)` clause to select the corresponding record(s).

Options (a) and (b) are incorrect because they select a list of all quantities excluding the maximum, rather than a single second-highest value. Option (c) is incorrect because it simply returns the absolute maximum quantity.
