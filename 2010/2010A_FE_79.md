# 2010A_FE_79

Q79. Which of the following is a law that protects coded expressions of programs rather than ideas or algorithms in programs?

a) Copyright law
b) Industrial design law
c) Patent law
d) Trademark law
?
a) Copyright law

### Explanation
Copyright law protects the specific "expression" of ideas, which includes the source code and object code of software programs. It prevents unauthorized copying of the code but does not protect the underlying ideas, algorithms, or methods of operation.

- Patent law protects novel and non-obvious inventions, which can include the underlying algorithms and methods themselves, not just their expression.
- Industrial design law protects the visual design and aesthetic aspects of objects.
- Trademark law protects logos, names, and brands used to identify goods or services.
