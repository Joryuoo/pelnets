# 2010A_FE_72

Q72. Which of the following is a mechanism used in electronic commerce to exchange data between companies?

a) CA
b) EDI
c) SSL
d) XBRL
?
b) EDI

### Explanation
EDI (Electronic Data Interchange) is a standard format for exchanging business data (such as purchase orders and invoices) between companies electronically, making it a foundational mechanism for B2B electronic commerce.

- CA (Certificate Authority) is an entity that issues digital certificates.
- SSL (Secure Sockets Layer) is a cryptographic protocol for securing communications over a network.
- XBRL (eXtensible Business Reporting Language) is a framework for exchanging business reporting information, but EDI is the broader mechanism specifically aimed at exchanging transactional business data.
