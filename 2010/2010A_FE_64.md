# 2010A_FE_64

Which of the following is an appropriate responsibility of a system auditor for problems found during an audit? 

a) Correcting on his or her own the problems found  
b) Instructing the audited department to correct the problems found 
c) Reporting the problems found to the audit requester  
d) Reporting the problems found to the user departments of the system
?
c) Reporting the problems found to the audit requester

### Explanation
A system auditor must remain independent and objective. Their primary role is to evaluate systems and report their findings.

- c) is correct. The auditor reports the issues to the party that requested the audit (e.g., top management or board of directors).
- a) is incorrect because an auditor should not implement fixes themselves; this breaks their independence.
- b) is incorrect because auditors advise and report; they do not have direct authority to instruct departments on operations.
- d) is incorrect because the official report goes to the audit requester, who then decides how to handle the findings with the respective departments.
