### Explanation
To calculate the overall goal achievement level in percentage:

1. Calculate the maximum possible score:
- Maximum score per item = 3
- Total max score = (5 * 3) + (8 * 3) + (12 * 3) = 15 + 24 + 36 = 75

2. Calculate the actual score based on achievements:
- Labor saving: "Improved as planned" -> score 3. Weighted score = 5 * 3 = 15
- Time saving: "No change" -> score 0. Weighted score = 8 * 0 = 0
- Information integration: "Partially improved" -> score 1. Weighted score = 12 * 1 = 12
- Total actual score = 15 + 0 + 12 = 27

3. Calculate percentage:
- Goal achievement level = (Actual Score / Max Score) * 100
- Goal achievement level = (27 / 75) * 100 = 36%
