# 2010A_FE_63

Which of the following is an effective measure to protect information systems from overvoltage caused by lightning? 

a) Connecting communication cables to computers via SPD (Surge Protection Device) 
b) Digitizing the control circuits of power supply facilities 
c) Preparing a private power generator 
d) Using two communication lines that pass through different routes
?
a) Connecting communication cables to computers via SPD (Surge Protection Device)

### Explanation
Lightning strikes can cause sudden high-voltage spikes (surges) on communication and power lines.

- a) is the correct measure. An SPD (Surge Protection Device) redirects or absorbs the excess voltage, protecting the connected equipment.
- b) does not protect against physical voltage surges.
- c) ensures continuous power during an outage but doesn't protect from surges.
- d) provides redundancy for communication but both lines could still be susceptible to lightning strikes if not protected by an SPD.
