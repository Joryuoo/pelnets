# 2010A_FE_80

Q80. Which of the following sets of standards should be applied for the purpose of developing the new products that are expected to be employed by government agencies of countries who are members of the WTO?

a) ANSI standards
b) FCC standards
c) IEEE standards
d) ISO standards
?
d) ISO standards

### Explanation
The World Trade Organization (WTO) Agreement on Technical Barriers to Trade (TBT) encourages members to use international standards as a basis for their technical regulations to avoid creating unnecessary obstacles to international trade.

The International Organization for Standardization (ISO) develops such international standards that are recognized and applied globally among WTO member countries.

- ANSI (American National Standards Institute) and FCC (Federal Communications Commission) are U.S.-specific standards organizations.
- IEEE is an international professional association that creates standards (mainly in electrical engineering and computer science), but ISO is the primary global standard body referenced for universal product standards among WTO members.
