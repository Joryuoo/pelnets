# 2010A_FE_40

Q40. Which of the following protocols can be used to access e-mail messages that are stored on a remote, and possibly shared, mail server, and thereby are manipulated from a desktop computer at home, a workstation at the office, and a notebook computer while traveling, without the need to transfer messages back and forth between these computers?

a) FTP
b) IMAP
c) POP3
d) SMTP
?
b) IMAP

### Explanation
IMAP (Internet Message Access Protocol) allows a client to view and manage email messages directly on the mail server. Because the messages remain on the server, actions such as reading, moving, or deleting emails are synced across all clients. This makes it the ideal protocol for accessing a single email account from multiple devices (like a desktop, office workstation, and a notebook).
- POP3 (Post Office Protocol version 3) is an older email retrieval protocol that typically downloads emails to the local device and then deletes them from the server, making it difficult to keep emails synchronized across multiple computers.
- SMTP (Simple Mail Transfer Protocol) is used for sending emails, not receiving or accessing them.
- FTP (File Transfer Protocol) is used for transferring files between a client and a server, unrelated to email management.
