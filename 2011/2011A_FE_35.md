# 2011A_FE_35

Q35. Which of the following is an appropriate set of characteristics of a data warehouse in comparison with traditional databases?

a) Integrated, non-subject-oriented, volatile, and time-variant
b) Integrated, subject-oriented, non-volatile, and time-variant
c) Integrated, subject-oriented, volatile, and time-invariant
d) Non-integrated, subject-oriented, volatile, and time-variant
?
b) Integrated, subject-oriented, non-volatile, and time-variant

### Explanation
A data warehouse is defined by William Inmon (the father of the data warehouse) with four key characteristics:
1. **Subject-oriented:** Data is organized around major subjects (e.g., sales, customers) rather than specific applications.
2. **Integrated:** Data is gathered from various disparate sources and unified.
3. **Time-variant (or Time-series):** Data is maintained over a historical time perspective (not just current data).
4. **Non-volatile:** Once data enters the warehouse, it is not modified or deleted (updates are typically appended as new records), unlike traditional operational databases which are highly volatile due to continuous CRUD operations.

Therefore, option b is the correct set of characteristics.
