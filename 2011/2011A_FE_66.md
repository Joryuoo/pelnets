# 2011A_FE_66

Which of the following is an appropriate explanation of SCM (Supply Chain Management)?

a) It is a business concept for managing customer information collected through various channels in a centralized manner and for building closer ties with customers by using such information.
b) It is a business process for managing the series of business operations linking purchase, production, sale, and distribution from the viewpoint of total optimization among companies in order to reduce delivery lead time and stock.
c) It is a business system for managing the skills and behavioral characteristics of employees and for implementing appropriate personnel allocation and evaluation from the viewpoint of personnel strategy.
d) It is a systematic approach to sharing skills, experiences, and best practices across work units, jobs, and locations, including the effective use of available data and organizational learning.
?
b) It is a business process for managing the series of business operations linking purchase, production, sale, and distribution from the viewpoint of total optimization among companies in order to reduce delivery lead time and stock.

### Explanation
SCM (Supply Chain Management) optimizes the entire flow of materials, information, and finances from supplier to manufacturer to wholesaler to retailer to consumer. Its goal is to improve efficiency, reduce inventory costs, and shorten lead times.

- **a** describes CRM (Customer Relationship Management).
- **c** describes HRM (Human Resource Management).
- **d** describes KM (Knowledge Management).
