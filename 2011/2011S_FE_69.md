# 2011S_FE_69

Among the costs related to computerization, which of the following is included in the running cost?

a) Consulting cost for developing information systems strategy
b) Cost of systems engineers who define requirements
c) Installation cost of software packages
d) Maintenance cost for information equipment such as servers
?
d) Maintenance cost for information equipment such as servers

### Explanation
Running costs recur while an information system is in service, including maintenance of servers and related equipment. Consulting, requirements work, and initial installation are development or setup costs.

Sort costs by timing: strategy consulting (a) and requirements work (b) occur before operation; package installation (c) is setup; equipment maintenance (d) recurs while the system runs. That recurring nature makes (d) a running cost.
