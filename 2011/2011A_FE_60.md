# 2011A_FE_60

An IT service is provided under the conditions described below. In order to meet the SLA, the maximum allowable downtime should be less than how many hours during the service hours per month? Here, the number of business days in a month is 30.
[Conditions of the SLA]
- Service hours: 7 a.m. to 9 p.m. (i.e. 14 hours per day) on each business day
- Availability:
99.5% or more

a) 1
b) 2
c) 3
d) 4
?
b) 2

### Explanation
### Explanation
1. Calculate the total service hours per month:
   - Service hours per day = 14 hours.
   - Business days per month = 30 days.
   - Total service hours = $14 \text{ hours/day} \times 30 \text{ days} = 420 \text{ hours}$.
2. Calculate the minimum required uptime based on 99.5% availability:
   - Availability = (Uptime / Total Service Time) $\times$ 100
   - Uptime required = $420 \text{ hours} \times 0.995 = 417.9 \text{ hours}$.
3. Calculate the maximum allowable downtime:
   - Maximum Downtime = Total Service Time - Uptime
   - Maximum Downtime = $420 - 417.9 = 2.1 \text{ hours}$.
Since the maximum allowable downtime must be less than 2.1 hours, the closest choice that is less than or equal to 2.1 without violating the condition from the options is 2 hours. Looking at the options, the exact cutoff is 2.1, so the maximum allowable from the choices that is just under 2.1 is roughly 2 (or slightly above, meaning it must be less than 2.1. The question asks 'less than how many hours', if it was less than 3, it could be 2.5 which is > 2.1. If less than 2, then we are safe.
