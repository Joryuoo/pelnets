# 2011A_FE_70

The marketing strategies are categorized into four basic types: leader, challenger, follower, and nicher.  The market follower is a firm that is not a market leader and that does not attempt to be a market leader.  In addition, the follower can often find the reinvention of ideas easier than the originators.  Which of the following is the basic strategy of the follower as a competitive position?

a) All-around strategy for covering the entire market and ensuring the largest share
b) Differentiation strategy needed to attack the leader in pursuit of market share
c) Imitation strategy for quickly responding to market opportunities
d) Specialization strategy for focusing on a specific product or market segment
?
c) Imitation strategy for quickly responding to market opportunities

### Explanation
In marketing strategy (specifically Philip Kotler's competitive market strategies), companies are categorized by their market position:

- **Leader**: Uses an all-around strategy to cover the entire market and maintain the largest share (matches choice a).
- **Challenger**: Uses a differentiation strategy to attack the leader and expand its market share (matches choice b).
- **Follower**: Uses an imitation strategy to quickly copy successful products and respond to market opportunities with lower risk and R&D costs (matches choice c).
- **Nicher**: Uses a specialization strategy to focus on a specific, narrow market segment where it can dominate (matches choice d).

Since the question asks for the strategy of a market follower, the imitation strategy is correct.
