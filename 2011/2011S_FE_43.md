# 2011S_FE_43

Which of the following is an appropriate description concerning the PIN (Personal Identification Number) of an IC card used for user authentication?

a) Every PIN is set by combining the number information stamped on the surface of an IC card.
b) Since each user can be identified with an IC card, all users have a common PIN to reduce administrative burden.
c) When an IC card is lost, a new one is issued and a PIN is set. After that, the lost IC card is canceled.
d) When an IC card is sent, its PIN is not sent under the same cover. The user is notified of the PIN in another way.
?
d) When an IC card is sent, its PIN is not sent under the same cover. The user is notified of the PIN in another way.

### Explanation
Sending the IC card and its PIN separately ensures two-factor authentication (something you have and something you know) remains secure during transit. If they were sent together, an interceptor could immediately use the card.

Why the other choices are wrong:
- **a)** Deriving a PIN from public information on the card makes it easily guessable by anyone who sees or steals the card.
- **b)** A common PIN across all users weakens security significantly, as knowing the PIN compromises everyone's authentication factor.
- **c)** A lost IC card must be canceled immediately to prevent unauthorized use, prior to or simultaneously with issuing a new one, not after.
