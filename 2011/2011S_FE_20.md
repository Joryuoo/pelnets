### Explanation
**Availability** is the proportion of time a system is functioning correctly and available for use. 
It is calculated by dividing the average uptime (MTBF) by the total cycle time of uptime and downtime (MTBF + MTTR).

$$ \text{Availability} = \frac{\text{MTBF}}{\text{MTBF} + \text{MTTR}} $$

* **MTBF (Mean Time Between Failures):** The average time the system is operational.
* **MTTR (Mean Time To Repair):** The average time required to repair a failed system.
* Choices c and d represent time durations, not a proportion. Choice b represents the total cycle time.
