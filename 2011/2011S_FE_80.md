# 2011S_FE_80

Which of the following explains the characteristic of the break-even point?

a) The sales at the break-even point are equal to the sum of the fixed cost and variable cost.
b) When the fixed cost remains unchanged, the break-even point rises if the ratio of the variable cost lowers.
c) When the fixed cost remains unchanged, the change in the ratio of the variable cost is directly proportional to the change in the break-even point.
d) When the ratio of the variable cost remains unchanged, the break-even point rises if the fixed cost is reduced.
?
a) The sales at the break-even point are equal to the sum of the fixed cost and variable cost.

### Explanation
The break-even point is the level of sales where total revenue equals total costs, meaning the business makes exactly zero profit. Total costs are the sum of fixed and variable costs.

- **a)** Correct. At break-even, Sales = Fixed Cost + Variable Cost.
- **b)** Incorrect. If the variable cost ratio lowers, the contribution margin increases, meaning fewer sales are needed to cover fixed costs. Therefore, the break-even point falls, not rises.
- **c)** Incorrect. Break-even sales = Fixed Costs / (1 - Variable Cost Ratio). The relationship is not directly proportional; as the variable ratio changes, the denominator changes, making it a non-linear inverse relationship with the contribution margin.
- **d)** Incorrect. If fixed costs are reduced while the variable cost ratio remains unchanged, less revenue is required to cover those fixed costs, so the break-even point falls, not rises.
