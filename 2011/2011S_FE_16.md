# 2011S_FE_16

When a PC is equipped with a sound card, which of the following ports can be used for enabling the PC to communicate and synchronize with electronic musical instruments, such as synthesizers and drum machines?

a) DVI
b) HDMI
c) MIDI
d) RJ45
?
c) MIDI

### Explanation
**MIDI** (Musical Instrument Digital Interface) is the standard protocol and interface for exchanging musical performance messages and synchronizing electronic musical instruments.

* **a) DVI:** Digital Visual Interface, used for transmitting video displays.
* **b) HDMI:** High-Definition Multimedia Interface, used for transmitting both audio and video signals to displays.
* **d) RJ45:** The standard connector for Ethernet network cables.
