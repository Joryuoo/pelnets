# 2011A_FE_7

A leap year in the Gregorian calendar includes an extra day at the end of February. Any year that is divisible by 4 and does not end in 00 is a leap year. Years ending in 00 are leap years only if they are divisible by 400. How many leap years are there between 1895 and 2011 in the Gregorian calendar?

a) 27
b) 28
c) 29
d) 30
?
b) 28

### Explanation
Let's count the leap years in the range 1895 to 2011.
First, find the number of years divisible by 4 in this period.
The first multiple of 4 after 1895 is 1896, and the last one before 2011 is 2008.
The number of multiples of 4 is:
`(2008 - 1896) / 4 + 1 = 112 / 4 + 1 = 28 + 1 = 29`.

Next, we apply the rule for century years (ending in 00). In this range, there are two century years: 1900 and 2000.
- `1900` is divisible by 4 but NOT by 400. Therefore, 1900 is NOT a leap year. We must subtract 1 from our count.
- `2000` is divisible by 4 and also by 400. Therefore, 2000 IS a leap year.

Total leap years = 29 (initial count) - 1 (for 1900) = 28.
Therefore, the correct answer is (b) 28.
