### Explanation
In graph theory and networking, a **spanning tree** of a connected undirected graph is a subgraph that includes all the vertices of the original graph, is connected, and has no cycles (loops).
- Choice (a) contains disconnected elements or cycles depending on interpretation of specific diagrams.
- Choice (b) contains a cycle or leaves some nodes disconnected.
- Choice (c) represents a valid tree structure (no cycles) connecting all nodes.
- Choice (d) contains cycles.
Hence, only (c) is a spanning tree.
