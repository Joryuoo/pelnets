# 2011A_FE_77

Which of the following is the appropriate rule for "brainstorming" that is a method of resolving problems?

a) Participants should avoid presenting better ideas based on another participant's.
b) Participants should criticize another participant's ideas without hesitation.
c) Participants should listen to free-wheeling opinions, including unique ideas.
d) Participants should select and present only high-quality ideas among their own ideas.
?
c) Participants should listen to free-wheeling opinions, including unique ideas.

### Explanation
### Explanation
Brainstorming adheres to four main rules to encourage creative thinking:
1. **Encourage free-wheeling (c):** Welcome unusual or wild ideas.
2. **No criticism (contradicts b):** Judgment is suspended until the end.
3. **Quantity over quality (contradicts d):** Generate as many ideas as possible.
4. **Piggyback on others' ideas (contradicts a):** Combine and improve upon ideas presented by others.
