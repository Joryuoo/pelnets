# 2011S_FE_67

Which of the following is the activity that is performed in the phase of requirements definition?

a) Creating a policy on the computerization needed to achieve business objectives and targets
b) Developing software that meets the needs described by the customer
c) Identifying the specifications of the business operations and system to be newly established and clarifying the scope of computerization
d) Operating software products and providing users with operations support
?
c) Identifying the specifications of the business operations and system to be newly established and clarifying the scope of computerization

### Explanation
Requirements definition turns business needs into the functions, specifications, and scope of the proposed system. Strategy, implementation, and operational support occur in other phases.

Requirements definition specifies **what** the new business operations and system must provide and where computerization starts and stops. Policy/strategy formation (a) occurs earlier; coding (b) follows specification; operation and user support (d) follow deployment.
