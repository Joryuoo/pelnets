### Explanation
The original circuit computes `(A AND B) OR (NOT B)`. Using Boolean algebra, this can be simplified:
`(A AND B) OR (NOT B)` = `(A OR NOT B) AND (B OR NOT B)` = `(A OR NOT B) AND 1` = `A OR NOT B`. This simplified expression corresponds to option b.
