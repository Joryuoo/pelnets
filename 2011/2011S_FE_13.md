### Explanation
Based on the provided von Neumann architecture diagram:
* **A is the Control Unit:** The dashed arrows representing control signals originate from A and direct the other components.
* **B is the Operation Unit (ALU):** It handles the arithmetic and logical operations within the CPU, receiving data from memory.
* **C is the Main Memory:** It sits on the data path between the input/output devices and the CPU, storing instructions and data.

The other choices misidentify these core components and their relationships as depicted by the control and data flow arrows.
