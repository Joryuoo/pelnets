# 2011S_FE_78

![[2011S_FE_AM_Q78_full.png]]
?
b) 600

### Explanation
We need to calculate the net requirement for part **b** based on the bill of materials and current stock levels.

1. **Calculate net requirements for A:**
   300 A needed − 100 A in stock = 200 A to build.
2. **Calculate requirements for components of A:**
   - To build 200 A, we directly need `200 × 2 = 400` units of **b**.
   - We also need `200 × 3 = 600` units of **a**.
3. **Calculate net requirements for a:**
   600 **a** needed − 100 **a** in stock = 500 **a** to build.
4. **Calculate additional requirements for b (to build a):**
   - To build 500 **a**, we need `500 × 1 = 500` units of **b**.
5. **Calculate total net requirements for b:**
   - Gross **b** needed: 400 (direct for A) + 500 (for a) = 900 **b**.
   - Net **b** needed: 900 gross − 300 **b** in stock = 600 **b**.

- **b)** Correct.
- **a, c, d)** Incorrect. These choices likely result from omitting one of the stock deductions or incorrectly multiplying component levels.
