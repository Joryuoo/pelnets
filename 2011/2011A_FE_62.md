# 2011A_FE_62

When a hearing is conducted during a system audit, which of the following is the appropriate action to be taken by a system auditor?

a) When a difference of opinion arises with the auditee department during the hearing, thorough consultations are conducted until the audited party is convinced.
b) When a problematic matter is found as a result of the hearing, backup data is collected and on-site investigations are conducted.
c) When the auditee department has several persons subject to the hearing, the auditor intensively hears from the person of the highest rank.
d) When the current status based on the hearing is regarded as a problem, some technical advice on the ideal status is given to the auditee department.
?
b) When a problematic matter is found as a result of the hearing, backup data is collected and on-site investigations are conducted.

### Explanation
In a system audit, a hearing (or interview) is a method to gather information. If a problematic matter is identified, the auditor must obtain objective evidence to corroborate the findings, such as by collecting backup data and conducting on-site investigations.

- **a is incorrect** because an auditor's job is not to force an agreement or "convince" the auditee through argument, but rather to report objective findings.
- **c is incorrect** because hearings should involve the personnel who actually perform the operational work to understand the reality, not just the highest-ranking official.
- **d is incorrect** because the auditor's primary role is to evaluate and point out issues, not to provide technical implementation advice or consulting services (which could compromise their independence).
