# 2011S_FE_2

![[2011S_FE_AM_Q2_full.png]]
?
c) (A ∪ B ∪ C) − (A ⊕ B ⊕ C)

### Explanation
The shaded regions belong to exactly two of the three sets. The union includes every point in any circle; removing the exclusive-or region, which contains points in exactly one or three sets, leaves only the pairwise-only overlaps.

A useful membership check is:

| Region | Shaded? | Retained by c? |
|---|---|---|
| In exactly one circle | No | No |
| In exactly two circles | Yes | Yes |
| In all three circles | No | No |

Option a includes the three-way center as well. Option b keeps the center rather than the pairwise-only regions; option d leaves the single-circle regions after removing only the center.
