# 2011A_FE_46

Which of the following is a collection of infected computers that have been compromised without their owners realizing it and are being remotely controlled by attackers, in order to typically send spam or attack other computers?

a) Botnet
b) Honeynet
c) Rootkit
d) Web beacon
?
a) Botnet

### Explanation
### Explanation
A **Botnet** is a network of compromised computers (often referred to as "zombies") infected with malware. The attacker (the "botmaster") remotely controls this network via command-and-control servers to perform malicious tasks in bulk, such as launching Distributed Denial of Service (DDoS) attacks or sending spam e-mails.

**Why other options are wrong:**
- **b) Honeynet**: A network of decoy systems (honeypots) set up by security researchers or defenders to trap attackers and study their methods.
- **c) Rootkit**: Malware designed to hide its presence and maintain privileged (root/administrator) access to a compromised system.
- **d) Web beacon**: A small, often invisible image embedded in a webpage or email used to track user activity, commonly used in analytics and marketing.
