# 2011A_FE_45

Which of the following is a technique of social engineering that obtains sensitive or confidential data by searching for residual data left in a computer after job execution or by examining discarded or stolen media such as printed papers, tapes, and discs?

a) Salami technique
b) Scavenging
c) Sniffing
d) Spoofing
?
b) Scavenging

### Explanation
### Explanation
**Scavenging** (also known as Dumpster Diving) is a social engineering or physical security attack where the attacker searches through discarded materials, trash, or leftover residual data (such as un-wiped hard drives or printed papers) to find sensitive information.

**Why other options are wrong:**
- **a) Salami technique**: A financial fraud technique where an attacker steals tiny fractions of money (like rounding off fractions of a cent) across many transactions.
- **c) Sniffing**: Intercepting and reading network traffic by monitoring data packets sent over a network.
- **d) Spoofing**: Masquerading or impersonating another person, device, or IP address to gain unauthorized access or trick a user.
