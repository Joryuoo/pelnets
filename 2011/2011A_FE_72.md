# 2011A_FE_72

Which of the following is a type of information system that helps companies change or otherwise alter their business strategy and structure and thus create a competitive advantage?

a) DSS
b) KMS
c) MIS
d) SIS
?
d) SIS

### Explanation
### Explanation
- **SIS (Strategic Information System):** A system designed to support or change a company's business strategy, directly contributing to competitive advantage (d).
- **DSS (Decision Support System):** Assists management in making decisions by analyzing data.
- **KMS (Knowledge Management System):** Facilitates capturing, sharing, and utilizing organizational knowledge.
- **MIS (Management Information System):** Provides routine reports and information to managers for operational control.
