# 2011S_FE_45

Which of the following is an explanation of a risk transfer?

a) Breaking down or aggregating risks into components that can be easily handled
b) Eliminating risk factors
c) Reducing the incidence of losses
d) Taking financial measures such as purchasing insurance
?
d) Taking financial measures such as purchasing insurance

### Explanation
Risk transfer involves shifting the financial consequences of a risk to a third party. Purchasing insurance is a classic example of transferring the financial impact of a loss to an insurance company.

Why the other choices are wrong:
- **a)** Breaking down risks is part of risk analysis or risk identification, not a treatment strategy.
- **b)** Eliminating risk factors is known as risk avoidance, where actions are taken to completely remove the threat.
- **c)** Reducing the incidence or impact of losses is known as risk mitigation (or reduction).
