# 2011A_FE_57

It takes 36 days for Mr. A to complete a certain task, and 18 days for Mr. B to complete the same task. When Mr. A performs the task together with Mr. B, approximately how many days does it take to complete the task? Here, the task can be divided and performed in parallel, and both Mr. A and Mr. B can achieve an average working efficiency of 90 % every day during the cooperative task.

a) 11
b) 12
c) 13
d) 14
?
d) 14

### Explanation
### Explanation
1. Calculate the daily work rate for each person (let the total work be 1 unit):
   - Mr. A's rate: $1 / 36$ per day
   - Mr. B's rate: $1 / 18$ per day
2. Calculate their combined rate when working together at 100% efficiency:
   - Combined rate = $(1 / 36) + (1 / 18) = (1 / 36) + (2 / 36) = 3 / 36 = 1 / 12$ per day.
3. Adjust for their 90% average working efficiency:
   - Effective combined rate = $(1 / 12) \times 0.9 = 0.9 / 12 = 9 / 120 = 3 / 40$ per day.
4. Calculate the days required to complete the task:
   - Time = $1 / (Effective\ rate) = 1 / (3 / 40) = 40 / 3 = 13.33$ days.
Rounding up to ensure completion yields approximately 14 days.
