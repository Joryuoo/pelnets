### Explanation
SMTP (Simple Mail Transfer Protocol) carries outgoing mail from a client to a server and relays it between mail servers. POP3 (Post Office Protocol version 3) is used by a client to retrieve mail from its mailbox on the server.

The sequence in the diagram is `client --SMTP--> server --SMTP--> server --POP3--> receiving client`. Thus positions (1) and (3) are SMTP and (2) is POP3. 
- The other choices incorrectly use POP3 for sending (which it cannot do) or for server-to-server relay (which is always done by SMTP).
