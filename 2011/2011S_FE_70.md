d) Selection of the supplier

### Explanation
After setting the evaluation method, the buyer issues the RFP, evaluates submitted proposals, selects a supplier, and then procures from it. The third blank C is therefore supplier selection.

The procurement flow is `evaluation method → issue RFP (A) → evaluate proposals (B) → select supplier (C) → procure (D)`. Issuing the RFP, evaluating it, or placing the procurement in blank C would put supplier selection in the wrong position.
