### Explanation
WBS stands for Work Breakdown Structure. It is a hierarchical decomposition of the total scope of work to be carried out by the project team to accomplish the project objectives and create the required deliverables.

- **CPM (Critical Path Method)** and **PERT (Program Evaluation and Review Technique)** are scheduling techniques based on network diagrams.
- **EVM (Earned Value Management)** is a project performance measurement technique that integrates scope, time, and cost data.
