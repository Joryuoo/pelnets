### Explanation
In UML class diagrams, a filled (solid) diamond denotes composition. This represents a strong "whole-part" relationship where the part (TicketOffice) cannot exist independently of the whole (MovieTheater).

Why the other choices are wrong:
- **a) Aggregation**: Denoted by a hollow (unfilled) diamond. It represents a weaker "whole-part" relationship where parts can exist independently (like the Movie relationship in the diagram).
- **b) Association**: Denoted by a plain line (with or without an arrow). It indicates a general relationship or communication path between classes without a whole-part dependency.
- **d) Generalization**: Denoted by a hollow triangle pointing to the superclass. It represents an inheritance relationship ("is-a").
