### Explanation
In the product portfolio matrix (BCG matrix), the vertical axis represents the market growth rate (A) and the horizontal axis is the relative market share (B).

- **a)** Correct. Matches vertical growth (A) and horizontal share (B).
- **b) & d)** Incorrect. Gross-profit ratios are not axes of this matrix.
- **c)** Incorrect. This choice reverses the standard axes of the matrix.
