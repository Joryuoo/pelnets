# 2011A_FE_33

Q33. Which of the following is the primary purpose of locking a resource in database management systems?

a) To improve overall performance
b) To maintain data integrity
c) To prevent risk of deadlocks
d) To prevent unauthorized access
?
b) To maintain data integrity

### Explanation
Locking is a concurrency control mechanism used in database management systems (DBMS). Its primary purpose is to ensure that multiple transactions can occur concurrently without interfering with each other, thus preventing data inconsistency and maintaining data integrity (e.g., preventing lost updates or dirty reads).

- **a) To improve overall performance:** Locking actually introduces overhead and can reduce concurrency, potentially impacting performance.
- **c) To prevent risk of deadlocks:** Locking is actually what *causes* deadlocks in a database when multiple transactions wait for locks held by each other.
- **d) To prevent unauthorized access:** This is handled by authentication and authorization mechanisms (like permissions/privileges), not locking.
