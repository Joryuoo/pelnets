### Explanation
Material Requirements Planning (MRP) explodes the production schedule through the bill of materials, subtracts available stock to find net requirements, and schedules orders based on lead times.

- **a) CAD:** Incorrect. Computer-Aided Design supports the creation, modification, and analysis of designs, not production material planning.
- **b) ERP:** Incorrect. Enterprise Resource Planning integrates many enterprise functions (finance, HR, etc.), whereas the flowchart specifically details MRP.
- **c) JIT:** Incorrect. Just-In-Time emphasizes continuous flow and minimal inventory, rather than this specific forward-looking calculation of material requirements.
- **d) MRP:** Correct. The flowchart perfectly maps to the MRP process of determining what materials are needed and when.
