# 2011S_FE_53

Which of the following is the most appropriate description concerning maintenance and management activities that are performed in a development environment for embedded systems used during the development of a product?

a) It is desirable to maintain and manage the development environment by updating it to the latest environment, even if it is not frequently used.
b) It is desirable to maintain and manage the development environment such as through periodic operational checks regardless of usage frequency.
c) It is not necessary to maintain the development environment after the commercialization of the product because the environment is not used again.
d) The development environment leased from a rental company is always maintained under the responsibility of the rental company.
?
b) It is desirable to maintain and manage the development environment such as through periodic operational checks regardless of usage frequency.

### Explanation
Embedded products may require fixes and support long after commercialization. Keeping the original development environment usable through periodic checks allows reproducible builds and maintenance.

Maintenance may be needed years later, so periodic checks preserve the ability to rebuild and diagnose a released product. Updating every tool to the newest version (a) can break reproducibility; stopping maintenance after release (c) loses support capability; renting equipment (d) does not automatically transfer every operational responsibility.
