# 2011A_FE_68

In verification of stakeholder requirements, which of the following means the ability to keep track of the process until the implementation of a change request related to a defined requirement is completed?

a) Interoperability
b) Maintainability
c) Traceability
d) Usability
?
c) Traceability

### Explanation
Traceability in requirements engineering refers to the ability to link requirements to their origin, track their development through design and implementation, and verify them through testing. It ensures that every requirement and any changes requested can be tracked end-to-end.

- **a (Interoperability)** is the ability of different systems to communicate and work together.
- **b (Maintainability)** measures how easily a software system can be modified, repaired, or updated.
- **d (Usability)** measures how easy and intuitive a system is for the end user to operate.
