c) Z = A * B + C * D

### Explanation
Based on typical logic circuit problems in the FE exam involving four variables A, B, C, D and product/sum notation:
- `A * B` represents an AND gate taking inputs A and B.
- `C * D` represents an AND gate taking inputs C and D.
- `+` represents an OR gate.
The output Z is the result of OR-ing the outputs of the two AND gates. Therefore, the boolean equation is `Z = A * B + C * D`.
- Choices (a) and (b) typically represent incorrect groupings or operations.
- Choice (d) typically uses wrong operations.
