# 2011S_FE_42

In e-commerce such as mail-order business, there is a risk that dealers or users are damaged by falsifying, spoofing, and repudiation at the time of sales order or purchase order. Which of the following is an appropriate security technology that can cope well with all of these three threats?

a) Digital signature
b) Firewall
c) Junk mail filter
d) Virus check
?
a) Digital signature

### Explanation
A digital signature binds a message to the signer's private key, allowing the recipient to verify the origin and integrity of the message using the signer's public key. It prevents falsification (integrity), spoofing (authentication), and repudiation (non-repudiation).

Why the other choices are wrong:
- **b) Firewall**: Controls incoming and outgoing network traffic based on security rules, but does not authenticate the contents or origin of specific orders.
- **c) Junk mail filter**: Identifies and blocks spam emails, but does not provide non-repudiation or prevent data falsification.
- **d) Virus check**: Scans and removes malicious software, which is unrelated to authenticating users and ensuring order integrity.
