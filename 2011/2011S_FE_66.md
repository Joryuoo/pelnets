# 2011S_FE_66

Which of the following enables users to connect to the system of a service provider via the Internet and make online use of the necessary functions of the applications offered by the service provider as needed?

a) ERP
b) SaaS
c) SCM
d) XBRL
?
b) SaaS

### Explanation
Software as a Service provides applications hosted by a provider for use through the Internet as needed. ERP and SCM describe business systems, while XBRL is a reporting format.

The service model is `provider hosts application → user connects over Internet → user runs needed functions`, which is SaaS. ERP (a) and SCM (c) are types of business applications, not the delivery model; XBRL (d) is a structured business-reporting language.
