# 2011A_FE_29

Which of the following is the image processing technique that is used for pasting a two-dimensional image such as a pattern or design onto the surface of a modeled object?

a) Anti-aliasing
b) Blending
c) Ray tracing
d) Texture mapping
?
d) Texture mapping

### Explanation
### Explanation
Texture mapping is a 3D computer graphics technique used to wrap or paste a 2D image (called a texture) onto the surface of a 3D model, giving it color, detail, and realism.

* **a) Anti-aliasing** smooths jagged edges (stair-stepping) on curved lines and diagonals.
* **b) Blending** combines two colors or images together, often used for transparency effects.
* **c) Ray tracing** simulates the physical behavior of light (reflections, refractions, shadows) to generate highly realistic 3D scenes.
