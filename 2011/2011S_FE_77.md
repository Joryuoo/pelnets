### Explanation
To maximize profit, allocate the 12,000 available minutes based on the highest profit per minute of assembly time.

1. **Calculate profit per minute:**
   - Product X: $18 / 6 min = $3/min
   - Product Y: $25 / 10 min = $2.50/min
   - Product Z: $30 / 15 min = $2/min
2. **Allocate time by priority:**
   - First, satisfy maximum demand for X: 1,000 units × 6 min = 6,000 minutes. Profit = $18,000.
   - Remaining time: 12,000 - 6,000 = 6,000 minutes.
   - Next, make Y with the remaining time: 6,000 minutes / 10 min = 600 units (max demand is 1,500, so we just make 600). Profit = 600 × $25 = $15,000.
3. **Total Profit:** $18,000 (from X) + $15,000 (from Y) = $33,000.

- **d)** Correct.
- **a, b, c)** Incorrect. These represent suboptimal allocations (like making Z first or evenly dividing time), which leave profitable time unused or allocated to lower-return products.
