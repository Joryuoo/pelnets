### Explanation
Multiply each score by its weight: Plan 1 = 4×6 + 3×5 + 3×6 = 57; Plan 2 = 4×8 + 3×5 + 3×4 = 59; Plan 3 = 56; Plan 4 = 53. Plan 2 has the highest weighted total.

| Plan | Weighted score |
|---|---:|
| 1 | $4(6)+3(5)+3(6)=57$ |
| 2 | $4(8)+3(5)+3(4)=59$ |
| 3 | $4(2)+3(9)+3(7)=56$ |
| 4 | $4(5)+3(5)+3(6)=53$ |

Plan 2 has the highest total. The alternatives look attractive on individual criteria, but the question requires the weighted **combined** score.
