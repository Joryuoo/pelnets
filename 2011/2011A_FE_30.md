# 2011A_FE_30

Which of the following is a concept that is included in both E-R and Enhanced E-R diagrams?

a) Cardinality
b) Generalization
c) Specilization
d) Superclass
?
a) Cardinality

### Explanation
### Explanation
Cardinality describes the numerical relationship between entities (e.g., 1-to-1, 1-to-many, many-to-many) and is a fundamental concept in traditional Entity-Relationship (E-R) diagrams. 

Enhanced E-R (EER) diagrams build upon the traditional E-R model by adding object-oriented concepts. Generalization (b), Specialization (c), and Superclass/Subclass (d) are all features introduced in the Enhanced E-R model, meaning they are absent from standard E-R diagrams. Therefore, only Cardinality is shared by both.
