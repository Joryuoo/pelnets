# 2011A_FE_36

Q36. Which of the following is the broadcast MAC address that is used by multiple protocols, such as ARP (Address Resolution Protocol) and RIP (Routing Information Protocol), in order to transmit data to all of the hosts on the local subnet?

a) 00-00-00-00-00-00
b) EE-FF-EE-FF-EE-FF
c) F0-F0-F0-F0-F0-F0
d) FF-FF-FF-FF-FF-FF
?
d) FF-FF-FF-FF-FF-FF

### Explanation
In Ethernet networking, the broadcast MAC address is defined as an address consisting of all 1s in binary, which corresponds to all Fs in hexadecimal: `FF-FF-FF-FF-FF-FF`. When a frame is sent to this MAC address, a switch will flood the frame out of all ports (except the incoming port), allowing all hosts on the local subnet to receive it.

- **a) 00-00-00-00-00-00:** This is not a broadcast address; it's often a placeholder or a null MAC address.
- **b) and c)** are invalid broadcast addresses as they do not consist of all 1s.
