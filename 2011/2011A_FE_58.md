### Explanation
1. Calculate the total person-days required for coding:
   - Input processing: $20 \text{ programs} \times 1 \text{ person-day/program} = 20 \text{ person-days}$
   - Output processing: $10 \text{ programs} \times 3 \text{ person-days/program} = 30 \text{ person-days}$
   - Calculation processing: $5 \text{ programs} \times 9 \text{ person-days/program} = 45 \text{ person-days}$
   - Total coding person-days = $20 + 30 + 45 = 95 \text{ person-days}$.
2. Calculate the total person-days for design and testing:
   - The problem states these require eight times as many person-days as coding.
   - Design and testing = $95 \times 8 = 760 \text{ person-days}$.
3. Calculate the total person-days for the entire project:
   - Total project effort = $95 \text{ (coding)} + 760 \text{ (design & testing)} = 855 \text{ person-days}$.
4. Calculate the minimum number of staff members for 95 days:
   - Staff = $Total\ effort / Duration = 855 / 95 = 9$.
Therefore, 9 staff members are required.
