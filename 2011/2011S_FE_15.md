# 2011S_FE_15

There are various types of USB connectors. When an external storage device is connected to a PC by using a USB cable, which of the following types of plugs should be inserted into an available USB receptacle of the PC?

a) USB A
b) USB B
c) USB mini A
d) USB mini B
?
a) USB A

### Explanation
The standard USB host receptacle on a PC is designed to accept a **USB Type-A** plug. 

* **b) USB B:** Typically used on the peripheral device side for larger devices like printers and scanners.
* **c) USB mini A / d) USB mini B:** Mini and Micro USB connectors are generally used on the peripheral end for smaller portable devices (e.g., older digital cameras or mobile phones).
