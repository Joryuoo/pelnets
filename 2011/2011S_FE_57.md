# 2011S_FE_57

The function that starts up a computer via a LAN is called WOL (Wake on LAN). Which of the following can be performed efficiently by using this function?

a) Detection of a system malfunction
b) Maintenance of a PC at a remote location
c) Management of the traffic condition
d) Monitoring of unauthorized access
?
b) Maintenance of a PC at a remote location

### Explanation
Wake on LAN can power up a networked PC without someone at its keyboard, letting an administrator run maintenance after hours or from another location.

Remote maintenance often needs a PC powered on before patches or diagnostics can run; WOL sends a network wake signal for that purpose. It does not itself diagnose faults (a), optimize traffic (c), or monitor intrusion (d).
