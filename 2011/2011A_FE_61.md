### Explanation
The format of the ID is XXNNN, where X is an uppercase alphabet (26 possibilities) and N is a digit (10 possibilities). The total number of possible unique IDs is $26 \times 26 \times 10 \times 10 \times 10 = 676,000$.

From the table, the number of IDs issued increases by roughly 65,000 per year:
- 2008: $383,000 - 317,000 = 66,000$
- 2009: $447,000 - 383,000 = 64,000$
- 2010: $512,000 - 447,000 = 65,000$

Assuming this trend of adding $\approx 65,000$ IDs per year continues:
- End of 2011: $512,000 + 65,000 = 577,000$
- End of 2012: $577,000 + 65,000 = 642,000$
- End of 2013: $642,000 + 65,000 = 707,000$

Since the maximum capacity is 676,000, Company A will run out of IDs during the fiscal year 2013.
