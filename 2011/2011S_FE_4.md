# 2011S_FE_4

c) 1/4

### Explanation
A sum of 8 has five ordered outcomes and a sum of 9 has four. There are 36 equally likely ordered outcomes for two dice, so the probability is (5 + 4) / 36 = 1/4.

Count the ordered dice pairs rather than just the two target sums:

| Sum | Ordered pairs | Count |
|---|---|---:|
| 8 | (2,6), (3,5), (4,4), (5,3), (6,2) | 5 |
| 9 | (3,6), (4,5), (5,4), (6,3) | 4 |

The result is $9/36=1/4$. Choices a and b count too few favorable pairs; d counts 12 of 36 outcomes, which is too many.
