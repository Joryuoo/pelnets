# 2011S_FE_65

Which of the following dramatically improves a company’s performance, including cost, quality, service, speed, and so on, by fundamentally reviewing and redesigning business processes?

a) Alliance
b) Core competence
c) Going concern
d) Reengineering
?
d) Reengineering

### Explanation
Business process reengineering fundamentally redesigns workflows to achieve major improvements in cost, quality, service, and speed. It is more than an incremental efficiency adjustment.

Reengineering asks how to redesign the process from the ground up, rather than merely optimize one step. An alliance (a) is a partnership, core competence (b) is a distinctive strength, and going concern (c) is the assumption a business continues operating. None names the fundamental process redesign in the question.
