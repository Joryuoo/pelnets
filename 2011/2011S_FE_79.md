# 2011S_FE_79

Which of the following is an appropriate case where ABC analysis is applied?

a) It is applied in the case of identifying detailed sales forecasts in order to avoid running out of stock.
b) It is applied in the case of identifying hot-selling products from the sales value and gross profit for each product.
c) It is applied in the case of identifying market needs by conducting questionnaire surveys of the opinion leaders in each region.
d) It is applied in the case of identifying the combination of purchased products when a customer makes a purchase.
?
b) It is applied in the case of identifying hot-selling products from the sales value and gross profit for each product.

### Explanation
ABC analysis is an inventory categorization technique that divides items into three classes (A, B, and C) based on their value and importance (e.g., sales value or gross profit), allowing management to focus on the high-value "A" items.

- **a)** Incorrect. Detailed sales forecasting involves time-series analysis or predictive modeling, not ABC analysis.
- **b)** Correct. ABC analysis identifies and prioritizes the most important products based on contribution to sales or profit.
- **c)** Incorrect. This describes market research and survey analysis, unrelated to inventory classification.
- **d)** Incorrect. This describes market basket analysis (or association rule learning), not ABC analysis.
