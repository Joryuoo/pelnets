Before shortening F, path A–D–F takes 5 + 8 + 7 = 20 days and is critical. After F drops to one day, that path takes 14; path A–E then takes 15 and becomes critical. Completion falls from 20 to 15 days, a five-day reduction.

| Path | Before F reduction | After F reduction |
|---|---:|---:|
| A–D–F | 20 | 14 |
| A–E | 15 | 15 |
| B–C | 13 | 13 |
