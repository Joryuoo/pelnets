# 2011S_FE_72

Which of the following is a niche strategy for business management?

a) Emphasis on cash flow
b) Lead time reduction
c) Market specialization
d) Vertical integration
?
c) Market specialization

### Explanation
A niche strategy involves deliberately serving a narrow, specialized market segment where the business can meet distinct needs, rather than competing across the broad market.

- **a) Emphasis on cash flow:** Incorrect. This is a financial priority or management strategy, not a market positioning strategy.
- **b) Lead time reduction:** Incorrect. This is an operational efficiency improvement.
- **c) Market specialization:** Correct. This directly describes focusing on a specific niche market.
- **d) Vertical integration:** Incorrect. This refers to expanding business operations into different steps on the same production path (e.g., a manufacturer buying its supplier).
