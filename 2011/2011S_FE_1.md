# 2011S_FE_1

According to a survey of 100 students, there are 40 students studying English, 30 studying French, and 25 studying Spanish. In addition, 8 students are studying English and French, 6 are studying English and Spanish, 5 are studying French and Spanish, and 22 are not studying any of the three languages. Which of the following is the number of students studying all three languages?

a) 1
b) 2
c) 3
d) 4
?
b) 2

### Explanation
By inclusion-exclusion, 78 students study at least one language. The three individual counts total 95; subtract the pairwise overlaps 8, 6, and 5, then add the triple overlap. Thus 95 − 19 + x = 78, so x = 2.

The count can be pictured as a Venn accounting step: `at least one = singles − pairwise overlaps + triple overlap`. Here, `100 − 22 = 78`, so `40 + 30 + 25 − 8 − 6 − 5 + x = 78` and `x = 2`. Choices a, c, and d would give 77, 79, and 80 students respectively, so none matches the survey total.
