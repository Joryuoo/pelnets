# 2011A_FE_64

From the viewpoint of internal controls, which of the following is a framework that can secure data integrity by means of a check and balance system in the organization?

a) In order to implement an efficient data entry system that meets the business needs, the information systems department develops such a data entry system and enters data.
b) The information systems department remains independent of the user departments that enter data, in order to ensure that the user departments can maintain the precision and accuracy of data.
c) The staff members of the information systems department remain in the same department for a long time so that they can secure data consistency in system development by taking advantage of their experience.
d) When the staff members of the information systems department are transferred to another department, they take the relevant reference materials with them so as to make contribution in an emergency such as a system failure.
?
b) The information systems department remains independent of the user departments that enter data, in order to ensure that the user departments can maintain the precision and accuracy of data.

### Explanation
A key principle of internal controls is the "separation of duties" (or check and balance system). By keeping the information systems department (which maintains the system) separate from the user departments (which input the operational data), the organization prevents a single entity from having total control over both the system and the data. This segregation helps prevent fraud and errors, ensuring data integrity.

- **a is incorrect** because having the developers also enter the data violates the separation of duties.
- **c is incorrect** because keeping staff in the same position indefinitely increases the risk of undetected errors or fraud (job rotation is actually a good internal control practice).
- **d is incorrect** because removing reference materials when transferring departments is a poor security practice and violates information asset control.
