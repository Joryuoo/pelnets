### Explanation
Incident management restores service quickly. Problem management investigates underlying causes, reduces the effect of known problems, and prevents recurring incidents.

A simple ITIL distinction is `incident: restore service now` versus `problem: find and remove the underlying cause`. Availability management (a) plans service uptime, continuity management (b) addresses major disruptions, and service-level management (d) negotiates and monitors agreed service targets.
