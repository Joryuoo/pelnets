$11{,}000 \text{ samples/second} \times 8 \text{ bits/sample} = 88{,}000 \text{ bits/second}$

$512 \times 10^6 \text{ bytes} / 11{,}000 \text{ bytes/second} \approx 46{,}545.45 \text{ seconds}$

Finally, convert seconds to minutes:
