# 2011S_FE_7

![[2011S_FE_AM_Q7_full.png]]
?
b) (A + B × C) / ((A + B) × C)

### Explanation
The root is division. Its left subtree adds A to the product B × C, while its right subtree multiplies the sum A + B by C. Infix traversal therefore yields the expression in option b.

Follow the tree from the root: `/` combines a left `+` subtree and a right `×` subtree. The left side is `A + (B × C)`; the right side is `(A + B) × C`. Choice a groups `A × B` on the left, while c and d move operators or omit the needed grouping.
