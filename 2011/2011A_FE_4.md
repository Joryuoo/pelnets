# 2011A_FE_4

An empty tank can be filled with water in 20 minutes by using Pipe A or in 30 minutes by Pipe B, and the tank filled with water can be emptied of water in 40 minutes by using Pipe C. When the three pipes A, B, and C work together, approximately how long (in minutes) does it take to fill the empty tank with water?

a) 9.2
b) 10.0
c) 17.1
d) 24.0
?
c) 17.1

### Explanation
To find the total time to fill the tank, we must first calculate the work rate of each pipe per minute:
- Pipe A fills the tank at a rate of `1/20` per minute.
- Pipe B fills the tank at a rate of `1/30` per minute.
- Pipe C empties the tank at a rate of `-1/40` per minute.

When working together, their combined net rate is:
`Net Rate = 1/20 + 1/30 - 1/40`
To add these fractions, find a common denominator, which is 120:
- `1/20 = 6/120`
- `1/30 = 4/120`
- `-1/40 = -3/120`

`Net Rate = (6 + 4 - 3) / 120 = 7/120` per minute.
This means they fill `7/120` of the tank per minute. The total time `T` to fill the entire tank is the reciprocal of the net rate:
`T = 120 / 7 ≈ 17.14` minutes.

Therefore, choice (c) 17.1 is the correct answer.
