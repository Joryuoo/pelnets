To determine the correct tables for blanks A through D, we trace the flowchart's processes and identify the data they require:

1. **Display week day and the time period**: This step presents available times to the user. The system must retrieve the list of days and time slots from the database. Therefore, it requires the `Time_slot` (e) and `Weekday` (f) tables. These correspond to blanks A and B.
2. **Display all available labs**: After the user selects a time and day, the system determines which labs are open during that period. This information is stored in the `Bookable_period` table (a), which links labs to available days and slots. This corresponds to blank C.
3. **Add the new reservation record**: Once the user chooses an available lab, the final step is to store their reservation. This writes data to the `Reservation` table (c). This corresponds to blank D.

Thus, the sequence of correct tables is **e** (`Time_slot`), **f** (`Weekday`), **a** (`Bookable_period`), and **c** (`Reservation`).
