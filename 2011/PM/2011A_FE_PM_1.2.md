**Blanks A and B:**
The truth table for `Z3` has four groups of `1`s in its Karnaugh map, representing the Boolean sum-of-products for the second least significant bit (the 2s place).
- The problem states that Group 1 generates `-X1•X2•Y1` and Group 2 generates `X2•Y1•-Y2`.
- The remaining two groups of `1`s on the `Z3` Karnaugh map correspond to the remaining combinations where the product has a `1` in the 2s place.
- By grouping these `1`s on the K-map, the generated terms are `X1•-Y1•Y2` (choice **g**) and `X1•-X2•Y2` (choice **b**).

**Blank C:**
As established in Subquestion 1, `Z4` is `1` only when the LSBs of both inputs are `1`. The logical expression for this is simply `X2 AND Y2`, which is denoted as `X2•Y2` (choice **d**).

**Blank D:**
Gate **D** in Figure 3 produces the output `Z4` using the inputs `X2` and `Y2`. Since the logic expression for `Z4` is `X2•Y2` (the logical AND of `X2` and `Y2`), gate **D** must be an **AND gate** (choice **a**). The other choices (NAND, OR, NOR, XOR) would output incorrect bits.
