### Explanation
To determine the routes leading to the "Activate Account" function, we examine the system's design:
- **Create New Account:** After creating an account, a user is directed to activate it (Function 1 to 2).
- **Re-Request Activation Key:** If a user re-requests their key, they will subsequently need to activate their account (Function 3 to 2).
- **Welcome:** Since all user stories start from either the Welcome page or My Account page, a user who previously created an account but returns later to enter their emailed key will navigate to Activate Account directly from the Welcome page.

Therefore, the correct list of routes is Create New Account, Re-Request Activation Key, and Welcome (Choice d).
