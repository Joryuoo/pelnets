**Blank A (Average Rotational Latency of Product A):**
- Product A's rotational speed is 4,200 rpm (revolutions per minute).
- Revolutions per second = 4,200 / 60 = 70 rev/sec.
- Time for one full revolution = 1,000 msec / 70 ≈ 14.286 msec.
- The average rotational latency is half the time of a full revolution: 14.286 / 2 ≈ 7.143 msec.
- Rounded to the first decimal place, this is **7.1 msec** (Answer **e**).

**Blank B (Time to transfer 100 k bytes for Product A):**
- Product A's data transfer rate is 20 M bytes/sec.
- Convert to k bytes/msec: 20,000 k bytes / 1,000 msec = 20 k bytes/msec.
- Time to transfer 100 k bytes = 100 k bytes / 20 k bytes/msec = **5.0 msec** (Answer **b**).

**Blank C (Difference in average access time):**
- Product A's average access time = 14.0 msec (seek) + 7.1 msec (latency) + 5.0 msec (transfer) = 26.1 msec.
- Product B's average access time = 12.0 msec (seek) + 5.5 msec (latency) + 3.3 msec (transfer) = 20.8 msec.
- The difference is 26.1 - 20.8 = **5.3 msec** (Answer **c**).
