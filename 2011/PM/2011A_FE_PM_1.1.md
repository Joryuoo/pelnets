### Explanation
The output `Z4` represents the least significant bit (LSB) of the 4-bit product `Z = X * Y`.

- The LSB of a multiplication product is `1` if and only if the LSBs of both operands are `1` (i.e., both numbers are odd).
- The multiplicand `X` is denoted as `X1X2`, where `X2` is the LSB.
- The multiplier `Y` is denoted as `Y1Y2`, where `Y2` is the LSB.
- Therefore, `Z4` will be `1` when `X2 = 1` and `Y2 = 1`.

Looking at the options, only truth table **b)** has `1`s exactly where `X1X2` is `01` or `11` (meaning `X2 = 1`), and `Y1Y2` is `01` or `11` (meaning `Y2 = 1`). Other tables represent different logical functions that do not match this bitwise AND behavior.
