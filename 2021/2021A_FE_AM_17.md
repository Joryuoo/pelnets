### Explanation
The time slot is 10 ms. The processes arrive at time 0 in order P1, P2, P3, P4.
Round 1:
- P1 executes for 10 ms. Remaining: 0 ms. (Finishes at t=10).
- P2 executes for 10 ms. Remaining: 13 ms. (Runs t=10 to t=20).
- P3 executes for 7 ms. Remaining: 0 ms. (Finishes at t=27).
- P4 executes for 3 ms. Remaining: 0 ms. (Finishes at t=30).

Round 2:
- P2 executes for 10 ms. Remaining: 3 ms. (Runs t=30 to t=40).

Round 3:
- P2 executes for 3 ms. Remaining: 0 ms. (Finishes at t=43).

Waiting time = Total turnaround time - Execution time.
Turnaround time for each process (completion time - arrival time):
P1: finishes at 10. Turnaround = 10. Wait = 10 - 10 = 0.
P2: finishes at 43. Turnaround = 43. Wait = 43 - 23 = 20.
P3: finishes at 27. Turnaround = 27. Wait = 27 - 7 = 20.
P4: finishes at 30. Turnaround = 30. Wait = 30 - 3 = 27.

Average waiting time = (0 + 20 + 20 + 27) / 4 = 67 / 4 = 16.75 ms.
