# 2021A_FE_AM_50
According to the Capability Maturity Model Integration, which of the following is the final and highest level of maturity?

a) Auditable
b) Custom
c) Optimizing
d) Quantitatively Managed
?
c) Optimizing
### Explanation
The Capability Maturity Model Integration (CMMI) defines five maturity levels for process improvement:
1. Initial (unpredictable, poorly controlled)
2. Managed (processes planned and executed)
3. Defined (processes well characterized and understood)
4. Quantitatively Managed (processes measured and controlled)
5. Optimizing (focus on continuous process improvement)
Therefore, Level 5: "Optimizing" is the final and highest level of maturity.
