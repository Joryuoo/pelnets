# 2021A_FE_AM_64
In computerization investment, which of the following is a technique that is used to create categories on the basis of the similarities of risks and investment values and perform optimum resource allocation?

a) 3C analysis
b) Benchmarking
c) Enterprise architecture
d) IT portfolio
?
d) IT portfolio
### Explanation
IT portfolio management is an approach to managing IT investments where projects are categorized and evaluated based on their risks, costs, and value to optimize resource allocation across the organization, similar to financial portfolio management.
