### Explanation
**Blank D:** PC3 is unable to connect to the network. Pinging the loopback address is successful, which confirms that PC3's TCP/IP software stack is installed and functioning correctly. However, the gateway cannot be reached. 
Physical layer indicators show that the switch port is operational when other PCs are connected, meaning the switch is not faulty. Since the LED on PC3's network card is off and the switch interface light corresponding to PC3 is also off, this points directly to a Layer 1 (physical) disconnection between PC3 and the switch. The most likely cause is that **the network cable is faulty** (c) or disconnected.
