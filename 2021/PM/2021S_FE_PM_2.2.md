### Explanation
**Preemptive SJF Algorithm (Blanks B and C)**
In a preemptive SJF algorithm, a running process is interrupted if a newly arrived process has a shorter CPU burst time than the remaining burst time of the current process.
- **Time 0:** P5 arrives (burst time 8). Starts execution.
- **Time 1:** P6 arrives (burst time 4). P5 has 7 remaining. Since 4 < 7, P6 preempts P5 and runs to completion at time 5.
- **Time 2:** P7 arrives (burst time 9). P6 has 3 remaining. P6 continues.
- **Time 3:** P8 arrives (burst time 5). P6 has 2 remaining. P6 continues.
- **Time 5:** P6 finishes. Remaining processes are P5 (7), P7 (9), and P8 (5). P8 is the shortest, so it runs from time 5 to 10.
- **Time 10:** P8 finishes. Remaining processes are P5 (7) and P7 (9). P5 runs from time 10 to 17.
- **Time 17:** P5 finishes. P7 runs from time 17 to 26.

- **Blank B (Sequence for Preemptive SJF):** P5, P6, P8, P5. This matches **d**.
- **Blank C (Results for P8):** P8 arrives at time 3, starts at 5, and ends at 10.
  - Waiting time = 5 - 3 = 2
  - Response time = 5 - 3 = 2
  - Turnaround time = 10 - 3 = 7
  This matches **b**.

**Non-Preemptive SJF Algorithm (Blanks D and E)**
In a non-preemptive SJF algorithm, the running process finishes its entire burst time before the next process is selected.
- **Time 0:** P5 arrives and runs to completion at time 8.
- **Time 8:** By this time, P6 (4), P7 (9), and P8 (5) have all arrived. P6 is the shortest, so it runs from time 8 to 12.
- **Time 12:** Remaining processes are P7 (9) and P8 (5). P8 is shorter, so it runs from time 12 to 17.
- **Time 17:** P8 finishes. P7 runs from time 17 to 26.

- **Blank D (Sequence for Non-Preemptive SJF):** P5, P6, P8. This matches **a**.
- **Blank E (Results for P8):** P8 arrives at time 3, starts at 12, and ends at 17.
  - Waiting time = 12 - 3 = 9
  - Response time = 12 - 3 = 9
  - Turnaround time = 17 - 3 = 14
  This matches **d**.
