# 2021A_FE_AM_55
Which of the following corresponds to benchmarking in process improvement of service management?

a) The availability, reliability, and performance are measured at the service level, and a report is sent to the customers.
b) The current status of IT service management is analyzed from the viewpoint of strengths, weaknesses, opportunities, and threats.
c) The performance of IT services is measured from the viewpoint of finance, customers, internal processes, and learning and growth, and strategic activities are supported.
d) The service quality and performance level are evaluated in comparison with the best practices within and outside the industry.
?
d) The service quality and performance level are evaluated in comparison with the best practices within and outside the industry.
### Explanation
**Benchmarking** is the practice of comparing business processes and performance metrics to industry bests and best practices from other companies. Dimensions typically measured are quality, time and cost. Option D correctly describes benchmarking. Option A describes Service Level Management reporting. Option B describes SWOT analysis. Option C describes the Balanced Scorecard.
