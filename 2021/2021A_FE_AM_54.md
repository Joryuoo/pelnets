# 2021A_FE_AM_54
![[2021A_FE_AM_Q54_full.png]]
a) The project duration is not affected.
b) The project duration is extended by 1 day.
c) The project duration is extended by 2 days.
d) The project duration is extended by 3 days.
?
c) The project duration is extended by 2 days.
### Explanation
By calculating the early and late start/finish times for all activities in the network diagram, we determine the critical path and the total float for each activity. In this specific scenario, Activity C has a total float of 1 day. If its duration is extended by 3 days, it will consume the 1 day of float and exceed it by 2 days. This will shift the critical path to include Activity C, resulting in the overall project duration being extended by 2 days.
