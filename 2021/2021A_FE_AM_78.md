# 2021A_FE_AM_78
Which of the following is the appropriate description concerning creation and operation of a Business Continuity Plan (BCP)?

a) In addition to natural disasters, device failure in information systems and malware infections are also included in the scope of examination.
b) The planning contents are important matters related to the business strategy and therefore, only senior managers are notified.
c) The planning contents are restricted to those activities performed by the company.
d) The recovery of business processes dependent on IT is prioritized according to technical easiness of recovery.
?
a) In addition to natural disasters, device failure in information systems and malware infections are also included in the scope of examination.
### Explanation
A Business Continuity Plan (BCP) is designed to protect a business from major disruptions and help it recover operations quickly. Major disruptions are not limited to natural disasters; they also encompass human-made disasters, cyberattacks (such as malware infections), and infrastructure failures (like severe device failures).
