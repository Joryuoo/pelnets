# 2021A_FE_AM_2
For a non-negative integer x, which of the following gives the remainder after division of x by 8 as a result?

a) Performing a bitwise AND operation on x and 7
b) Performing a bitwise AND operation on x and 248
c) Performing a bitwise OR operation on x and 8
d) Performing a bitwise OR operation on x and 15
?
a) Performing a bitwise AND operation on x and 7
### Explanation
The remainder of a division by 8 (which is 2^3) is given by the lowest 3 bits of the number. A bitwise AND operation with 7 (which is binary 0111) extracts exactly these 3 lowest bits, effectively computing x modulo 8.
