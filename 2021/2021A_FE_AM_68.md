# 2021A_FE_AM_68
![[2021A_FE_AM_Q68_full.png]]
a) Among the purchasers of product A, 1,000 people are estimated to purchase the new product Q.
b) Of the prospective purchasers of the new product P, 50% are estimated to be purchasers of product A.
c) The number of new customers of the new product S are estimated to be fewer than the number of people who will purchase the new product S among the purchasers of product C.
d) The purchasers of product B are estimated to purchase any one (1) of the new products P, Q, R, and S.
?
c) The number of new customers of the new product S are estimated to be fewer than the number of people who will purchase the new product S among the purchasers of product C.
### Explanation
Based on the table provided in the problem statement, we can verify choice (c): Purchasers of product C who will purchase new product S is calculated by 80,000 * 0.3 = 24,000. The number of new customers for new product S is given as 23,000. Since 23,000 is fewer than 24,000, choice (c) is the correct statement.
