# 2021A_FE_AM_75
Which of the following is a management organization that creates a cross-functional team between project and functional organizations to flexibly respond to changes in the business environment?

a) Divisional organization
b) Line and staff organization
c) Matrix organization
d) Project organization
?
c) Matrix organization
### Explanation
A matrix organization is a company structure in which the reporting relationships are set up as a grid, or matrix, rather than in the traditional hierarchy. In other words, employees have dual reporting relationships - generally to both a functional manager and a product or project manager, enabling flexible responses and cross-functional teamwork.
