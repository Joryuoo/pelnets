# 2021A_FE_AM_40
According to ISO/IEC 27000:2018 (Information security management systems - Overview and vocabulary), which of the following is the definition of “level of risk”?

a) Magnitude of a risk expressed in terms of the combination of consequences and their likelihood
b) Terms of reference for evaluating the significance of a risk
c) The priority order assigned to the risks to be handled
d) Weakness of an asset or control that can be exploited by threats
?
a) Magnitude of a risk expressed in terms of the combination of consequences and their likelihood
### Explanation
According to ISO/IEC 27000:2018, the level of risk is defined as its magnitude, which is generally expressed as the combination of consequences (impact) of an event and the associated likelihood (probability) of occurrence.
