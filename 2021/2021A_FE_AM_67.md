# 2021A_FE_AM_67
The sales prices of products are decided according to different sales price setting methods. Which of the following approaches sets a high sales price by using the consumer psychology that high quality equals to high price?

a) Cost plus pricing
b) Differential pricing
c) Marketing price following pricing
d) Prestige pricing
?
d) Prestige pricing
### Explanation
Prestige pricing is a strategy where prices are set higher than normal because lower prices will hurt sales, exploiting the psychological bias that higher price implies better quality and exclusivity.
