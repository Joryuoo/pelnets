c) 0.90
### Explanation
Availability is calculated as the total uptime (normal working hours) divided by the total time.
Total time = 300 hours.
Total uptime = (60 - 0) + (145 - 70) + (240 - 150) + (300 - 255)
= 60 + 75 + 90 + 45
= 270 hours.

Availability = Uptime / Total Time
= 270 / 300
= 0.90.

The availability of the system is 0.90.
