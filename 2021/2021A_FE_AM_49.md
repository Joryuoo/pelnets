# 2021A_FE_AM_49
In an agile software development project, when can the customer review the first piece of working software?

a) After the completion of the first epic
b) After the completion of the first few releases
c) After the completion of the first iteration
d) After the completion of the whole project
?
c) After the completion of the first iteration
### Explanation
In Agile software development, work is divided into short iterations (also called sprints). The goal of each iteration is to produce a potentially shippable, working increment of software. Therefore, the customer can review the first piece of working software at the end of the first iteration.
