### Explanation
In a randomized quicksort algorithm, the average-case time complexity is O(n log n) because the randomly chosen pivot divides the array into roughly equal halves on average. However, the worst-case time complexity remains O(n^2), which occurs when the random choices consistently pick the smallest or largest element as the pivot.
