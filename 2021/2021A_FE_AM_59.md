# 2021A_FE_AM_59
Which of the following is the most appropriate point to be checked in the audit of a system test?

a) The test cases were created comprehensively.
b) The test plan was approved only by the responsible person on the user’s side.
c) The test was performed in an environment where the actual business operations are performed.
d) The test was performed only by the person in charge on the user’s side.
?
a) The test cases were created comprehensively.
### Explanation
The objective of a system test audit is to ensure the testing process was adequate and verified the system's compliance with requirements. Therefore, checking that the test cases were created comprehensively (covering all functional and non-functional requirements) is the most appropriate audit point. Tests should typically be conducted in an isolated test environment (not the actual production environment), and both the development team and user representatives might be involved in approval and execution.
