d) 0.81
### Explanation
Let $H$ be the hit ratio.
The formula for Effective Access Time (EAT) is:
EAT = (Access time of cache) * H + (Access time of main memory) * (1 - H)
Given:
EAT = 250 ns
Cache access time = 100 ns
Main memory access time = 900 ns

Substitute the values:
250 = 100 * H + 900 * (1 - H)
250 = 100H + 900 - 900H
250 = 900 - 800H
800H = 900 - 250
800H = 650
H = 650 / 800
H = 0.8125

Therefore, the approximate hit ratio is 0.81.
