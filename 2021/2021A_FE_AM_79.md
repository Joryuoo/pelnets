d) Conditions  8x + 10y ≥ 60,   6x + 3y ≤ 40,    x ≥ 0,   y ≥ 0 Objective function 2000x + 800y -> minimize
### Explanation
Let x be the number of TV ads and y be the number of Radio ads.
The company wants an exposure of AT LEAST 60 points in the city. TV ads give 8 points and Radio ads give 10 points in the city: 8x + 10y ≥ 60.
The company wants an exposure of AT MOST 40 points outside the city. TV ads give 6 points and Radio ads give 3 points outside the city: 6x + 3y ≤ 40.
The objective function to minimize total cost is: Cost = 2000x + 800y.
This corresponds to option d.
