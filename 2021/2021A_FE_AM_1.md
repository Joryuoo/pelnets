# 2021A_FE_AM_1
What is the octal equivalent value of the hexadecimal number 7B5?

a) 735
b) 3665
c) 7551
d) 7561
?
b) 3665
### Explanation
Hexadecimal 7B5 to binary:
7 = 0111
B = 1011
5 = 0101
Combined binary: 011110110101
Group by 3 for octal:
011 110 110 101
3   6   6   5
Thus, the octal equivalent is 3665.
