# 2021A_FE_AM_14
Which of the following is a connection method that is referred to as a daisy chain?

a) A keyboard, a mouse, and a printer are connected to a USB hub, and the USB hub is connected to a PC.
b) A PC and a measuring device are connected with RS-232C, and the PC and a printer are connected by USB.
c) A PC is connected from its own Thunderbolt connection port with a cable to one of the two (2) 4K displays, each of which is equipped with two (2) Thunderbolt connection ports, and the display is connected to the other display with a cable.
d) Several network cameras and PCs are connected to a network hub.
?
c) A PC is connected from its own Thunderbolt connection port with a cable to one of the two (2) 4K displays, each of which is equipped with two (2) Thunderbolt connection ports, and the display is connected to the other display with a cable.
### Explanation
A daisy chain is a wiring scheme in which multiple devices are wired together in sequence or in a ring (e.g., Device A -> Device B -> Device C).
- Option (a) describes a star topology using a hub.
- Option (b) describes point-to-point connections with different interfaces on a single PC.
- Option (c) correctly describes a daisy chain where the signal passes from the PC to the first display, and then from the first display to the second display in series.
- Option (d) describes a star topology using a network hub.
