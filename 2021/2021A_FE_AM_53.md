# 2021A_FE_AM_53
In project time management, which of the following is the amount of time that the start of a successor activity requires to be delayed after the finish of a predecessor activity?

a) Free float
b) Lag
c) Lead
d) Total float
?
b) Lag
### Explanation
In project schedule management, **lag** is a directed delay in the start of a successor activity. For example, if you need to wait 2 days for paint to dry before starting the next activity, that 2-day wait is a lag. **Lead** is an acceleration of a successor activity. Float is the amount of time an activity can be delayed without delaying the early start of a successor (free float) or the project finish date (total float).
