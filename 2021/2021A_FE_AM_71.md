# 2021A_FE_AM_71
Which of the following is a product that can take advantage of the cell production system?

a) A product that needs to be produced in large quantities to improve productivity
b) A product that requires a division of labor on the basis of standardization, simplification, and specialization
c) A product that requires a variety of types and needs flexible production
d) A product whose specifications do not change over a long period of time
?
c) A product that requires a variety of types and needs flexible production
### Explanation
Cell production is a manufacturing process where workstations and equipment are arranged in a sequence that supports a smooth flow of materials and components through the production process with minimal transport or delay. It is well-suited for manufacturing a variety of products in small quantities, requiring flexible production.
