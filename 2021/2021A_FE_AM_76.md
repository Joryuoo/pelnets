# 2021A_FE_AM_76
Which of the following is an example of Green IT?

a) Development of a powerful in-house data center
b) Development of efficient in-house software
c) Using a desktop PC instead of a thin client laptop
d) Using web conferencing instead of travelling to meetings
?
d) Using web conferencing instead of travelling to meetings
### Explanation
Green IT (Green Information Technology) is the practice of environmentally sustainable computing. This includes designing, manufacturing, using, and disposing of computers, servers, and associated subsystems efficiently and with minimal or no impact on the environment. Using web conferencing instead of traveling significantly reduces carbon emissions and energy consumption, which aligns with the goals of Green IT.
