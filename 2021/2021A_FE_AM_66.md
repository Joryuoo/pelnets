# 2021A_FE_AM_66
Which of the following is a technique that is used to identify and evaluate the internal and external factors that are helpful or harmful to the objectives of business organizations or projects?

a) Five forces analysis
b) PPM analysis
c) SWOT analysis
d) Value chain analysis
?
c) SWOT analysis
### Explanation
SWOT analysis is a strategic planning technique used to identify Strengths and Weaknesses (internal factors), and Opportunities and Threats (external factors) related to business competition or project planning.
