# 2025A_FE-A_49

Which of the following is an explanation of benchmarking that is used for corporate management?

a) It refers to a qualitative and quantitative understanding of a company's own products, services, and operations through comparison with those of competitors or advanced companies.
b) It refers to drastically reforming the quality and structure of a company by redesigning its business processes from a customer viewpoint and by taking full advantage of information technology.
c) It refers to the ability to plan and manage the allocation of company-wide management resources in an effective and integrated manner in order to improve management efficiency.
d) It refers to the concentration of management resources on the unique skills and technologies of a company that can generate profit and that are superior to those of other companies.
?
a) It refers to a qualitative and quantitative understanding of a company's own products, services, and operations through comparison with those of competitors or advanced companies.

### Explanation
Let's define the corporate management concepts described in each option:

- **a) Benchmarking:** This is the process of measuring a company's own products, services, and practices against the best in the industry or top competitors. The goal is to identify areas for improvement and adopt best practices. This matches the description perfectly.
- **b) Business Process Reengineering (BPR):** This refers to the fundamental rethinking and radical redesign of business processes to achieve dramatic improvements in critical measures of performance (cost, quality, service, and speed).
- **c) Enterprise Resource Planning (ERP):** This is the integrated management of main business processes, often in real-time and mediated by software and technology, to efficiently allocate company-wide resources.
- **d) Core Competence:** This refers to focusing management resources on a company's unique, defining capabilities or advantages that distinguish it from competitors and generate profit.
