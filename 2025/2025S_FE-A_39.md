# 2025S_FE-A_39

Which of the following is the term that represents the process of generating a UML class diagram from a program? 
a) Backtracking 
b) Forward engineering 
c) Re-engineering 
d) Reverse engineering
?
d) Reverse engineering

### Explanation
**Reverse engineering** in software development is the process of analyzing a subject system to identify its current components and their dependencies, often extracting higher-level abstractions like UML class diagrams from the source code.
- **Forward engineering** is the traditional process of moving from high-level design (like a UML diagram) to physical implementation (source code).
- **Re-engineering** involves analyzing and altering an existing system to reconstitute it in a new form (often involving both reverse engineering to understand it, and then forward engineering to rebuild it).
- **Backtracking** is an algorithmic technique for solving problems recursively by trying to build a solution incrementally, removing solutions that fail to satisfy the constraints of the problem.
