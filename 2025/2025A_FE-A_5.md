### Explanation

This question deals with inserting a new node into a **Doubly Linked List**.
Let's analyze the original structure:
`[Head] <-> Employee A (100) <-> Employee K (300) <-> Employee T (200) <-> [Tail]`

We want to insert **Employee G (400)** between **A (100)** and **K (300)**.
The new sequence will be:
`[Head] <-> Employee A (100) <-> Employee G (400) <-> Employee K (300) <-> Employee T (200) <-> [Tail]`

Let's check which pointers change among $a$ through $f$:
- **Employee A (100)**: 
  - NextPointer ($a$): Used to point to K (300). Now it points to G (400). **(Changes)**
  - PreviousPointer ($b$): Still points to 0. *(No change)*
- **Employee T (200)**:
  - NextPointer ($c$): Still points to 0. *(No change)*
  - PreviousPointer ($d$): Still points to K (300). *(No change)*
- **Employee K (300)**:
  - NextPointer ($e$): Still points to T (200). *(No change)*
  - PreviousPointer ($f$): Used to point to A (100). Now it points to G (400). **(Changes)**

So, only $a$ and $f$ change among the pointers labeled $a$ through $f$. 
Note: $x$ and $y$ are also new values (400's Next and Prev pointers), but they are not listed as options to select among $a-f$.
