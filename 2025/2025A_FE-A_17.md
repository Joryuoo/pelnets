# 2025A_FE-A_17

Which of the following is a method used to improve the appearance of a jagged edge of a slanted line so that it appears smooth on a screen, such as that of an LCD?

a) Anti-aliasing
b) Bump mapping
c) Shading
d) Texture mapping
?
a) Anti-aliasing

### Explanation
When displaying diagonal or curved lines on a grid of square pixels (like an LCD screen), a stair-step effect occurs, resulting in jagged edges known as "jaggies" or "aliasing".

- **Anti-aliasing** is a technique used in computer graphics to smooth out these jagged edges by blending the colors of the pixels at the boundaries with the background color, creating an optical illusion of a smooth line.
- **Bump mapping** is a rendering technique used to simulate bumps and wrinkles on the surface of an object without changing its underlying geometry.
- **Shading** refers to the process of altering the color of an object based on its angle to lights and its distance from lights to create a 3D effect.
- **Texture mapping** is a method for defining high-frequency detail, surface texture, or color information on a 3D model.
