# 2025S_FE-A_41

Which of the following is an appropriate explanation of earned value in project management?

a) A measure of work completed in terms of budget authorized for that work.
b) A time-phased budget that estimates the total cost of a project
c) An authorized budget for the planned work
d) Realized cost incurred for the work performed on an activity during a specific time period
?
a) A measure of work completed in terms of budget authorized for that work.

### Explanation
**Earned Value Management (EVM)** is a project management technique for measuring project performance and progress.

Here are the core metrics used in EVM:

| Metric | Name | Explanation | Corresponding Option |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **EV** | Earned Value | A measure of work completed in terms of the budget authorized for that work. | **a) (Correct)** |
| **PV** | Planned Value | An authorized budget assigned to scheduled work. | c) |
| **AC** | Actual Cost | Realized cost incurred for the work performed on an activity during a specific time period. | d) |
| **BAC** | Budget at Completion | A time-phased budget that estimates the total cost of a project. | b) |

Therefore, option **a** is the correct explanation for Earned Value.
