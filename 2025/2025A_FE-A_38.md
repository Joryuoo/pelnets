### Explanation
Let's break down the calculation step-by-step:

1. **Calculate the total number of potential defects:**
   $$ \text{Total Defects} = \text{Program Size} \times \text{Defective Rate} $$
   $$ \text{Total Defects} = 2,000 \text{ k steps} \times 0.04 \text{ defects/k steps} = 80 \text{ defects} $$

2. **Calculate the number of defects discovered in the first year:**
   $$ \text{Discovered Defects} = \text{Total Defects} \times \text{Annual Discovery Rate} $$
   $$ \text{Discovered Defects} = 80 \times 20\% = 16 \text{ defects} $$

3. **Calculate the number of major defects discovered:**
   $$ \text{Major Defects} = \text{Discovered Defects} \times \text{Major Impact Percentage} $$
   $$ \text{Major Defects} = 16 \times 20\% = 3.2 \text{ defects} $$

4. **Calculate the expected correction cost in the first year:**
   Since only major defects are corrected in the first year, we only calculate the cost for those.
   $$ \text{Cost} = \text{Major Defects} \times \text{Cost per Major Defect} $$
   $$ \text{Cost} = 3.2 \times 20,000 \text{ dollars} = 64,000 \text{ dollars} $$

Thus, the expected correction cost is **64,000 dollars**.
