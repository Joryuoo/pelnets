# 2025A_FE-A_56

Which of the following is an example of Green IT?

a) Development of a powerful in-house data center
b) Development of efficient in-house software
c) Using a desktop PC instead of a thin client laptop
d) Using web conferencing instead of traveling to meetings
?
d) Using web conferencing instead of traveling to meetings

### Explanation
**Green IT** (Green Information Technology) is the practice of environmentally sustainable computing. It involves designing, manufacturing, using, and disposing of computers, servers, and associated subsystems efficiently and effectively with minimal or no impact on the environment.

Green IT includes two main concepts:
1. **Greening of IT**: Reducing the environmental footprint of IT itself (e.g., energy-efficient servers, thin clients).
2. **Greening by IT**: Using IT to reduce the environmental footprint of other activities.

Option **d** (Using web conferencing instead of traveling) falls under "Greening by IT," as it leverages technology to reduce carbon emissions from transportation.

- **a)** Developing a powerful in-house data center typically increases energy consumption.
- **b)** While efficient software is good, it isn't the primary hallmark of Green IT compared to tangible emission reductions.
- **c)** Desktop PCs generally consume more power than thin clients; switching *to* a desktop is contrary to Green IT.
