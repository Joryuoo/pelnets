# 2025S_FE-A_2

Which of the following is the binary fraction that is the nearest equivalent to the decimal fraction 23.375?

a) 10111.011
b) 10111.110
c) 11101.011
d) 11101.110
?
a) 10111.011

### Explanation
To convert a decimal fraction to binary, we convert the integer part and the fractional part separately.

**Step 1: Convert the integer part (23) to binary**
Divide by 2 and record the remainders:
- $23 \div 2 = 11$ remainder $1$
- $11 \div 2 = 5$ remainder $1$
- $5 \div 2 = 2$ remainder $1$
- $2 \div 2 = 1$ remainder $0$
- $1 \div 2 = 0$ remainder $1$

Reading the remainders from bottom to top gives: **`10111`**

**Step 2: Convert the fractional part (0.375) to binary**
Multiply by 2 and record the integer parts:
- $0.375 \times 2 = 0.75$ (Integer part: $0$)
- $0.75 \times 2 = 1.5$ (Integer part: $1$, fractional part becomes $0.5$)
- $0.5 \times 2 = 1.0$ (Integer part: $1$, fractional part becomes $0.0$)

Reading the integer parts from top to bottom gives: **`.011`**

**Step 3: Combine both parts**
Integer part + Fractional part = `10111` + `.011` = **`10111.011`**
