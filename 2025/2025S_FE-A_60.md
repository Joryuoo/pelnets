# 2025S_FE-A_60

Company A purchased a machine for an original cost of $126,000. The salvage value after 6 years is $6,000. What is the annual depreciation expense (in dollars) by using the straight-line method?
a) 12,000 b) 20,000 c) 21,000 d) 36,000
?
b) 20,000

### Explanation
The **straight-line depreciation method** distributes the depreciation evenly over the useful life of the asset.

Formula:
$$ \text{Annual Depreciation Expense} = \frac{\text{Original Cost} - \text{Salvage Value}}{\text{Useful Life}} $$

Given values:
- Original Cost = \$126,000
- Salvage Value = \$6,000
- Useful Life = 6 years

Calculation:
$$ \text{Annual Depreciation Expense} = \frac{126,000 - 6,000}{6} = \frac{120,000}{6} = 20,000 $$

Thus, the annual depreciation expense is **\$20,000**.
