# 2025A_FE-A_48

In a business scenario where marketing managers analyze customer interests and sales patterns, design promotional campaigns, and then send targeted emails to customers, which of the following is a modeling technique used to represent how information flows between internal processes, data stores, and external entities?

a) Class Diagram
b) Data Flow Diagram (DFD)
c) Entity-Relationship Diagram (ERD)
d) Use Case Diagram
?
b) Data Flow Diagram (DFD)

### Explanation
Different modeling techniques serve specific purposes in systems analysis and design:

| Modeling Technique | Purpose and Core Elements |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Data Flow Diagram (DFD)** | Focuses on the **flow of data** through a system. It explicitly models how information moves between **processes, data stores, and external entities**. This makes it the perfect tool for visualizing business scenarios involving data processing and transfers. |
| **Class Diagram** | An Object-Oriented (UML) diagram that shows the static structure of a system by depicting its **classes, attributes, operations, and the relationships** among objects. |
| **Entity-Relationship Diagram (ERD)** | A data modeling technique used in database design to show the logical structure of a database, focusing on **entities and their relationships**, without showing the flow of data or processes. |
| **Use Case Diagram** | A UML diagram that describes the functional requirements of a system from the user's perspective, showing **actors and their interactions (use cases)** with the system, rather than the internal data flows. |

Since the scenario requires representing information flow between internal processes, data stores, and external entities, the **Data Flow Diagram (DFD)** is the correct modeling technique.
