### Explanation
Let's analyze the given logic circuit. Based on the standard ITPEC graphic symbols:
1. The first gate (on the left) receives inputs $A$ and $B$. It is a D-shaped gate (AND) with a small circle (bubble) at its output, making it a **NAND gate**.
   - Output of NAND gate = $\overline{A \cdot B} = \overline{A} + \overline{B}$
2. The second gate (on the right) is a D-shaped gate without a bubble, making it an **AND gate**.
   - Its top input is the output of the NAND gate: $\overline{A \cdot B}$
   - Its bottom input is the original input $B$ (branching from the $B$ line).

The final output $Y$ is the logical AND of these two inputs:
$$Y = (\overline{A \cdot B}) \cdot B$$

Using De Morgan's Laws ($\overline{A \cdot B} = \overline{A} + \overline{B}$):
$$Y = (\overline{A} + \overline{B}) \cdot B$$

Distributing $B$:
$$Y = (\overline{A} \cdot B) + (\overline{B} \cdot B)$$

Since $\overline{B} \cdot B = 0$:
$$Y = (\overline{A} \cdot B) + 0$$
$$Y = \overline{A} \cdot B$$

Thus, the logical circuit is equivalent to $\overline{A} \cdot B$.
