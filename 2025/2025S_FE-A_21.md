# 2025S_FE-A_21

![[2025S_FE-A_Q21_full.png]]
?
a) GROUP BY ProductID HAVING SUM(UnitPrice * Quantity) >= 5000;

### Explanation
- **`GROUP BY ProductID`**: This groups the query results by `ProductID` so that aggregate functions can be applied per product.
- **`HAVING SUM(UnitPrice * Quantity) >= 5000`**: This filters the grouped records to only include those where the total sales amount (price multiplied by quantity) is at least $5000. 
- In SQL, the `HAVING` clause is used to filter data after the grouping has occurred, whereas the `WHERE` clause filters data before grouping. Thus, `HAVING` must follow `GROUP BY`.
