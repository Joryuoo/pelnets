# 2025A_FE-A_13

Five processes A, B, C, D and E arrive at almost the same time in the order of A, B, C, D and E. The processes have estimated running times of 10, 6, 2, 8, and 4 milliseconds, respectively. Which of the following is the average process turnaround time with the First-Come-First-Served (FCFS) scheduling algorithm?

a) 14
b) 19.2
c) 20
d) 22
?
c) 20

### Explanation
**First-Come-First-Served (FCFS)** is a non-preemptive scheduling algorithm where processes are executed in the exact order they arrive.

Since they arrive at "almost the same time", we assume their arrival times are $0$, and they enter the ready queue in the given order: $A \rightarrow B \rightarrow C \rightarrow D \rightarrow E$.

Let's calculate the completion time (turnaround time, since arrival time = 0) for each process.

| Process | Running Time | Start Time | Completion (Turnaround) Time |
| :---: | :---: | :---: | :---: |
| A | 10 | 0 | 10 |
| B | 6 | 10 | 16 (10 + 6) |
| C | 2 | 16 | 18 (16 + 2) |
| D | 8 | 18 | 26 (18 + 8) |
| E | 4 | 26 | 30 (26 + 4) |

**Turnaround Time** = Completion Time - Arrival Time (0)

Average Turnaround Time = $\frac{10 + 16 + 18 + 26 + 30}{5} = \frac{100}{5} = 20$ milliseconds.
