### Explanation
In UML (Unified Modeling Language) Class Diagrams, different lines and arrowheads represent different types of relationships:
- **Generalization (Inheritance)** is shown with a **solid line and a hollow, unfilled triangle (arrowhead)** pointing to the superclass. Option (c) shows this relationship, meaning a "Sports car" is a specialized kind of "Automobile".
- Option (a) shows an **Association**, depicting a structural relationship between classes (a 1-to-1 relationship here).
- Option (b) shows a **Dependency**, indicated by a dashed line with an open arrowhead, meaning changes in Automobile affect Driver.
- Option (d) shows **Composition/Aggregation**, represented by a diamond shape at the whole/container class end.
