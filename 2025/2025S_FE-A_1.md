# 2025S_FE-A_1

For an 8-bit binary integer, which of the following is the operation that extracts the most significant 4-bits while making the other bits zero?

a) Bitwise AND operation with 11110000.
b) Bitwise NAND operation with 11110000.
c) Bitwise OR operation with 00001111.
d) Bitwise XOR operation with 00001111.
?
a) Bitwise AND operation with 11110000.

### Explanation
To extract specific bits while setting others to zero, we use a **Bitwise AND** operation with a **bitmask**.

A bitmask works by applying the following boolean logic properties:
- `x AND 1 = x` (Preserves the original bit)
- `x AND 0 = 0` (Forces the bit to zero)

For an 8-bit integer, the most significant 4 bits are the top 4 bits (leftmost), and the least significant 4 bits are the bottom 4 bits (rightmost).
To preserve the top 4 bits and zero out the bottom 4 bits, our mask must be `11110000`.

Let's test this with an arbitrary 8-bit integer `10101101`:
```text
  1010 1101  (Original value)
& 1111 0000  (Bitmask)
-----------------
  1010 0000  (Result)
```
As shown, the most significant 4 bits (`1010`) are extracted, and the other bits are made zero.
