# 2025A_FE-A_50

Which of the following is an explanation of relationship marketing?

a) It is a marketing technique of setting a time limit for a short period, ranging from a few hours to a few days, and selling products on the Internet only during that period.
b) It is a marketing technique of using the GPS function of a smartphone to send advertisements from stores near the current location.
c) It is a marketing technique that aims at a diversified approach to consumers by using several media such as television, newspapers, and magazines.
d) It is a marketing technique that aims to acquire stable sales over a long period of time from each customer by maintaining a good connection with the customer.
?
d) It is a marketing technique that aims to acquire stable sales over a long period of time from each customer by maintaining a good connection with the customer.

### Explanation
Different marketing strategies are described in the options:

- **a) Flash Sales / Time-Based Marketing:** This relies on creating urgency and scarcity by offering discounts or products for a very limited time.
- **b) Location-Based Marketing (Geofencing/Geotargeting):** This uses location tracking (like GPS) to deliver highly relevant, localized advertisements to consumers when they are near a specific physical location.
- **c) Multi-Channel / Cross-Media Marketing:** This strategy uses a mix of different media platforms (TV, print, digital) to reach consumers in various ways and increase campaign visibility.
- **d) Relationship Marketing:** This is a strategy designed to foster customer loyalty, interaction, and long-term engagement. Rather than focusing on short-term customer acquisition and individual sales, it aims to build strong, lasting connections with customers to ensure recurring revenue and stable sales over time. This accurately describes relationship marketing.
