# 2025S_FE-A_15

Q15. When it takes 10 ns to copy a byte, which of the following is the approximate time (in ms) to copy the data for a 1024 × 768-pixel graphics screen with 24-bit color? Here, any overhead time for copying can be ignored. 

a) 22 
b) 23 
c) 24 
d) 25
?
c) 24

### Explanation
To calculate the total time required to copy the screen data, follow these steps:

1. **Calculate the total number of pixels on the screen:**
   $$ 1024 \times 768 = 786,432 \text{ pixels} $$

2. **Calculate the data size per pixel in bytes:**
   The color depth is 24 bits.
   $$ 24 \text{ bits} / 8 \text{ bits per byte} = 3 \text{ bytes per pixel} $$

3. **Calculate the total data size to be copied:**
   $$ 786,432 \text{ pixels} \times 3 \text{ bytes/pixel} = 2,359,296 \text{ bytes} $$

4. **Calculate the total copy time:**
   It takes 10 nanoseconds (ns) to copy one byte.
   $$ \text{Total time} = 2,359,296 \text{ bytes} \times 10 \text{ ns/byte} = 23,592,960 \text{ ns} $$

5. **Convert nanoseconds (ns) to milliseconds (ms):**
   $$ 1 \text{ ms} = 1,000,000 \text{ ns} $$
   $$ \text{Total time in ms} = \frac{23,592,960}{1,000,000} = 23.59296 \text{ ms} $$

Rounding to the nearest whole number gives **24 ms**.
