### Explanation
To find the physical address corresponding to the virtual address 8300, follow these steps:

1. **Determine the Virtual Page**
   The virtual address space is divided into pages of size 4096 ($4\text{K}$).
   - $0\text{K}-4\text{K}$ : $0 - 4095$
   - $4\text{K}-8\text{K}$ : $4096 - 8191$
   - $8\text{K}-12\text{K}$ : $8192 - 12287$
   
   The virtual address `8300` falls in the range of **$8\text{K}-12\text{K}$**.

2. **Calculate the Offset**
   The offset is the remainder when the virtual address is divided by the page size.
   $$ \text{Offset} = 8300 - 8192 = 108 $$

3. **Find the Mapped Page Frame**
   Look at the provided diagram to see where the virtual page $8\text{K}-12\text{K}$ maps to in physical memory.
   According to the mapping arrows in the diagram, the $8\text{K}-12\text{K}$ virtual page maps to the **$24\text{K}-28\text{K}$** physical page frame.

4. **Calculate the Physical Address**
   The base address of the mapped physical frame ($24\text{K}-28\text{K}$) is `24576` (since $6 \times 4096 = 24576$).
   Add the offset to the base address of the physical frame:
   $$ \text{Physical Address} = \text{Frame Base} + \text{Offset} = 24576 + 108 = 24684 $$

Therefore, the corresponding physical address is 24684.
