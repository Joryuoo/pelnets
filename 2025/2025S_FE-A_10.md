### Explanation
We are tasked with finding the cache hit ratio ($h$) when the **Effective Access Time (EAT)** is identical for both System A and System B.

The formula for Effective Access Time is:
$$ \text{EAT} = (\text{Hit Ratio} \times \text{Cache Access Time}) + ((1 - \text{Hit Ratio}) \times \text{Main Memory Access Time}) $$

Let the hit ratio be $h$.
The miss ratio is $(1 - h)$.

**For System A:**
- Cache memory access time = $14 \text{ ns}$
- Main memory access time = $80 \text{ ns}$
$$ \text{EAT}_A = 14h + 80(1 - h) $$
$$ \text{EAT}_A = 14h + 80 - 80h = 80 - 66h $$

**For System B:**
- Cache memory access time = $12 \text{ ns}$
- Main memory access time = $90 \text{ ns}$
$$ \text{EAT}_B = 12h + 90(1 - h) $$
$$ \text{EAT}_B = 12h + 90 - 90h = 90 - 78h $$

**Set the Effective Access Times equal to each other:**
$$ \text{EAT}_A = \text{EAT}_B $$
$$ 80 - 66h = 90 - 78h $$

**Solve for $h$:**
$$ 78h - 66h = 90 - 80 $$
$$ 12h = 10 $$
$$ h = \frac{10}{12} = \frac{5}{6} $$
$$ h \approx 0.8333... $$

The approximate hit ratio is **0.83**.
