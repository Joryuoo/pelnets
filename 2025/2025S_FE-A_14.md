### Explanation
The system uses **preemptive priority scheduling**, where a smaller priority number means a higher priority.

Let's trace the execution step-by-step:
- **Time 0**: P1 arrives (burst = 8, pri = 2). It is the only process, so it starts executing.
- **Time 2**: P2 arrives (burst = 1, pri = 1). Since P2's priority (1) is higher than P1's (2), **P2 preempts P1**. 
  - *P1 remaining burst = 8 - 2 = 6 ms.*
- **Time 2 to 3**: P2 executes for its full burst of 1 ms and finishes at **time 3**.
- **Time 3**: P1 resumes execution.
- **Time 4**: P3 arrives (burst = 2, pri = 3). P1's priority (2) is higher than P3's (3), so P1 continues executing.
- **Time 6**: P4 arrives (burst = 3, pri = 4). P1's priority (2) is higher than P4's (4), so P1 continues.
- **Time 9**: P1 finishes execution. (It ran from 0-2 and 3-9, total 8 ms).
- **Time 9**: Both P3 and P4 are waiting. P3 has higher priority (3 < 4), so P3 starts executing.
- **Time 11**: P3 finishes execution (ran for 2 ms). P4 starts executing.
- **Time 14**: P4 finishes execution (ran for 3 ms).

**Calculate Waiting Time:**
Waiting Time = Turnaround Time - Burst Time = (Completion Time - Arrival Time) - Burst Time

| Process | Arrival | Burst | Completion | Turnaround (C - A) | Waiting (T - B) |
|---------|---------|-------|------------|--------------------|-----------------|
| P1      | 0       | 8     | 9          | $9 - 0 = 9$      | $9 - 8 = 1$   |
| P2      | 2       | 1     | 3          | $3 - 2 = 1$      | $1 - 1 = 0$   |
| P3      | 4       | 2     | 11         | $11 - 4 = 7$     | $7 - 2 = 5$   |
| P4      | 6       | 3     | 14         | $14 - 6 = 8$     | $8 - 3 = 5$   |

**Average Waiting Time:**
$$ \frac{1 + 0 + 5 + 5}{4} = \frac{11}{4} = 2.75 \text{ ms} $$
