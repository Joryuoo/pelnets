### Explanation
Input validation checks are essential to ensure the accuracy and integrity of data entered into a system. 

The requirement is to check whether the "order date is a business day" and whether it is "the same as or prior to the entering date." This requires evaluating the meaning and logical relationship of the data fields against business rules.

- **a) Duplication check:** Ensures that the same transaction or record is not entered more than once.
- **b) Format check:** Validates that the data follows a prescribed format (e.g., date entered as YYYY/MM/DD) and contains valid characters.
- **c) Logical check (or Plausibility check / Consistency check):** Evaluates the logical validity of the data or compares multiple fields to ensure they make sense together (e.g., ensuring an order date is not in the future compared to the entry date, and checking if it falls on a valid business day). This is the correct answer.
- **d) Sequence check:** Ensures that a batch of input records is in the correct sequential order (e.g., numerical or chronological).
