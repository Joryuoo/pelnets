### Explanation

The scenario describes a two-part cyber attack combining social engineering and a brute-force/dictionary attack.

**First Half (Blank A):**
- **The Attack:** The attacker called a receptionist and used psychological manipulation (a "fantastic voice" and conversational tactics) to extract personal information about an IT administrator. This is a textbook **Social Engineering** attack (specifically, pretexting or phishing via phone/vishing).
- **The Countermeasure:** Technical safeguards cannot easily prevent humans from being manipulated over the phone. The most effective countermeasure against social engineering is **security awareness training and education for employees** so they can recognize and correctly handle suspicious requests.

**Second Half (Blank B):**
- **The Attack:** The attacker used the collected personal information to generate a custom password list and launched a remote attack against the server (likely a dictionary attack or credential stuffing) to guess the password.
- **The Countermeasure:** To prevent an attacker from systematically guessing passwords by trying hundreds of combinations, the server should limit the number of allowed login attempts. **Implementing an account lockout policy** after a certain number of consecutive incorrect password attempts will block the automated guessing attack.
