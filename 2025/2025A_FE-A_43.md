### Explanation
To find the maximum allowable downtime, we first need to calculate the total agreed service hours in a month.

**Step 1: Calculate the daily service hours.**
The service operates from 7 AM to 11 PM.
11 PM is equivalent to 23:00 in a 24-hour format.
Service hours per day = $23 - 7 = 16$ hours/day.

**Step 2: Calculate the total service hours in a month.**
The service operates for 30 business days a month.
Total service hours = $30 \text{ days} \times 16 \text{ hours/day} = 480 \text{ hours/month}$.

**Step 3: Calculate the maximum allowable downtime.**
The agreed availability is 99% or more. This means the system must be available for at least 99% of the total service hours. 
The maximum allowable downtime is the remaining 1% of the total service hours.
Maximum downtime = $480 \text{ hours} \times 0.01 = 4.8 \text{ hours}$.

Therefore, the system can experience up to **4.8 hours** of downtime in a month while still satisfying the SLA.
