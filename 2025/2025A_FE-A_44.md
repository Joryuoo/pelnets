# 2025A_FE-A_44

Which of the following is an appropriate description of the relationship between a record of an incident and a record of a problem in IT service management?

a) A cross-reference to the incident that triggered the problem is included in the record of the problem.
b) If known errors have been identified at the time of ending the record of the problem, the record of the incident that triggered the record of the problem is deleted.
c) One (1) problem record is always associated with one (1) incident record.
d) Problems are classified and recorded by a different criterion from the classification of incidents.
?
a) A cross-reference to the incident that triggered the problem is included in the record of the problem.

### Explanation
In IT Service Management (such as ITIL framework):
- An **Incident** is an unplanned interruption to an IT service or reduction in the quality of an IT service. The goal of Incident Management is to restore normal service operation as quickly as possible.
- A **Problem** is the underlying cause of one or more incidents. The goal of Problem Management is to investigate the root cause of incidents and prevent them from happening again.

Let's evaluate the options:
- **a) Correct.** When a problem record is created to investigate the root cause of an incident, a cross-reference (link) is established between the problem record and the triggering incident(s) to maintain traceability.
- **b) Incorrect.** Incident records are historical logs of service interruptions. They are never deleted, even after a known error or problem resolution is found, because they serve as vital audit trails and reporting metrics.
- **c) Incorrect.** Multiple incidents can be caused by a single problem. Therefore, a single problem record may be linked to many different incident records.
- **d) Incorrect.** To effectively analyze trends and link problems to incidents, both incidents and problems typically share a common categorization and classification scheme.
