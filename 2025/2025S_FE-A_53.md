# 2025S_FE-A_53

Which of the following is the appropriate explanation for product innovation in the management of technology? 
a) Acquiring business profits through the achievements of technology development 
b) Developing new products or products that enable differentiation from other companies 
c) Making innovative reforms in business processes 
d) Strategically managing business with technology as the core
?
b) Developing new products or products that enable differentiation from other companies

### Explanation
In Management of Technology (MOT), **innovation** is generally categorized into *product innovation* and *process innovation*.

- **Product innovation** involves creating new products or significantly improving existing ones to differentiate from competitors.
- **Process innovation** involves making innovative changes to production, delivery, or business processes (such as Option c).

- **Option a** relates to general technological monetization.
- **Option b** correctly defines *product innovation*.
- **Option c** defines *process innovation*.
- **Option d** describes *Management of Technology (MOT)* as a whole.
