# 2025S_FE-A_18

Q18. Of the functions provided by a DBMS, which of the following is a means for achieving protection for data confidentiality?

a) Checking referential constraints when the data is updated 
b) Managing a transaction that combines a series of processes as a logical unit 
c) Managing the data access rights of users 
d) Placing an exclusive lock to data before updates
?
c) Managing the data access rights of users

### Explanation
Information security is built on three core principles known as the CIA Triad: **Confidentiality, Integrity, and Availability**.

- **Confidentiality** ensures that data is not disclosed or made available to unauthorized individuals or processes. Managing data access rights (Option C) restricts access so that only authorized users can read or modify the data, thereby protecting its confidentiality.
- **Integrity** ensures that data is accurate, complete, and consistent. Checking referential constraints (Option A) and placing exclusive locks (Option D) help maintain data integrity and prevent corruption during concurrent updates.
- **Transactions** (Option B) ensure both integrity and consistency by adhering to ACID (Atomicity, Consistency, Isolation, Durability) properties, meaning a series of database operations either all succeed or all fail safely.

Therefore, access rights management is specifically aimed at confidentiality.
