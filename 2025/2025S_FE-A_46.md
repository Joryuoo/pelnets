# 2025S_FE-A_46

Which of the following is a technique that is used to perform the optimal resource allocation for a computerization investment in each category classified on the basis of the similarities in risks and investment value?

a) 3C analysis
b) Benchmarking
c) Enterprise architecture
d) IT portfolio
?
d) IT portfolio

### Explanation
Let's define each of the given techniques:

| Technique | Description |
| :--- | :--- |
| **3C analysis** | A marketing strategy framework focusing on the **C**ompany, **C**ompetitors, and **C**ustomers. It is used to identify success factors in a business environment. |
| **Benchmarking** | The practice of comparing business processes and performance metrics to industry bests and best practices from other companies. |
| **Enterprise architecture (EA)** | A conceptual blueprint that defines the structure and operation of an organization, focusing on aligning IT with business goals. |
| **IT portfolio (Management)** | The application of systematic management to the investments, projects, and activities of enterprise IT departments. Similar to financial portfolios, it involves **categorizing IT investments based on risk and value** (return on investment) to optimize resource allocation. |

Since the question explicitly mentions optimal resource allocation based on "risks and investment value" for computerization investments, **IT portfolio** is the correct answer.
