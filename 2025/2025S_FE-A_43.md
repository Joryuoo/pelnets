### Explanation
To find the maximum allowed downtime, we must first calculate the total agreed service hours for the month.

**Step 1: Calculate daily service hours**
The service period is from 8:00 AM to 10:00 PM.
$\text{10:00 PM (22:00)} - \text{8:00 AM (08:00)} = 14 \text{ hours/day}$

**Step 2: Calculate total monthly service hours**
There are 30 business days in the month.
$\text{Total Service Hours} = 14 \text{ hours/day} \times 30 \text{ days} = 420 \text{ hours}$

**Step 3: Calculate maximum allowable downtime**
The SLA requires an availability of 99.5% or more. This means the service can be down for at most 0.5% of the total service time.
$\text{Maximum Downtime} = \text{Total Service Hours} \times (1 - \text{Availability})$
$\text{Maximum Downtime} = 420 \text{ hours} \times 0.005 = 2.1 \text{ hours}$

Therefore, the maximum downtime that satisfies the SLA is **2.1 hours**.
