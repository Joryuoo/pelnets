# 2025A_FE-A_47

Which of the following is a measure for the risk of a decline in the system maintenance and operation quality during the outsourcing of an IT service?

a) Clearly stating the detailed fee structure by service in the contract or other documents, in order to validate the service costs
b) Requesting a proposal for the human resource development plan of the company after outsourcing during the stage of subcontractor selection, and clearly stating it in the contract or other documents
c) Securing human resources with capabilities to evaluate the appropriateness of service costs for system maintenance and operation within the company
d) Using indexes that demonstrate the service quality in order to promote improvement activities after agreeing on the desired level of quality with the subcontractor
?
d) Using indexes that demonstrate the service quality in order to promote improvement activities after agreeing on the desired level of quality with the subcontractor

### Explanation
When outsourcing IT services, one of the primary risks is that the subcontractor might deliver a lower quality of service than expected or required. 

To mitigate this risk, it is essential to establish objective, measurable standards for service quality and hold the subcontractor accountable to them. 
- **Option d** is the correct measure because it describes the use of a **Service Level Agreement (SLA)** paired with **Key Performance Indicators (KPIs)** or quality indexes. By agreeing on the desired level of quality and continuously monitoring it using these indexes, both parties can objectively assess performance and promote continuous improvement activities.
- **Options a and c** focus on validating and managing **service costs**, not service quality.
- **Option b** focuses on the subcontractor's internal human resource development, which is indirectly related to quality but is not a direct measure or control mechanism for operational service quality delivery.
