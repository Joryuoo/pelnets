# 2025S_FE-A_8

For a CPU with a clock frequency of 800 MHz, where one instruction is executed in an average time of 4 clock cycles, what is the CPU performance in MIPS?

a) 0.2
b) 20
c) 200
d) 3,200
?
c) 200

### Explanation
**MIPS** stands for **Millions of Instructions Per Second**. It is a standard metric used to measure CPU execution performance.

**Step 1: Understand the given parameters**
- Clock frequency ($f$) = $800 \text{ MHz} = 800 \times 10^6 \text{ cycles/second}$
- Cycles Per Instruction ($CPI$) = $4 \text{ cycles/instruction}$

**Step 2: Calculate the number of instructions executed per second**
$$
\text{Instructions per second} = \frac{\text{Clock frequency (cycles/second)}}{CPI \text{ (cycles/instruction)}}
$$
$$
\text{Instructions per second} = \frac{800 \times 10^6}{4} = 200 \times 10^6 \text{ instructions/second}
$$

**Step 3: Convert to MIPS**
To find MIPS, we divide the instructions per second by $10^6$ (one million):
$$
\text{MIPS} = \frac{200 \times 10^6}{10^6} = 200
$$

The CPU can execute 200 Million Instructions Per Second.
