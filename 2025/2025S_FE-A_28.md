### Explanation
- **Principle of Least Privilege**: This fundamental information security concept states that users and applications should be granted only the minimum level of access necessary to perform their required functions.
- The application in question only needs to *retrieve* and *display* user information. It does not need to insert, update, delete records, or alter tables.
- Therefore, only the **Reference right** (which allows selecting a record) is needed. Granting Update or Administrator rights would violate the principle of least privilege and increase the risk of unauthorized data modification or leakage if the application is compromised.
