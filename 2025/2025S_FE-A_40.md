# 2025S_FE-A_40

Which of the following is an appropriate explanation of a Sprint in the Scrum methodology? 
a) A daily meeting where team members review progress and plan the day's work 
b) A fixed-length period dedicated to developing a product increment 
c) A self-organizing team responsible for product development 
d) List of tasks and features the Scrum team has yet to complete
?
b) A fixed-length period dedicated to developing a product increment

### Explanation
In the Scrum framework:
- A **Sprint** is a timeboxed, fixed-length period (usually 1 to 4 weeks) during which a dedicated team completes a set amount of work to deliver a usable and potentially releasable product increment. Option b describes this.
- Option a describes the **Daily Scrum** (or stand-up meeting).
- Option c describes the **Scrum Team** (or Development Team).
- Option d describes the **Product Backlog** (or Sprint Backlog depending on the scope).
