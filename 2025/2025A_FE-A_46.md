# 2025A_FE-A_46

Which of the following is an appropriate feasibility evaluation when checking whether the lifetime benefits of the proposed information system are greater than its lifetime costs?

a) Economic feasibility
b) Operational feasibility
c) Scheduling feasibility
d) Technical feasibility
?
a) Economic feasibility

### Explanation
A feasibility study evaluates the practicality and viability of a proposed project. There are several types of feasibility evaluations:

| Feasibility Type | Focus Area | Example Questions |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Economic feasibility** | **Costs and benefits (ROI, profitability).** | Do the lifetime benefits of the system outweigh its lifetime costs? Is the project financially viable? |
| **Operational feasibility** | **Business processes and user acceptance.** | Will the system be used effectively by the staff? Does it fit into the current business operations? |
| **Scheduling feasibility** | **Time constraints and deadlines.** | Can the project be completed within the required timeframe? |
| **Technical feasibility** | **Technology and infrastructure.** | Do we have the technical resources, hardware, and software capabilities to build the system? |

Since the question specifically mentions evaluating **lifetime benefits versus lifetime costs**, this is a classic cost-benefit analysis, which falls entirely under **Economic feasibility**.
