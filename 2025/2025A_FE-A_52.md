# 2025A_FE-A_52

Which of the following is the appropriate description of process innovation?

a) Development of an innovative new product on the basis of an original and high technology
b) Development of innovative manufacturing procedures that improve the quality of a product
c) Outsourcing manufacturing to another company that has a semiconductor manufacturing process
d) Wide adoption after passing the competition, resulting in the establishment of a de facto standard
?
b) Development of innovative manufacturing procedures that improve the quality of a product

### Explanation
**Process Innovation** refers to the implementation of a new or significantly improved production or delivery method. This includes significant changes in techniques, equipment, and/or software.

- **a)** Refers to **Product Innovation** (creating a new product).
- **b)** Refers to **Process Innovation** (improving the manufacturing/procedures).
- **c)** Refers to **Outsourcing/Offshoring**.
- **d)** Refers to achieving a **De facto standard**.

Therefore, the correct description for process innovation is **b**.
