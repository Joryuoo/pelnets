### Explanation
Let's perform the four given tree traversal techniques on the provided binary tree (exploring the left child first).

The tree structure is:
- Root: `18`
- Left child of `18`: `5` (with left child `3`)
- Right child of `18`: `20` (with right child `22`)

**1. Breadth-First (Level-Order) Traversal:**
Visits nodes level by level from top to bottom, left to right.
- Output: `18` $\rightarrow$ `5` $\rightarrow$ `20` $\rightarrow$ `3` $\rightarrow$ `22`
- Last two nodes: **3, 22**

**2. Pre-Order Traversal (Node, Left, Right):**
- Visit Node (18), Left Subtree (5), Right Subtree (20)
- Output: `18` $\rightarrow$ `5` $\rightarrow$ `3` $\rightarrow$ `20` $\rightarrow$ `22`
- Last two nodes: **20, 22**

**3. In-Order Traversal (Left, Node, Right):**
- Visit Left Subtree (5), Node (18), Right Subtree (20)
- Output: `3` $\rightarrow$ `5` $\rightarrow$ `18` $\rightarrow$ `20` $\rightarrow$ `22`
- Last two nodes: **20, 22**

**4. Post-Order Traversal (Left, Right, Node):**
- Visit Left Subtree (5), Right Subtree (20), Node (18)
- Output: `3` $\rightarrow$ `5` $\rightarrow$ `22` $\rightarrow$ `20` $\rightarrow$ `18`
- Last two nodes: **20, 18**

Comparing the results, the **In-order** and **Pre-order** traversals both end with the same last two nodes visited: **20 and 22**. Therefore, option d) is the correct combination.
