### Explanation
In an **affiliate program model**:
1. The web site operator agrees to participate and places an advertisement on their site.
2. The web site viewer visits the site and clicks the advertisement.
3. The viewer goes to the linked product sales page.
4. The viewer purchases the product.
5. The advertiser pays a success fee to the web site operator based on the generated sales.

Therefore, operation (5) corresponds to paying the success/referral fee to the website operator.
