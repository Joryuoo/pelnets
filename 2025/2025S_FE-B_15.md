### Explanation

The `calcDistance` function computes the Minkowski distance (or L-p norm distance) between two arrays `p1` and `p2`, defined mathematically as:
$D = \left( \sum_{i=1}^{m} |p1[i] - p2[i]|^n \right)^{\frac{1}{n}}$

Given: `p1 = {3, 1, 5, 2}`, `p2 = {4, 6, 2, 3}`

**For `n = 1` (Blank A):**
We calculate the absolute differences raised to the power of 1, and sum them up:
- $|3 - 4|^1 = 1$
- $|1 - 6|^1 = 5$
- $|5 - 2|^1 = 3$
- $|2 - 3|^1 = 1$
Sum (`distance`) $= 1 + 5 + 3 + 1 = 10$
Next, `ex = 1 ÷ 1 = 1`.
Finally, `distance = pow(10, 1) = 10`. So, **A = 10**.

**For `n = 2` (Blank B):**
We calculate the absolute differences squared, and sum them up:
- $|3 - 4|^2 = 1^2 = 1$
- $|1 - 6|^2 = 5^2 = 25$
- $|5 - 2|^2 = 3^2 = 9$
- $|2 - 3|^2 = 1^2 = 1$
Sum (`distance`) $= 1 + 25 + 9 + 1 = 36$
Next, `ex = 1 ÷ 2 = 0.5`.
Finally, `distance = pow(36, 0.5) = \sqrt{36} = 6$. So, **B = 6**.
