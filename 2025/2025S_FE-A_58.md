### Explanation
A **cause and effect diagram** (also known as an Ishikawa or fishbone diagram) shows the causes of a specific event. 
In the structure of the diagram:
- The "spine" points to the main problem or effect.
- The "bones" (branches) point to the causes contributing to the effect.
- A sub-branch pointing into a main branch represents a *sub-cause* contributing to the *main cause*.

Therefore, branch B pointing to branch A indicates that B is a cause or sub-cause contributing to A.
