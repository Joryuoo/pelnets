### Explanation
To find the average access time, we sum the four components given:

1. **Average seek time:** Given as $5\text{ ms}$.
2. **Controller overhead:** Given as $0.1\text{ ms}$.
3. **Rotational latency:** The average rotational latency is half the time of one full rotation.
   - Rotation speed = $6,000\text{ RPM}$ (Revolutions Per Minute)
   - Rotations per second = $6,000 \div 60 = 100\text{ rev/s}$
   - Time for one full rotation = $1 \div 100\text{ seconds} = 10\text{ ms}$
   - Average rotational latency = $10 \div 2 = 5\text{ ms}$.
4. **Transfer time:** Time to transfer $16\text{ kB}$ of data.
   - Transfer rate = $1\text{ MB/s} = 1,024\text{ kB/s}$
   - Transfer time = $16\text{ kB} \div 1,024\text{ kB/s} = 1 \div 64\text{ seconds}$
   - In milliseconds: $(1 \div 64) \times 1000 = 15.625\text{ ms}$.

Total Average Access Time = $5 + 0.1 + 5 + 15.625 = 25.725\text{ ms}$.
