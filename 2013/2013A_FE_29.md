### Explanation
Let us trace the logic expressions through the NAND-based circuit:

1. **Central NAND Gate:**
   Receives inputs $A$ and $B$. Its output is:
   $$G_1 = \overline{A \cdot B}$$

2. **Upper Combinational Path for $C$:**
   - The upper NAND gate receives input $A$ and $G_1 = \overline{A \cdot B}$:
     $$G_2 = \overline{A \cdot \overline{A \cdot B}} = \overline{A \cdot (\overline{A} + \overline{B})} = \overline{A \cdot \overline{B}}$$
   - The lower NAND gate receives input $B$ and $G_1 = \overline{A \cdot B}$:
     $$G_3 = \overline{B \cdot \overline{A \cdot B}} = \overline{B \cdot (\overline{A} + \overline{B})} = \overline{B \cdot \overline{A}} = \overline{\overline{A} \cdot B}$$
   - The final NAND gate for output $C$ receives $G_2$ and $G_3$:
     $$C = \overline{G_2 \cdot G_3} = \overline{\overline{A \cdot \overline{B}} \cdot \overline{\overline{A} \cdot B}} = (A \cdot \overline{B}) + (\overline{A} \cdot B) = A \oplus B$$
   Hence, output $C$ is the **Exclusive OR (XOR)** of $A$ and $B$, corresponding to the Sum bit of a half adder.

3. **Lower Inverting NAND Gate for $D$:**
   - The NAND gate producing $D$ has both of its inputs tied to $G_1 = \overline{A \cdot B}$. A NAND gate with identical inputs acts as a NOT inverter:
     $$D = \overline{G_1} = \overline{\overline{A \cdot B}} = A \cdot B$$
   Hence, output $D$ is the **logical product (AND)** of $A$ and $B$, corresponding to the Carry bit of a half adder.

Matching $C = A \oplus B$ and $D = A \cdot B$ in the options table yields option **b**.
