# 2013A_FE_55
A chart or a diagram is created to resolve a quality problem. After the trends of factors are analyzed, it is determined whether more than 80% of all the factors belong to a small group of factors. Which of the following is effective for this purpose?

a) Cause and effect diagram
b) Control chart
c) Pareto chart
d) Scatter diagram
?
c) Pareto chart
### Explanation
A Pareto chart contains both bars and a line graph, displaying the frequency of defects and their cumulative impact. It is used to identify the most significant factors causing a problem, illustrating the Pareto principle (80/20 rule) where roughly 80% of problems come from 20% of causes.

* **a) Cause and effect diagram (Fishbone):** Identifies potential causes for an effect or problem.
* **b) Control chart:** Monitors how a process changes over time.
* **d) Scatter diagram:** Shows the relationship between two variables.
