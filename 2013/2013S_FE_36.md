# 2013S_FE_36
![[2013Apr_FE_AM_Q36_p1.png]]

b) 57
### Explanation
The relationship between frequency ($f$), speed of light ($c$), and wavelength ($\lambda$) is $f = \frac{c}{\lambda}$.

Given $c = 2 \times 10^8$ m/s.

1. **Calculate frequency for the shortest wavelength (1000 nm):**
   * $\lambda_1 = 1000 \text{ nm} = 1000 \times 10^{-9} \text{ m} = 1 \times 10^{-6} \text{ m}$
   * $f_1 = \frac{2 \times 10^8}{1 \times 10^{-6}} = 2 \times 10^{14} \text{ Hz} = 200 \text{ THz}$

2. **Calculate frequency for the longest wavelength (1400 nm):**
   * $\lambda_2 = 1400 \text{ nm} = 1400 \times 10^{-9} \text{ m} = 1.4 \times 10^{-6} \text{ m}$
   * $f_2 = \frac{2 \times 10^8}{1.4 \times 10^{-6}} \approx 1.428 \times 10^{14} \text{ Hz} \approx 143 \text{ THz}$

3. **Calculate the bandwidth:**
   * Bandwidth = $|f_1 - f_2| = 200 \text{ THz} - 143 \text{ THz} = 57 \text{ THz}$.
