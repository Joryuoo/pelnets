### Explanation

**Blank A:**
Blank A represents the condition evaluated before entering "Invitation ticket processing" in the flowchart. 
According to Requirement (2) in the scenario: "One invitation ticket is sent to a customer whose total purchase amount for all the products the customer purchases during the period is 50,000 yen or more."
Therefore, the program must check if the **Total purchase amount ≥ 50000** before assigning an invitation ticket. This corresponds to choice **e**.

*Why other choices are incorrect for A:*
- Choices (a) and (b) incorrectly check the product code range `A001` to `A199`. The product code restriction applies to campaign products for *sweepstakes* tickets (Requirement 1), not for the total purchase amount related to invitation tickets.
- Choice (c) checks if the number of invitation tickets is greater than 0, but this condition is evaluated to decide whether to *award* the invitation ticket in the first place.

**Blank B:**
Blank B is the condition evaluated before proceeding to "Address processing" (where the customer is written to the address file). 
According to Requirement (3): "For every customer to whom sweepstakes tickets or an invitation ticket are sent, one record is created in the address file."
This means an address record should be written if the customer has earned **either** a sweepstakes ticket **or** an invitation ticket.
Therefore, the condition must check if the **Number of sweepstakes tickets > 0 or number of invitation tickets > 0**. This corresponds to choice **f**.

*Why other choices are incorrect for B:*
- Choice (e) uses an `and` logical operator. This would incorrectly require the customer to have earned *both* types of tickets to be added to the address file.
- Choices (d) and (g) are incomplete because they only check for one specific type of ticket or the purchase amount, neglecting the other condition for inclusion into the address file.
