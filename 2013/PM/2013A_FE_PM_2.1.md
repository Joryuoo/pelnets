### Explanation
To convert the `Flight` relation to the Third Normal Form (3NF), we need to eliminate partial and transitive dependencies based on the functional dependencies provided in Table 2.

The original relation is:
`Flight (FlightID, FlightNumber, FlightDate, DepartureTime, ArrivalTime, OriginAirport, DestinationAirport, AirplaneType)`

**Step 1: Identify Candidate Keys**
From FD1, `FlightID` determines all other attributes, making it a primary key.
From FD2, `{FlightNumber, FlightDate}` determines `FlightID`, `DepartureTime`, and `AirplaneType`. Since `FlightID` determines everything else, `{FlightNumber, FlightDate}` is also a candidate key.

**Step 2: Eliminate Transitive and Partial Dependencies**
A transitive dependency occurs when a non-key attribute depends on another non-key attribute. A partial dependency occurs when a non-key attribute depends on only a part of a composite candidate key.
- From FD4: `FlightNumber -> OriginAirport, DestinationAirport`. 
  `FlightNumber` is part of the candidate key `{FlightNumber, FlightDate}`. Thus, `OriginAirport` and `DestinationAirport` are partially dependent on this candidate key (and transitively dependent on `FlightID`). We must move them to a new relation with `FlightNumber` as the primary key: `(FlightNumber, OriginAirport, DestinationAirport)`.
- From FD3: `{FlightNumber, DepartureTime} -> ArrivalTime`.
  `ArrivalTime` is dependent on `{FlightNumber, DepartureTime}`, which is not a candidate key. We must separate it into a new relation: `(FlightNumber, DepartureTime, ArrivalTime)`.

**Step 3: Keep the Remaining Attributes**
After removing `OriginAirport`, `DestinationAirport`, and `ArrivalTime` to their own relations, the remaining attributes in the original `Flight` relation are:
`(FlightID, FlightNumber, FlightDate, DepartureTime, AirplaneType)`

This combination of relations exactly matches option **c**.

**Why the other choices are wrong:**
* **a:** Incorrectly splits attributes without following the functional dependencies (e.g., separating `FlightDate` from `DepartureTime` and `AirplaneType` without a key).
* **b:** Incorrectly creates a separate relation for `(FlightNumber, FlightDate, AirplaneType)`, which is unnecessary since `AirplaneType` is functionally dependent on `FlightID` and can stay in the main relation.
* **d:** Incorrectly groups `ArrivalTime`, `OriginAirport`, and `DestinationAirport` together. `ArrivalTime` depends on `DepartureTime`, while the airports do not.
* **e:** This is the original, unnormalized relation (not in 3NF).
