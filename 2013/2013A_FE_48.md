### Explanation
In a Data Flow Diagram (DFD):
- Data flows cannot go directly from one data store to another data store without going through a process. 
- Data flows cannot go directly from an external entity to a data store, or from a data store to an external entity, without passing through a process.
- Data flows cannot go directly between two external entities.

Flow C represents a direct flow from a data store to another data store (or similar invalid flow without a process in between). This is fundamentally invalid in DFD modeling, as data cannot move or transform itself without a process.
