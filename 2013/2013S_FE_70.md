### Explanation
A SWOT analysis matrix categorizes factors into Internal/External and Helpful/Harmful to achieving business objectives:
- **Strengths (Internal, Helpful):** Positive attributes and resources internal to the organization.
- **Weaknesses (Internal, Harmful):** Negative attributes and limitations internal to the organization.
- **Opportunities (External, Helpful):** Favorable external factors that could give a competitive advantage.
- **Threats (External, Harmful):** Unfavorable external factors that could cause problems for the business.

Matrix **c)** correctly places Strengths and Weaknesses as Internal factors, and Opportunities and Threats as External factors, matching them with their Helpful/Harmful nature.
