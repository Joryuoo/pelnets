# 2013S_FE_1
Which of the following is the result of calculating the arithmetic expression “B6–7C”? Here, all numbers are represented in unsigned hexadecimal.

a) 32
b) 3A
c) B2
d) BA
?
b) 3A
### Explanation
Calculating `B6 - 7C` in hexadecimal:
- In the rightmost column (16s^0): `6 - C`. Since `6` is smaller than `C` (which is 12 in decimal), we borrow `1` (which is 16 in decimal) from the next column.
- `(16 + 6) - 12 = 22 - 12 = 10`. `10` in hexadecimal is `A`.
- In the next column (16s^1): `B` (which is 11) becomes `A` (which is 10) because we borrowed 1.
- `A - 7 = 10 - 7 = 3`.

Putting it together, the result is `3A`.
