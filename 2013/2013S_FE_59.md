# 2013S_FE_59
Which of the following is a service delivery process that is defined, agreed, recorded, and managed in IT service management?

a) Quality management
b) Risk management
c) Schedule management
d) Service level management
?
d) Service level management
### Explanation
In IT service management (ITSM), **Service level management (SLM)** is the process of negotiating, defining, agreeing, recording, and managing the levels of IT services through Service Level Agreements (SLAs).
- Quality, Risk, and Schedule management are broader project management or general management practices, whereas Service level management specifically deals with the agreed-upon delivery metrics between an IT service provider and the customer.
