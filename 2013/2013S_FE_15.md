# 2013S_FE_15
The magnetic head of a hard disk drive is currently positioned at cylinder number 100, and the cylinder numbers 120, 90, 70, 80, 140, 110, and 60 are lined up in the I/O request queue. What is the total number of cylinders that the head moves to satisfy the conditions below?
[Conditions]
(1) The seek optimization method is applied to I/O requests, which are sorted in order of increasing cylinder number so that the head can move in the same direction to the extent possible.
(2) The head is currently moving in the direction where cylinder number increases.
(3) When there are no more I/O requests in the current direction, the direction of movement is reversed.
(4) The results are not affected by the processing order of I/O requests.
(5) New I/O requests do not occur during the process.

a) 80
b) 120
c) 160
d) 220
?
b) 120
### Explanation
This scenario describes the **SCAN (elevator) disk scheduling algorithm**.

The head starts at cylinder 100 and is moving **upwards** (increasing direction).
The queued requests are: 60, 70, 80, 90, 110, 120, 140.

1. **Moving Upwards (Increasing):**
   The head services requests greater than 100 in increasing order.
   * `100 -> 110` (moves 10)
   * `110 -> 120` (moves 10)
   * `120 -> 140` (moves 20)
   **Total upward movement = 40 cylinders.** (140 - 100)

2. **Moving Downwards (Decreasing):**
   Having reached the highest requested cylinder (140) and finding no more requests in the upward direction, the head reverses. It services the remaining requests in decreasing order: 90, 80, 70, 60.
   * `140 -> 90` (moves 50)
   * `90 -> 80` (moves 10)
   * `80 -> 70` (moves 10)
   * `70 -> 60` (moves 10)
   **Total downward movement = 80 cylinders.** (140 - 60)

**Total movement** = $40 + 80 = 120$ cylinders.
