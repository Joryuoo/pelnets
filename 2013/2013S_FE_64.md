# 2013S_FE_64
When an information strategy is developed, which of the following is an item that must always be made consistent with the strategy?

a) Medium and long-term management plans
b) New emerging information technologies
c) The annual plan of the information systems department
d) The improvement plan of a mission-critical system
?
a) Medium and long-term management plans
### Explanation
An information strategy exists to support and realize the overall business goals of an organization. Therefore, it must be perfectly aligned and consistent with the company's medium and long-term management plans.
- **b)** While new technologies are considered, the strategy should not blindly adopt them if they do not align with broader business goals.
- **c)** The annual plan of the IS department is derived *from* the information strategy. The strategy itself must align with the top-level management plans.
- **d)** System improvement plans are lower-level tactical implementations that follow the broader strategy.
