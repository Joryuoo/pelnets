### Explanation
Note: The provided answer key is `c) 300 / 200`. Let's evaluate this mixture:
- 300 liters of 40% (Type A) = 120 liters of acid.
- 200 liters of 20% (Type B) = 40 liters of acid.
- Total acid = 160 liters. Total volume = 500 liters.
- Concentration = 160 / 500 = 32%.
The question asks for a 24% solution. The correct mixture for 24% would be 100 liters of Type A and 400 liters of Type B (giving 40 + 80 = 120 liters of acid, 120/500 = 24%), which is choice `a`.
