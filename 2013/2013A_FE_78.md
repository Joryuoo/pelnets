# 2013A_FE_78
![[2013Oct_FE_AM_Q78_full.png]]

a) Bungee jumping
b) Mountain biking
c) River rafting
d) Scuba diving
?
d) Scuba diving
### Explanation
The expected profit is calculated by multiplying the profit of each outcome by its probability and summing the results for each tour option.
- **Mountain biking**: (400 * 0.95) + (200 * 0.05) = 380 + 10 = **390**
- **River rafting**: (600 * 0.90) + (-400 * 0.10) = 540 - 40 = **500**
- **Bungee jumping**: (300 * 0.97) + (250 * 0.03) = 291 + 7.5 = **298.5**
- **Scuba diving**: (900 * 0.80) + (-1000 * 0.20) = 720 - 200 = **520**

Scuba diving yields the highest expected profit.
