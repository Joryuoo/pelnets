a) 18
### Explanation
Function point (FP) value is calculated by multiplying each function count by its corresponding weighting factor, summing these up, and then multiplying by the complexity correction coefficient.

1. **Calculate the unadjusted function points:**
   - External input: 1 × 4 = 4
   - External output: 2 × 5 = 10
   - Internal logical file: 1 × 10 = 10
   - External interface file: 0 × 7 = 0
   - External inquiry: 0 × 4 = 0
   - **Total:** 4 + 10 + 10 = 24

2. **Apply the correction coefficient:**
   - 24 × 0.75 = 18

The final function point value is 18.
