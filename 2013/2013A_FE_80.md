### Explanation
A **negative correlation** means that as one variable increases on the x-axis, the other variable decreases on the y-axis. On a scatter plot, this is represented by a cluster of points trending downwards from left to right.
- **b)** shows a clear downward trend, indicating a strong negative correlation.
- **a)** shows a clear upward trend, indicating a strong positive correlation.
- **c)** and **d)** show points scattered without a clear linear directional trend, indicating little to no correlation.
