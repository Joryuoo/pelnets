# 2013S_FE_8
![[2013Apr_FE_AM_Q8_full.png]]

a) The pointers for New York and Philadelphia are set to 90 and 110 respectively.
b) The pointers for New York and Philadelphia are set to 110 and 50 respectively.
c) The pointers for New York and Philadelphia are set to 110 and 70 respectively.
d) The pointers for New York and Philadelphia are set to 110 and 90 respectively.
?
d) The pointers for New York and Philadelphia are set to 110 and 90 respectively.
### Explanation
In a singly-linked list, each element points to the address of the next element. 
Currently, **New York** (at address 50) points to **Washington DC** (at address 90).

To insert **Philadelphia** (at address 110) between them:
1. **New York's pointer** must be updated to point to the new node, Philadelphia, so it becomes `110`.
2. **Philadelphia's pointer** must point to the node that followed New York originally, which is Washington DC. So, it becomes `90`.
