c) c: 1, s: 0
### Explanation
A full adder computes the sum of three binary inputs (typically two significant bits and a carry-in bit) and outputs a sum bit ($s$) and a carry-out bit ($c$).
The inputs are $x = 1$, $y = 0$, and $z = 1$.
Adding these together in binary:
$1 + 0 + 1 = 10_2$ (which is 2 in decimal).
In the binary result $10_2$, the sum bit ($s$) is the least significant bit, which is 0. The carry-out bit ($c$) is the most significant bit, which is 1.

Therefore, $c = 1$ and $s = 0$.
