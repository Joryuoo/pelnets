# 2013A_FE_67
According to Michael Porter, who is the author of "Competitive Strategy", which of the following is the tool that is used to classify a company’s business activities into main activities and supporting activities on a function-by-function basis in order to identify key activities that generate the profit for the company and the possible benefit for customers?

a) 3C analysis
b) Five forces analysis
c) SWOT analysis
d) Value chain analysis
?
d) Value chain analysis
### Explanation
Value chain analysis, introduced by Michael Porter, is a strategic tool used to classify a company's activities into primary (main) activities (like inbound logistics, operations, outbound logistics, marketing and sales, service) and support activities (like procurement, human resource management, technological development, infrastructure). This classification helps identify which activities create value and competitive advantage.
- **a) 3C analysis** focuses on Company, Competitors, and Customers.
- **b) Five forces analysis** (also by Porter) analyzes industry attractiveness based on five competitive forces.
- **c) SWOT analysis** identifies Strengths, Weaknesses, Opportunities, and Threats.
