# 2013S_FE_12
When the average instruction execution time of a computer is 20 nanoseconds, what is the performance of this computer in MIPS?

a) 5
b) 10
c) 20
d) 50
?
d) 50
### Explanation
MIPS stands for Millions of Instructions Per Second, which is a measure of a computer's processing speed.

To find the performance in MIPS:
1. Determine the time per instruction in seconds: $20 \text{ nanoseconds} = 20 \times 10^{-9} \text{ seconds}$.
2. Calculate the number of instructions executed per second:
   $$\frac{1 \text{ second}}{20 \times 10^{-9} \text{ seconds/instruction}} = 50,000,000 \text{ instructions/second}$$
3. Convert to MIPS by dividing by $10^6$ (one million):
   $$\frac{50,000,000}{1,000,000} = 50 \text{ MIPS}$$
