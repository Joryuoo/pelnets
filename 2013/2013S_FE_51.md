# 2013S_FE_51
![[2013Apr_FE_AM_Q51_full.png]]

a) A signle order is not made from multiple suppliers. 
b) Multiple products are not ordered with a single order. 
c) Order slips correspond to delivery slips on a one-to-one basis. 
d) The identical product is purchased from a signle supplier. 
?
a) A signle order is not made from multiple suppliers.
### Explanation
In the given E-R diagram notation (from the actual exam), interpreting the relationship correctly reveals that a single order is mapped to one supplier, meaning it cannot be made from multiple suppliers.
