# 2013A_FE_74
![[2013Oct_FE_AM_Q74_p1.png]]

a) Continuous production
b) Individual production
c) Lot production
d) Process production
?
b) Individual production
### Explanation
Production system "A" is characterized by small production quantity, build-to-order production, many types of products, high frequency of arrangements, and assembly processing. This aligns with **Individual Production** (or job-shop production).
- **a) Continuous production** aligns with system C (large quantity, build-to-stock, few product types).
- **c) Lot production** aligns with system B (medium quantity, build-to-order/build-to-stock).
- **d) Process production** aligns with system D (chemical processing, very low frequency of arrangements).
