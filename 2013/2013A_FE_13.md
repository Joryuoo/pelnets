### Explanation
This question compares the characteristics of **SRAM** (Static RAM) with **DRAM** (Dynamic RAM).
- **SRAM**: Has a **lower** integration degree because each cell requires multiple transistors (often 4 to 6 flip-flops). However, it is much **faster** than DRAM and does not require periodic refreshing. Because of its high speed and higher cost, it is typically used for **cache memory**. Its circuit components are **flip-flops**.
- **DRAM**: Has a higher integration degree, uses condensers (capacitors) and transistors, is slower, and is typically used for main memory.
Thus, the correct combination for SRAM is lower integration, faster access, cache memory usage, and flip-flop circuit components.
