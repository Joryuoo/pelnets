a) Second normal form
### Explanation
The table has a primary key of **Employee number**. 
* The attributes **Employee name**, **Year of employment**, and **Position** depend directly on the primary key.
* The attribute **Position allowance** depends on **Position**, which in turn depends on the primary key. This is a transitive dependency.

Because there is a transitive dependency, the table is not in Third Normal Form (3NF). However, it is in Second Normal Form (2NF) because all non-key attributes are fully functionally dependent on the entire primary key, and there are no partial dependencies (as the primary key consists of a single attribute).
