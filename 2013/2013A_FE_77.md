# 2013A_FE_77
Which of the following is a specialization-oriented organization that is divided into groups, such as research and development, manufacturing, sales, human resources, general affairs, and accounting, according to the type of work performed?

a) Divisional organization
b) Functional organization
c) Matrix organization
d) Project organization
?
b) Functional organization
### Explanation
A **Functional organization** groups people by the specialized functions they perform, such as R&D, manufacturing, sales, and accounting, promoting high specialization and efficiency within those specific departments.
- **a) Divisional organizations** group employees by product, market, or geographic location rather than function.
- **c) Matrix organizations** combine functional and project-based structures, meaning employees typically report to multiple managers.
- **d) Project organizations** are temporary teams formed specifically to complete a project and are dissolved afterward.
