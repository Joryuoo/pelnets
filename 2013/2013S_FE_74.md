# 2013S_FE_74
Which of the following is an appropriate example of the effective utilization of RFID?

a) Identification and control of humans or objects by using a small wireless chip
b) Information entry by scanning a digital code printed on paper through a reader
c) Short-range data communication using infrared rays
d) Voice data communications between a cell phone and headphones
?
a) Identification and control of humans or objects by using a small wireless chip
### Explanation
RFID (Radio Frequency Identification) uses electromagnetic fields to automatically identify and track tags (small wireless chips) attached to objects or embedded in items/cards.
- **b)** Describes scanning barcodes or QR codes.
- **c)** Describes IrDA (Infrared Data Association) communication.
- **d)** Describes Bluetooth communication.
