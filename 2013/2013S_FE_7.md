### Explanation
In a **min-heap**, every parent node must have a value less than or equal to its children. 
When a new node (value `11`) is inserted, it is first added to the next available position at the bottom level of the tree.
To restore the min-heap property, the node `11` "bubbles up" by swapping with its parent as long as it is smaller than its parent. It will continue swapping upward until it reaches a valid position (where its parent is smaller than `11`). Choice b correctly represents the tree structure after `11` has been bubbled up to its correct position.
