# 2013S_FE_13
There is a digital video recording system installed with four security cameras. In this system, the video data from each camera is captured at 30 frames per second with a resolution of 640×480 pixels and a color depth of 16 bits per pixel, and then it is stored in the hard disk drive. Which of the following is the approximate storage capacity that is required for recording all video data for one minute? Here, 1MB is $10^6$ bytes, and 1GB is $10^9$ bytes.

a) 19 MB
b) 74 MB
c) 1.2 GB
d) 4.5 GB
?
d) 4.5 GB
### Explanation
Let's calculate the total data required step-by-step:

1. **Data per frame for one camera:**
   $640 \times 480 \text{ pixels} \times 16 \text{ bits/pixel} = 4,915,200 \text{ bits}$
   Convert to bytes: $4,915,200 \text{ bits} \div 8 \text{ bits/byte} = 614,400 \text{ bytes}$
2. **Data per second for one camera:**
   $614,400 \text{ bytes/frame} \times 30 \text{ frames/second} = 18,432,000 \text{ bytes/second}$
3. **Data per second for all 4 cameras:**
   $18,432,000 \text{ bytes} \times 4 = 73,728,000 \text{ bytes/second}$
4. **Data for one minute (60 seconds):**
   $73,728,000 \text{ bytes/second} \times 60 \text{ seconds} = 4,423,680,000 \text{ bytes}$
5. **Convert to GB:**
   $4,423,680,000 \text{ bytes} \div 10^9 \text{ bytes/GB} \approx 4.42 \text{ GB}$

This is approximately **4.5 GB**.
