?
c) 1, 0..*, 0..*, 1
### Explanation
In a standard resolution of a many-to-many relationship using an association class, the newly introduced class (StockHolding) takes the "many" (0..*) side of the relationship for both ends, while the original classes (Warehouse and Product) take the "one" (1) side. 

Therefore, Warehouse to StockHolding is 1 to 0..*, and Product to StockHolding is 1 to 0..*. This matches 1, 0..*, 0..*, 1 for blanks A, B, C, D respectively.
