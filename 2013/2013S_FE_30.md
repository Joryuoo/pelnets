# 2013S_FE_30
Among the descriptions concerning three-dimensional computer graphics, which of the following is an explanation of polygon?

a) It is a basic element that is used for constituting a polyhedron taking the form of a closed solid or for approximating a quadric surface or a free-form surface.
b) It is a basic element, such as a pattern or design, which is pasted on the surface of a modeled object.
c) It is an image obtained by converting a model recorded inside a computer to a two-dimensional form so that it can be rendered on the screen.
d) It is an image that transforms smoothly from an image a including an object A to an image b including a different object B having another shape.
?
a) It is a basic element that is used for constituting a polyhedron taking the form of a closed solid or for approximating a quadric surface or a free-form surface.
### Explanation
In 3D computer graphics, a **polygon** is a basic geometric shape (like a triangle or quadrilateral) used as a building block to model 3D surfaces and objects. By connecting many polygons together, complex solid shapes and free-form surfaces can be approximated (a polygon mesh).

Brief explanation of the other choices:
- **b)** Describes a **texture** or texture mapping, where a 2D image is wrapped onto a 3D surface.
- **c)** Describes **rendering**, the process of generating a 2D image from a 3D model.
- **d)** Describes **morphing**, an animation technique that smoothly transitions one image or shape into another.
