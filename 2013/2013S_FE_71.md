# 2013S_FE_71
Which of the following is an appropriate form of transaction where one company (i.e., the bidder) gains a decisive share of the basic equity of another target business?

a) Acquisition
b) Business alliance
c) Capital participation
d) Merger
?
a) Acquisition
### Explanation
An acquisition is a transaction where one company purchases a decisive share of another company's equity to gain control of it. 
- **b) Business alliance:** An agreement between two or more companies to cooperate and share resources to achieve a common goal without a change in equity control.
- **c) Capital participation:** A transaction involving the acquisition of a minority stake in another company, usually for partnership rather than total control.
- **d) Merger:** A combination of two companies to form a new single entity, rather than one acquiring the other outright.
