# 2013S_FE_79
![[2013Apr_FE_AM_Q79_p1.png]]

a) 402
b) 938
c) 1220
d) 1340
?
b) 938
### Explanation
Under the **periodic average method** (also known as the weighted average cost method), the average cost per unit is calculated at the end of the period based on total goods available for sale, and this average cost is applied to all units sold.

1. **Calculate Total Units Available:**
   Beginning Inventory: 10 units
   Purchases: 40 units + 50 units
   Total Units Available = $10 + 40 + 50 = 100$ units

2. **Calculate Total Cost of Goods Available:**
   Beginning Inventory: $10 \text{ units} \times 12 \text{ dollars} = 120$ dollars
   First Purchase: $40 \text{ units} \times 13 \text{ dollars} = 520$ dollars
   Second Purchase: $50 \text{ units} \times 14 \text{ dollars} = 700$ dollars
   Total Cost = $120 + 520 + 700 = 1340$ dollars

3. **Calculate Average Unit Cost:**
   Average Cost = $1340 \text{ dollars} / 100 \text{ units} = 13.4 \text{ dollars per unit}$

4. **Calculate Cost of Sales:**
   Total Units Sold = $40 \text{ units} + 30 \text{ units} = 70$ units
   Cost of Sales = $70 \text{ units} \times 13.4 \text{ dollars per unit} = 938$ dollars
