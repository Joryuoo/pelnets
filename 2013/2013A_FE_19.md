### Explanation
In system dependability and performance evaluation, **RAS** stands for **Reliability, Availability, and Serviceability**:
1. **Reliability:** The ability of a system to maintain uninterrupted operation over a given duration under specified conditions. The standard quantitative indicator is **MTBF** (Mean Time Between Failures), measuring the average working time between successive failures.
2. **Availability:** The proportion of time a system is fully operational and capable of performing its required functions. It is given by the operational ratio:
   $$\text{Availability} = \frac{\text{MTBF}}{\text{MTBF} + \text{MTTR}}$$
3. **Serviceability (Maintainability):** The ease, speed, and effectiveness with which a system can be diagnosed and repaired after a failure. The standard quantitative indicator is **MTTR** (Mean Time To Repair), measuring the average duration required to repair and restore the system.

Matching these indexes to the columns of the table yields row **b**.
