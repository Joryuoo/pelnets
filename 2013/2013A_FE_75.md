# 2013A_FE_75
Which of the following is the methodology that performs both production scheduling and inventory control on the basis of steps (1) through (3) described below?
(1) Based on the types and quantities as well as the component list of the product planned to be manufactured during a fixed period of time in the future, the required quantity of components is calculated.
(2) The net order quantity of each component is calculated from the amount of inventory that can be allocated.
(3) The ordering point of each component is determined in consideration of the manufacturing or procurement lead time.

a) CAM
b) FMS
c) JIT
d) MRP
?
d) MRP
### Explanation
**MRP** (Material Requirements Planning) is a production planning, scheduling, and inventory control system used to manage manufacturing processes by breaking down end items into required components and determining their order points based on lead times.
- **a) CAM** (Computer-Aided Manufacturing) uses software to control machine tools in manufacturing.
- **b) FMS** (Flexible Manufacturing System) is a manufacturing system designed to easily adapt to changes in the type and quantity of the product being manufactured.
- **c) JIT** (Just-In-Time) focuses on reducing flow times within production systems and response times from suppliers by receiving goods only as they are needed.
