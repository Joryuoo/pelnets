### Explanation
The question asks for the logical expression that results in zero (0) *only* when the binary values of all inputs (x1, x2, ..., xn) are all zeros or all ones. 
This means the expression must evaluate to true (1) for any mixed combination of 0s and 1s, and false (0) when the inputs are perfectly uniform.
