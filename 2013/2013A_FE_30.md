# 2013A_FE_30
According to ISO 9241-210: 2010, which of the following is defined as the “extent to which a system, product, or service can be used by specified users to achieve specified goals with effectiveness, efficiency, and satisfaction in a specified context of use”?

a) Accessibility
b) Adaptability
c) Availability
d) Usability
?
d) Usability
### Explanation
### Correct Answer
- **d) Usability**

### Explanation
- In **ISO 9241-210:2010** (*Ergonomics of human-system interaction — Part 210: Human-centred design for interactive systems*), **Usability** is formally defined as the:
  > "extent to which a system, product, or service can be used by specified users to achieve specified goals with **effectiveness**, **efficiency**, and **satisfaction** in a specified **context of use**."

### Why Other Choices Are Incorrect
- **a) Accessibility:** The degree to which systems, products, or services can be used by individuals with the widest range of user capabilities and requirements, including people with diverse disabilities.
- **b) Adaptability:** The capacity of software or systems to dynamically adjust their interface, configuration, or functionality to varying contexts, user profiles, or execution environments.
- **c) Availability:** The measure of readiness for correct service, representing the proportion of scheduled time that a system or service is operational and accessible to authorized users.
