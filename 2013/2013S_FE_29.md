# 2013S_FE_29
When a 60-minute monaural audio signal is digitalized using a PCM format with a sampling frequency of 44.1 kHz and a quantization bit rate of 16 bits, what is the approximate data volume in Mbytes? Here, the data is not compressed.

a) 80
b) 160
c) 320
d) 640
?
c) 320
### Explanation
To calculate the total data volume of the uncompressed audio, multiply the following components:
- **Duration:** 60 minutes = $60 \times 60 = 3600$ seconds
- **Sampling frequency:** 44.1 kHz = 44,100 samples per second
- **Quantization:** 16 bits = 2 bytes per sample
- **Channels:** Monaural = 1 channel

Data volume in bytes = Duration $\times$ Sampling frequency $\times$ Bytes per sample $\times$ Channels
$= 3600 \text{ s} \times 44100 \text{ samples/s} \times 2 \text{ bytes/sample} \times 1$
$= 317,520,000 \text{ bytes}$

To convert to Megabytes (Mbytes), divide by $10^6$ (or $2^{20}$ for standard binary prefixes, though metric prefixes are often used in these estimations):
$317,520,000 \text{ bytes} \approx 317.5 \text{ Mbytes}$

This value is closest to 320 Mbytes.
