### Explanation
The flowchart shows the standard procurement process for an information system:
1. **A**: The buyer requests information (RFI - Request for Information) to gather general info on technologies or suppliers.
2. **B**: The buyer specifies the system to be procured and procurement conditions, and requests a proposal. This is the **RFP (Request for Proposal)**.
3. **C**: The buyer decides the supplier based on proposals (Selection of supplier).
4. **D**: The buyer and supplier confirm roles and responsibilities in writing (Conclusion of a contract).

Since block B corresponds to requesting the submission of a proposal based on specified conditions, it represents the RFP.
