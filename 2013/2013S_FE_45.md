# 2013S_FE_45
As a security measure on the Internet, a type of challenge-response test called a CAPTCHA can be used to determine whether the client is a human or a computer program. Which of the following is an appropriate purpose of using such a measure?

a) To protect against a relay attack
b) To protect against a virus or a worm
c) To protect against automated spamming
d) To protect against spyware or adware
?
c) To protect against automated spamming
### Explanation
CAPTCHA (Completely Automated Public Turing test to tell Computers and Humans Apart) ensures that the user attempting to access a service or submit a form is a human and not an automated bot. This measure specifically protects against automated spamming, bulk account creation, or automated malicious scraping.
