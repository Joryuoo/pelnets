# 2013A_FE_38
In the traditional IPv4 addressing scheme, which of the following is the private IP address range that is reserved for Class B?

a) 10.0.0.0 through 10.255.255.255
b) 169.254.0.0 through 169.254.255.255
c) 172.16.0.0 through 172.31.255.255
d) 192.168.0.0 through 192.168.255.255
?
c) 172.16.0.0 through 172.31.255.255
### Explanation
The reserved private IPv4 address ranges defined by RFC 1918 are:
* **Class A:** `10.0.0.0` – `10.255.255.255`
* **Class B:** `172.16.0.0` – `172.31.255.255`
* **Class C:** `192.168.0.0` – `192.168.255.255`

Choice b (`169.254.0.0`) is the link-local address range (APIPA), not a standard private IP range.
