# 2013S_FE_78
Which of the following is a qualitative and exploratory forecasting approach that is used for obtaining the most reliable consensus of opinions from a group of experts through a series of questionnaires?

a) Brainstorming
b) Delphi method
c) Focus group
d) Tree diagram method
?
b) Delphi method
### Explanation
The Delphi method is a structured forecasting and decision-making technique that relies on a panel of experts. The experts answer multiple rounds of questionnaires anonymously, with a facilitator providing summary feedback after each round, aiming to converge toward a reliable consensus.
- **a) Brainstorming:** An unstructured or semi-structured group session designed to generate a large number of ideas rapidly.
- **c) Focus group:** A moderated discussion with a group of people (usually consumers) to gather opinions and feedback about a product, service, or concept.
- **d) Tree diagram method:** A visual tool used to break down broad categories into finer levels of detail hierarchically.
