# 2013S_FE_4
There is a series of project activities to be performed by two staff members, Mr. X and Mr. Y. When each of them works alone, it can be completed by Mr. X in 3 hours and by Mr. Y in 6 hours. When Mr. X starts to work at 9:00 a.m. and then Mr. Y starts to work with Mr. X at 10:00 a.m., which of the following is the time at which all the activities are completed? Here, the activities can be divided and performed in parallel by the two members without any loss of productivity.

a) 10:20 a.m.
b) 10:40 a.m.
c) 11:20 a.m.
d) 11:40 a.m.
?
c) 11:20 a.m.
### Explanation
First, determine their individual work rates (portions of the project completed per hour):
- Mr. X's rate = 1/3 of the project per hour
- Mr. Y's rate = 1/6 of the project per hour

From 9:00 a.m. to 10:00 a.m. (1 hour), Mr. X works alone:
- Work completed by Mr. X = 1/3
- Work remaining = 1 - 1/3 = 2/3

At 10:00 a.m., they start working together. Their combined rate is:
- Combined rate = (1/3) + (1/6) = (2/6) + (1/6) = 3/6 = 1/2 of the project per hour.

Time required to finish the remaining 2/3 of the project together:
- Time = Work remaining / Combined rate = (2/3) / (1/2) = 4/3 hours
- 4/3 hours is 1 hour and 20 minutes.

Adding 1 hour and 20 minutes to 10:00 a.m. results in **11:20 a.m.**
