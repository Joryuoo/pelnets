### Explanation
The image typically shows a combination of basic logic gates (like AND, OR, and NOT gates, or NAND/NOR gates) that form a standard XOR (Exclusive OR) circuit. 

An XOR gate outputs 1 only when the number of 1s in the inputs is odd (i.e., when the inputs are different: one is 1 and the other is 0). It outputs 0 when the inputs are the same.
