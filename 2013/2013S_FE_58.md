# 2013S_FE_58
According to the ISO/IEC 20000 series, which of the following has the objective that aims to minimize disruption to the business by proactive identification and analysis of the cause of incidents and by managing problems to closure?

a) Business relation management
b) Change management
c) Incident management
d) Problem management
?
d) Problem management
### Explanation
**Problem management** aims to identify the root cause of incidents and manage them to closure, minimizing disruption to the business and preventing incidents from recurring.
- **Incident management:** Aims to restore normal service operation as quickly as possible, reacting to disruptions rather than finding underlying root causes.
- **Change management:** Ensures that standard methods and procedures are used for efficient and prompt handling of all changes to minimize impact on service quality.
- **Business relationship management:** Focuses on maintaining a good relationship between the service provider and the customer.
