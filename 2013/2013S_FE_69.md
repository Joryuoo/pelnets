# 2013S_FE_69
In the product life cycle, which of the following is the stage where the market begins to understand the value of the product, and both product lines and sales channels need to be expanded?

a) Introduction stage
b) Decline stage
c) Growth stage
d) Maturity stage
?
d) Maturity stage
### Explanation
*Note: The description provided in the question strongly characterizes the **Growth stage** (where the market begins to understand and accept the product, leading to a need to expand channels and product lines). However, according to the provided answer key, the expected answer is **d) Maturity stage**.*

During the maturity stage, companies often expand product lines (product differentiation) and explore new sales channels to maintain market share and prolong the product's life cycle as market growth slows down.
- **a) Introduction:** The product is just introduced; focus is on awareness.
- **b) Decline:** Sales drop; focus is on phasing out or reducing costs.
- **c) Growth:** Market acceptance increases rapidly.
