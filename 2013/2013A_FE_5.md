d) v is an aunt of w.
### Explanation
The formula defines a relationship $F(v, w)$ based on predicates $P(x, y)$ (x is a parent of y) and $W(x)$ (x is female).
- The logical conditions describe that $v$ is female ($W(v)$).
- There exists some $x$ who is the parent of both $v$ and $y$, where $v \neq y$ (making $v$ and $y$ siblings).
- $y$ is the parent of $w$.
- Therefore, $v$ is the sister of $w$'s parent, which means $v$ is the aunt of $w$.
