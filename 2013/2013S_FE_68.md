# 2013S_FE_68
Which of the following is an explanation of core competence management?

a) A flat organization structure with minimal hierarchical layers is maintained to accelerate decision making.
b) Comparative analysis with other successful companies is performed to drive management innovation.
c) Knowledge scattered across the company is shared to improve the overall problem solving capability.
d) Management is centered on proprietary know-how and technology that are not easy for other companies to imitate.
?
d) Management is centered on proprietary know-how and technology that are not easy for other companies to imitate.
### Explanation
Core competence refers to the defining capabilities or advantages that distinguish an enterprise from its competitors.
- **d)** Core competence management focuses on leveraging unique, hard-to-imitate resources, proprietary know-how, and technologies to maintain a competitive advantage.
- **a)** describes maintaining a flat organizational structure.
- **b)** describes benchmarking.
- **c)** describes knowledge management.
