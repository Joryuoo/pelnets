# 2013A_FE_65
Among the costs related to computerization, which of the following is included in the running cost?

a) Consulting cost of information systems strategy planning
b) Cost of systems engineers performing requirements definition
c) Initial installation cost of software packages
d) Maintenance cost of information equipment such as servers
?
d) Maintenance cost of information equipment such as servers
### Explanation
Running costs (also known as operational costs or OPEX) refer to the ongoing expenses required to operate, maintain, and support systems after they have been installed. The maintenance cost of servers is a continuous expense incurred during the system's operational lifecycle.
- **a), b), and c)** are all considered initial costs (capital expenditures or CAPEX) associated with planning, development, and deployment of the system before it enters normal operation.
