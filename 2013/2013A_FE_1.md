# 2013A_FE_1
When the hexadecimal value 2A is stored in an 8-bit register and is logically shifted left three bits, which of the following is the resulting value in decimal? Here, the bit positions vacated on the right are filled with zeros, and bits shifted out on the left are discarded.

a) 2
b) 5
c) 80
d) 160
?
c) 80
### Explanation
### Method Explanation

1. **Convert Hexadecimal 2A to Binary:**
   - Hexadecimal digit 2 in 4-bit binary: `0010`
   - Hexadecimal digit A (decimal 10) in 4-bit binary: `1010`
   - Therefore, `2A₁₆` = `0010 1010₂` (which equals 2 × 16 + 10 = 42 in decimal).

2. **Perform Logical Left Shift by 3 Bits:**
   - Original 8-bit register: `0010 1010`
   - Shifting left by 3 bits:
     - The 3 leftmost bits (`001`) are shifted out and discarded.
     - The remaining bits shift 3 positions to the left: `01010`.
     - The 3 bit positions vacated on the right are filled with zeros (`000`).
   - Resulting 8-bit register value: `0101 0000₂`.

3. **Convert Binary Result to Decimal:**
   - `0101 0000₂` = 2⁶ + 2⁴ = 64 + 16 = 80.

### Why Other Options Are Incorrect
- **a) 2:** Arithmetic / calculation error.
- **b) 5:** Corresponds to a logical **right** shift by 3 bits (`0010 1010₂ >> 3` = `0000 0101₂` = 5 in decimal).
- **d) 160:** Corresponds to shifting left by 2 bits without overflow (40 × 4 = 160), rather than shifting left by 3 bits.
