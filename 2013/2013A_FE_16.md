# 2013A_FE_16
Which of the following is an appropriate explanation of a plasma display?

a) It consists of many tiny cells between two glass panels containing a mixture of noble gases with a phosphor coating, and displays images by using a gas discharge.
b) It displays images by making an electron beam discharged from an electron gun hit against the fluorescent material on the surface of the tube to emit light.
c) It displays images by making use of the fact that light is emitted when a voltage is applied to an organic compound sandwiched between electrodes.
d) It does not emit light by itself, so the backlight is used to enhance the visibility of images to be displayed.
?
a) It consists of many tiny cells between two glass panels containing a mixture of noble gases with a phosphor coating, and displays images by using a gas discharge.
### Explanation
### Method Explanation

A **Plasma Display Panel (PDP)** consists of millions of tiny cells (sub-pixels) sandwiched between two glass substrates:
- Each cell is filled with a mixture of noble gases (typically neon and xenon).
- Applying an electric voltage across electrodes ionizes the gas mixture, creating a **gas discharge (plasma)** that emits ultraviolet (UV) radiation.
- The UV rays excite the **phosphor coating** on the cell walls, causing the phosphors to emit red, green, or blue visible light.

### Why Other Options Are Incorrect
- **b) Electron beam from an electron gun striking fluorescent material:** Describes a **CRT (Cathode Ray Tube)** display.
- **c) Light emitted when voltage is applied to an organic compound between electrodes:** Describes an **OLED (Organic Light-Emitting Diode)** display.
- **d) Does not emit light by itself and uses a backlight:** Describes an **LCD (Liquid Crystal Display)**, which requires backlighting (such as CCFL or LED) and liquid crystal shutters to display images.
