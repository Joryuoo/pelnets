# 2013A_FE_16
Which of the following is an appropriate explanation of a plasma display?

a) It consists of many tiny cells between two glass panels containing a mixture of noble gases with a phosphor coating, and displays images by using a gas discharge.
b) It displays images by making an electron beam discharged from an electron gun hit against the fluorescent material on the surface of the tube to emit light.
c) It displays images by making use of the fact that light is emitted when a voltage is applied to an organic compound sandwiched between electrodes.
d) It does not emit light by itself, so the backlight is used to enhance the visibility of images to be displayed.
?
d) It does not emit light by itself, so the backlight is used to enhance the visibility of images to be displayed.
### Explanation
*Note: The provided answer key states 'd', which actually describes an **LCD** (Liquid Crystal Display). The correct technical definition of a plasma display is 'a'.*
- **a) Plasma Display:** Uses tiny cells containing noble gases and phosphors; it creates images via gas discharge (plasma).
- **b) CRT (Cathode Ray Tube):** Uses an electron gun to strike fluorescent material.
- **c) OLED (Organic Light-Emitting Diode):** Emits light when voltage is applied to an organic compound.
- **d) LCD (Liquid Crystal Display):** Does not emit its own light and requires a backlight to illuminate the pixels.
