### Explanation
In the Product Portfolio Management (PPM) matrix (also known as the BCG Matrix), products are categorized into four quadrants based on market growth rate and market share:
- **Cash Cow (C):** High market share in a low-growth market. They generate more cash than they consume, providing stable funds.
- **Star (Top-Left):** High market share in a high-growth market. They require heavy investment but yield high returns.
- **Question Mark (Top-Right):** Low market share in a high-growth market. They require significant investment with uncertain returns.
- **Dog (Bottom-Right):** Low market share in a low-growth market. They generate little cash and have low potential.

Based on the axes shown in the diagram, "C" represents the high market share and low market growth rate quadrant.
