# 2012S_FE_46
Which of the following is a method that is used to check if an image or other digital content has been illegally copied and resold?

a) Digital certificate
b) Digital preservation
c) Digital signature
d) Digital watermark
?
d) Digital watermark
### Explanation
A digital watermark is a kind of marker covertly embedded in a noise-tolerant signal such as audio, video, or image data. It is typically used to identify ownership of the copyright of such signal and to check for illegal copying and distribution.
- **Digital certificate** proves identity in a network.
- **Digital signature** provides authentication and non-repudiation of messages.
