# 2012A_FE_43
From a viewpoint of security measures, which of the following is an appropriate purpose of confirming the destination address with the sender at the time of sending e-mail?

a) To prevent any delay or wrong relay of e-mail
b) To prevent e-mail from being sent to a wrong recipient
c) To prevent spam e-mail by using OP25B (Outbound Port 25 Blocking)
d) To prevent spam e-mail by using SPF (Sender Policy Framework)
?
b) To prevent e-mail from being sent to a wrong recipient
### Explanation
Confirming the destination address directly addresses the risk of **misdirected emails** (sending sensitive or private information to the wrong person). This is a crucial security measure to prevent accidental data leaks or information disclosure.

- **Choice a:** Delays or incorrect relays are network or mail server configuration issues.
- **Choice c:** OP25B prevents infected machines inside an ISP's network from directly sending spam by blocking outbound port 25 traffic.
- **Choice d:** SPF is a DNS-based authentication mechanism that helps prevent email spoofing, not accidental wrong recipients.
