# 2012A_FE_61
Which of the following is an appropriate description concerning application maintenance?

a) The completion of maintenance is recorded in order to prevent maintenance activities from being left unfinished.
b) The maintenance activity is completed when the test is finished by the maintenance engineer in charge.
c) The production library is directly updated when the change can be considered to be simple.
d) The program after the successful completion of the test is promptly registered in the production library, and then its completion is reported to the maintenance approver.
?
a) The completion of maintenance is recorded in order to prevent maintenance activities from being left unfinished.
### Explanation
In application maintenance, proper management processes are critical. Recording the completion of maintenance prevents activities from being left unfinished and maintains an accurate audit trail of changes.
* **b) is incorrect** because completing a test is not the end of the process; documentation, approval, and release procedures must follow.
* **c) is incorrect** because directly updating the production library, even for simple changes, bypasses quality control and version management, risking system stability.
* **d) is incorrect** because a program should not be registered in the production library *before* its completion is reported to and approved by the maintenance approver. Approval must precede the final release.
