### Explanation
Based on UML Class Diagram notations:
- **A** points to a hollow diamond, representing **Aggregation** (Catalog aggregates CatalogItems).
- **B** is a directed arrow, representing **Navigability** (Order navigates to Catalog).
- **C** points to an open triangle, representing **Generalization/Inheritance** (PhoneOrder and MailOrder are subclasses of Order).
- **D** points to a solid diamond, representing **Composition** (Order is composed of OrderItems; if the order is destroyed, items are destroyed).
