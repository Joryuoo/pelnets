# 2012A_FE_44
Which of the following is the public key cryptography algorithm that is named after the initials of its three researchers and is based on the difficulty of factorizing extremely large numbers into prime factors?

a) AES
b) DES
c) DSA
d) RSA
?
d) RSA
### Explanation
**RSA (Rivest–Shamir–Adleman)** is a widely used public-key cryptosystem. It is named after its inventors, Ron Rivest, Adi Shamir, and Leonard Adleman. The security of RSA relies on the practical difficulty of integer factorization (specifically, the product of two large prime numbers).

- **Choice a (AES) & Choice b (DES):** These are symmetric-key algorithms, not public-key algorithms.
- **Choice c (DSA):** Digital Signature Algorithm, which is based on the discrete logarithm problem, not prime factorization, and is not named after three researchers' initials.
