### Explanation
Fast-tracking is a schedule compression technique where activities that would normally be done in sequence are overlapped or performed in parallel.
By dividing activity E into E1, E2, and E3 (as shown in the provided revised plan), some of these sub-activities can be started earlier or overlapped with other tasks.
