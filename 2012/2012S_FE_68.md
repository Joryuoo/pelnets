# 2012S_FE_68
![[2012S_FE_AM_Q68_full.png]]

a) It currently serves as a primary supply source of cash, and no additional investments should be made.  
b) It has little need for additional investments in business continuity, and the possibility of withdrawal must be considered in the future.  
c) It is currently generating a large inflow of cash, but it also needs continuous investment into the future.  
d) It is currently generating a small inflow of cash, but some additional investments may make it change to a supply source of cash in the future. 
?
c) It is currently generating a large inflow of cash, but it also needs continuous investment into the future.  
### Explanation
This question refers to the BCG (Boston Consulting Group) matrix or Portfolio matrix. Category B represents a "Star" which is characterized by high market growth and high relative market share.
- **a)** Refers to a "Cash Cow" (high market share, low market growth).
- **b)** Refers to a "Dog" (low market share, low market growth).
- **c)** This is correct. A "Star" generates a large amount of cash due to its high market share, but it also consumes a lot of cash (needs continuous investment) to maintain its position in a high-growth market.
- **d)** Refers to a "Question Mark" or "Problem Child" (low market share, high market growth).
