# 2012S_FE_54
Which of the following is an appropriate explanation concerning a milestone in project management?

a) A milestone is a major event in a project schedule that is easily identifiable by such things as the completion of a significant deliverable and the occurrence of an event.
b) A milestone is conducted at the mid-term or end of a project by an outside facilitator in order to provide the project stakeholders with the opportunity to be candid.
c) The goal of a milestone is to identify and mitigate problems with large projects as early in the project as possible to minimize their impact.
d) The purpose of a milestone is to visually and structurally provide the relationships among all the project deliverables and their various components.
?
a) A milestone is a major event in a project schedule that is easily identifiable by such things as the completion of a significant deliverable and the occurrence of an event.
### Explanation
In project management, a **milestone** represents a critical point or major event in a project's timeline, such as the completion of a phase or a major deliverable. It has zero duration and is used to monitor progress.

- Choice b) refers to an audit, retrospective, or post-mortem.
- Choice c) describes risk management or early issue identification.
- Choice d) describes a Work Breakdown Structure (WBS).
