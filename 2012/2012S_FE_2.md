# 2012S_FE_2
Which of the following is equivalent to the logical expression below? Here, "AND" has higher precedence than "OR", and "NOT" has the highest precedence.

x AND (y OR z) OR y AND (x OR z) OR x AND y AND z

a) x AND y OR x AND z OR y AND z
b) x AND y OR x AND z OR y AND z OR x AND (NOT y)
c) x AND y OR z OR y AND x OR z OR x AND y AND z
d) x AND z OR y AND z OR x AND y AND z
?
a) x AND y OR x AND z OR y AND z
### Explanation
Let's simplify the boolean expression:
`x(y + z) + y(x + z) + xyz`
Expand the terms:
`xy + xz + xy + yz + xyz`
Combine like terms (`xy + xy = xy`):
`xy + xz + yz + xyz`
Factor out `xy` from `xy + xyz`:
`xy(1 + z) + xz + yz`
Since `(1 + z) = 1`, this simplifies to:
`xy + xz + yz`

This matches choice a: `x AND y OR x AND z OR y AND z`.
