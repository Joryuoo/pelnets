### Explanation
When adding two 2-bit numbers ($x_1 x_0$ and $y_1 y_0$) to get $z_2 z_1 z_0$:
- The lowest bit $z_0$ is the XOR of the lowest bits: $x_0 \oplus y_0$.
- The carry from the lowest bit to the next bit is $c_1 = x_0 \cdot y_0$.
- The next bit $z_1$ is the XOR of $x_1$, $y_1$, and the carry $c_1$.
Thus, $z_1 = x_1 \oplus y_1 \oplus (x_0 \cdot y_0)$.
This matches choice (a).
