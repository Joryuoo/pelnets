# 2012S_FE_57
Which of the following is the project management knowledge area that includes the processes of creating a staffing management plan, acquiring project team, developing project team, and managing project team?

a) Communications management
b) Human resource management
c) Integration management
d) Scope management
?
b) Human resource management
### Explanation
In the PMBOK (Project Management Body of Knowledge), **Project Human Resource Management** includes the processes that organize, manage, and lead the project team. This encompasses:
- Human Resource Planning (creating a staffing management plan)
- Acquire Project Team
- Develop Project Team
- Manage Project Team

*Note: In newer PMBOK versions, this is often referred to as Project Resource Management, but historical exams refer to it as Human Resource Management.*
