d) 0.90
### Explanation
*(Note: The provided answer key states 'd', but the mathematical answer is 'a' (0.72).)*

To calculate the availability of a system:
1. **Availability formula**: $A = \frac{\text{MTBF}}{\text{MTBF} + \text{MTTR}}$
2. **Device A**: $A_A = \frac{80}{80 + 20} = \frac{80}{100} = 0.80$
3. **Device B**: $A_B = \frac{180}{180 + 20} = \frac{180}{200} = 0.90$
4. **Series Connection**: For systems in series, both devices must be operational. The total availability is the product of their individual availabilities:
   $A_{Total} = A_A \times A_B = 0.80 \times 0.90 = 0.72$

Therefore, the correct availability is 0.72.
