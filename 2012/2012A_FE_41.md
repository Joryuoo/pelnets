# 2012A_FE_41
Which of the following is the IP address range that is reserved for Class B in traditional IPv4 addresses?

a) 127.0.0.1 through 127.255.255.255
b) 128.0.0.0 through 191.255.255.255
c) 172.16.0.1 through 172.31.255.255
d) 192.168.0.1 through 192.168.255.255
?
b) 128.0.0.0 through 191.255.255.255
### Explanation
In traditional classful IPv4 addressing, IP addresses are divided into classes based on their leading bits:
- **Class A:** Leading bit `0`. Range: 0.0.0.0 to 127.255.255.255.
- **Class B:** Leading bits `10`. Range: 128.0.0.0 to 191.255.255.255.
- **Class C:** Leading bits `110`. Range: 192.0.0.0 to 223.255.255.255.
- **Class D:** Leading bits `1110` (Multicast). Range: 224.0.0.0 to 239.255.255.255.
- **Class E:** Leading bits `1111` (Reserved). Range: 240.0.0.0 to 255.255.255.255.

Choice a is the loopback address range. Choice c and d are private IP address ranges for Class B and Class C, respectively.
