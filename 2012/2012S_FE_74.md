b) Functional organization

d) Project organization
?
b) Functional organization
### Explanation
A functional organization is structured around specialized functions or departments such as Sales, Manufacturing, and Research & Development. The hierarchy categorizes people according to their specific skills and job functions. 
- A divisional organization is typically structured around products, markets, or geographical areas.
- A matrix organization combines functional and divisional structures.
- A project organization is structured around specific projects, where teams are assembled for the duration of the project.
