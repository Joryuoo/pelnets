# 2012S_FE_16
When analog audio signals are converted into digital audio signals using the PCM method at a sampling rate of 44.1 kHz, which of the following determines the amount of the encoded digital data.

a) The maximum amplitude of the audio signals
b) The maximum frequency of the audio signals
c) The playback frequency of the audio data
d) The quantization bit width of the audio data
?
d) The quantization bit width of the audio data
### Explanation
In Pulse Code Modulation (PCM), the amount of encoded digital data (bitrate) is determined by the formula:
`Data Amount = Sampling Rate × Quantization Bit Width × Number of Channels`

Since the sampling rate (44.1 kHz) is already given, the remaining key factor that determines the file size (amount of data) is the **quantization bit width** (e.g., 16-bit, 24-bit).

* **a) & b)** The amplitude and frequency are characteristics of the original analog signal, not direct determinants of the resulting digital file size.
* **c)** Playback frequency refers to how the audio is played, which is dependent on the sampling rate, but does not represent the bit depth.
