# 2012S_FE_63
Which of the following is an appropriate measure that management takes to guarantee the apparent independence of internal system auditors? 

a) Establishing the leaning system of audit techniques for auditors  
b) Making it obligatory for auditors to learn about IT continuously  
c) Placing the department of auditors under the direct control of management  
d) Specifying and announcing the knowledge and experience that auditors need  
?
c) Placing the department of auditors under the direct control of management  
### Explanation
Independence is a core requirement for system auditors to ensure objective evaluations.
- **a), b), d)** These are related to auditor competence and skills, not organizational independence.
- **c)** This is correct. Placing the audit department under the direct control of top management ensures they are independent of the operational departments they are auditing, guaranteeing organizational independence.
