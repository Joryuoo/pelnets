# 2012A_FE_79
Which of the following is a financial statement that represents the assets, liabilities, and net assets of a company at a certain point in time and indicates the financial condition of the company?

a) Balance sheet
b) Cash flow statement
c) Income statement
d) Statements of shareholders’ equity
?
a) Balance sheet
### Explanation
The **balance sheet** is a financial statement that reports a company's assets, liabilities, and shareholder equity (net assets) at a specific point in time, providing a snapshot of what the company owns and owes, as well as the amount invested by shareholders.
- **Cash flow statement** (Choice b) shows the flow of cash in and out of the business over a period.
- **Income statement** (Choice c) shows the company's revenues and expenses over a period to determine profit or loss.
- **Statements of shareholders’ equity** (Choice d) show the changes in the value of shareholders' equity over a period.
