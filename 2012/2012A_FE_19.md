# 2012A_FE_19
Which of the following is an appropriate description of a capacitive touch panel?

a) An electric field is formed on the surface of the touch panel, and the touched position is detected by capturing the change in the surface charge.
b) Electrode switches are arranged in a matrix, and the touched position is detected by sensing the pressed electrode switch.
c) The touched position is detected by capturing the change in infrared reflection when the panel is touched and an infrared beam is interrupted.
d) Voltage is applied to a resistance film, and the touched position is detected by capturing the change in the resistance value.
?
a) An electric field is formed on the surface of the touch panel, and the touched position is detected by capturing the change in the surface charge.
### Explanation
A **capacitive touch panel** relies on the electrical properties of the human body:

- **Option a is correct**: An electrostatic field is distributed across the conductive surface of the panel. When a conductive object (such as a human finger) touches the screen, a small electrical charge is drawn to the point of contact, altering the capacitance. Sensors located at the edges or in a grid detect the change in charge/capacitance to calculate the exact coordinate of the touch.
- **Option b is incorrect**: This describes a **matrix switch** (membrane or mechanical switch array), where pressing a point physically closes an electrical contact between row and column electrodes.
- **Option c is incorrect**: This describes an **infrared touch panel** or optical touch panel, which uses infrared emitters and photodetectors around the edges to detect beam interruptions or light reflection.
- **Option d is incorrect**: This describes a **resistive touch panel**, where pressure causes flexible upper and rigid lower conductive layers to touch, changing the resistance and creating a voltage divider that reveals the touch location.
