# 2012A_FE_19
Which of the following is an appropriate description of a capacitive touch panel?

a) An electric field is formed on the surface of the touch panel, and the touched position is detected by capturing the change in the surface charge.
b) Electrode switches are arranged in a matrix, and the touched position is detected by sensing the pressed electrode switch.
c) The touched position is detected by capturing the change in infrared reflection when the panel is touched and an infrared beam is interrupted.
d) Voltage is applied to a resistance film, and the touched position is detected by capturing the change in the resistance value.
?
c) The touched position is detected by capturing the change in infrared reflection when the panel is touched and an infrared beam is interrupted.
### Explanation
*Note: A capacitive touch panel is correctly described by option (a). However, aligning with the provided answer key (c):*

- **Option (c)** actually describes an **Infrared Touch Panel**, which detects interruptions in an infrared light grid.
- **Capacitive Touch Panel (Option a):** Uses an electric field; touching it with a conductive object (like a finger) changes the surface capacitance.
- **Matrix Switch (Option b):** Detects physical presses on an array of switches.
- **Resistive Touch Panel (Option d):** Detects touches through changes in resistance when two conductive layers are pressed together.
