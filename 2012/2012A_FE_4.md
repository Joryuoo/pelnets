### Explanation
A string of length $k$ has elements at positions $1, 2, \dots, k$.
An $m$-substring is a contiguous sequence of $m$ elements. 
The starting position of the $m$-substring can range from position $1$ up to position $k - m + 1$.
Therefore, the number of starting positions, and thus the number of $m$-substrings, is $k - m + 1$.
This corresponds to expression (c).
