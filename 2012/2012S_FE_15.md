# 2012S_FE_15
Which of the following is an example of a daisy chain connection?

a) A keyboard, a mouse, and a printer are connected to a USB hub, and then the USB hub is connected to a PC.
b) A PC is connected to a measuring device and a printer via RS-232C and USB respectively.
c) Industrial cameras with each having two IEEE 1394 ports are serially connected, and then one end is connected to a PC.
d) Several network cameras are connected to a PC through an Ethernet hub or an Ethernet switch.
?
c) Industrial cameras with each having two IEEE 1394 ports are serially connected, and then one end is connected to a PC.
### Explanation
A **daisy chain connection** is a wiring scheme in which multiple devices are wired together in sequence or in a ring, similar to a garland of daisy flowers (Device A -> Device B -> Device C). 

* **c)** Correct. The cameras are connected serially to each other, and the chain connects to the PC. IEEE 1394 (FireWire) natively supports daisy chaining.
* **a)** This describes a **star topology** (or tree topology when hubs are nested), not a daisy chain.
* **b)** This describes independent point-to-point connections to different ports.
* **d)** This describes a **star topology**, where all cameras connect to a central hub/switch.
