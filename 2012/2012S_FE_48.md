### Explanation
In a Data Flow Diagram (DFD), two parallel lines (or an open-ended rectangle) represent a **data store** where data is held at rest (e.g., a database or file).
- **Data flow** is represented by arrows.
- **External entity** is typically represented by a square or rectangle.
- **Process** is represented by a circle or a rounded rectangle.
