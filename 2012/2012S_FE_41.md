# 2012S_FE_41
Which of the following is used for encoding and interpreting binary files, images, video, and non-ASCII character sets within an e-mail message on the Internet?

a) IMAP
b) MIME
c) POP3
d) SMTP
?
b) MIME
### Explanation
MIME (Multipurpose Internet Mail Extensions) extends the format of email messages to support text in character sets other than ASCII, as well as attachments of audio, video, images, and application programs.
- **IMAP** and **POP3** are email retrieval protocols.
- **SMTP** is an email transmission protocol.
