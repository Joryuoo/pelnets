b) (CID, VID, RDate)
### Explanation
A candidate key must uniquely identify each tuple (row) in a relational database table without any extraneous attributes.
- Customers can rent different videos on the same day, or the same video could be rented on different days.
- (CID, VID, RDate) ensures that the specific event of a particular customer renting a specific physical video copy on a specific date is uniquely tracked.
