# 2012A_FE_71
Which of the following is an appropriate explanation of demographic segment that is one of the market segments?

a) A group of a population segmented based on actual consumers’ knowledge, their uses of products, and their responses to certain products
b) A group of a population segmented based on social class, lifestyle, or personality characteristics
c) A group of a population segmented by factors such as age, gender, family size, income, occupation, education, religion, race, generation, and nationality
d) A group of a population segmented by factors such as nations, states, regions, counties, cities, or neighborhoods
?
c) A group of a population segmented by factors such as age, gender, family size, income, occupation, education, religion, race, generation, and nationality
### Explanation
Market segmentation involves dividing a broad target market into subsets of consumers. 
- **Demographic segmentation** (Choice c) segments the market based on measurable characteristics of populations, such as age, gender, income, education, and nationality.
- Choice (a) describes **behavioral segmentation**.
- Choice (b) describes **psychographic segmentation**.
- Choice (d) describes **geographic segmentation**.
