b) Reliability: T, Availability: T/(T + S)
### Explanation
In the context of RASIS (Reliability, Availability, Serviceability, Integrity, Security):
* **$T$** represents the Mean Time Between Failures (MTBF), which is the average time the system is operating correctly. MTBF is the primary metric for **Reliability**.
* **$S$** represents the Mean Time To Repair (MTTR), which is the average time taken to recover the system from a failure.
* **Availability** is the proportion of time a system is in a functioning condition. It is calculated as the ratio of the operating time to the total time: $MTBF / (MTBF + MTTR)$, which corresponds to $T / (T + S)$.
