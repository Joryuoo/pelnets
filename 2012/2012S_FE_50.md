# 2012S_FE_50
From internal and external viewpoints concerning software product development and maintenance, which of the following is an appropriate quality characteristic that includes several sub-characteristics such as maturity, fault tolerance, recoverability, and availability?

a) Functional suitability
b) Maintainability
c) Portability
d) Reliability
?
d) Reliability
### Explanation
According to ISO/IEC 25010 (SQuaRE) software quality model, **Reliability** is the degree to which a system, product, or component performs specified functions under specified conditions for a specified period of time. Its sub-characteristics include maturity, availability, fault tolerance, and recoverability.
