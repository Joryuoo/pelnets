# 2012S_FE_78
![[2012S_FE_AM_Q78_p1.png]]

a) 250
b) 490
c) 500
d) 625
?
d) 625
### Explanation
To find the break-even sales volume, we first determine the total fixed costs and the variable cost ratio.
- Total Fixed Cost = Fixed cost of sales + Fixed selling, general, and administrative expenses = $200 + 300 = 500$ million dollars.
- Total Variable Cost = Variable cost of sales + Variable selling, general, and administrative expenses = $100 + 40 = 140$ million dollars.
- Variable Cost Ratio = Total Variable Cost / Sales = $140 / 700 = 0.2$ (or 20%).
- Marginal Profit Ratio (Contribution Margin Ratio) = $1 - 0.2 = 0.8$ (or 80%).

The break-even point in sales is where the marginal profit equals total fixed costs:
- Break-even Sales = Total Fixed Cost / Marginal Profit Ratio = $500 / 0.8 = 625$ million dollars.
