c) Choice c
### Explanation
In the worst-case and average-case scenarios, a standard bubble sort on $n$ elements makes $(n-1)$ comparisons in the first pass, $(n-2)$ comparisons in the second pass, and so on, down to $1$ comparison in the final pass.

The total number of comparisons is the sum of the first $(n-1)$ integers:
$1 + 2 + 3 + ... + (n-1)$

Using the arithmetic series sum formula $S = \frac{k(k+1)}{2}$ where $k = n-1$:
$S = \frac{(n-1)(n-1+1)}{2} = \frac{n(n-1)}{2}$

This corresponds to choice c.
