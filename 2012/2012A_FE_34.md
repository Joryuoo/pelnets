# 2012A_FE_34
![[2012Oct_FE_AM_Q34_p1.png]]

a) Changing the Purchase_price of the product with a Product_code of S003 to 90,000
b) Changing the Purchase_price of the product with a Product_code of S005 to 90,000
c) Changing the Sales_price of the product with a Product_code of S001 to 130,000
d) Changing the Sales_price of the product with a Product_code of S005 to 130,000
?
c) Changing the Sales_price of the product with a Product_code of S001 to 130,000
### Explanation
The view `Profitable_product` selects products where `Sales_price - Purchase_price >= 40000`.
Currently:
- S001: 150,000 - 100,000 = 50,000 (Included)
- S003: 200,000 - 170,000 = 30,000 (Excluded)
- S005: 140,000 - 80,000 = 60,000 (Included)

To reduce the number of rows, we need to change an included product so it is no longer included (i.e., its margin drops below 40,000).
- **a)** S003 margin becomes 200,000 - 90,000 = 110,000 (Included - increases row count).
- **b)** S005 margin becomes 140,000 - 90,000 = 50,000 (Included - no change).
- **c)** S001 margin becomes 130,000 - 100,000 = 30,000 (Excluded - reduces row count). Correct.
- **d)** S005 margin becomes 130,000 - 80,000 = 50,000 (Included - no change).
