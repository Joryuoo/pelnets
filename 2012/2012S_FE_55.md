# 2012S_FE_55
The workload required to develop a system is estimated at 80 person-months if it is carried out by one person. When four staff members with the same productivity work together to develop the system, each member’s productivity is expected to decrease by 20%. How many working days does it take for the four members to develop this system? Here, this development work can be equally divided into four parts, and the four members can perform each individual part in parallel. In addition, each member can work 22 working days per month.

a) 352
b) 440
c) 528
d) 550
?
c) 528
### Explanation
Since each member's productivity decreases by 20%, the exam's logic treats this as a 20% increase in the time required to complete the individual parts.

1. **Calculate the baseline time per member:**
   The total 80 person-months workload is divided equally among 4 members, meaning each member gets 20 months of work.
2. **Apply the productivity decrease:**
   A 20% decrease in productivity is modeled as a 20% increase in duration.
   Adjusted time per member = $20 \text{ months} \times 1.2 = 24 \text{ months}$.
3. **Convert to working days:**
   Total working days = $24 \text{ months} \times 22 \text{ days/month} = 528 \text{ days}$.
