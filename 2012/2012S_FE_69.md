# 2012S_FE_69
Which of the following is the technique and concept for improving management efficiency by planning and controlling the company-wide management resources in an effective and integrated way? 

a) CRM  
b) ERP  
c) MRP  
d) SCM  
?
b) ERP  
### Explanation
ERP (Enterprise Resource Planning) is an integrated management system of main business processes, often in real time and mediated by software and technology.
- **a)** CRM (Customer Relationship Management) focuses on managing customer data and interactions.
- **b)** This is correct. ERP integrates and centrally manages company-wide resources (human, material, financial, information) to optimize business operations and improve management efficiency.
- **c)** MRP (Material Requirements Planning) is a production planning, scheduling, and inventory control system used to manage manufacturing processes.
- **d)** SCM (Supply Chain Management) focuses on optimizing the flow of materials, information, and finances across the entire supply chain.
