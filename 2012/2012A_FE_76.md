# 2012A_FE_76
Which of the following is an organization that is formed for a certain limited period of time to solve a problem and is disbanded when the problem is solved?

a) Divisional organization
b) Functional organization
c) Matrix organization
d) Project organization
?
d) Project organization
### Explanation
A **project organization** is a temporary organizational structure set up to achieve a specific goal or solve a specific problem within a defined timeframe, and it is disbanded once the objective is met.
- **Divisional organization** (Choice a) and **Functional organization** (Choice b) are permanent organizational structures based on products/regions or functions, respectively.
- **Matrix organization** (Choice c) combines functional and project or product structures, but is typically a permanent overarching structure.
