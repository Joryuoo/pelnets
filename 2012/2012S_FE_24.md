a) 2.5
### Explanation
Let's analyze the execution timeline on a first-come, first-served (FCFS) basis:
- **Job A** arrives at 0:00. It starts immediately at 0:00 and finishes at 0:04. (Waiting time = 0)
- **Job B** arrives at 0:02. It waits for A to finish, starting at 0:04 and finishing at 0:10. (Waiting time = 4 - 2 = 2 minutes)
- **Job C** arrives at 0:05. It waits for B to finish, starting at 0:10 and finishing at 0:13. (Waiting time = 10 - 5 = 5 minutes)
- **Job D** arrives at 0:10. It waits for C to finish, starting at 0:13 and finishing at 0:15. (Waiting time = 13 - 10 = 3 minutes)

Average waiting time = $\frac{0 + 2 + 5 + 3}{4} = \frac{10}{4} = 2.5$ minutes.
