### Explanation
In a "declining metered rate method", the charge per unit of usage decreases as the total usage increases.
When plotting **Total charge** against **Total usage**:
* The graph will still go up (total charge increases as usage increases), but the *slope* of the curve (which represents the charge per unit) will gradually become flatter (decrease).
* Choice (c) typically shows a curve that starts steep and bends downwards, representing a decreasing slope, which matches the declining metered rate.
* A straight line with a positive slope would represent a constant metered rate. A curve getting steeper would represent an increasing metered rate.
