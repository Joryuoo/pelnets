### Explanation
To convert an infix expression like $a - b \times c / d + e$ to postfix (Reverse Polish Notation):
Assuming standard operator precedence (multiplication and division first, then addition and subtraction):
1. $b \times c$ becomes $bc\times$
2. $(b \times c) / d$ becomes $bc\times d\div$
3. $a - ((b \times c) / d)$ becomes $abc\times d\div-$
4. $(a - ((b \times c) / d)) + e$ becomes $abc\times d\div-e+$
