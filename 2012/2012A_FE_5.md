### Explanation
We are given the infix arithmetic expression:
$$-a + b \times c \div d - e$$
where $a, b, c, d, e$ are variables, and standard operator precedence applies:
1. Unary minus has the highest precedence.
2. Multiplication and division have equal precedence and associate left-to-right.
3. Addition and subtraction have equal precedence and associate left-to-right.

Converting the expression into postfix notation (Reverse Polish Notation) step-by-step:

1. **Unary minus on $a$**:
   - Infix: $(-a)$
   - Postfix: $a -$

2. **Multiplication $b \times c$**:
   - Infix: $b \times c$
   - Postfix: $b c \times$

3. **Division by $d$**:
   - Infix: $(b \times c) \div d$
   - Postfix: $b c \times d \div$

4. **Addition with $(-a)$**:
   - Infix: $(-a) + ((b \times c) \div d)$
   - Postfix: $a - b c \times d \div +$

5. **Subtraction with $e$**:
   - Infix: $[(-a) + ((b \times c) \div d)] - e$
   - Postfix: $a - b c \times d \div + e -$

Thus, the equivalent postfix expression is **$a - b c \times d \div + e -$**, which corresponds to **Choice a**.

- **Option b is incorrect**: Places operators $+$, $\times$, and $\div$ in an incorrect sequential order.
- **Option c is incorrect**: Fails to respect operator associativity and placement of operands.
- **Option d is incorrect**: Treats the unary minus as a prefix notation element rather than postfix.
