### Explanation
C counts local and mobile calls separately by summing 1 only for `Tariff = 1` and `Tariff = 2` (choice **e**). D counts both categories together with `Tariff IN (1, 2)` (choice **g**). E sums the charge for those same categories (choice **h**). `COUNT(... ELSE 0)` would count the zero-valued rows too, and summing tariff codes does not count calls. The printed query also has inconsistent aliases and lacks a `GROUP BY` for a real per-extension report; these are source-paper issues.
