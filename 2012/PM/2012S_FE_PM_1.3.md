### Explanation
Chip-select decoding uses the five high address lines A15–A11. Each chip is selected by one combination of asserted and inverted lines; changing A12 and A11 moves between adjacent memory blocks. D uses pattern **b**, and E uses pattern **c** from the printed answer group.
