### Explanation
A pushes each framed input row on `mazeRows`. B records the current row number for the entry cell. C pops a row into `store[row]`, restoring the original row order while adding top and bottom walls. D pushes a viable neighbor as `new Cell(row, col)` on the backtracking stack. E and F read `currentCell.x` and `.y` as row and column. G tests the upper neighbor; H tests the right neighbor. Because candidates are pushed upper, lower, left, right onto a LIFO stack, the next path tried is right, left, down, up. Visited cells are marked `.` to avoid repeating explored paths.
