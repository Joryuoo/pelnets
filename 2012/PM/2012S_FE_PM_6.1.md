### Explanation
A advances `s` by the current prime `p`, moving through its multiples (choice **g**, `s + p`). B advances `p` one index at a time until it reaches a still-marked candidate (choice **c**, `p + 1`). C prints the index `i` of a surviving element, not the stored flag `numbers[i]`, so choose **a**. This is the standard sieve: composites become 0 and prime-number indices remain 1.
