### Explanation
Changing the initial ch from a space to 'A' does not affect the first word: the loop reads a fresh character with fgetc before classifying ch, so statement b is true. Repeated spaces are harmless because the separator branch prints a word only when word_pos > 0; after the first space, later spaces are skipped, so f is also true. The answer key permits b and f in either order. Raising the threshold to word_pos > 1 would lose one-character words, and testing only lowercase letters would miss uppercase characters.
