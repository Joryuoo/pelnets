### Explanation
When no requested page or empty frame is found, a replacement is needed, so Fault increases by one (A = d). LRU replaces the frame with the greatest Count, the longest time since use, hence B compares Count[j] > Count[Found] (b). The selected frame is then loaded with NextPage (C = e). In the final loop, j = Found (D = e) resets that frame's age to 1; other occupied frames age by one. This reproduces the shown frame and fault trace.
