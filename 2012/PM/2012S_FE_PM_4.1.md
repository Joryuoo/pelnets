### Explanation
A: LAN1 hosts use Server1 (`192.168.10.1`) as their gateway, but the router's LAN1 interface is `192.168.10.100`. Their default gateways must point to the router; LAN2 already points to its router interface. B: moving PC2 to LAN2 requires a LAN2 IP address and mask plus gateway `172.17.10.100`, so choose **a**. C: traffic for LAN1's network `192.168.10.0/24` leaves Router1 through `192.168.10.100`, so choose **c**. D: ARP broadcasts stay on the local LAN. PC1 can resolve local Router1 and Server1 but cannot directly resolve remote PC3's MAC address, so choose **a**.
