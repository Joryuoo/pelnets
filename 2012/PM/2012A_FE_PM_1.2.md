### Explanation
Point T is the output of the AND gate. One of its inputs is the feedback NAND output, which is NOT F on the preceding cycle. An AND output of 1 requires both inputs to be 1, so the previous F must have been 0. This does not force X and Y to have the same value; it only requires X OR Y to be 1.
