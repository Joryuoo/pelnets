### Explanation
The feedback NAND has both inputs tied to the previous F, so it outputs NOT F(previous). The middle gate gives T = (X OR Y) AND NOT F(previous), and the final NOR gives F = NOT (T OR NOT (Y OR Z)). Evaluate each waveform one clock cycle at a time, using the preceding output as feedback. In the first figure F changes from 1 to 0 and remains 0 (g); in the second it remains 1 (h). In the third, X must remain 1 (h) to keep F at 0 while Y and Z change.
