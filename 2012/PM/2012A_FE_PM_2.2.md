### Explanation
A Member has one Account and the account belongs to one member, giving F the one-to-one association (f). One Member may be associated with many Books, giving G a one-to-many association (e) in this high-level model. Hard-copied Book and Electronic Book are both kinds of Book, so each H is a generalization relationship (c), rather than ownership or composition.
