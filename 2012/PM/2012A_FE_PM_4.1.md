### Explanation
An IDS sensor between the firewall and LAN observes traffic crossing the LAN/DMZ boundary and can raise alerts, giving A = a. To deny LAN telnet to the DB server, the rule needs source 192.168.99.0/24, destination 10.0.0.20/8, TCP port 23, Deny, and Ethernet0 inbound, which is B = e. Traceroute can reveal network hops and layout (C = d). The answer key then pairs blocking ICMP at the firewall with changing the WAN address (D = b). That is the exam's proposed recovery measure; in practice, an IP change or ICMP block alone is not a complete DDoS defense.
