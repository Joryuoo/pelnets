### Explanation
The quantity left in a lot is delivered quantity minus released quantity, QTY_DLV - QTY_REL (E = e). The same expression in WHERE removes lots with no stock remaining. SQL sorts the selected rows with ORDER BY (F = d), and ASC (G = a) puts the earliest expiry date first. Thus the final clause is ORDER BY EXPIRY ASC.
