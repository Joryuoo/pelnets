### Explanation
The transition from Issued to the next state is Renew permit (E = f); the target state is Renewed (F = g). Renewal can repeat, which explains the self-loop on Renewed. Checking for expired permits (G = a) can move either an issued or renewed permit to the Expired state (H = d) after its current validity ends.
