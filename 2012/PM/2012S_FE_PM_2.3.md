### Explanation
Multiplication has higher precedence than addition, so first form `A × B` and `C × D`. In postfix form each multiplication follows its operands: `A B × C D ×`. Add the two products, then E: `A B × C D × + E +`. This is choice **c**; the other sequences apply an operator before its intended operands are on the stack.
