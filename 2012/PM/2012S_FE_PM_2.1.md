### Explanation
The parenthesized `B op C` must form a subtree and be evaluated first. The result is combined with A; the last operation combines that result with D. Thus the root's left branch represents `A op (B op C)` and its right branch is D, which is tree **e**. Trees placing D inside the parentheses or evaluating A with B first change the expression.
