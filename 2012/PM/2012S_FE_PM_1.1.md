### Explanation
The 2 KB ROM occupies `0000 H` through `07FF H`. Another 2 KB is reserved, ending at `0FFF H`, so the first two RAM chips' 4 KB range begins at **A = 1000 H** (d). Four KB is `1000 H` addresses, making its final address **B = 1FFF H** (f). The third, 2 KB RAM chip ends at `FFFF H`, so it begins 2 KB earlier at **C = F800 H** (g). The address lines A11–A15 select a chip, while A0–A10 locate a byte within the selected 2 KB chip.
