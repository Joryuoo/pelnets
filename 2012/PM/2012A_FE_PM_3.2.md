### Explanation
Receiving a delivery increases stock on hand. Inserting the DELIVERY row and incrementing ITEM.QTY_AT_HAND must therefore happen in one locked transaction, with both operations committed or rolled back together (a). Inserting a REQUEST does not yet remove physical stock. The other choices use 'always' or omit cases such as expired and rejected releases, for which no request quantity should be updated.
