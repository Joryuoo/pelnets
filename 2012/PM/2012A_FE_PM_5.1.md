### Explanation
The common Permit attributes are the holder's name, address, phone number and permit class (A = e). Registration date belongs to the Registered state (B = g), while issue date exists once the permit is Issued (C = c). The Card payment subclass records card number, issuing bank, card issue date and card expiry date (D = b); Internet payment instead records an account number. These attributes follow the described lifecycle and payment method.
