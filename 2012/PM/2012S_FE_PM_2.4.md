### Explanation
The expression groups as `(A + B) + (C × D)`. Its postfix form is `A B + C D × +`. Reading it left to right produces `push(A)`, `push(B)`, `add`, `push(C)`, `push(D)`, `mul`, `add`, exactly choice **c**. Each operator pops two operands and pushes the result.
