### Explanation
The loop continues while ch is not EOF (A = c), and a word is emitted on a space, newline or EOF separator (B = f). word[word_pos++] stores the character at the current position before advancing it (C = d). Converting ch with tolower(ch) lets the vowel switch recognize both uppercase and lowercase letters (D = e). After the alphabetic case, isdigit(ch) identifies numeric characters (E = b). The separator branch also resets the counters for the next word.
