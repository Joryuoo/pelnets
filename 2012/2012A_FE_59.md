# 2012A_FE_59
Which of the following is a chart or a diagram that shows the quality of a product in order of time and is used for determining whether or not the production process is in a stable state within the control limits?

a) Cause and effect diagram
b) Control chart
c) Pareto chart
d) Scatter diagram
?
b) Control chart
### Explanation
* **Control chart** (b) is a graph used to study how a process changes over time. It plots data in time order and includes upper and lower control limits to determine if a process is stable or out of control.
* **Cause and effect diagram** (Fishbone/Ishikawa diagram) (a) is used to identify and organize the root causes of a problem.
* **Pareto chart** (c) is a bar graph where items are ordered by frequency or impact, helping to identify the most significant factors (applying the 80/20 rule).
* **Scatter diagram** (d) plots paired data on an X-Y axis to show the relationship or correlation between two variables.
