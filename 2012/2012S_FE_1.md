# 2012S_FE_1
Which of the following is the binary fraction that is equivalent to the decimal fraction 115.625?

a) 1100111.1001110001
b) 1100111.101
c) 1110011.1001110001
d) 1110011.101
?
d) 1110011.101
### Explanation
To convert the decimal number 115.625 to binary, we convert the integer part and the fractional part separately.
For the integer part (115):
- 115 = 64 + 32 + 16 + 2 + 1 = 1110011 in binary.

For the fractional part (0.625):
- 0.625 x 2 = 1.25 (1)
- 0.25 x 2 = 0.50 (0)
- 0.50 x 2 = 1.00 (1)
So, 0.625 is .101 in binary.

Combining both parts gives 1110011.101.
Therefore, the correct answer is d.
