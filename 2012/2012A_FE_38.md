# 2012A_FE_38
Time Division Multiplexing (TDM) is a type of digital multiplexing where two or more bit streams (or signals) are transferred apparently simultaneously as multiple channels or time slots in one physical communication line, but are physically taking turns on the channel. The time domain is divided into several recurrent time slots of fixed length, one for each channel. One TDM frame consists of one time slot per channel and its related overhead such as synchronization and error correction. Approximately how long in seconds does it take to transmit 4 Kbytes of data over one channel of a 512 Kbps communication line using TDM with 8 channels? Here, any overhead for synchronization or other purposes can be ignored.

a) 0.06
b) 0.5
c) 8
d) 64
?
b) 0.5
### Explanation
1. **Total line capacity:** 512 Kbps
2. **Number of channels:** 8
3. **Capacity per channel:** $512 \text{ Kbps} / 8 = 64 \text{ Kbps}$
4. **Data to transmit:** 4 Kbytes = $4 \times 8 \text{ Kbits} = 32 \text{ Kbits}$
5. **Transmission time:** $32 \text{ Kbits} / 64 \text{ Kbps} = 0.5 \text{ seconds}$
