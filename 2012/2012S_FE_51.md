# 2012S_FE_51
Which of the following is a type of integration test that has the characteristics listed below?

[Characteristics]
- All of the related program modules are combined and tested at once.
- Neither drivers nor stubs are required.
- It is useful for small programs, but not for commercial development projects.

a) Big bang test
b) Bottom-up test
c) Sandwich test
d) Top-down test
?
a) Big bang test
### Explanation
- **Big bang test** involves integrating all modules simultaneously and testing them as a whole. No stubs or drivers are needed since everything is connected at once. However, isolating errors is difficult, making it unsuitable for large commercial projects.
- **Bottom-up test** integrates modules from the bottom upwards. It requires test drivers to simulate higher-level modules.
- **Top-down test** integrates from the top downwards, requiring stubs to simulate lower-level modules.
- **Sandwich test** combines top-down and bottom-up approaches.
