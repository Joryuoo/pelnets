# 2012S_FE_28
Which of the following is the name of a phenomenon where multiple ON/OFF signals are generated during a few milliseconds after a push-button switch with a mechanical contact is pressed once?

a) Buffering
b) Chattering
c) Filtering
d) Switching
?
b) Chattering
### Explanation
**Chattering** (also known as contact bounce) is a common phenomenon in mechanical switches and relays where the physical metal contacts rapidly bounce open and closed several times before settling into a steady state, generating multiple rapid ON/OFF signals.
- **a) Buffering**: Temporarily storing data in memory while it is being moved.
- **c) Filtering**: Removing unwanted components or features from a signal.
- **d) Switching**: The general action of making or breaking an electrical connection.
