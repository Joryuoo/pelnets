To determine the 8-bit one's complement and two's complement representations of the decimal integer $-24$:

1. **Represent positive $+24$ in 8-bit binary**:
   $$24_{10} = 16 + 8 = 00011000_2$$

2. **One's complement of $-24$**:
   Invert all bits (bitwise NOT) of the positive binary representation:
   $$\text{NOT}(00011000) = 11100111_2$$

3. **Two's complement of $-24$**:
   Add 1 to the one's complement representation:
   $$11100111_2 + 1_2 = 11101000_2$$
