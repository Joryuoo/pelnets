# 2012S_FE_21
Which of the following provides a job management function in OS that selects a program with the highest execution priority in the job queue and allocates resources required for the program?

a) Initiator
b) Master scheduler
c) Reader
d) Writer
?
a) Initiator
### Explanation
In a batch processing system or an OS job management function, the **initiator** selects the highest priority job from the job queue, allocates the required resources, and initiates its execution.
- **b) Master scheduler**: Handles high-level scheduling and overall job flow but usually does not directly select and execute jobs from the queue.
- **c) Reader**: Reads jobs and places them into the job queue.
- **d) Writer**: Handles outputting the results of processed jobs.
