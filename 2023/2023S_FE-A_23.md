# 2023S_FE-A_23

Which of the following is a case where outline fonts rather than bitmap fonts are suitable for use? 

a) To display characters as quickly as possible 
b) To display characters in monospace 
c) To display single-byte and double-byte characters at the same time 
d) To enlarge characters by a given scale factor 
?
d) To enlarge characters by a given scale factor

### Explanation
Fonts are generally classified into two types based on how they represent characters:

1. **Bitmap Fonts:** Store characters as a fixed grid of pixels (dots). 
   - *Pros:* Very fast to render.
   - *Cons:* When enlarged, they become blocky, jagged, and pixelated because the individual pixels are just scaled up.
2. **Outline Fonts (Vector Fonts):** Store characters as mathematical formulas (lines and curves).
   - *Pros:* They can be scaled to any size (enlarged or reduced) while maintaining smooth edges and high quality.
   - *Cons:* Requires slightly more processing power to render.

Therefore, **outline fonts** are perfectly suited for situations where characters need to be enlarged by a given scale factor without losing visual quality.
