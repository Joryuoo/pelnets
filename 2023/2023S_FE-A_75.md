# 2023S_FE-A_75

Which of the following is a characteristic of OJT?

a) A dedicated approach to education is possible outside of the workplace.
b) Because the boss or senior associates provide education in hands-on knowledge and technical skills by staying close to practical affairs, the required competency can be acquired.
c) Education that focuses on generalized knowledge and technology can be acquired.
d) Regardless of the ability of the boss or senior associates, occupational knowledge of a specific standard can be acquired.
?
b) Because the boss or senior associates provide education in hands-on knowledge and technical skills by staying close to practical affairs, the required competency can be acquired.

### Explanation
**OJT (On-the-Job Training)** is a practical approach to acquiring new competencies and skills needed for a job in a real, or close to real, working environment.

- **OJT vs Off-JT:** Off-JT (Off-the-Job Training) occurs away from the actual work environment, often in classrooms or seminars, focusing on general knowledge. OJT occurs during actual work, taught by experienced colleagues.

| Option | Type of Training | Explanation |
|---|---|---|
| a | Off-JT | Training conducted outside the workplace (e.g., workshops, seminars). |
| b | **OJT** | **Correct.** Practical, hands-on training provided by supervisors or experienced peers in the actual work environment. |
| c | Off-JT / General Education | General knowledge is usually taught outside the specific day-to-day workflow. |
| d | E-Learning / Standardized Training | OJT heavily depends on the skill and teaching ability of the specific boss or senior associate providing it, so it is *not* guaranteed to be standardized regardless of their ability. |
