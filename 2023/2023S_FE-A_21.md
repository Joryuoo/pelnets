### Explanation
The circuit consists of three NAND gates. Let's analyze the logic step-by-step:

1. **Top NAND Gate:** 
   Both inputs are tied to $A$.
   Output $= \overline{A \cdot A} = \overline{A}$ (This acts as a NOT gate)

2. **Bottom NAND Gate:**
   Both inputs are tied to $B$.
   Output $= \overline{B \cdot B} = \overline{B}$ (This acts as a NOT gate)

3. **Right NAND Gate:**
   The inputs are $\overline{A}$ and $\overline{B}$.
   Output $= \overline{\overline{A} \cdot \overline{B}}$

By applying **De Morgan's Laws** ($\overline{X \cdot Y} = \overline{X} + \overline{Y}$), we get:
$$ \overline{\overline{A} \cdot \overline{B}} = \overline{\overline{A}} + \overline{\overline{B}} = A + B $$

The expression $A + B$ corresponds to an **OR gate**.

Therefore, the entire logic circuit is equivalent to an OR gate, which matches choice **c)**.
