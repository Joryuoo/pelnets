# 2023S_FE-A_73

Which of the following is an appropriate explanation of digital signage?

a) A technology to embed identification information in data to prevent abuse
b) An economic or social gap among regions or individuals, arising from lack of opportunities and abilities to utilize information technology
c) An electronic billboard that displays information such as images and characters
d) An electronic signature that is used to ensure the validity of information
?
c) An electronic billboard that displays information such as images and characters

### Explanation
**Digital signage** is a sub-segment of electronic signage. Digital displays use technologies such as LCD, LED, projection and e-paper to display digital images, video, web pages, weather data, restaurant menus, or text.

| Option | Concept | Explanation |
|---|---|---|
| a | Digital Watermarking | Embedding hidden identifying information to protect copyright. |
| b | Digital Divide | The gap between demographics and regions that have access to modern information and communications technology. |
| c | **Digital Signage** | **Correct.** Electronic screens and billboards displaying multimedia content. |
| d | Digital Signature | A mathematical scheme for verifying the authenticity of digital messages or documents. |
