### Explanation

To determine the successful bidder, we must calculate the **comprehensive evaluation points** for each eligible company. The comprehensive points are the sum of the **price points** and the **technology points**.

**Rules and Conditions:**
1. Target price = 100,000 dollars. Bids exceeding this are disqualified.
2. Price points = $[1 - (\text{Bidding price} / \text{Target price})] \times 100$
3. Comprehensive points = Price points + Technology points

Let's evaluate each company:

**1. Filter by Target Price (100,000 dollars):**
* Company A: 70,000 $\le$ 100,000 (Eligible)
* Company B: 80,000 $\le$ 100,000 (Eligible)
* Company C: 90,000 $\le$ 100,000 (Eligible)
* **Company D**: 110,000 $>$ 100,000 (**Disqualified**)

**2. Calculate Price Points for Eligible Companies:**
* **Company A:** $[1 - (70/100)] \times 100 = (1 - 0.70) \times 100 = 30$ points
* **Company B:** $[1 - (80/100)] \times 100 = (1 - 0.80) \times 100 = 20$ points
* **Company C:** $[1 - (90/100)] \times 100 = (1 - 0.90) \times 100 = 10$ points

**3. Calculate Comprehensive Evaluation Points:**

| Company | Price Points | Technology Points | Comprehensive Points |
|---|---|---|---|
| A | 30 | 50 | $30 + 50 = 80$ |
| B | 20 | 65 | $20 + 65 = 85$ |
| **C** | **10** | **80** | **$10 + 80 = 90$** |
| D | N/A | 100 | Disqualified |

**Company C** has the highest comprehensive evaluation points (90 points), making it the successful bidder.
