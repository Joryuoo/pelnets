# 2023S_FE-A_24

What is the approximate minimum required bandwidth for delivering a 30-frames/second video in 800 x 600 pixel and 24-bit full color? Here, the data is not compressed during communication. 

a) 350 kbps
b) 3.5 Mbps 
c) 35 Mbps
d) 350 Mbps 
?
d) 350 Mbps

### Explanation
To find the required bandwidth, we need to calculate the total number of bits transmitted per second. 

**Step 1: Calculate the total number of pixels per frame**
$$ \text{Pixels per frame} = 800 \times 600 = 480,000 \text{ pixels} $$

**Step 2: Calculate the number of bits per frame**
Given that the video uses 24-bit full color, each pixel requires 24 bits.
$$ \text{Bits per frame} = 480,000 \text{ pixels} \times 24 \text{ bits/pixel} = 11,520,000 \text{ bits} $$

**Step 3: Calculate the total bits per second (bps)**
The video runs at 30 frames per second (fps).
$$ \text{Bits per second} = 11,520,000 \text{ bits/frame} \times 30 \text{ frames/second} = 345,600,000 \text{ bps} $$

**Step 4: Convert to Megabits per second (Mbps)**
$$ 345,600,000 \text{ bps} = 345.6 \text{ Mbps} $$

The value $345.6 \text{ Mbps}$ is approximately **350 Mbps**.
