# 2023S_FE-A_79

A company plans to provide a product with a fixed cost of $128,000, a variable cost per unit of $48, and a selling price per unit of $80. Which of the following is the point of sales (in dollars) to become break-even?

a) 240,000
b) 320,000
c) 480,000
d) 560,000
?
b) 320,000

### Explanation
The **break-even point** is the point at which total cost and total revenue are equal, meaning there is no loss or gain.

**Formula for Break-even Point (in Units):**
$$ \text{Break-even Volume} = \frac{\text{Fixed Costs}}{\text{Selling Price per Unit} - \text{Variable Cost per Unit}} $$

**Calculation:**
1. **Contribution Margin per Unit** = Selling Price $-$ Variable Cost
   $$ \text{Contribution Margin} = \$80 - \$48 = \$32 $$
2. **Break-even Volume** (Units) = Fixed Cost / Contribution Margin
   $$ \text{Units} = \frac{\$128,000}{\$32} = 4,000 \text{ units} $$
3. **Break-even Sales (in dollars)** = Break-even Volume $\times$ Selling Price per Unit
   $$ \text{Sales} = 4,000 \times \$80 = \$320,000 $$

Therefore, the break-even point in sales is **$320,000**. Option b) is the correct answer.
