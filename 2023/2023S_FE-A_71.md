# 2023S_FE-A_71

Which of the following is an explanation of cryptocurrency mining that is enabled by block chain?

a) Making payments or settlements by using cryptocurrency in brick-and-mortar stores
b) Obtaining capital gain by buying and selling cryptocurrency
c) Obtaining cryptocurrency as a reward for participation in checking or record calculation of cryptocurrency transactions
d) Transferring money to an individual or organization in cryptocurrency
?
c) Obtaining cryptocurrency as a reward for participation in checking or record calculation of cryptocurrency transactions

### Explanation
**Cryptocurrency mining** refers to the process where participants (miners) in a blockchain network verify and record new transactions into a new block. 
- In Proof of Work (PoW) systems like Bitcoin, miners compete to solve a complex mathematical puzzle.
- The first one to solve the puzzle adds the block to the blockchain and is rewarded with newly minted cryptocurrency.
Therefore, it is the process of obtaining cryptocurrency as a reward for checking and recording transactions.

| Option | Concept | Explanation |
|---|---|---|
| a | Crypto payments | This describes the usage of cryptocurrency as a medium of exchange. |
| b | Crypto trading | This describes speculation or investing (buying low, selling high). |
| c | **Crypto mining** | **Correct.** Checking transactions and performing calculations to add to the blockchain in exchange for block rewards. |
| d | Crypto transfer | This is simply a peer-to-peer transaction. |
