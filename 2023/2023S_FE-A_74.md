# 2023S_FE-A_74

Which of the following is an appropriate description of the constituent elements of IoT?

a) They cover all items that can connect to the Internet or a closed network.
b) They cover all tangible objects except humans and living things.
c) They cover digital type devices, and do not cover analog type devices.
d) They cover only devices that autonomously collect data and perform data analysis.
?
a) They cover all items that can connect to the Internet or a closed network.

### Explanation
**IoT (Internet of Things)** describes the network of physical objects—"things"—that are embedded with sensors, software, and other technologies for the purpose of connecting and exchanging data with other devices and systems over the internet or closed networks.

| Option | Explanation |
|---|---|
| **a** | **Correct.** IoT devices encompass any item that is given the ability to communicate over a network (e.g., smart appliances, vehicles, industrial machines). |
| b | Incorrect. IoT *can* include humans or animals, such as a person with a heart monitor implant or a farm animal with a biochip transponder. |
| c | Incorrect. Analog devices can be part of IoT if they are connected to edge gateways or sensors that convert analog signals to digital data for network transmission. |
| d | Incorrect. While many IoT devices collect data autonomously, they do not necessarily perform the data analysis themselves (analysis often happens in the cloud). |
