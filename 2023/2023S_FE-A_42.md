# 2023S_FE-A_42

Which of the following is residual risk?
a) Amount of risk that is reduced by implementing risk treatment
b) Risk that exists prior to performing any risk management activities
c) Risk that is identified prior to implementing risk treatment
d) Risk that remains after implementing risk treatment
?
d) Risk that remains after implementing risk treatment

### Explanation
In risk management, **residual risk** is the risk that remains even after security measures, controls, or risk treatments have been implemented. 

Here is a breakdown of the risk management terms:
*   **Inherent Risk:** The risk that exists prior to performing any risk management activities or implementing controls (Option B and C).
*   **Mitigated Risk:** The amount of risk that is reduced by implementing risk treatments or controls (Option A).
*   **Residual Risk:** The remaining risk after risk treatments have been applied (Option D).

$$ \text{Residual Risk} = \text{Inherent Risk} - \text{Mitigated Risk} $$
