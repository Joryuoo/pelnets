# 2023S_FE-A_60

When an information system department enters data sheets issued by a business operations department, which of the following is an appropriate task executed by the information system department?

a) The data sheets are retained in the information system department until the due date for processing, and the number of sheets received is checked and confirmed just before the due date.
b) To enable the business operations department to check the processing results of each data sheet, the processing results list is sent to that department.
c) To prevent unauthorized use or leakage of confidential information, data sheets are disposed of immediately after data entry is completed.
d) When the data sheet contains an obvious error, the data sheet is corrected, and an entry is made at the discretion of the person in charge of data entry.
?
b) To enable the business operations department to check the processing results of each data sheet, the processing results list is sent to that department.

### Explanation

In data entry and processing workflows between departments, it is essential to establish a control loop (feedback mechanism) to ensure data accuracy and accountability. 

The **information system department** should send the processing results (such as an edit report, error list, or confirmation log) back to the originating **business operations department**. This allows the business department to verify that their submitted data was entered and processed correctly.

| Option | Why it is incorrect |
| :--- | :--- |
| **a** | Retaining sheets until the due date delays processing unnecessarily and risks missing deadlines if issues arise. Intake verification should happen immediately upon receipt. |
| **c** | Disposing of data sheets immediately destroys the paper trail and original source documents needed for audits, troubleshooting, and error correction. |
| **d** | The IS department should **never** alter data based on their own discretion. Any errors must be returned to and resolved by the originating business department who owns the data. |
