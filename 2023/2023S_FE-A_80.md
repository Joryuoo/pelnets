# 2023S_FE-A_80

In developing new products that are expected to be employed by government agencies of WTO member countries, which of the following are the appropriate standards to be applied?

a) ANSI standards
b) FCC standards
c) IEEE standards
d) ISO standards
?
d) ISO standards

### Explanation
The **WTO (World Trade Organization)** Agreement on Technical Barriers to Trade (TBT Agreement) strongly encourages member countries to base their national standards and technical regulations on **international standards** to facilitate international trade. 

| Option | Standard Organization | Scope |
|---|---|---|
| a | ANSI (American National Standards Institute) | A private non-profit organization that oversees the development of voluntary consensus standards for products, services, processes, systems, and personnel in the **United States**. |
| b | FCC (Federal Communications Commission) | An independent agency of the **United States** government that regulates communications by radio, television, wire, satellite, and cable. |
| c | IEEE (Institute of Electrical and Electronics Engineers) | A professional association for electronic engineering and electrical engineering. While globally recognized, it is a professional society, not a general international standards body mandated by WTO. |
| d | **ISO (International Organization for Standardization)** | **Correct.** An **international** standard-setting body composed of representatives from various national standards organizations. WTO members are encouraged to use ISO standards for government procurement to prevent trade barriers. |
