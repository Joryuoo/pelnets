# 2023S_FE-A_76

To secure transparency in business administration, which of the following is a concept that supervises and monitors a company's activities from the perspective of questions such as for whom the company runs its business, how top management is structured, and whether the organization has self-improvement capabilities?

a) Core competence
b) Corporate governance
c) Corporate identity
d) Stakeholder analysis
?
b) Corporate governance

### Explanation
**Corporate Governance** refers to the system of rules, practices, and processes by which a firm is directed and controlled. It essentially involves balancing the interests of a company's many stakeholders, such as shareholders, senior management executives, customers, suppliers, financiers, the government, and the community.

| Option | Concept | Explanation |
|---|---|---|
| a | Core Competence | A harmonized combination of multiple resources and skills that distinguish a firm in the marketplace. |
| b | **Corporate Governance** | **Correct.** The mechanisms, processes, and relations by which corporations are controlled and directed, ensuring transparency and accountability. |
| c | Corporate Identity | The manner in which a corporation, firm or business enterprise presents itself to the public. |
| d | Stakeholder Analysis | The process of identifying the individuals or groups that are likely to affect or be affected by a proposed action. |
