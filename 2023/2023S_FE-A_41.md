# 2023S_FE-A_41

An asset valued at $200,000 has an exposure factor of 20% and an annual rate of occurrence of 0.5 for the risk of malware infection. How much (in dollars) is the annual loss expectancy of the asset due to malware infection? 
a) 20,000
b) 40,000
c) 80,000
d) 100,000
?
a) 20,000

### Explanation
To calculate the Annual Loss Expectancy (ALE), we use the following formulas:

1.  **Single Loss Expectancy (SLE)**: This is the monetary loss expected each time the risk occurs.
    $$\text{SLE} = \text{Asset Value (AV)} \times \text{Exposure Factor (EF)}$$
2.  **Annualized Loss Expectancy (ALE)**: This is the expected monetary loss for an asset due to a risk over a one-year period.
    $$\text{ALE} = \text{SLE} \times \text{Annualized Rate of Occurrence (ARO)}$$

**Step-by-step Calculation:**
*   Asset Value (AV) = $\$200,000$
*   Exposure Factor (EF) = $20\%$ or $0.20$
*   Annualized Rate of Occurrence (ARO) = $0.5$

First, calculate the SLE:
$$\text{SLE} = \$200,000 \times 0.20 = \$40,000$$

Next, calculate the ALE:
$$\text{ALE} = \$40,000 \times 0.5 = \$20,000$$

Therefore, the Annual Loss Expectancy is $\$20,000$.
