### Explanation
E-R Diagram Multiplicities
1. **Relationship A (Student to Registration):**
   - Each student must register their thesis topic. While they can work in groups, the `Registration` table shows that each registration entry is tied to a single `StudentID`. 
   - A student might not have registered yet, meaning a student has **zero or one** registration (`-o+`). 
   - A registration row always belongs to **exactly one** student (`-||`).
   - Thus, the relationship from Student to Registration is `||-------o+` (Option a).

2. **Relationship B (Instructor to Registration):**
   - Each instructor can instruct at most 5 students, meaning an instructor can have **zero or many** registrations (`-o<`). 
   - Each registration entry has **exactly one** `InstructorID` (`-||`).
   - Thus, the relationship from Instructor to Registration is `||-------o<` (Option b).
