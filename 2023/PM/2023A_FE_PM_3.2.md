Finding Unregistered Students
The goal is to find students who are graduating in 2023 but have *not* registered a topic.
To achieve this, we need to list all students and see if they have a matching record in the `Registration` table.
- **Blank C:** We must use a `LEFT OUTER JOIN` (Option b) from `Student` to `Registration`. This keeps all student records and appends `NULL` for the columns of the `Registration` table if a student hasn't registered.
- **Blank D:** To filter out students who *have* registered, we check if the registration record is missing by ensuring the primary key of the registration table is null: `r.ID IS NULL` (Option b).
