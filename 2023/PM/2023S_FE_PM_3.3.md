### Explanation
The campaign is for repeaters who paid twice or more from April 1 to April 15, 2023.
- The `WHERE` clause joins the tables (`c.CustomerID = p.CustomerID`). We also need to filter the payments by date.
- We group by the customer to count their transactions.
- We filter the grouped result using `HAVING` to only include those with 2 or more transactions.

Option `b)` perfectly matches this logic:
```sql
AND p.PayDate BETWEEN '2023-04-01' AND '2023-04-15'
GROUP BY c.CustomerID, c.LastName
HAVING COUNT(*) >= 2
```
This correctly filters the payment dates, groups the result set by customer details, and applies the aggregate condition `COUNT(*) >= 2`.
