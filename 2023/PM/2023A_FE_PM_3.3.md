### Explanation
Executing SQL3
The query determines the students receiving the excellent thesis award. It does this by finding the students who achieved the maximum grade within their respective faculty.

**Step 1: Understand the `Result` Table**
| StudentID | StudentName | FacultyID | Grade |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| S2101 | Sarah | FIT | 85 |
| S2102 | Karina | FIT | 90 |
| S2103 | Mike | FIT | 90 |
| S2104 | Selena | FEE | 85 |
| S2105 | Peter | FEE | 80 |

**Step 2: Subquery `mg` (Max Grade per Faculty)**
```sql
SELECT FacultyID, MAX(Grade) AS MaxGrade FROM Result GROUP BY Result.FacultyID
```
| FacultyID | MaxGrade |
| :--- | :--- |
| FIT | 90 |
| FEE | 85 |

**Step 3: INNER JOIN `Result` with `mg`**
The main subquery joins `Result r` with the max grades `mg` on `FacultyID` and matches the grades:
- `FIT` max grade is 90. Karina (S2102) and Mike (S2103) have 90. Both are selected.
- `FEE` max grade is 85. Selena (S2104) has 85. She is selected.

Therefore, **three students** (S2102, S2103, S2104) receive the award.
