# 2023S_FE-A_2

When the hexadecimal value ABCD in a 32-bit register is logically shifted right by two bits, which of the following is the resulting value in hexadecimal? 

a) 2AF3 
b) 6AF3 
c) AF34 
d) EAF3 
?
a) 2AF3

### Explanation
To logically shift the hexadecimal value `ABCD` right by two bits, we first convert the value to its binary representation.

**Step 1: Convert Hexadecimal to Binary**

| Hexadecimal | A | B | C | D |
| :--- | :---: | :---: | :---: | :---: |
| **Binary** | $1010$ | $1011$ | $1100$ | $1101$ |

So, `ABCD` in binary is `1010 1011 1100 1101`.

**Step 2: Logical Right Shift by 2 Bits**
A logical right shift moves all bits to the right and fills the vacated leftmost bits with zeros.

```mermaid
flowchart LR
    Original["1010 1011 1100 1101"] -- Shift Right by 2 --> Shifted["0010 1010 1111 0011"]
```

**Step 3: Convert Shifted Binary Back to Hexadecimal**
We group the shifted binary into sets of 4 bits:

| Binary | 0010 | 1010 | 1111 | 0011 |
| :--- | :---: | :---: | :---: | :---: |
| **Hexadecimal** | 2 | A | F | 3 |

The resulting value is `2AF3`.
