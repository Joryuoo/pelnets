# 2023S_FE-A_68

Which of the following is mostly tacit knowledge?

a) A list of countries and their capitals
b) An inventory of assets
c) Contents of a washing machine manual
d) How to ride a bicycle
?
d) How to ride a bicycle

### Explanation

In Knowledge Management (SECI model), knowledge is classified into two types: **Explicit Knowledge** and **Tacit Knowledge**.

* **Explicit Knowledge:** Knowledge that is easily articulated, written down, and shared (e.g., manuals, databases, lists, documents).
* **Tacit Knowledge:** Knowledge gained from personal experience that is difficult to formalize, write down, or transfer to others (e.g., skills, know-how, intuition).

| Option | Knowledge Type | Explanation |
|---|---|---|
| a) | Explicit | Easily documented as a simple list. |
| b) | Explicit | A concrete, structured database or spreadsheet. |
| c) | Explicit | Clearly written instructional text and diagrams. |
| **d)** | **Tacit** | **A physical skill learned through experience. You cannot easily teach someone to ride a bike just by giving them a written manual.** |
