### Explanation

We are looking for the logic circuit that represents the logical expression: $\mathbf{\overline{A}BC + AC}$.

This expression consists of two terms connected by an **OR** operation ($+$). Therefore, the final gate in the circuit must be an **OR gate** taking two inputs:
1.  **Term 1:** $\overline{A}BC$ (Requires a 3-input **AND gate** with inputs: A inverted, B, and C)
2.  **Term 2:** $AC$ (Requires a 2-input **AND gate** with inputs: A and C)

Let's analyze the logic circuits provided in the options by tracing their inputs and logic gates (D-shape = AND, Arrow-shape = OR, Bubble = NOT):

*   **Option a)**
    *   Top AND gate inputs: $A, B$ $\rightarrow$ Output is $AB$. This output is then inverted to $\overline{AB}$.
    *   Bottom AND gate inputs: $B, C$ $\rightarrow$ Output is $BC$.
    *   Final OR gate output: $\overline{AB} + BC$. *(Incorrect)*

*   **Option b)**
    *   First AND gate inputs: $\overline{A}$ (via NOT gate), $B$ $\rightarrow$ Output is $\overline{A}B$.
    *   Second AND gate inputs: $\overline{A}B$ and $C$ $\rightarrow$ Output is $\overline{A}BC$.
    *   Top line takes $A$ directly.
    *   Final OR gate output: $\overline{A}BC + A$. *(Incorrect)*

*   **Option c)**
    *   **Top AND gate:** Has two inputs. One connects to line $A$, the other connects to line $C$. $\rightarrow$ Output is **$AC$**.
    *   **Bottom AND gate:** Has three inputs. The top input connects to line $A$ but has a **bubble** at the entrance, meaning it's inverted ($\overline{A}$). The middle input connects to $B$. The bottom input connects to $C$. $\rightarrow$ Output is **$\overline{A}BC$**.
    *   **Final OR gate:** Takes the outputs of both AND gates. $\rightarrow$ Final Output is **$\overline{A}BC + AC$**. *(Correct)*

*   **Option d)**
    *   Top AND gate inputs: $\overline{A}$ (bubble on input), $B$ $\rightarrow$ Output is $\overline{A}B$.
    *   Bottom AND gate inputs: $B, C$ $\rightarrow$ Output is $BC$.
    *   Final OR gate output: $\overline{A}B + BC$. *(Incorrect)*

Therefore, the circuit in **c)** matches the logical expression.
