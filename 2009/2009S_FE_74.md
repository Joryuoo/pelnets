# 2009S_FE_74

As shown below, a shop purchases and sells a certain type of product.

Purchasing price : $10 per unit
Selling price : $15 per unit
Fixed cost : $1,000

How many units are sold at a “break even” point, where there is neither a profit nor a loss?

a) 100
b) 150
c) 200
d) 250
?
c) 200

### Explanation
The break-even point is the production level where total revenues equal total expenses, meaning there is neither a profit nor a loss.

- **Selling Price per Unit:** $15
- **Variable Cost per Unit (Purchasing price):** $10
- **Contribution Margin per Unit:** Selling Price - Variable Cost = $15 - $10 = $5
- **Fixed Cost:** $1,000

To find the break-even quantity, divide the total fixed cost by the contribution margin per unit:
Break-even Point = $1,000 / $5 = 200 units.
