The problem involves two D flip-flops connected in series. The behavior of a D flip-flop is such that it captures the value of the input signal (D) exactly at the rising edge of the clock signal (transition from low to high) and holds it at the output (Q) until the next rising edge.

- **First Flip-Flop:** The output $Q_1$ will follow the input $D_1$, but delayed by one clock cycle. It only changes its state right when the clock rises.
- **Second Flip-Flop:** The output $Q_2$ takes $Q_1$ as its input. Therefore, $Q_2$ will capture the value of $Q_1$ at the subsequent rising clock edge.

This cascading effect means that the original input signal $D_1$ is delayed by two full clock cycles before appearing at $Q_2$. Looking at the given timing charts, chart b) correctly illustrates this multi-cycle delay behavior.
