### Explanation
The "maximin" principle in decision theory is a pessimistic or conservative strategy where the decision-maker looks at the worst-case scenario (minimum payoff) for each option and then chooses the option that has the highest (maximum) of these minimum payoffs.

Looking at the minimum payoff for each stock:
- Stock A minimum is 10 (Medium growth).
- Stock B minimum is 5 (Medium growth).
- Stock C minimum is 5 (Low growth).
- Stock D minimum is -10 (Low growth).

The maximum of these minimum values is 10, which corresponds to Stock A. Therefore, Stock A should be selected.
