To find the standard deviation, we first calculate the arithmetic mean (average) of the scores, and then use it to find the variance.

1. **Calculate the mean (`x`)**:
   `x = (30 + 50 + 60 + 70 + 90) / 5 = 300 / 5 = 60`

2. **Calculate the squared deviations from the mean for each score**:
   - Mr. Brown: `(30 - 60)^2 = (-30)^2 = 900`
   - Ms. Rose: `(50 - 60)^2 = (-10)^2 = 100`
   - Mr. White: `(60 - 60)^2 = 0^2 = 0`
   - Ms. Green: `(70 - 60)^2 = 10^2 = 100`
   - Mr. Black: `(90 - 60)^2 = 30^2 = 900`

3. **Calculate the sum of the squared deviations**:
   `900 + 100 + 0 + 100 + 900 = 2000`

4. **Calculate the variance (average of squared deviations)**:
   `Variance = 2000 / 5 = 400`

5. **Calculate the standard deviation**:
   The standard deviation is the square root of the variance: `sqrt(400) = 20`.
