# 2009S_FE_26

When an image file is transferred from a PC at hand to another PC separated by a partition without any connection cables, which of the following interfaces can be used?

a) Bluetooth
b) IEEE 1394
c) IrDA
d) Serial ATA
?
a) Bluetooth

### Explanation
### Explanation
Bluetooth uses radio waves (2.4 GHz) which can pass through non-metallic partitions, allowing communication between devices without a direct line of sight.
- **IEEE 1394 (FireWire):** A wired connection.
- **IrDA (Infrared Data Association):** Uses infrared rays, which require a direct line of sight and cannot pass through partitions.
- **Serial ATA (SATA):** An internal wired connection for storage drives.
