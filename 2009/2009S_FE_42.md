In a Data Flow Diagram (DFD), a circle ("○") or bubble represents a **Process**, which transforms incoming data flows into outgoing data flows.
- **Data flow:** Represented by arrows (→).
- **Data store:** Represented by two parallel lines (=) or an open-ended rectangle.
- **External entity (Activity source/sink):** Represented by a square or rectangle.
