# 2009S_FE_57

In home and office networks of Fast or Gigabit Ethernet, which of the following cables can be used as a lower cost option for connecting a client PC to the network?

a) Coax
b) Fiber-optic
c) STP
d) UTP
?
d) UTP

### Explanation
**UTP (Unshielded Twisted Pair)** cables (such as Cat5e or Cat6) are the most common, cost-effective, and easiest to install networking cables used in standard home and office Ethernet environments.

- **STP (Shielded Twisted Pair):** Provides better shielding against electromagnetic interference (EMI) but is bulkier and more expensive.
- **Fiber-optic:** Supports extremely high speeds over long distances but is much more expensive and complex to terminate.
- **Coax (Coaxial):** Was used in early Ethernet deployments but is largely obsolete for standard local PC networking today.
