# 2009S_FE_41

Q41. A certain program module directly refers to the content of another module.  It executes a single unique function, and some concept, data structure, or resource is hidden within the single module.  From a standpoint of module independence, which of the following combinations appropriately shows the “module strength” and “module coupling” of this module?  

Module strength  Module coupling 
a) High  High  
b) High  Low  
c) Low  High  
d) Low  Low
?
a) High  High

### Explanation
### Explanation
- **Module strength (Cohesion):** Refers to how closely related the internal parts of a module are. The module "executes a single unique function" and hides a single concept or data structure, which describes **functional cohesion** or **informational cohesion**—the highest levels of module strength.
- **Module coupling:** Refers to the degree of interdependence between software modules. The module "directly refers to the content of another module," which describes **content coupling** (the highest and worst form of coupling).
Therefore, the module strength is High and the module coupling is High.
