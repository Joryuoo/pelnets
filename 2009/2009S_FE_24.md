# 2009S_FE_24

From a point of view of better performance, improved fault tolerance, and enhanced data integrity, which of the following RAID configurations is commonly suitable for enterprise systems?

a) RAID 0
b) RAID 1
c) RAID 3
d) RAID 5
?
d) RAID 5

### Explanation
### Explanation
RAID 5 distributes parity data across all drives in the array, offering an excellent balance of performance, high fault tolerance (can withstand a single drive failure), and efficient use of storage space.
- **RAID 0:** Striping only, no fault tolerance.
- **RAID 1:** Mirroring, high fault tolerance but expensive (50% storage efficiency) and limited performance gain.
- **RAID 3:** Byte-level striping with a dedicated parity disk; generally inferior performance for mixed read/write workloads compared to RAID 5.
