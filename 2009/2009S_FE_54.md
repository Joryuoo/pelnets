# 2009S_FE_54

Which of the following protocols is used in mail servers on a TCP/IP network?

a) DHCP
b) SMTP
c) SNMP
d) TELNET
?
b) SMTP

### Explanation
**SMTP (Simple Mail Transfer Protocol)** is the standard protocol used for email transmission across IP networks. It is used by mail servers to send and receive emails, and by mail clients to send emails to the server.

- **DHCP (Dynamic Host Configuration Protocol):** Automatically assigns IP addresses to devices on a network.
- **SNMP (Simple Network Management Protocol):** Used for monitoring and managing network devices.
- **TELNET:** An older protocol used for remote terminal access.
