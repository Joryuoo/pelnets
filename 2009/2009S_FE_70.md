# 2009S_FE_70

Which of the following is an appropriate description concerning risk analysis?

a) It takes too much time and cost to handle all potential risks, so the damage costs and occurrence probabilities should be estimated in advance and priority should be given in accordance with the magnitude of risks.
b) Risk analysis should not be repeated until all measures against the previously analyzed and evaluated risks are implemented.
c) The objective of risk analysis is to estimate the amount of damage associated with the occurrence of risks, so the cost of implementing countermeasures should be determined in proportion to the amount of damage.
d) The objective of risk analysis is to prevent future losses, so data accumulated from past similar projects should not be used as reference.
?
a) It takes too much time and cost to handle all potential risks, so the damage costs and occurrence probabilities should be estimated in advance and priority should be given in accordance with the magnitude of risks.

### Explanation
Risk analysis involves identifying, assessing, and prioritizing risks based on their likelihood of occurrence and potential impact (damage costs). Since organizations have limited resources, it is impractical to address every possible risk. Therefore, risks must be prioritized so that the most significant ones receive immediate attention and resources.

- Choice (b) is incorrect because risk analysis should be a continuous, iterative process.
- Choice (c) is incorrect because while damage estimates are important, countermeasure costs are based on reducing the risk effectively, not strictly linearly proportional to the damage.
- Choice (d) is incorrect because historical data is incredibly valuable for accurately assessing future risks.
