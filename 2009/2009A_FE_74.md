### Explanation
Currently:
- Sales = $10,000
- Fixed cost = $2,000
- Variable cost = $6,000
- Profit = $2,000

The unit price is given as $5.
The number of units sold currently = Sales / Unit price = 10,000 / 5 = 2,000 units.
The variable cost per unit = Total variable cost / Units sold = 6,000 / 2,000 = $3.
The marginal profit per unit = Unit price - Variable cost per unit = 5 - 3 = $2.

To earn twice or more in profit, the target profit = $2,000 * 2 = $4,000.
We know the profit formula is: Profit = (Units sold * Marginal profit per unit) - Fixed cost.
4,000 = (Units sold * 2) - 2,000
6,000 = Units sold * 2
Units sold = 3,000 units.

Thus, at least 3,000 units must be sold.
