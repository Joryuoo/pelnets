# 2009S_FE_51

Which of the following devices is used for the purpose of protecting systems against momentary power loss and supplying just enough power for systems to shut down at the time of power outages?

a) AVR
b) CVCF
c) Private power generator
d) UPS
?
d) UPS

### Explanation
UPS (Uninterruptible Power Supply) is a device that provides emergency battery backup power to a system when the main power source fails or drops to an unacceptable voltage level. It protects systems from momentary power loss and provides enough time to either continue operation briefly or perform a safe shutdown during a power outage.

- **AVR (Automatic Voltage Regulator):** Maintains a constant voltage level but does not provide battery backup during an outage.
- **CVCF (Constant Voltage Constant Frequency):** Ensures stable voltage and frequency but does not necessarily imply battery backup power.
- **Private power generator:** Provides long-term backup power but takes time to start up and switch over, meaning it cannot protect against momentary, instantaneous power loss.
