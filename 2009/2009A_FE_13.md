### Explanation
Let's trace the list using the table data provided in the problem:
- Blue is at address 120. Its pointer is 110 (Green).
- Green is at address 110. Its pointer is 100 (Pink).
- Pink is at address 100. Its pointer is 130 (Yellow).
- Yellow is at address 130. Its pointer is 0 (end of list).

The sequence is: Blue(120) -> Green(110) -> Pink(100) -> Yellow(130).

We want to replace Green(110) with Red(140). This means Blue(120) must now point to Red(140), and Red(140) must point to Pink(100).
- Blue's pointer changes to 140.
- Red's pointer is set to 100.

The correct operation is to set the pointers of Red and Blue to 100 and 140 respectively.
