# 2009A_FE_25

Which of the following USB transfer modes is mainly used for a mouse, joystick, etc.?

a) Bulk transfer
b) Control transfer
c) Interrupt transfer
d) Isochronous transfer
?
c) Interrupt transfer

### Explanation
### Explanation
The USB standard defines four transfer types:
- **Interrupt transfer:** Used for devices that send small amounts of data and require quick, periodic, low-latency responses, such as mice, keyboards, and joysticks.
- **Bulk transfer:** Used for large amounts of data where time is not critical, such as printers and scanners.
- **Control transfer:** Used for device configuration and sending commands.
- **Isochronous transfer:** Used for real-time data transfers where a constant data rate is required, such as audio and video streams.
