# 2009A_FE_50

When there are multiple business application systems available, which of the following is the most appropriate method for access control?

a) A common user ID is used for each application group in order to quickly respond to changes in the personnel in charge.
b) All users are registered to all the business application systems beforehand, based on the list of new employees.
c) Appropriate access permissions are granted for each business application system, irrespective of a user’s position and authority.
d) When staff reassignment occurs frequently, access permissions are changed all together at the beginning of the year.
?
c) Appropriate access permissions are granted for each business application system, irrespective of a user’s position and authority.

### Explanation
Access control should adhere to the principle of least privilege, where users are granted specific access rights based purely on their actual work requirements for each application system, rather than granting blanket access automatically based solely on broad job titles or positions. Group IDs (a) prevent individual accountability, blanket registration (b) violates least privilege, and delayed updates (d) create security risks.
