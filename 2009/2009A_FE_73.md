# 2009A_FE_73

Which of the following financial statements indicates the assets, liabilities, and net assets of a company at a specific time and shows the financial condition of the company?

a) Balance sheet
b) Cash flow statement
c) Income statement
d) Statements of shareholders’ equity
?
a) Balance sheet

### Explanation
The balance sheet is a financial statement that provides a snapshot of a company's financial position at a specific point in time, detailing its assets, liabilities, and net assets (or shareholders' equity). The cash flow statement shows cash inflows and outflows, the income statement shows profitability over a period, and the statement of shareholders' equity shows changes in equity over time.
