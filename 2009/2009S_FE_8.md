### Explanation
Let's define the groups based on the given information:
- Total employees = 400
- Total smokers = 100
- Total male employees = 250
- Male smokers = 75

We need to find the probability of selecting a female nonsmoker.
First, determine the total number of female employees:
- Total females = Total employees - Total males = $400 - 250 = 150$

Next, determine the number of female smokers:
- Female smokers = Total smokers - Male smokers = $100 - 75 = 25$

Then, find the number of female nonsmokers:
- Female nonsmokers = Total females - Female smokers = $150 - 25 = 125$

The probability of selecting a female nonsmoker is:
$P = \frac{\text{Female Nonsmokers}}{\text{Total Employees}} = \frac{125}{400}$

Dividing the numerator and denominator by 25 gives $P = \frac{5}{16}$.
