# 2009S_FE_72

Which of the following is the purpose of emphasizing the importance of stakeholders in corporate management?

a) Improving satisfaction of interested parties such as customers, shareholders, and employees; aiming for sustainable growth
b) Keeping management executives from exercising too much authority; creating a framework for sound management
c) Preventing scandals involving managers and employees that may jeopardize the company’s survival
d) Retaining business resources that serve to distinguish one’s company from competitors and strengthen competitiveness
?
a) Improving satisfaction of interested parties such as customers, shareholders, and employees; aiming for sustainable growth

### Explanation
Stakeholder management emphasizes addressing the needs and expectations of all groups with an interest in the company's success, which includes customers, shareholders, employees, suppliers, and society. The main purpose is improving the satisfaction of these interested parties to achieve sustainable corporate growth.

- Options b) and c) relate more to corporate governance and internal compliance (preventing fraud and limiting executive overreach).
- Option d) describes focusing on core competencies to gain a strategic competitive advantage, not stakeholder satisfaction.
