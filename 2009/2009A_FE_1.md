To convert the hexadecimal fraction `3A.5C` to a decimal fraction, we evaluate each hexadecimal digit based on its positional weight:

- The integer part `3A` (hex):
  `3 × 16^1 + 10 × 16^0 = 48 + 10 = 58`

- The fractional part `.5C` (hex):
  `5 × 16^-1 + 12 × 16^-2 = 5/16 + 12/256`

Combine both parts over a common denominator (256):
- Integer: `58 = 14848 / 256`
- Fraction: `5/16 = 80/256`, plus `12/256` gives `92 / 256`
- Total: `(14848 + 92) / 256 = 14940 / 256`

Simplify the resulting fraction by dividing the numerator and denominator by 4:
`14940 ÷ 4 = 3735`
`256 ÷ 4 = 64`

This gives `3735 / 64`, which corresponds to choice **b**.
