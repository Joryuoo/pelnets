### Explanation
An Operating Characteristic (OC) curve describes the performance of an acceptance sampling plan. The horizontal axis represents the fraction defective in the lot, and the vertical axis represents the probability of acceptance (pass rate).

- $p_1$ represents the Acceptable Quality Level (AQL), where the pass rate $L_1$ is high.
- $p_2$ represents the Lot Tolerance Percent Defective (LTPD), an unacceptable defect level where the pass rate should be low.

The curve shows that if a lot has a high fraction defective (greater than $p_2$), the probability of it passing the inspection is very low (at most $L_2$). This represents the consumer's risk, making option b the correct interpretation.
