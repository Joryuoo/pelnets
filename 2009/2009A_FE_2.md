# 2009A_FE_2

Which of the following hexadecimal numbers, representing signed 16-bit binary numbers, results in overflow when multiplied by 4? Here, a negative number is represented in 2’s complement.

a) 1FFF
b) DFFF
c) E000
d) FFFF
?
b) DFFF

### Explanation
### Explanation
Multiplying a number by 4 in binary is equivalent to a logical left shift by 2 bits. In a 16-bit signed integer (using 2's complement), overflow occurs if the sign bit changes during the shift.

Let's analyze the binary representation of each choice:
- **a) 1FFF**: `0001 1111 1111 1111`. Shifting left by 2 yields `0111 1111 1111 1100` (7FFC). The sign bit remains `0` (positive). No overflow.
- **b) DFFF**: `1101 1111 1111 1111`. Shifting left by 2 yields `0111 1111 1111 1100` (7FFC). The sign bit changes from `1` (negative) to `0` (positive). **Overflow occurs**.
- **c) E000**: `1110 0000 0000 0000`. Shifting left by 2 yields `1000 0000 0000 0000` (8000). The sign bit remains `1`. No overflow.
- **d) FFFF**: `1111 1111 1111 1111`. Shifting left by 2 yields `1111 1111 1111 1100` (FFFC). The sign bit remains `1`. No overflow.

Thus, DFFF is the only one that results in an overflow.
