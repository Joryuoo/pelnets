The correct answer is **a)** `SELECT * FROM X, Y`.
This statement performs a Cartesian product (cross join) of tables X and Y. If table X has 3 rows and table Y has 3 rows, the result will contain 3 × 3 = 9 rows. This is the maximum possible number of rows among the given options, whereas a `UNION` or `JOIN` with conditions would yield fewer rows.
