# 2009S_FE_58

Which of the following is a device that interconnects LANs at the physical layer of the OSI basic reference model?

a) Bridge
b) Gateway
c) Repeater
d) Router
?
c) Repeater

### Explanation
A **Repeater** operates at the **Physical layer (Layer 1)** of the OSI model. Its sole function is to receive electrical, radio, or optical signals, regenerate and amplify them, and transmit them further to extend the range of a network.

- **Bridge:** Operates at the Data Link layer (Layer 2) and forwards frames based on MAC addresses.
- **Router:** Operates at the Network layer (Layer 3) and forwards packets based on IP addresses.
- **Gateway:** Operates at the upper layers (usually Layer 4 and above) to translate between entirely different network protocols or architectures.
