# 2009S_FE_35

Which of the following is an appropriate explanation of availability concerning RASIS?

a) A computer system is maintained in such a state that it can be used whenever necessary.
b) An average time required for repair and maintenance is measured during a certain period of time within the life of an individual functional unit.
c) Measures are taken to prevent a computer system from being destroyed or data from being stolen by unauthorized access.
d) Problem determination, diagnosis, and repair actions are performed effectively in a computer system.
?
a) A computer system is maintained in such a state that it can be used whenever necessary.

### Explanation
### Explanation
RASIS is an acronym used to evaluate computer systems, standing for Reliability, Availability, Serviceability, Integrity, and Security.

- **Availability (Choice a):** Refers to the probability that a system is operational and accessible when required for use.
- **Serviceability (Choices b and d):** Refers to how easily and efficiently a system can be maintained and repaired. Choice (b) relates to Mean Time To Repair (MTTR).
- **Security (Choice c):** Refers to protecting the system and its data from unauthorized access or destruction.
