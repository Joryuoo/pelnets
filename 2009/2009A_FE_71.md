# 2009A_FE_71

Which of the following shows the appropriate sequence of items 1 through 3 that are required for establishing ISMS according to ISO/IEC27001:2005?

[Items]
1. Prepare a Statement of Applicability
2. Select control objectives and controls for the treatment of risks
3. Analyze and evaluate the risks

a) 1 → 2 → 3
b) 1 → 3 → 2
c) 2 → 3 → 1
d) 3 → 2 → 1
?
d) 3 → 2 → 1

### Explanation
According to ISO/IEC 27001, risk management involves first identifying and analyzing the risks (3), then selecting appropriate control objectives and controls for risk treatment (2), and finally preparing a Statement of Applicability (1) which summarizes the chosen controls. Thus, the correct sequence is 3 → 2 → 1.
