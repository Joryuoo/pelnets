# 2009A_FE_6

There are three different sizes of white balls and three different sizes of green balls. When these six balls are laid out in a line so that the three white balls can be placed in adjacent positions, how many arrangements of all the balls can be made?

a) 10
b) 144
c) 576
d) 720
?
b) 144

### Explanation
### Explanation
Because the balls are of different sizes, they are distinct and can be individually identified. We want to find the number of ways to arrange the 6 balls such that the 3 white balls are always adjacent to each other.

1. **Treat the adjacent white balls as a single block**:
   Since the three white balls must stay together, we can group them into a single unit or "block". 
   This block, along with the 3 distinct green balls, gives us a total of 4 items to arrange.
2. **Arrange the 4 items**:
   The number of ways to arrange these 4 items in a line is `4! = 4 * 3 * 2 * 1 = 24`.
3. **Arrange the white balls within their block**:
   The 3 distinct white balls can be arranged among themselves within their block in `3! = 3 * 2 * 1 = 6` ways.
4. **Calculate the total arrangements**:
   Multiply the two results: `24 * 6 = 144` ways.
