### Explanation
ABC analysis is an inventory categorization technique that divides items into three categories (A, B, and C) based on their total annual value. Group A items are the most valuable and require the highest management control, typically making up the top 70-80% of total revenue.

Let's calculate the annual revenue (Sales × Unit Price) for the top products:
- Product 2: 60 × 40 = 2,400
- Product 5: 50 × 12 = 600
- Product 8: 150 × 2 = 300
- Total overall revenue of all 10 products is 3,825.

Product 2 alone accounts for 2,400 / 3,825 ≈ 62.7% of total revenue.
Products 2 and 5 together account for (2,400 + 600) / 3,825 = 3,000 / 3,825 ≈ 78.4%.

Thus, Products 2 and 5 represent the vast majority of the annual revenue and should be handled as Group A management items.
