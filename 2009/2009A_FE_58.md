# 2009A_FE_58

Which of the following methods is used for avoiding unnecessary collisions in the IEEE 802.11 family of wireless LAN standards?

a) CDMA
b) CSMA/CA
c) CSMA/CD
d) TDMA
?
b) CSMA/CA

### Explanation
The IEEE 802.11 standard (Wireless LAN / Wi-Fi) uses **CSMA/CA (Carrier Sense Multiple Access with Collision Avoidance)**.
Because wireless nodes cannot easily listen for collisions while simultaneously transmitting (as required by CSMA/CD), they use collision avoidance. Nodes listen to the channel before transmitting. If it's busy, they wait for a random backoff period before trying again to minimize the chance of colliding with other waiting nodes.

- **CSMA/CD** (Collision Detection) is used in wired Ethernet (IEEE 802.3).
