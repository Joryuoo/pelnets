### Explanation
To find the probability that the weather is clear two days later given it is rainy today, we must sum the probabilities of all possible two-day paths from Rainy to Clear. Based on the transition diagram, the daily transition probabilities are:
- $P(\text{Rainy} \to \text{Rainy}) = 0.2$
- $P(\text{Rainy} \to \text{Clear}) = 0.3$
- $P(\text{Rainy} \to \text{Cloudy}) = 0.5$
- $P(\text{Clear} \to \text{Clear}) = 0.4$
- $P(\text{Cloudy} \to \text{Clear}) = 0.3$

The possible paths from Rainy to Clear over two days are:
1. Rainy $\to$ Rainy $\to$ Clear: $0.2 \times 0.3 = 0.06$
2. Rainy $\to$ Clear $\to$ Clear: $0.3 \times 0.4 = 0.12$
3. Rainy $\to$ Cloudy $\to$ Clear: $0.5 \times 0.3 = 0.15$

Total probability = $0.06 + 0.12 + 0.15 = 0.33$.
