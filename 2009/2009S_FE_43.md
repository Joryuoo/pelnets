# 2009S_FE_43

Q43. Which of the following is an explanation of encapsulation in the object-oriented paradigm?  

a) A subclass inherits attributes from a specific base class.  
b) Attributes common to classes are extracted to create a base class.  
c) Data and its related operational procedures are hidden inside objects.  
d) Multiple objects with the same attributes are abstracted and classified.
?
c) Data and its related operational procedures are hidden inside objects.

### Explanation
### Explanation
**Encapsulation** is an object-oriented programming concept that binds together the data and the functions (operational procedures) that manipulate the data, keeping both safe from outside interference and misuse. It hides the internal state of objects from the outside world.
- a) Refers to **Inheritance**.
- b) Refers to **Generalization**.
- c) Correctly describes **Encapsulation**.
- d) Refers to **Class Abstraction**.
