To obtain the `TotalOrder` table with the schema `TIRE_ID` and `SUM_ORDER_QUANTITY` (which represents the total order quantity for each tire type), we must sum the `ORDER_QUANTITY` for each `TIRE_ID` in the `SPC` table.

- The `GROUP BY SPC.TIRE_ID` clause is required to group the orders by tire type.
- The `SUM(ORDER_QUANTITY)` function calculates the total quantity for each group.
- We must also use `AS SUM_ORDER_QUANTITY` to alias the result so that the resulting column is named `SUM_ORDER_QUANTITY` as required by the table definition.

Choice **f** correctly groups by `TIRE_ID`, sums the `ORDER_QUANTITY`, aliases it as `SUM_ORDER_QUANTITY`, and selects into `TotalOrder`.

- **a** and **b**: Do not group or sum the quantities.
- **c**: Lacks a `GROUP BY` clause, which is invalid when selecting a non-aggregated column alongside an aggregated one.
- **d**: Lacks the `SUM` function.
- **e**: Uses the `SUM` function and `GROUP BY`, but does not alias the aggregated column as `SUM_ORDER_QUANTITY`.
