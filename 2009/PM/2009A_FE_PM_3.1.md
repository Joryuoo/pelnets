### Explanation
Based on the requirements:
- **Admission:** "Any patient who is admitted must have exactly one admitting physician" and "A physician may admit any number of patients". This creates a one-to-many relationship between Physician (one) and Patient (many) for admission.
- **Treatment:** "Once admitted, a patient must be treated by at least one physician" and "A particular physician may treat any number of patients, or may not treat any patients". This forms a many-to-many relationship between Patient and Physician for treatment.
