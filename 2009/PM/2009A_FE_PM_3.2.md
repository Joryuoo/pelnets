### Explanation
In a relational database, many-to-many relationships cannot be directly implemented and must be resolved using an associative (junction) table. 
- The many-to-many **Treatment** relationship between Patient and Physician is resolved by creating a `Treatment` associative table.
- This creates two one-to-many relationships: one Physician handles zero or more Treatments, and one Patient has one or more Treatments.
- The original one-to-many **Admission** relationship between Physician and Patient remains intact.
