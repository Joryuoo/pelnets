**Blank E:** The Assignment calendar is a matrix where the rows represent personnel and the columns represent dates (from 01 to 15). To easily populate this grid row by row, the system needs to read the assignments sequentially for each personnel. Therefore, the `Assignment file` should be sorted or indexed by `Personnel code` (choice a).

**Blank F:** When the Commit function key (F10) is pressed, the system commits the whole session and updates the files. 
- The `Assignment file` is updated with the new date assignments encoded by the section chief. 
- The `Request file` is updated because, according to the file description, completed requests are removed, and newly scheduled assignments might be flagged as assigned.
Therefore, the files updated are the `Assignment file` and the `Request file` (choice c).
- *Why not other files?* The `Customer file` and `Personnel file` store master data (like addresses and phone numbers) which do not change when making an assignment.

**Blank G:** The Customer view displays the customer name, request code, and the assigned visit dates (with an asterisk for the due date). 
- `Customer file`: Needed to retrieve the customer name.
- `Request file`: Needed to retrieve the request code and its corresponding due date.
- `Assignment file`: Needed to retrieve the visit dates to place in the calendar grid.
Therefore, the files used to prepare this view are the `Assignment file`, `Customer file`, and `Request file` (choice b).
