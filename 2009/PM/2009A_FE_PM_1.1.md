This question traces Booth's multiplication algorithm for multiplying 5 (`0101`) and -3 (`1101`).

The algorithm repeats for $i=4$ cycles. In each cycle, we examine $Q_0$ (the least significant bit of register Q) and $Q_{-1}$.

- If $Q_0 Q_{-1} = 1 0$, subtract M from A ($A \leftarrow A - M$).
- If $Q_0 Q_{-1} = 0 1$, add M to A ($A \leftarrow A + M$).
- If $Q_0 Q_{-1} = 0 0$ or $1 1$, A remains unchanged.

After the addition/subtraction step, perform an Arithmetic Shift Right (ASR) on the combined registers A, Q, and $Q_{-1}$.

Let's trace the values from Cycle 1 to Cycle 3:

**End of Cycle 1:**
- $A = 0001$, $Q = 1010$, $Q_{-1} = 1$, $M = 1101$

**Cycle 2:**
- We examine $Q_0 Q_{-1} = 0 1$. We must perform $A \leftarrow A + M$.
- $A = 0001 + 1101 = 1110$ ($-2$ in two's complement). This is **Blank A (g)**.
- Next, perform ASR on A, Q, $Q_{-1}$ (`1110 1010 1`).
- Since A's MSB is `1`, the shifted A becomes `1111`. This is **Blank B (h)**.
- The LSB of A (`0`) shifts into the MSB of Q, making Q `0101`. The LSB of Q (`0`) shifts into $Q_{-1}$, making it `0`.

**Cycle 3:**
- We examine $Q_0 Q_{-1} = 1 0$. We must perform $A \leftarrow A - M$.
- $A = 1111 - 1101 = 0010$ ($+2$). This is **Blank C (c)**.
- Next, perform ASR on A, Q, $Q_{-1}$ (`0010 0101 0`).
- Since A's MSB is `0`, the shifted A becomes `0001`. This is **Blank D (b)**.
- The LSB of A (`0`) shifts into the MSB of Q, making Q `0010`. The LSB of Q (`1`) shifts into $Q_{-1}$, making it `1`.

Therefore, the correct choices to fill the blanks are **g, h, c, b**.
