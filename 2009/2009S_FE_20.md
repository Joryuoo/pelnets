# 2009S_FE_20

In a certain intrusion detection system, a detector is driven by a 4 KHz crystal clock oscillator and checks intrusion every 1,000 clock pulses. How many times per minute does the detector check intrusion in this system?

a) 24
b) 120
c) 240
d) 250
?
c) 240

### Explanation
### Explanation
First, convert the oscillator frequency into pulses per second:
- $4 \text{ KHz} = 4,000 \text{ Hz} = 4,000$ clock pulses per second.

The detector checks for intrusion every 1,000 clock pulses. Thus, the number of checks performed per second is:
- $\frac{4,000 \text{ pulses/second}}{1,000 \text{ pulses/check}} = 4 \text{ checks per second}$.

To find the number of checks per minute, multiply the checks per second by 60 seconds:
- $4 \text{ checks/second} \times 60 \text{ seconds/minute} = 240 \text{ checks/minute}$.

Therefore, the detector checks intrusion 240 times per minute.
