# 2009A_FE_22

When a microprocessor works at a clock speed of 200 MHz and the average CPI (“cycles per instruction” or “clocks per instruction”) is 4, how long does it take to execute one instruction on average?

a) 5 nanoseconds
b) 20 nanoseconds
c) 5 milliseconds
d) 20 milliseconds
?
b) 20 nanoseconds

### Explanation
### Explanation
First, calculate the clock cycle time:
- Clock speed = 200 MHz = 200 × 10^6 Hz
- Clock cycle time = 1 / (200 × 10^6) seconds = 5 × 10^-9 seconds = 5 nanoseconds (ns)

Since the average CPI (Cycles Per Instruction) is 4, it takes 4 clock cycles to execute one instruction on average.
- Average execution time = 4 × 5 ns = 20 nanoseconds.
