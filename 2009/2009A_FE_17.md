### Explanation
A circuit constructed solely of NAND gates can implement any Boolean function. Based on the answer `X + Y`, this logic circuit implements a logical OR function. By De Morgan's laws, an OR gate can be constructed using NAND gates by first inverting the inputs (using NAND gates as NOT gates) and then feeding them into another NAND gate: `NOT( (NOT X) AND (NOT Y) )` simplifies to `X OR Y`.
