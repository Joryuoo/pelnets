# 2009S_FE_63

Which of the following is an appropriate description concerning the recovery process of databases?

a) At the restart known as “cold start,” the checkpoint log and update information log are used for recovering the database.
b) At the restart known as “warm start,” control goes back to the checkpoint, and the update information log is used for recovering the database.
c) “Roll back” refers to the operation of reflecting the transactions that completed normally after the checkpoint, by checking the update information log.
d) “Roll forward” refers to the operation of returning incomplete transactions at the occurrence of a failure to the status before update.
?
b) At the restart known as “warm start,” control goes back to the checkpoint, and the update information log is used for recovering the database.

### Explanation
A "warm start" is a recovery method used after a non-destructive system failure (such as a sudden power outage). It involves returning to the most recent checkpoint and using the update information (transaction log) to redo (roll forward) committed transactions and undo (roll back) incomplete ones.

- **Cold start** (choice a) typically involves restoring the database from a full backup without relying solely on recent logs.
- **Roll back** (choice c) cancels incomplete transactions to maintain consistency, not completed ones.
- **Roll forward** (choice d) re-applies committed transactions from the log to restore them, not incomplete ones.
