# 2009S_FE_29

When a printing operation is performed under the conditions shown below, what is the minimum number of megabytes necessary as the total capacity of the spool file?

[Conditions]
(1) The same job is executed continuously four times with multiplicity 1.
(2) It takes 20 minutes to execute the job in a standalone mode.
(3) The standalone job secures a 400-Mbyte print-spool file, where the printing data is stored.
(4) After the job is executed, the contents of the spool file are processed by the printing function of the OS.
(5) The OS deletes the spool file at the completion of the printing. Here, the time for this deletion can be ignored.
(6) There is exactly one printer, and its printing speed is 10 Mbytes per minute.
(7) Job execution and printing can be performed in parallel without one affecting the other.

a) 400
b) 800
c) 1,200
d) 1,600
?
c) 1,200

### Explanation
### Explanation
Let's analyze the timeline with space being freed only upon completion:
- **T=0 to 20**: Job 1 executes.
- **T=20**: Job 1 finishes. Secures 400 MB spool. Spool usage = 400 MB.
  - Job 2 begins executing. Printer begins printing Job 1 (takes 40 minutes: 400 MB / 10 MB/min).
- **T=40**: Job 2 finishes. Secures 400 MB spool. Spool usage = 400 + 400 = 800 MB.
  - Job 3 begins executing. Printer is still printing Job 1.
- **T=60**: Job 3 finishes. Secures 400 MB spool. Spool usage = 800 + 400 = 1200 MB.
  - Printer finishes printing Job 1. OS deletes Job 1 spool. Spool usage drops to 1200 - 400 = 800 MB.
  - Job 4 begins executing. Printer begins printing Job 2.
- **T=80**: Job 4 finishes. Secures 400 MB. Spool usage = 800 + 400 = 1200 MB.
  - Printer is still printing Job 2.

The maximum concurrent spool usage occurs just before the OS deletes a completed file, peaking at 1,200 MB at T=60 (right before Job 1 is deleted) or T=80.
So the minimum necessary capacity is 1,200 MB.
