# 2009A_FE_59

VLAN (Virtual LAN) is used to logically segment a LAN into different broadcast domains. In which of the following network devices should the function of VLAN be supported?

a) Bridge
b) Hub
c) Repeater
d) Switch
?
d) Switch

### Explanation
A **Switch** operates at Layer 2 (Data Link Layer) and can inspect MAC addresses. A VLAN-capable switch logically partitions the physical ports into separate broadcast domains by tagging frames. 
- **Hubs** and **Repeaters** operate at Layer 1 (Physical Layer) and simply forward electrical signals to all ports without understanding frames or VLAN tags.
- While **Bridges** also operate at Layer 2, modern VLAN segmentation is almost universally handled by switches, which act as multi-port bridges optimized for managing these virtual domains.
