# 2009A_FE_49

When the development department and the operations department of a system are organized separately, which of the following is the appropriate method for smooth and effective transfer from development to operations?

a) After the operational test is completed, the development department explains the system specifications and the operations method to the operations department.
b) Only the operations department performs the operational test without support from the development department for efficiency.
c) The development department performs the operational test, creates an operations manual, and delivers it to the operations department.
d) The operations department proactively participates in the system development and provides advice from the viewpoint of operations.
?
d) The operations department proactively participates in the system development and provides advice from the viewpoint of operations.

### Explanation
To ensure a smooth transition from development to operations, it is best practice for the operations team to be involved proactively during the development phase. Their input ensures that operational requirements (e.g., monitoring, maintenance, scalability) are addressed early on.
