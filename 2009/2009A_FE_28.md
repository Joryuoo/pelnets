The effective access time can be calculated using the formula:
**Effective Access Time = (Cache Access Time) + (Main Memory Access Time × Miss Ratio)**
*Note: Miss Ratio = 1 - Hit Ratio.*

Let's calculate the effective access time for each option:
- **a)** 8 + 40 × (1 - 0.60) = 8 + 40 × 0.40 = 8 + 16 = 24 ns
- **b)** 8 + 40 × (1 - 0.70) = 8 + 40 × 0.30 = 8 + 12 = 20 ns
- **c)** 10 + 30 × (1 - 0.70) = 10 + 30 × 0.30 = 10 + 9 = 19 ns
- **d)** 10 + 30 × (1 - 0.80) = 10 + 30 × 0.20 = 10 + 6 = 16 ns

Option d) results in the shortest effective access time (16 ns).
