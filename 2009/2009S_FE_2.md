# 2009S_FE_2

When the binary fraction 11101.110 is subtracted from the binary fraction 101101.101, 
what is the correct result?  
 
a) 111.001 
b) 111.111  
c) 1111.001 
d) 1111.111
?
d) 1111.111

### Explanation
Align the binary points and subtract:
```text
  101101.101
- 011101.110
------------
```
To perform the subtraction, borrow as needed:
$101101.101 - 011101.110 = 001111.111_2$
Thus, the correct result is $1111.111$.
