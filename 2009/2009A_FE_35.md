### Explanation
The system diagram shows two parallel branches connected in series. The text states: "when either of the parallel connections is operating, the corresponding parallel portion can be regarded as operating." This implies the system consists of a parallel block (A and C) in series with another parallel block (B and D).

Availability of A = 0.9, C = 0.9. Availability of B = 0.8, D = 0.8.

Availability of parallel block (A and C) = $1 - (1 - 0.9) \times (1 - 0.9) = 1 - (0.1 \times 0.1) = 1 - 0.01 = 0.99$
Availability of parallel block (B and D) = $1 - (1 - 0.8) \times (1 - 0.8) = 1 - (0.2 \times 0.2) = 1 - 0.04 = 0.96$

Overall availability = $0.99 \times 0.96 = 0.9504 \approx 0.95$

- **d) Correct**: The overall availability of the system is approximately 0.95.
