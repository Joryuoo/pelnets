A resolution of 600 dpi (dots per inch) means there are 600 dots in one inch. When the same image is printed at a lower resolution of 300 dpi, each dot becomes twice as wide and twice as tall.

Since the physical width is multiplied by 2 and the physical height is multiplied by 2, the total printed area is:
- Area = Width × Height = 2 × 2 = 4 times as large as the original image area.
