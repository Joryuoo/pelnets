### Explanation
Let's analyze the decision table for the "Accept order" condition. The order is accepted for the following combinations of rules:
- Rule 5: X = N, Y = Y, Z = Y
- Rule 6: X = N, Y = Y, Z = N
- Rule 7: X = N, Y = N, Z = Y

From these rules, "Accept order" is true when:
$(\neg X \land Y \land Z) \lor (\neg X \land Y \land \neg Z) \lor (\neg X \land \neg Y \land Z)$

We can factor out $\neg X$:
$\neg X \land [ (Y \land Z) \lor (Y \land \neg Z) \lor (\neg Y \land Z) ]$

Simplify the expression inside the brackets:
$(Y \land Z) \lor (Y \land \neg Z)$ simplifies to $Y$.
So we have:
$\neg X \land [ Y \lor (\neg Y \land Z) ]$

By the distributive law of Boolean algebra, $Y \lor (\neg Y \land Z)$ simplifies to $Y \lor Z$.
Thus, the final simplified expression is:
$\neg X \land (Y \lor Z)$
This matches the logical expression: `(NOT X) AND (Y OR Z)`.
