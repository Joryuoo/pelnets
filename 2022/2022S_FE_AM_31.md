# 2022S_FE_AM_31

Audio data of 2.4 Mbytes encoded at 192 kbit/s is to be downloaded and played back
without interruptions over a network with a communication speed of 128 kbit/s. What is the
minimum number of seconds required as the data buffering time before the start of
playback

a) 50
b) 100
c) 150
d) 250

?
a) 50
### Explanation
To solve this problem, we need to calculate two main things:
1. **Total Playback Time:** How long the audio track lasts.
2. **Total Download Time:** How long it takes to download the entire audio file over the network.

If the download takes longer than the playback, we must wait (buffer) before starting the audio so that it doesn't pause to load in the middle.

### Step 1: Convert Units
First, let's align our units to bits (or kilobits), as network speeds and encoding rates are given in **kbit/s**.
- File size: $2.4 \text{ Mbytes}$
- $1 \text{ Byte} = 8 \text{ bits}$
- $2.4 \text{ Mbytes} = 2.4 \times 8 \text{ Mbits} = 19.2 \text{ Mbits}$
- Converting Megabits to kilobits (assuming $1 \text{ Mbit} = 1000 \text{ kbits}$): 
  $19.2 \text{ Mbits} = 19,200 \text{ kbits}$

### Step 2: Calculate Playback and Download Times
Now, we calculate the time durations based on the given speeds.

**Total Playback Time:**
$$ \text{Playback Time} = \frac{\text{Total Data}}{\text{Encoding Rate}} = \frac{19,200 \text{ kbits}}{192 \text{ kbit/s}} = 100 \text{ seconds} $$

**Total Download Time:**
$$ \text{Download Time} = \frac{\text{Total Data}}{\text{Network Speed}} = \frac{19,200 \text{ kbits}}{128 \text{ kbit/s}} = 150 \text{ seconds} $$

### Step 3: Calculate Required Buffering Time
Because the download takes $150 \text{ seconds}$ but the playback only takes $100 \text{ seconds}$, if you start playing immediately, the audio will finish before the download completes, causing a pause (interruption).

To ensure uninterrupted playback, the playback must finish exactly when (or after) the download finishes.

Let $T$ be the buffering time (how long we wait before hitting play).
The total time from the start of the download until the playback finishes will be:
$$ \text{Buffering Time } (T) + \text{Playback Time} \ge \text{Total Download Time} $$
$$ T + 100 \text{ seconds} \ge 150 \text{ seconds} $$
$$ T \ge 150 - 100 $$
$$ T \ge 50 \text{ seconds} $$

### Visualizing the Timeline



By waiting $50 \text{ seconds}$ before pressing play, the 100-second playback will end exactly at the $150$-second mark, which is precisely when the download finishes.

Therefore, the correct answer is **a**.
