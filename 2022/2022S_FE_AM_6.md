# 2022S_FE_AM_6

Which of the following is an appropriate explanation of feedback control

a) It detects disturbance and takes a corrective action to prevent its influence.
b) It is vulnerable to disturbance and the influence of the disturbance is amplified.
c) It predicts disturbance and takes a corrective action accordingly.
d) It takes a corrective action after detecting the influence of a disturbance.

?
a) It detects disturbance and takes a corrective action to prevent its influence.
### Explanation
In control theory and systems architecture, there are two primary types of control mechanisms used to manage disturbances:

1.  **Feedforward Control:** Detects disturbances *before* they affect the output and takes preemptive corrective action to prevent their influence. 
2.  **Feedback Control:** Measures the actual output, detects any deviation (the *influence* of a disturbance), and then takes corrective action to bring the output back to the desired target.

### Analysis of the Options

*   **a) It detects disturbance and takes a corrective action to prevent its influence.**
    *   Strictly speaking, this is the definition of **feedforward control**. However, in the context of this specific exam's answer key, this is marked as the correct answer. 
*   **b) It is vulnerable to disturbance and the influence of the disturbance is amplified.**
    *   Incorrect. Control systems aim to mitigate disturbances, not amplify them.
*   **c) It predicts disturbance and takes a corrective action accordingly.**
    *   This is related to predictive control.
*   **d) It takes a corrective action after detecting the influence of a disturbance.**
    *   This is the standard engineering definition of **feedback control**.



*Note: While Option D is the classic definition of feedback control, the provided official answer key selects Option A. This may be due to a translation issue or a specific nuance in the exam's syllabus regarding preemptive feedback loops.*
