# 2022S_FE_AM_12

![[2022S_FE_AM_Q12_full.png]]

a) 11.67
b) 12.87
c) 15.83
d) 25.
?
b) 12.87
### Explanation
The approximate average access time for a magnetic disk is defined in this problem as:
$$\text{Average Access Time} = \text{Average Seek Time} + \text{Track-to-Track Seek Time} + \text{Average Rotational Delay}$$

We are given:
*   **Average seek time:** $7.5 \text{ ms}$
*   **Track-to-track seek time:** $1.2 \text{ ms}$
*   **Rotational speed:** $7,200 \text{ rpm (revolutions per minute)}$

### Step 1: Calculate Average Rotational Delay
First, find the time it takes for one full revolution.
$$7,200 \text{ rev} \longrightarrow 60 \text{ seconds}$$
$$1 \text{ rev} \longrightarrow \frac{60}{7,200} \text{ seconds} = \frac{1}{120} \text{ seconds}$$
$$\frac{1}{120} \text{ s} \approx 0.008333 \text{ s} = 8.333 \text{ ms}$$

The **average** rotational delay is statistically half of the time it takes for one full revolution (since, on average, the disk needs to rotate a half-turn to reach the target sector).
$$\text{Average Rotational Delay} = \frac{8.333 \text{ ms}}{2} = 4.167 \text{ ms}$$

### Step 2: Sum the Times
Now, substitute all values into the original formula:
$$\text{Average Access Time} = 7.5 \text{ ms} + 1.2 \text{ ms} + 4.167 \text{ ms}$$
$$\text{Average Access Time} = 12.867 \text{ ms}$$

Rounding to two decimal places gives **12.87 ms**.
