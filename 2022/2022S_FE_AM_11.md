# 2022S_FE_AM_11

Which of the following is the average cycles per instruction (CPI) of a computer that can execute 1 billion instructions per second at a clock rate of 2.4 GHz

a) 0.04
b) 0.12
c) 2.4
d) 25
?
c) 2.4
### Explanation
**CPI (Cycles Per Instruction)** is a metric used to measure processor performance. It indicates how many clock cycles, on average, the CPU requires to execute a single instruction.

We are given:
*   **Instruction Execution Rate:** $1 \text{ billion instructions per second} = 10^9 \text{ IPS}$
*   **Clock Rate:** $2.4 \text{ GHz} = 2.4 \times 10^9 \text{ cycles per second (Hz)}$

### Formula

The relationship between Clock Rate, IPS, and CPI is defined as:
$$\text{Clock Rate} = \text{IPS} \times \text{CPI}$$

Therefore, we can solve for CPI:
$$\text{CPI} = \frac{\text{Clock Rate}}{\text{IPS}}$$

### Calculation

$$\text{CPI} = \frac{2.4 \times 10^9 \text{ cycles/sec}}{1 \times 10^9 \text{ instructions/sec}}$$
$$\text{CPI} = 2.4 \text{ cycles/instruction}$$
