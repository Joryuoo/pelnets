# 2022S_FE_AM_32

There exists a system that connects a head office to a factory via a leased line and sends
form data. The size of each form is 2,000 bytes, and header data totaling 400 bytes is
attached to every two (2) forms to be sent through this system. On average, 100,000 forms
per hour are generated. When the line speed is 1 Mbit/s, what is the line’s utilization rate in
percentage

a) 6.1
b) 44
c) 49
d) 53 – 14 –

?
c) 49
### Explanation
To calculate the line's utilization rate, we need to determine the ratio of the total data transmitted per unit of time to the total line capacity per unit of time.

### 1. Calculate the Size of Each Transmission Unit

The data consists of forms and a header. A header of 400 bytes is attached to every two (2) forms. Let's analyze the transmission unit for 2 forms.

| Component | Size (Bytes) |
| :--- | :--- |
| Form 1 | $2,000$ |
| Form 2 | $2,000$ |
| Header | $400$ |
| **Total Size for 2 forms** | **$4,400$** |

This means the average data transmitted **per form** is:
$$ \text{Size per form} = \frac{4,400 \text{ bytes}}{2 \text{ forms}} = 2,200 \text{ bytes/form} $$

### 2. Calculate the Total Data Rate Required

The system generates $100,000$ forms per hour. We can calculate the total data generated per hour and convert it to bits.

$$ \text{Total Data per Hour} = 100,000 \text{ forms/hour} \times 2,200 \text{ bytes/form} = 220,000,000 \text{ bytes/hour} $$

Since $1 \text{ byte} = 8 \text{ bits}$:
$$ \text{Total Bits per Hour} = 220,000,000 \text{ bytes/hour} \times 8 \text{ bits/byte} = 1,760,000,000 \text{ bits/hour} $$

### 3. Calculate the Line Capacity

The line speed is given as $1 \text{ Mbit/s}$ (Megabits per second).
$$ 1 \text{ Mbit/s} = 1,000,000 \text{ bits/second} $$

To compare it with the required data rate, we convert the line capacity to bits per hour:
$$ \text{Line Capacity per Hour} = 1,000,000 \text{ bits/second} \times 3,600 \text{ seconds/hour} = 3,600,000,000 \text{ bits/hour} $$

### 4. Calculate Utilization Rate

The utilization rate is the ratio of required data rate to line capacity:
$$ \text{Utilization Rate} = \frac{\text{Total Data Required per Hour}}{\text{Line Capacity per Hour}} \times 100\% $$

$$ \text{Utilization Rate} = \frac{1,760,000,000}{3,600,000,000} \times 100\% \approx 48.89\% $$

Rounding to the nearest whole number given in the choices gives **$49\%$**.
