### Explanation
To formulate the linear programming model, we need to define our **objective function** and our **constraints** based on the given problem.

**1. Define the Variables:**
*   $x$: Number of units of product A.
*   $y$: Number of units of product B.

**2. Formulate the Objective Function:**
The company wants to maximize its total profit.
*   Profit from product A = 40 dollars/unit $\times\ x$ units = $40x$
*   Profit from product B = 30 dollars/unit $\times\ y$ units = $30y$
*   **Objective function:** $40x + 30y$ (to be maximized)

**3. Formulate the Constraints:**
*   **Production Capacity:** 
    *   Product A takes 3 person-hours per unit.
    *   This is "three times longer than that of product B", which means product B takes $3 / 3 = 1$ person-hour per unit.
    *   Total capacity is 3,000 person-hours.
    *   Constraint: $3x + 1y \le 3,000$
*   **Market Limitations:**
    *   Maximum units of product A that can be sold is 8,000.
    *   Constraint: $x \le 8,000$
    *   Maximum units of product B that can be sold is 1,200.
    *   Constraint: $y \le 1,200$
*   **Non-negativity:**
    *   You cannot produce a negative number of units.
    *   Constraint: $x \ge 0$, $y \ge 0$

Matching these mathematical expressions with the given options, **option d** provides the correct set of inequalities:
*   Objective function: $40x + 30y \to$ maximize
*   Constraints:
    *   $3x + y \le 3,000$
    *   $x \le 8,000$
    *   $y \le 1,200$
    *   $x \ge 0, y \ge 0$
