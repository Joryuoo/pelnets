# 2022S_FE_AM_16

Which of the following is the system configuration that has the highest availability? Here, when systems are connected in parallel, the systems are considered to be operational if at least one (1) of them is operating.

a) A single system with an availability of 99%
b) Four (4) identical systems, each with an availability of 70%, are connected in parallel.
c) Three (3) identical systems, each with an availability of 80%, are connected in parallel.
d) Two (2) identical systems, each with an availability of 90%, are connected in parallel.

?
c) Three (3) identical systems, each with an availability of 80%, are connected in parallel.

### Explanation
The availability of a parallel system is calculated by finding the probability that ALL systems fail, and subtracting that from 1 (100%). If the availability of a single unit is $A$, its failure rate is $1 - A$.

Let's calculate the availability for each option:
- **a)** Single system: $99\% = 0.99$
- **b)** 4 systems in parallel (70%): $1 - (1 - 0.7)^4 = 1 - 0.3^4 = 1 - 0.0081 = 0.9919$ (99.19%)
- **c)** 3 systems in parallel (80%): $1 - (1 - 0.8)^3 = 1 - 0.2^3 = 1 - 0.008 = 0.9920$ (99.2%)
- **d)** 2 systems in parallel (90%): $1 - (1 - 0.9)^2 = 1 - 0.1^2 = 1 - 0.01 = 0.9900$ (99.0%)

The highest availability is 99.2%, which is option **c**.
