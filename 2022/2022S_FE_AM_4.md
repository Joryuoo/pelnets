c) 5  3  4  −  ×
### Explanation
Reverse Polish Notation (RPN), also known as postfix notation, is a mathematical notation in which operators follow their operands. It is easily evaluated using a **stack** data structure.

### Evaluating the Original Expression
The original expression is: `3  4  −  5  ×`

| Step | Token | Action | Stack State |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 1 | `3` | Push `3` | `[3]` |
| 2 | `4` | Push `4` | `[3, 4]` |
| 3 | `−` | Pop `4`, Pop `3`, Evaluate `3 - 4 = -1`, Push `-1` | `[-1]` |
| 4 | `5` | Push `5` | `[-1, 5]` |
| 5 | `×` | Pop `5`, Pop `-1`, Evaluate `-1 × 5 = -5`, Push `-5` | `[-5]` |

The evaluated result is **-5**.

### Evaluating the Options

Let's evaluate each option to find which one yields `-5`.

*   **a) `3  4  5  ×  −`**
    *   `4 × 5 = 20`
    *   `3 - 20 = -17` (Incorrect)
*   **b) `4  5  −  3  ×`**
    *   `4 - 5 = -1`
    *   `-1 × 3 = -3` (Incorrect)
*   **c) `5  3  4  −  ×`**
    *   `3 - 4 = -1`
    *   `5 × -1 = -5` (**Correct!**)
*   **d) `5  4  3  −  ×`**
    *   `4 - 3 = 1`
    *   `5 × 1 = 5` (Incorrect)
