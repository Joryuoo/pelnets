# 2022S_FE_AM_14

A 12-point character is to be displayed on a 96-dpi display in bitmap. How many dots is the height of a square font? Here, 1 point is 1/72 inch.

a) 8
b) 9
c) 12
d) 16

?
d) 16

### Explanation
To find the height in dots, we first convert the font size in points to inches, and then multiply by the display resolution in dpi (dots per inch).

1. Convert points to inches: 12 points * (1/72 inch/point) = 1/6 inch.
2. Convert inches to dots: (1/6 inch) * 96 dots/inch = 16 dots.

Therefore, the height is 16 dots.
