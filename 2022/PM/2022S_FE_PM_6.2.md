### Explanation
Bit Counting Algorithm**
1. **Blanks F & G (Checking if rightmost bit is 1):** 
   - If the rightmost bit of `Work` is 1, doing a bitwise OR with `'00000001'B` will not change its value. Hence, `Work | '00000001'B == Work`. Thus, **F = c**.
   - Doing a bitwise AND with `'11111110'B` will clear the rightmost bit, guaranteeing it changes its value. Hence, `Work & '11111110'B != Work`. Thus, **G = b**.
2. **Blank H (Minimum processing time for Program 3):** 
   The minimum processing time occurs when `Data` is `'00000000'B`. 
   Tracing the execution:
   - `Work <- Data` (1)
   - `Count <- 0` (1)
   - `WHILE (Work != '00000000'B)` evaluates to False (2)
   - `return Count` (2)
   Total minimum time = $1 + 1 + 2 + 2 = 6$. Thus, **H = a**.
3. **Blank I (Bit replacement efficiency):**
   - Let `Work` = `'01100110'B`
   - `Work - 1` = `'01100101'B`
   - `Work & (Work - 1)` = `'01100100'B`. 
   This operation cleverly clears the least significant 1-bit in `Work`. Thus, **I = d**.
