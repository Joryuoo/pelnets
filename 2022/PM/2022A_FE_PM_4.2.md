### Explanation
*
* **Blank E:** The devices were upgraded to support 10 Gbps and 1 Gbps interfaces. However, the physical infrastructure is still using **UTP Cat-5 cables**. 
  Category 5 (Cat-5) cables reliably support up to 100 Mbps. While they can sometimes support 1 Gbps (1000BASE-T) over short distances, they are completely incapable of supporting **10 Gbps** links (which connect the switches to the routers). To utilize the new 10 Gbps bandwidth, the physical cabling must be upgraded to **Cat-6a** (or better), which is rated for 10 Gbps over standard distances. (Correct Answer: **a**)
