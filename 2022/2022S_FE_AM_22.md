### Explanation
The question requires us to find a logical expression that evaluates to `1` (true) when a **valid pattern** is read, and `0` (false) when an **invalid pattern** is read. 

Let the variables $A, B, C, D, E$ represent the five rows of holes on the tape. 
- A punched hole corresponds to logic `1`.
- An unpunched hole corresponds to logic `0`.

### Defining the Patterns

According to the problem description:
1. **Invalid Pattern:** No holes are punched (all 0s) **OR** all five holes are punched (all 1s).
2. **Valid Pattern:** At least one hole is punched **AND** at least one hole is not punched.

Let's represent the **invalid pattern** as a Boolean expression:
- **All 0s (no holes punched):** $\overline{A} \cdot \overline{B} \cdot \overline{C} \cdot \overline{D} \cdot \overline{E}$
- **All 1s (all holes punched):** $A \cdot B \cdot C \cdot D \cdot E$

Thus, the condition for an **invalid pattern** is:
$$ \text{Invalid} = (A \cdot B \cdot C \cdot D \cdot E) + (\overline{A} \cdot \overline{B} \cdot \overline{C} \cdot \overline{D} \cdot \overline{E}) $$

### Finding the Valid Pattern Expression

A **valid pattern** is simply the exact opposite (negation) of the invalid pattern. Therefore, we just need to invert the expression for the invalid pattern:

$$ \text{Valid} = \overline{ \text{Invalid} } $$
$$ \text{Valid} = \overline{ (A \cdot B \cdot C \cdot D \cdot E) + (\overline{A} \cdot \overline{B} \cdot \overline{C} \cdot \overline{D} \cdot \overline{E}) } $$

This matches the structure of option **d**, which essentially wraps the entire "invalid" condition in a NOT logic overline.

### Alternative Form (Using De Morgan's Laws)
By applying De Morgan's laws, the expression can also be rewritten as:
$$ \text{Valid} = \overline{(A \cdot B \cdot C \cdot D \cdot E)} \cdot \overline{(\overline{A} \cdot \overline{B} \cdot \overline{C} \cdot \overline{D} \cdot \overline{E})} $$
$$ \text{Valid} = (\overline{A} + \overline{B} + \overline{C} + \overline{D} + \overline{E}) \cdot (A + B + C + D + E) $$
This confirms our logic: *"Not all holes are punched AND not all holes are unpunched."*
