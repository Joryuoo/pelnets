# 2022S_FE_AM_23

![[2022S_FE_AM_Q23_full.png]]

a) Duplication check
b) Format check
c) Logical check
d) Sequence check
?
c) Logical check
### Explanation
The question asks for the appropriate data validation method to ensure that an **order date** falls on a business day and is less than or equal to the **entering date**.

Let's break down the types of checks provided in the choices:

| Check Type | Description | Example |
|---|---|---|
| **Duplication check** | Checks whether the entered data already exists in the system to prevent redundant entries. | Ensuring that an order with the same `Form number` is not entered twice. |
| **Format check** | Checks whether the data is entered in the correct structural format (e.g., length, alphanumeric). | Ensuring the `Order date` is inputted as `YYYY-MM-DD`. |
| **Logical check** | Checks whether the data makes sense in the context of business rules or other related data fields. | **Ensuring the order date is $\le$ the entering date, and falls on a valid business day.** |
| **Sequence check** | Checks if a series of inputs or records are in a predefined logical or numerical order. | Ensuring `Form number` 1002 is entered after 1001. |

Since the validation requires evaluating a business rule (it must be a business day) and a relationship between two dates (`Order date` $\le$ `Entering date`), it is a **Logical check**. 

Therefore, the correct answer is **c**.
