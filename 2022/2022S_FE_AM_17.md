a) 0, 1, 4, 10

### Explanation
Under the **First-Come-First-Serve (FCFS)** scheduling algorithm, processes are executed in the exact order they arrive. The CPU is non-preemptive, meaning once a process starts, it runs to completion.

Let's calculate the start, finish, and waiting times for each process:

1. **P1** arrives at time 0. Since the CPU is free, it starts immediately at time 0. 
   - Burst time = 3. Finishes at time 3.
   - **Waiting time = Start time - Arrival time = 0 - 0 = 0.**
2. **P2** arrives at time 2. The CPU is busy with P1 until time 3. It starts at time 3.
   - Burst time = 5. Finishes at time 8.
   - **Waiting time = 3 - 2 = 1.**
3. **P3** arrives at time 4. The CPU is busy with P2 until time 8. It starts at time 8.
   - Burst time = 7. Finishes at time 15.
   - **Waiting time = 8 - 4 = 4.**
4. **P4** arrives at time 5. The CPU is busy with P3 until time 15. It starts at time 15.
   - Burst time = 2. Finishes at time 17.
   - **Waiting time = 15 - 5 = 10.**

The waiting times for P1, P2, P3, and P4 are 0, 1, 4, and 10, respectively.
