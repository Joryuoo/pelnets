# 2014A_FE_68
![[2014Oct_FE_AM_Q68_full.png]]

a) To evaluate the current position of a company so that the promotion effect set as an objective can be measured
b) To evaluate the current position of a company as the basis for setting an objective and prioritizing resource allocation
c) To evaluate the marketing tactics for maintaining a competitive edge in the market by setting an objective and improving product quality
d) To evaluate the change in the market by considering seasonal fluctuation factors and geographical distribution, in order to set an objective
?
b) To evaluate the current position of a company as the basis for setting an objective and prioritizing resource allocation
### Explanation
Product Portfolio Management (PPM), such as the BCG Matrix shown in the question, helps organizations analyze their business units or product lines across two main dimensions: market growth rate (business attractiveness) and relative market share (business strength). 

The primary purpose of using this matrix is to evaluate the current strategic position of various products to make informed decisions on how to prioritize and allocate resources. For example, a company might invest heavily in "Stars" (high share, high growth) while divesting "Dogs" (low share, low growth).

* **a** is incorrect because PPM is not for measuring promotional effects.
* **c** is incorrect because PPM is a high-level strategic tool, not a tactical tool for product quality improvement.
* **d** is incorrect because seasonal and geographical factors are not the primary focus of PPM matrices.
