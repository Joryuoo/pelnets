# 2014A_FE_70
Which of the following is the primary purpose of implementing an ERP system that is used for business management?

a) To build a long term relationship with the customer as a result of deepened understanding of customer’s needs and desire
b) To integrate all departments and functions across a company into a single computer system that can serve the entire company’s needs
c) To maximize the value added along the series of business operations including purchase, production, sale, and distribution
d) To transform raw data into meaningful and useful information for business purposes and implement an effective strategy that can provide a competitive market advantage
?
b) To integrate all departments and functions across a company into a single computer system that can serve the entire company’s needs
### Explanation
ERP (Enterprise Resource Planning) refers to software that organizations use to manage and integrate core business processes, such as finance, HR, manufacturing, supply chain, and procurement, typically in real-time and mediated by software and technology. The main goal of ERP is integration across the entire enterprise.

* **a** describes Customer Relationship Management (CRM) systems.
* **c** describes Supply Chain Management (SCM) or value chain management.
* **d** describes Business Intelligence (BI) systems.
