# 2014S_FE_17
When a 12-point character is displayed in bitmap on a 96-dpi display screen, how many dots are used for the height of a square font? Here, 1 point is 1/72 inch.

a) 8
b) 9
c) 12
d) 16
?
d) 16
### Explanation
First, calculate the physical height of a 12-point character in inches:
* $\text{Height in inches} = 12 \text{ points} \times \frac{1 \text{ inch}}{72 \text{ points}} = \frac{1}{6} \text{ inch}$

Next, convert this height into the number of dots using the screen resolution (96 dpi, or dots per inch):
* $\text{Dots} = \frac{1}{6} \text{ inch} \times 96 \text{ dots/inch} = 16 \text{ dots}$

Thus, 16 dots are used for the height of the character.
