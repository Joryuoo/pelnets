# 2014S_FE_21
Which of the following is a term that shows the amount of work that is processed per unit of time by a computer system?

a) Response time
b) Throughput
c) Time slice
d) Turnaround time
?
b) Throughput
### Explanation
- **Throughput** refers to the amount of work or data processed by a computer system in a given unit of time.
- **Response time** is the time from when a user inputs a command until the system begins to output a response.
- **Time slice** is the short period of CPU time allocated to a process in multitasking systems.
- **Turnaround time** is the total time taken from submitting a batch job until its completion.
