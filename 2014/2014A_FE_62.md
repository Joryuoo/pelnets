# 2014A_FE_62
Which of the following is an appropriate activity that is undertaken by a system auditor who makes an audit of access control?

a) Creation and storage of management records for access control concerning software
b) Establishment of a management policy for access control concerning network
c) Implementation of operations management for access control concerning hardware
d) Verification of the management status of access control concerning data
?
d) Verification of the management status of access control concerning data
### Explanation
A system auditor's role is to independently assess and evaluate systems, controls, and operations to ensure compliance, security, and efficiency. To maintain objectivity and independence, auditors must not perform the operational activities they are auditing.

* **a, b, c** are incorrect because they involve direct operational responsibilities (creation, establishment, and implementation). An auditor cannot evaluate a process they themselves have set up.
* **d** is correct because "verification" is a standard auditing activity used to check if the existing management controls are functioning appropriately.
