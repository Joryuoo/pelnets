# 2014A_FE_76
Which of the following is a case example that is suitable for ABC analysis?

a) A detailed forecast of sales and stock requirements needs to be analyzed to prevent a shortage of stock.
b) Information about the combination of products that are purchased by customers needs to be analyzed.
c) Market demand needs to be analyzed by conducting a questionnaire survey of opinion leaders in each region.
d) The hot-selling products need to be analyzed on the basis of the sales value and gross profit of each product.
?
d) The hot-selling products need to be analyzed on the basis of the sales value and gross profit of each product.
### Explanation
ABC analysis is a categorized inventory and sales management technique where items are ranked (A, B, or C) based on their importance, such as sales volume or gross profit, allowing the business to focus on the most valuable items.
- a describes MRP (Material Requirements Planning) or demand forecasting.
- b is market basket analysis (association rule learning).
- c is market research.
