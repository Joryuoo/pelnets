# 2014A_FE_57
Which of the following is an appropriate policy for creating an SLA?

a) Setting a compensation as a penalty for every item that has a service level
b) Setting a service level for every item that can be taken into consideration
c) Setting a service level that is maintained into the future without any changes
d) Setting a service level under the cost and needs of a service provider and its customers
?
d) Setting a service level under the cost and needs of a service provider and its customers
### Explanation
A Service Level Agreement (SLA) is an agreement between an IT service provider and a customer. It describes the IT service, documents service level targets, and specifies the responsibilities of the IT service provider and the customer.
When establishing an SLA, it is crucial to balance the customer's needs and expectations for service quality with the service provider's capability and the associated costs to deliver that level of service. 
- It is not practical to set a service level for *every* possible item (b) or penalize every miss (a). 
- SLAs should be reviewed and updated regularly to adapt to changing needs, not maintained indefinitely without changes (c).
