### Explanation
ABC analysis is a method of inventory control and categorization that divides items into three categories (A, B, and C) based on their importance or value. 

The graph typically used for ABC analysis is a Pareto chart, which combines a bar chart showing the individual values (usually sorted in descending order) and a line graph showing the cumulative percentage of the total.
