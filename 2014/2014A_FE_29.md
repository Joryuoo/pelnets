### Explanation
In relational algebra, extracting specific **columns** from a table is called a **Projection**. The SQL statement `SELECT A1, A3, A5 FROM R` retrieves only the columns A1, A3, and A5 from table R, making it a projection operation.

* **Selection:** Extracts specific **rows** that meet a certain condition (equivalent to the `WHERE` clause in SQL).
* **Join:** Combines columns from two or more tables based on a related column between them.
* **Difference:** Returns rows that appear in the first table but not in the second table.
