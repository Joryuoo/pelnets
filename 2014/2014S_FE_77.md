# 2014S_FE_77
Which of the following is an explanation of an affinity diagram?

a) It is a method by which the causal relationship between events is clarified for events intertwined with complex factors.
b) It is a method by which the collected information is grouped according to the mutual relationship, and the problems to be resolved are clarified.
c) It is a method by which the means and measures are reviewed for problems that might have several outcomes along with the progress of a situation, and the process leading to the desired result is decided.
d) It is a method by which the means and measures for achieving the purpose and objectives are developed sequentially, and the most appropriate means or measures are searched for.
?
b) It is a method by which the collected information is grouped according to the mutual relationship, and the problems to be resolved are clarified.
### Explanation
An affinity diagram (also known as the KJ method) is a business tool used to organize large amounts of brainstorming ideas, data, or facts into logical groups based on their natural relationships or "affinity".

- **a)** describes a Cause-and-Effect Diagram (or Relations Diagram).
- **c)** describes a Process Decision Program Chart (PDPC).
- **d)** describes a Tree Diagram.
