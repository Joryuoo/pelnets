### Explanation
Total delay is the sum of transmission delay and propagation delay.

1. **Transmission Delay** (time to put the data onto the medium):
   - Message size = $200 \text{ bytes} \times 8 \text{ bits/byte} = 1600 \text{ bits}$
   - Line speed = $100 \text{ Kbps} = 100,000 \text{ bps}$
   - Delay = $1600 / 100,000 = 0.016 \text{ seconds} = 16.0 \text{ ms}$

2. **Propagation Delay** (time for data to travel across the cable):
   - Distance = $10 \text{ km} = 10,000 \text{ m}$
   - Propagation speed = $2 \times 10^8 \text{ m/sec}$
   - Delay = $10,000 / (2 \times 10^8) = 0.00005 \text{ seconds} = 0.05 \text{ ms}$

Total delay = $16.0 \text{ ms} + 0.05 \text{ ms} = 16.05 \text{ ms}$.
