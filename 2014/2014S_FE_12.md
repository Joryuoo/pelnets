d) 40
### Explanation
To find the performance in MIPS (Million Instructions Per Second), we first calculate the average execution time per instruction:

* Instruction A: 10 ns × 40% = 4 ns
* Instruction B: 20 ns × 30% = 6 ns
* Instruction C: 50 ns × 30% = 15 ns

**Average Execution Time** = 4 ns + 6 ns + 15 ns = 25 ns per instruction.
This is $25 \times 10^{-9}$ seconds.

**MIPS Calculation:**
$MIPS = \frac{1 \text{ second}}{25 \times 10^{-9} \text{ seconds}} = 40,000,000 \text{ instructions/second}$
Therefore, the performance is **40 MIPS**.
