# 2014A_FE_42
Which of the following is regarded as risk sharing or risk transfer? 
 
a) Breaking down or aggregating risks into manageable units  
b) Distributing risk to other parties by purchasing insurance  
c) Eliminating the source of a risk  
d) Reducing the rate of occurrence of loss or damage  
?
b) Distributing risk to other parties by purchasing insurance
### Explanation
Risk sharing or risk transfer involves shifting the financial consequences or the burden of a risk to a third party. Purchasing insurance is a classic example of transferring risk to an insurance company.

- **a)** This is an analytical step in risk assessment.
- **c)** This describes **risk avoidance**, which is changing the plan to eliminate the risk or condition.
- **d)** This describes **risk mitigation** or reduction, which is taking steps to reduce the probability and/or impact of a risk.
