b) %UNIX%
### Explanation
In SQL, the `LIKE` operator is used with wildcard characters to perform pattern matching. The `%` wildcard represents zero, one, or multiple characters.

To find all titles that *include* the word "UNIX" anywhere within the text, you place the `%` wildcard on both sides: `'%UNIX%'`.

* `'%UNIX'` finds strings that **end** with "UNIX".
* `'UNIX%'` finds strings that **start** with "UNIX".
* `'UNIX'` finds strings that are **exactly** "UNIX".
