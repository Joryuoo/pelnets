# 2014A_FE_66
When a computerization plan is made, which of the following is an item to be considered?

a) Clarifying the cost and investment effect of the development, maintenance, and operations of the system
b) Describing the test plan, operation manuals, and measures against failures in a specific and clear way
c) Installing an equivalent system used by other companies in the same industry in order to avoid failures
d) Proceeding with the plan on the premise that the system is developed by a company’s own staff in view of system operations
?
a) Clarifying the cost and investment effect of the development, maintenance, and operations of the system
### Explanation
A computerization plan outlines the strategic direction and justification for an IT project. A critical component at this high-level planning stage is evaluating the total cost of ownership (development, maintenance, and operations) and the expected return on investment (investment effect) to ensure business viability.

* **b** is incorrect because detailed test plans and operations manuals are created during the design and development phases, not the strategic planning stage.
* **c** is incorrect because simply copying competitors is not a sound strategic plan; the system must meet the specific needs of the company.
* **d** is incorrect because assuming in-house development prematurely restricts options. Decisions on whether to build (in-house) or buy (outsource) should be made after evaluating resources and requirements.
