### Explanation
Let's find the number of patients with cancer and a positive test result ($A$) and without cancer and a positive test result ($C$).
Accuracy for patients with cancer: $A / 100 = 99 / 100 \implies A = 99$.
Accuracy for patients without cancer: $D / 99,900 = 96 / 100 \implies D = 95,904$.
Patients without cancer who tested positive (false positive): $C = 99,900 - 95,904 = 3,996$.
Total patients with a positive test result: $A + C = 99 + 3,996 = 4,095$.
Probability that a patient with a positive test has cancer:
$P = \frac{A}{A + C} = \frac{99}{4095} \approx 0.024175...$
In percentage: $2.418\%$.
