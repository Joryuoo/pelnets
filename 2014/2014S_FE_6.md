a) 2^n
### Explanation
A heap is structured as a complete binary tree. 
- Level 0 (root) has $2^0 = 1$ node.
- Level 1 has $2^1 = 2$ nodes.
- In general, level $k$ has $2^k$ nodes.

For a tree to have a depth of $n$, it must have all levels from $0$ up to $n-1$ completely filled, plus at least one node at level $n$.
- Total nodes up to level $n-1$ is the sum of a geometric series: $2^0 + 2^1 + \dots + 2^{n-1} = 2^n - 1$.
- To reach depth $n$, there must be a minimum of $1$ node at the $n$-th level.
- Therefore, the minimum total number of nodes is $(2^n - 1) + 1 = 2^n$.
