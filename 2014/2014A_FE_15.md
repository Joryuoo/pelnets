# 2014A_FE_15
![[2014Oct_FE_AM_Q15_full.png]]

a) Confirmation of changes in external factors
b) Identification of slow processes
c) Investigation of the hit ratio of cache memory
d) Verification of the status of data storage
?
d) Verification of the status of data storage
### Explanation
The possible cause for blank C is "An increase in disk I/O because of fragmentation." To investigate fragmentation, you need to check how the data is stored on the disk. Thus, "Verification of the status of data storage" is the correct investigation item.

* **a) Confirmation of changes in external factors** corresponds to the coexistence of another system (blank A).
* **b) Identification of slow processes** corresponds to checking for atypical searches (blank B).
* **c) Investigation of the hit ratio of cache memory** corresponds to a lack of buffer capacity (blank D).
