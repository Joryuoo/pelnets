b) Improvement in availability
### Explanation
The diagram shows a **Duplex configuration** (active-standby or active-passive cluster). If Server 1 goes down, Server 2 immediately takes over to ensure the service continues uninterrupted.

This direct redundancy aims to minimize downtime, thereby directly improving **availability**, which is the guarantee of reliable access to information and systems for authorized users.

* **Accountability:** Tracing actions to individuals (e.g., audit logs).
* **Confidentiality:** Keeping data secret from unauthorized entities (e.g., encryption).
* **Integrity:** Ensuring data is not maliciously or accidentally altered (e.g., hashing).
