a) Blocked by number 1 
### Explanation
The firewall processes the packet filtering rules in order from top to bottom.
Packet A has a Source Address of `10.1.2.3` and a Destination Port of `25`.
- **Rule 1**: Matches any packet with the Source Address `10.1.2.3`. Since Packet A's source address is exactly `10.1.2.3`, Rule 1 is triggered.
- **Action for Rule 1**: Blocked.

Once a rule is matched, subsequent rules are ignored. Therefore, the packet is blocked by rule number 1.
