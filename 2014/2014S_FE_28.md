# 2014S_FE_28
Which of the following is a characteristic of DRAM, in comparison with SRAM?

a) DRAM has the disadvantage of higher cost.
b) DRAM is suitable for a higher level of integration.
c) DRAM stores data in an electronic circuit called a flip-flop.
d) DRAM transfers data at a faster speed.
?
b) DRAM is suitable for a higher level of integration.
### Explanation
- **DRAM (Dynamic RAM)** uses capacitors to store data, requiring a single transistor per bit. Its simple structure allows for a very **high level of integration** (high density/capacity) and lower cost per bit. However, it is slower and requires periodic refreshing.
- **SRAM (Static RAM)** uses a flip-flop circuit (option c) typically involving 4 to 6 transistors per bit, making it faster (option d) and more expensive (option a), but less dense.
