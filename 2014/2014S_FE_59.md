# 2014S_FE_59
The development scale of a new system is estimated as 500 Function Points (FPs). In the project for developing this system, in addition to the person-hours for development, 10 person-months are necessary for the installation of the system and the education of developers. Also, 10% of the person-hours for development, installation, and education are required for project management. How many person-months in total are necessary for this project? Here, the productivity in development is 10 FPs per one person-month.

a) 51
b) 61
c) 65
d) 66
?
d) 66
### Explanation
First, calculate the person-months required for development:
- 500 FPs ÷ 10 FPs/person-month = 50 person-months

Next, sum the person-months for development, installation, and education:
- 50 (development) + 10 (installation & education) = 60 person-months

Finally, calculate the project management effort (10% of the sum) and add it to the total:
- Project management = 10% of 60 = 6 person-months
- Total person-months = 60 + 6 = 66 person-months.
