# 2014A_FE_71
Which of the following is an appropriate explanation concerning product innovation in the management of technology?

a) Developing a new or differentiated product that is a key to success
b) Making an operating profit through the results of technology development
c) Making progressive reforms in the business process
d) Managing strategic business that revolves around technology
?
a) Developing a new or differentiated product that is a key to success
### Explanation
Product innovation focuses on creating new products or significantly improving existing ones to stand out in the market. 
- b describes the general financial goal of technology management.
- c describes process innovation (improving how things are done, rather than the product itself).
- d is a broad definition of strategic technology management, not specifically product innovation.
