# 2014S_FE_29
Which of the following is the case where outline fonts rather than bitmap fonts are effective and suitable for use on an output device such as a display or a printer?

a) Both single-byte and double-byte characters are displayed.
b) Characters are displayed on the screen as quickly as possible.
c) Characters are displayed with uniform width.
d) Characters are enlarged using a given scale factor.
?
d) Characters are enlarged using a given scale factor.
### Explanation
- **Outline fonts** (vector fonts) define characters using mathematical curves and lines. They can be scaled (enlarged or reduced) to any size without losing quality or appearing jagged.
- **Bitmap fonts** define characters using a grid of pixels. Scaling them often results in pixelation (jagged edges).
Therefore, outline fonts are highly effective when characters need to be enlarged using a given scale factor.
