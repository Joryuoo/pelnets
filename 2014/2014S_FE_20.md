# 2014S_FE_20
![[2014S_FE_AM_Q20_p1.png]]

a) 0.02, 3.00
b) 0.03, 3.97
c) 3.97, 0.02
d) 3.97, 0.03
?
d) 3.97, 0.03
### Explanation
We are given two disks with reliability rates of $0.99$ and $0.97$.
* Failure rate of Disk A = $1 - 0.99 = 0.01$
* Failure rate of Disk B = $1 - 0.97 = 0.03$

**RAID0 (Striping)**
A RAID0 array fails if *at least one* of its disks fails. Its overall reliability is the product of the reliability of the individual disks.
* $\text{Reliability} = 0.99 \times 0.97 = 0.9603$
* $\text{Failure rate} = 1 - 0.9603 = 0.0397$ or **3.97%**

**RAID1 (Mirroring)**
A RAID1 array fails only if *all* of its disks fail. Its overall failure rate is the product of the failure rates of the individual disks.
* $\text{Failure rate} = 0.01 \times 0.03 = 0.0003$ or **0.03%**
