# 2014A_FE_10
In a paged virtual storage system, when a TLB (Translation Lookaside Buffer) is used to translate a virtual address to a physical address under the conditions shown below, how many clock cycles on average are required to translate an address through the TLB?

[Conditions]
1. It takes 1 clock cycle to translate an address at the time of a TLB hit.
2. It takes 40 clock cycles to translate an address at the time of a TLB miss hit.
3. The TLB miss rate is 0.5%.

a) 1.039
b) 1.040
c) 1.195
d) 1.200
?
c) 1.195
### Explanation
The average translation time (effective access time) for the TLB is calculated by taking the weighted average of the hit time and the miss time.
* **TLB miss rate**: $0.5\% = 0.005$
* **TLB hit rate**: $100\% - 0.5\% = 99.5\% = 0.995$
* **Hit time**: 1 clock cycle
* **Miss time**: 40 clock cycles (the problem states "at the time of a TLB miss hit", meaning the total time required upon a miss is 40).
Average clock cycles = $(\text{Hit Rate} \times \text{Hit Time}) + (\text{Miss Rate} \times \text{Miss Time})$
$= (0.995 \times 1) + (0.005 \times 40)$
$= 0.995 + 0.200$
$= 1.195$ clock cycles.
