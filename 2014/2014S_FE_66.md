# 2014S_FE_66
From a viewpoint of corporate management, which of the following is an explanation of BPM?

a) A reduction of inventory and lead time is achieved by exchanging information about a series of business processes from the supply of components up to the sale of products on a real-time basis.
b) Business processes ranging from the sales of products up to after-sales service and also including queries and claims are consistently managed on the basis of a customer database.
c) The management cycle of analysis, design, execution, and improvement is incorporated into business processes so that business processes can be continuously reviewed for improvement and integrated with optimum processes.
d) The overall optimization of corporate management resources is achieved by managing business-critical information about business operations or activities, such as production, logistics, sales, finance, and human affairs, in an integrated manner.
?
c) The management cycle of analysis, design, execution, and improvement is incorporated into business processes so that business processes can be continuously reviewed for improvement and integrated with optimum processes.
### Explanation
**BPM (Business Process Management)** involves managing and continually improving an organization's business processes using continuous cycles (like PDCA: Plan-Do-Check-Act or analysis, design, execution, improvement).
* **a) Reducing inventory and lead time across supply:** This describes **SCM (Supply Chain Management)**.
* **b) Managing processes on a customer database:** This describes **CRM (Customer Relationship Management)**.
* **d) Optimization of resources by integrating finance, production, etc.:** This describes **ERP (Enterprise Resource Planning)**.
