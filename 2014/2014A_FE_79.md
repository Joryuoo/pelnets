# 2014A_FE_79
![[2014Oct_FE_AM_Q79_p1.png]]

a) 26,290
b) 26,450
c) 27,250
d) 27,586
?
b) 26,450
### Explanation
Using the FIFO (First-In, First-Out) method, the first units placed in inventory are the first ones sold.

1. **Initial Inventory:** 100 units at $200 each.
2. **5th Purchase:** 50 units at $215 each.
3. **15th Sales (70 units):** Taken entirely from the initial 100 units.
   - Cost: $70 \times 200 = \$14,000$
   - Remaining: 30 units at $200, 50 units at $215.
4. **20th Purchase:** 100 units at $223 each.
5. **25th Sales (60 units):** Take the oldest units first.
   - 30 units from the initial batch at $200 = \$6,000$
   - 30 units from the 5th purchase at $215 = \$6,450$
   - Cost: $6,000 + 6,450 = \$12,450$

**Total Cost of Sales:** $14,000 + 12,450 = \$26,450$.
