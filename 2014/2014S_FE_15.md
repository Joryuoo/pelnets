### Explanation
We calculate the effective access time (EAT) for each memory configuration. The formula is:
**EAT = (Hit Ratio × Cache Access Time) + ((1 - Hit Ratio) × Main Memory Access Time)**

* **A**: No cache used, access time = **15 ns**
* **B**: No cache used, access time = **30 ns**
* **C**: EAT = (0.60 × 20) + (0.40 × 70) = 12 + 28 = **40 ns**
* **D**: EAT = (0.90 × 10) + (0.10 × 80) = 9 + 8 = **17 ns**

Sorting these times from shortest to longest gives:
15 ns (A), 17 ns (D), 30 ns (B), 40 ns (C).
