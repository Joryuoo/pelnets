# 2014S_FE_74
Which of the following is the procurement process that enables suppliers to bid against one another on the Internet in order to sell their products or services under the conditions specified by a general customer?

a) B to B
b) G to C
c) Reverse auction
d) Virtual mall
?
c) Reverse auction
### Explanation
A reverse auction is a type of auction in which the roles of buyer and seller are reversed. In a reverse auction, the sellers compete to obtain business from the buyer and prices will typically decrease as the sellers undercut each other. This matches the description of suppliers bidding against one another to sell to a customer.

- **a) B to B** (Business to Business) is a general transaction model, not a specific bidding process.
- **b) G to C** (Government to Citizen) refers to government services provided to citizens online.
- **d) Virtual mall** is an online marketplace hosting multiple merchants, like a physical mall but on the internet.
