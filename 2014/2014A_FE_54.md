# 2014A_FE_54
![[2014Oct_FE_AM_Q54_full.png]]

a) B is a cause of A.
b) B is a means of A.
c) B is a purpose of A.
d) B is an attribute of A.
?
a) B is a cause of A.
### Explanation
A fishbone diagram (also known as an Ishikawa diagram or cause-and-effect diagram) is used to systematically identify and present all the possible causes of a particular problem or effect.
In the diagram, the main arrow pointing to the right (A) represents the effect or the problem being analyzed. The arrows pointing into the main line (B) represent the causes or factors contributing to that effect. Therefore, B represents a cause of the result A.
