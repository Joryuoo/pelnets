# 2014S_FE_44
Which of the following is the most appropriate explanation of a digital certificate that is authorized by a trusted third party known as a CA (Certification Authority)?

a) A document that verifiably associates a public key with a particular person or party
b) A technique that is used to hide the secret data into a digital image, audio, or video file
c) A tool that serves to protect the copyright and authenticity of multimedia content
d) An electronic signature that protects the integrity and authenticity of a document
?
a) A document that verifiably associates a public key with a particular person or party
### Explanation
A digital certificate issued by a Certification Authority (CA) binds a public key to an entity's identity, ensuring that the public key belongs to them.
- **Choice b)** describes steganography.
- **Choice c)** describes digital watermarking or Digital Rights Management (DRM).
- **Choice d)** describes a digital signature, not the digital certificate itself.
