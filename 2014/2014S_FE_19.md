d) 0.83
### Explanation
**Availability** is calculated as the ratio of total operating time to total elapsed time.
* **Total Time**: The system operates 24 hours a day for 2 days.
  $2 \text{ days} \times 24 \text{ hours} \times 60 \text{ minutes} = 2,880 \text{ minutes}$

* **Total Repair Time**:
  Day 1: 12 failures × 30 mins/failure = 360 mins
  Day 2: 6 failures × 20 mins/failure = 120 mins
  Total = 360 + 120 = 480 minutes

* **Total Operating Time**:
  $2,880 \text{ mins} - 480 \text{ mins} = 2,400 \text{ mins}$

* **Availability**:
  $\text{Availability} = \frac{\text{Operating Time}}{\text{Total Time}} = \frac{2400}{2880} \approx 0.833$
