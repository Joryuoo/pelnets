# 2014A_FE_72
Among the various types of e-commerce transactions, which of the following is an appropriate example of B2C (i.e., “B to C”)?

a) Company A places an online order of materials from its supplier twice a month.
b) Mr. B buys a book from an online shop owned by Company A.
c) Mr. B pays an annual tax via a Web site hosted by the central tax office.
d) Mr. B posts an online advertisement to sell his car and finally Mr. C buys it.
?
b) Mr. B buys a book from an online shop owned by Company A.
### Explanation
B2C stands for Business-to-Consumer, which refers to a transaction between a business (Company A) and an individual consumer (Mr. B).
- a is an example of B2B (Business-to-Business).
- c is an example of C2G (Consumer-to-Government).
- d is an example of C2C (Consumer-to-Consumer).
