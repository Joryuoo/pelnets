# 2014S_FE_56
Which of the following is an activity that is performed during the WBS creation process in project scope management?

a) Arranging the schedule on the basis of the flow of project activities
b) Defining project activities that create deliverables
c) Estimating the cost by calculating person-hours for each project activity
d) Segmenting project activities hierarchically into smaller parts or pieces
?
d) Segmenting project activities hierarchically into smaller parts or pieces
### Explanation
A Work Breakdown Structure (WBS) decomposes the total scope of work of a project into smaller, more manageable components or tasks in a hierarchical manner. 
- **a)** is part of project schedule management.
- **b)** is defining activities, but WBS specifically involves the hierarchical segmentation of deliverables and work.
- **c)** is part of project cost management.
