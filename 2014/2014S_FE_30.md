c) 775
### Explanation
1. Determine the data rate (bytes per second):
   - Sampling rate = $11,000$ samples/second
   - Resolution = $8$ bits/sample = $1$ byte/sample
   - Data rate = $11,000 \times 1 = 11,000$ bytes/second

2. Calculate the total recording time in seconds:
   - Capacity = $512 \times 10^6$ bytes
   - Total time (seconds) = $(512 \times 10^6) / 11,000 \approx 46,545.45$ seconds

3. Convert seconds to minutes:
   - Total time (minutes) = $46,545.45 / 60 \approx 775.75$ minutes

The closest maximum full minutes that can be recorded is **775**.
