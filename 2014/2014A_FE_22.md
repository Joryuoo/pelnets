# 2014A_FE_22
A power supply unit has a rated output power of 500 watts and an efficiency of 80%. In order to obtain an output power of 500 watts from this power supply unit, how many watts is the minimum input power?

a) 100
b) 400
c) 625
d) 900
?
c) 625
### Explanation
Efficiency is the ratio of output power to input power. 
$$\text{Efficiency} = \frac{\text{Output Power}}{\text{Input Power}}$$

Given an efficiency of 80% (0.8) and an output power of 500 watts:
$$0.8 = \frac{500}{\text{Input Power}}$$
$$\text{Input Power} = \frac{500}{0.8} = 625 \text{ watts}$$
