# 2014S_FE_64
Which of the following is a situation where the independence of a system auditor is maintained?

a) A system auditor is recruited from an auditing firm and placed as an internal auditor, and is asked to audit the business system development of the company.
b) The auditing department of the IT vendor to whom the business system operations of the company are outsourced is requested and put in charge of external auditing of the business system operations of the company.
c) The staff member of the sales department is appointed as a member of the audit team, and is asked to audit the measures for the protection of personal information in the sales department.
d) The staff member who has developed a system in the system department is moved to the auditing department, and is put in charge of auditing the maintenance of the system.
?
a) A system auditor is recruited from an auditing firm and placed as an internal auditor, and is asked to audit the business system development of the company.
### Explanation
A system auditor must be independent and objective regarding the system being audited. They should not have any direct involvement in the development, operation, or management of the areas they review. 
* **a) Correct:** An auditor recruited externally has no prior involvement with the company's internal systems, maintaining full independence.
* **b) Incorrect:** The IT vendor's auditing department auditing their own outsourced operations constitutes a conflict of interest.
* **c) Incorrect:** A sales staff member auditing their own department lacks independence.
* **d) Incorrect:** A former developer auditing the maintenance of the very system they developed lacks objectivity.
