### Explanation
**Subquestion 2 Analysis**

**Blank E (Computationally equivalent statement):**
The original statement (*) is:
`TaxToPay[i] ← AllocatedIncome[i]  TaxRate[i]  100`

The program handles all numeric values as integers, and with integer division, the fractional part is truncated. The order of multiplication and division is critical to avoid premature truncation.

Let's evaluate the alternatives:
- **(1)** `TaxToPay[i] ← AllocatedIncome[i]  100  TaxRate[i]`
  If we divide by 100 first, any allocated income less than 100 will become 0 (e.g., 50 / 100 = 0), and then multiplying by `TaxRate[i]` results in 0. This loses precision and is not computationally equivalent.
- **(2)** `TaxToPay[i] ← TaxRate[i]  AllocatedIncome[i]  100`
  Since multiplication is commutative, `TaxRate[i]  AllocatedIncome[i]` is identical to `AllocatedIncome[i]  TaxRate[i]`. Dividing by 100 after the multiplication preserves the same precision as the original statement. This is computationally equivalent.
- **(3)** `TaxToPay[i] ← TaxRate[i]  100  AllocatedIncome[i]`
  Dividing `TaxRate[i]` by 100 first will result in 0 for any tax rate less than 100% (which are all values in Table 1). The entire result will be 0. This is not computationally equivalent.

Therefore, only statement (2) is computationally equivalent. This corresponds to **Choice d**.

**Blank F (Rounding to nearest integer):**
The original statement truncates the fractional part:
`IncomeTaxPerMonth ← IncomeTaxPerYear  12`

To round to the nearest integer when dividing by 12 using integer arithmetic, we must add half of the divisor (12 / 2 = 6) to the dividend before performing the integer division. 
If the remainder of `IncomeTaxPerYear / 12` is 6 or greater (corresponding to a fractional part of 0.5 or greater), adding 6 will push the dividend to the next multiple of 12, effectively rounding up. If the remainder is less than 6, adding 6 will not reach the next multiple, and integer division will truncate as normal (effectively rounding down).

Thus, the statement to round off to the nearest integer is:
`(IncomeTaxPerYear + 6)  12`

This corresponds to **Choice b**.
