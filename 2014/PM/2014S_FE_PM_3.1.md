### Explanation
To output a list of **ready sailors** who have experience working with any **officer post**, we need to apply filters on the `Job_status` and the `Rank_type`.

- **Blank A:** We are looking for "ready sailors". According to the `Sailor` table schema and description, ready sailors have a `Job_status` of `4`. Therefore, we need the condition `Job_status = 4`. This matches choice **b**.
- **Blank B:** We need sailors who have experience in "any officer post". According to the `Rank` table schema, officers have a `Rank_type` of `1` (1: officer, 2: non-officer). Therefore, we need the condition `Rank_type = 1`. This matches choice **c**.

**Why other choices are incorrect:**
- **a) `Job_status = 2`:** This refers to "signed on" sailors who are currently on a vessel, not "ready" sailors waiting for their next assignment.
- **d) `Sign_off_date IS NOT NULL` and e) `Sign_off_date IS NULL`:** These indicate whether a sea service record has ended or is still ongoing. While a ready sailor would have a sign off date, filtering by `Job_status = 4` is the direct and correct way to find ready sailors based on the system description.
