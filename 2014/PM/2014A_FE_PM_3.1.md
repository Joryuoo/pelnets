### Explanation
Based on the system requirements described:

- **Blank A (Course):** Requirement (6) states: "During the course, the main lecturer can make the new exercises or use the old ones." This implies that each course is assigned a main lecturer. To establish this relationship in the database, `LecturerID` must be added as a foreign key to the `Course` entity. Therefore, **A = c) Course**.
- **Blank B (Exercise):** Requirement (4) states: "The system records information about each exercise, such as the lecturer who created it...". This means each exercise must be linked to the lecturer who authored it. To map this relationship, `LecturerID` must be added as a foreign key to the `Exercise` entity. Therefore, **B = d) Exercise**.

**Why other choices are incorrect:**
- **BookSlide (a):** Requirement (3) mentions recording the "author name" as an attribute, but there is no requirement to link a `BookSlide` directly to the `Lecturer` entity via a foreign key.
- **Content (b):** A `Content` is merely a list for a subject and has no specified relationship with a lecturer.
- **QuestionAnswer (e):** While questions and answers belong to an exercise, the creator (lecturer) is tracked at the `Exercise` level according to Requirement (4), not at the individual question level.
- **Subject (f):** Subjects act as a template that contains many courses, and the lecturers are assigned to specific courses rather than owning the subject as a whole.
