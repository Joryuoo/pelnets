### Explanation
The average-case time complexity of Quick Sort, specifically when the pivot is selected randomly, is $O(n \log_2 n)$.

While the worst-case time complexity is $O(n^2)$ (which happens if the smallest or largest element is consistently chosen as the pivot, degrading the partitioning), randomizing the pivot guarantees that the expected average running time is $O(n \log_2 n)$. This is because a random pivot avoids consistently hitting the worst-case partitioning scenario.
