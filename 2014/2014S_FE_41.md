# 2014S_FE_41
Which of the following is the most appropriate term that corresponds to the method of illegally intercepting audio and/or data being transmitted or received via a network?

a) Salvaging
b) Scavenging
c) Sniffing
d) Spoofing
?
c) Sniffing
### Explanation
Sniffing is the process of monitoring and capturing all data packets passing through a given network using a packet sniffer. It corresponds to illegally intercepting data.
- **Spoofing** is falsifying data, such as changing an IP address or email sender to disguise the origin of communication.
- **Scavenging** typically refers to searching through residual data or trash (like dumpster diving) for information.
