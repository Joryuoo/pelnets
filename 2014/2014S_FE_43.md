# 2014S_FE_43
When information, such as an e-mail or a document file, is sent or received with a digital signature via the Internet, which of the following is the appropriate combination of security properties that ensure that such information has not been altered or tampered with during transmission?

a) Atomicity and consistency
b) Authenticity and integrity
c) Availability and confidentiality
d) Durability and isolation
?
b) Authenticity and integrity
### Explanation
A digital signature ensures:
- **Authenticity**: Verification that the sender is actually who they claim to be.
- **Integrity**: Verification that the message has not been altered or tampered with during transmission.

Other options:
- **Atomicity, consistency, durability, isolation (ACID)** are properties of database transactions.
- **Availability and confidentiality** are the other parts of the CIA triad (along with integrity), but they are not the primary properties ensured by a digital signature.
