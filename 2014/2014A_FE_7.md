# 2014A_FE_7
When the selection sort algorithm is used for sorting the dataset “2, 4, 6, 8, 10, 12, 14, 14” in descending order, how many data exchanges occur during the sorting process?

a) 4
b) 5
c) 6
d) 7
?
a) 4
### Explanation
Let's trace the selection sort algorithm sorting in descending order. In each pass, we find the maximum element in the remaining unsorted portion and swap it with the first element of that portion. If there are duplicates, the later one is often swapped depending on the specific implementation, but let's look at the swappings:
Original: `2, 4, 6, 8, 10, 12, 14, 14`
1. Swap 2 (index 0) and 14 (index 7): `14, 4, 6, 8, 10, 12, 14, 2`
2. Swap 4 (index 1) and 14 (index 6): `14, 14, 6, 8, 10, 12, 4, 2`
3. Swap 6 (index 2) and 12 (index 5): `14, 14, 12, 8, 10, 6, 4, 2`
4. Swap 8 (index 3) and 10 (index 4): `14, 14, 12, 10, 8, 6, 4, 2`
Now the array is fully sorted in descending order, resulting in exactly 4 data exchanges.
