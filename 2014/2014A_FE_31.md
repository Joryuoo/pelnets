# 2014A_FE_31
In computer networking, which of the following is the most appropriate technical term that means the variation in packet delay that causes the degradation of the QoS (Quality of Service) of real-time audio or video streaming service such as VoIP?

a) Crosstalk
b) Glitch
c) Jitter
d) Ripple
?
c) Jitter
### Explanation
**Jitter** refers to the variation in packet transit delay. In real-time communications like VoIP or video streaming, high jitter causes packets to arrive irregularly, which can lead to degraded audio or video quality (e.g., stuttering, artifacts).

* **a) Crosstalk:** Interference between communication channels or wires.
* **b) Glitch:** A sudden, temporary malfunction or fault.
* **d) Ripple:** A small variation in voltage in a power supply, or a wave-like variation.
