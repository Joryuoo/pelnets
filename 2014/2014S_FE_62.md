# 2014S_FE_62
Which of the following is an effective means of protecting an information system from the damage of over-voltage that is caused by a lightning strike?

a) Connecting computers and cables through a surge protection device
b) Digitalizing the control circuits of electric power facilities
c) Installing a private power generator
d) Providing two different communication paths
?
a) Connecting computers and cables through a surge protection device
### Explanation
A lightning strike can cause a sudden, massive increase in voltage known as a power surge or over-voltage. To protect equipment, a surge protection device (surge protector) is used to safely redirect excess voltage to the ground.
* **b) Digitalizing control circuits:** Does not provide protection against over-voltage.
* **c) Installing a private power generator:** Useful for power outages, not for over-voltage protection.
* **d) Providing two different communication paths:** A redundancy measure for network availability, not physical protection from lightning surges.
