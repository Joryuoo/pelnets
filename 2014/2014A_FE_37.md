# 2014A_FE_37
When security attacks to a computer system are classified into three categories (i.e., reconnaissance attack, access attack, and DoS attack), which of the following is classified as a reconnaissance attack?

a) Attempting to discover and map out systems, services, or vulnerabilities
b) Compromising the availability of a network, host, or application
c) Exploiting known vulnerabilities in authentication services or other Web services
d) Sending an extremely large number of requests over a network or the Internet
?
a) Attempting to discover and map out systems, services, or vulnerabilities
### Explanation
A **reconnaissance attack** involves the unauthorized discovery and mapping of systems, services, or vulnerabilities. It is typically the information-gathering phase an attacker performs before launching a full-scale attack (e.g., using port scanners or ping sweeps).

* **b) and d)** describe **DoS (Denial of Service) attacks**, which compromise availability by overwhelming resources.
* **c)** describes an **access attack**, where vulnerabilities are exploited to gain unauthorized entry or escalate privileges.
