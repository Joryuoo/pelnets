### Explanation
**Thrashing** is a condition in a virtual memory system where the system spends most of its time swapping pages between physical memory and disk rather than executing programs. 

This occurs in a **virtual storage system** when the degree of multiprogramming (multiplicity of programs) is **large**, meaning too many processes are competing for a limited amount of physical memory. The lack of sufficient memory frames per process leads to constant page faults.
