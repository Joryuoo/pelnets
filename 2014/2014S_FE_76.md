# 2014S_FE_76
![[2014S_FE_AM_Q76_full.png]]

a) 2
b) 3
c) 4
d) 5
?
b) 3
### Explanation
Let $x$ be the number of customers necessary for each seat per business day.

Total number of customers per month = $x \text{ (customers/seat/day)} \times 10 \text{ (seats)} \times 25 \text{ (days/month)} = 250x$

**Total Monthly Revenue:**
$250x \times 100 \text{ dollars} = 25000x$

**Total Variable Cost per month:**
$250x \times 20 \text{ dollars} = 5000x$

**Total Fixed Cost per month:**
$400 \text{ dollars/day} \times 25 \text{ days} = 10000 \text{ dollars}$

**Profit = Revenue - (Variable Cost + Fixed Cost)**
$50000 = 25000x - (5000x + 10000)$
$50000 = 20000x - 10000$
$60000 = 20000x$
$x = 3$

Therefore, 3 customers are necessary for each seat per business day.
