### Explanation
Traversing the given binary search tree follows these patterns:
- **Pre-order** (Root, Left, Right): Visit the node, then traverse left, then right. 
  Result: 15, 8, 2, 10, 18, 20
- **In-order** (Left, Root, Right): Traverse left, visit the node, then right. This always produces a sorted sequence for a BST.
  Result: 2, 8, 10, 15, 18, 20
- **Post-order** (Left, Right, Root): Traverse left, then right, then visit the node.
  Result: 2, 10, 8, 20, 18, 15

This exactly corresponds to choice c.
