# 2018A_FE_AM_62
![[2018A_FE_AM_Q62_full.png]]

a) 0.3
b) 0.5
c) 0.7
d) 1.0
?
c) 0.7
### Explanation
1. **Current rates (with 5 visits):**
   - Customer visit time per visit: $5.0 / 5 = 1.0\text{ hr}$
   - Visit preparation time per visit: $1.5 / 5 = 0.3\text{ hr}$

2. **New situation (with 6 visits):**
   - Customer visit hours: $6 \times 1.0 = 6.0\text{ hrs}$
   - Preparation time per visit is reduced by $0.1\text{ hr}$ using SFA: $0.3 - 0.1 = 0.2\text{ hr/visit}$
   - Total preparation hours: $6 \times 0.2 = 1.2\text{ hrs}$

3. **Reduction calculation:**
   - Remaining hours available for "Other operation":
     $$8.0 - (6.0 + 1.2) = 0.8\text{ hrs}$$
   - Hours to reduce from "Other operation":
     $$1.5 - 0.8 = 0.7\text{ hrs}$$
