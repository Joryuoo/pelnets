### Explanation
A binary search tree (BST) has the property that for every node, all keys in its left subtree are less than the node's key, and all keys in its right subtree are greater than the node's key.

Checking the trees:
- In option a), 15 is in the left subtree of 16 (valid), but 19 is in the left subtree of 16 (invalid, $19 > 16$).
- In option b), the root is 17. Left subtree has 14, 10, 16 ($14 < 17, 10 < 14, 16 > 14$). Right subtree has 19, 18 ($19 > 17, 18 < 19$). This satisfies all BST properties.
- In option c), 15 is in the right subtree of 16 (invalid, $15 < 16$).
- In option d), 20 is the root. Left child is 18, its left child is 10, its right child is 14 (invalid, $14 > 10$).
