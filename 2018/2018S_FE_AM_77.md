### Explanation
A Pareto chart is a type of chart that contains both bars and a line graph. The bars represent individual values in descending order (from highest frequency to lowest), and the line represents the cumulative total percentage, which monotonically increases and reaches 100% at the rightmost category. Choice (a) correctly depicts this structure with descending bars and a continuously increasing cumulative line.
