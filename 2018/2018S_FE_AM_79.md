# 2018S_FE_AM_79
When the inventory unit price is determined by dividing the total purchase amount by the total quantity of items purchased, which of the following is an appropriate inventory method to be used?

a) First-in first-out method
b) Periodic average method
c) Moving average method
d) Final acquisition cost method (Final purchase cost method)
?
b) Periodic average method
### Explanation
The periodic average method (also known as the weighted average cost method) calculates the unit cost of goods by dividing the total cost of goods available for sale (total purchase amount plus beginning inventory) by the total number of units available for sale during a specific accounting period. This average unit cost is then used to determine the value of the ending inventory.
