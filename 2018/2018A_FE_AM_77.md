# 2018A_FE_AM_77
![[2018A_FE_AM_Q77_full.png]]

a) S1
b) S2
c) S3
d) S4
?
a) S1
### Explanation
The weather probabilities tomorrow are:
- Sunny: $P(\text{Sunny}) = 0.30$
- Cloudy: $P(\text{Cloudy}) = 0.50$
- Rainy: $P(\text{Rainy}) = 0.20$

Calculate the expected value $E(S)$ for each strategy:
- **Strategy S1:**
  $$E(S1) = (0.30 \times 80) + (0.50 \times 20) + (0.20 \times -40) = 24 + 10 - 8 = \mathbf{26}$$
- **Strategy S2:**
  $$E(S2) = (0.30 \times 60) + (0.50 \times 10) + (0.20 \times -10) = 18 + 5 - 2 = \mathbf{21}$$
- **Strategy S3:**
  $$E(S3) = (0.30 \times 40) + (0.50 \times 30) + (0.20 \times -20) = 12 + 15 - 4 = \mathbf{23}$$
- **Strategy S4:**
  $$E(S4) = (0.30 \times 50) + (0.50 \times 15) + (0.20 \times -15) = 15 + 7.5 - 3 = \mathbf{19.5}$$

Strategy **S1** has the highest expected value ($26$), corresponding to choice **a)**.
