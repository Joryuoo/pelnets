d) 25
### Explanation
In First-Come, First-Served (FCFS) scheduling, processes execute non-preemptively in the order of their arrival. Since all processes arrive at $t = 0$, each process's turnaround time equals its completion time:

- **P1:** Finishes at $14\text{ ms}$ (Turnaround = $14$)
- **P2:** Finishes at $14 + 5 = 19\text{ ms}$ (Turnaround = $19$)
- **P3:** Finishes at $19 + 6 = 25\text{ ms}$ (Turnaround = $25$)
- **P4:** Finishes at $25 + 5 = 30\text{ ms}$ (Turnaround = $30$)
- **P5:** Finishes at $30 + 7 = 37\text{ ms}$ (Turnaround = $37$)

Calculate the average turnaround time across the five processes:
$$\text{Average Turnaround Time} = \frac{14 + 19 + 25 + 30 + 37}{5} = \frac{125}{5} = 25\text{ ms}$$
