# 2018S_FE_AM_70
Which of the following is the basic strategy of a niche in a competitive position?

a) A differentiation strategy necessary for a leader attack, such as a share pursuit
b) An imitation strategy that quickly responds to market opportunities
c) An omni-directional strategy that covers the entire market and secures the highest share
d) A specialization strategy that aims at the specialization of the product and market
?
d) A specialization strategy that aims at the specialization of the product and market
### Explanation
In competitive strategy, a "niche" strategy involves focusing on a narrow, specific segment of the market (a niche) rather than targeting the broader market. It relies on specialization, either by product or by specific market needs, to dominate a specialized market segment where larger competitors (like market leaders) might not be paying much attention.
