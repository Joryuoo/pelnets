### Explanation
The diagram shows the BCG (Boston Consulting Group) matrix (also known as the growth-share matrix). It plots the market share (strength of business) against the market growth rate (attractiveness of business). This framework helps companies analyze their product lines or business units to evaluate their strategic position, set objectives, and prioritize the allocation of resources appropriately across different business units (e.g., Stars, Cash Cows, Question Marks, and Dogs).
