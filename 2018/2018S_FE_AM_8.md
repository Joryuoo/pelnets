?
b) Quotient of x ÷ y / Remainder of x ÷ y
### Explanation
The flowchart represents the algorithm for division by repeated subtraction.
- $t$ is initialized to $x$ and $s$ is initialized to $0$.
- In the loop, as long as $t \ge y$ (NO branch of $t < y$), the algorithm subtracts $y$ from $t$ ($t \leftarrow t - y$) and increments the counter $s$ by 1 ($s \leftarrow s + 1$).
- When $t < y$, the loop terminates.
- At termination, $s$ contains the number of times $y$ can be subtracted from $x$, which is the **Quotient of $x \div y$**.
- $t$ contains the remaining amount after all possible $y$'s are subtracted, which is the **Remainder of $x \div y$**.
