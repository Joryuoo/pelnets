# 2018S_FE_AM_12
What is the average rotational latency of a hard disk drive in milliseconds when its rotation speed is 2000 rotations per minute?

a) 10
b) 15
c) 25
d) 30
?
b) 15
### Explanation
*   **Rotations per minute (RPM):** 2000 rotations / 60 seconds
*   **Time for one full rotation:** 60 seconds / 2000 rotations = 0.030 seconds = 30 milliseconds.
*   **Average rotational latency:** The average time is half of the time it takes for a full rotation (since on average, the disk needs to rotate a half-turn to reach the desired sector).
*   **Calculation:** 30 ms / 2 = 15 milliseconds.
