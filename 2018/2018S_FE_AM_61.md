# 2018S_FE_AM_61
Which of the following is a technique that is used to perform the optimum resource allocation for a computerization investment in each category classified on the basis of the similarities of risks and the investment value?

a) 3C analysis
b) Benchmarking
c) Enterprise architecture
d) IT portfolio
?
d) IT portfolio
### Explanation
An IT portfolio is a technique used to perform optimal resource allocation for IT and computerization investments. Similar to a financial portfolio, it classifies IT investments based on their risk and return (or investment value) to ensure a balanced and effective investment strategy across the enterprise.
