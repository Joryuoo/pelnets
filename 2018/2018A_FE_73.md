### Explanation
In a factory IoT maintenance system architecture:
- **A (Data collection):** Handled by sensors that monitor physical equipment conditions, such as a **temperature sensor for equipment** (c).
- **B (Data transmission):** Handled by network infrastructure, such as **wireless communication within factory** (d).
- **C (Data analysis):** Handled by automated analytical and decision software, such as an **abnormal value judgment tool** (a).
- **D (Data utilization):** Handled by human-machine interfaces and reporting tools, such as a **display for work instructions** (b).

Therefore, basic element **A** corresponds to choice **c)**.
