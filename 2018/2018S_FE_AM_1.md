# 2018S_FE_AM_1
There is a register where an integer value is stored in binary. Let x be a positive integer which is stored into the register, then perform the operations below.
(1) Shift the content of the register to the left by 2 bits
(2) Add x to the register.

How many times larger than the integer x is the content of the register?

a) 3
b) 4
c) 5
d) 6
?
c) 5
### Explanation
1. Shifting the binary content of the register to the left by 2 bits is equivalent to multiplying the value by $2^2$ (which is 4). Thus, the register now holds $4x$.
2. Adding $x$ to the current value in the register gives $4x + x = 5x$.

Therefore, the content of the register becomes 5 times the original integer $x$.
