# 2018A_FE_AM_16
![[2018A_FE_AM_Q16_p1.png]]

a) $ab^3c^2$
b) $a(1 − b^3)(1 − c^2)$
c) $a(1 − b)^3(1 − c)^2$
d) $a(1 − (1 − b)^3)(1 − (1 − c)^2)$
?
d) $a(1 − (1 − b)^3)(1 − (1 − c)^2)$
### Explanation
The system is considered available when the server, at least one of the three clients, and at least one of the two printers are operational simultaneously:

1. **Server:** Availability is $a$.
2. **Clients:** The probability that all 3 independent clients fail is $(1 - b)^3$. Thus, the probability that at least one client is running is $1 - (1 - b)^3$.
3. **Printers:** The probability that both independent printers fail is $(1 - c)^2$. Thus, the probability that at least one printer is running is $1 - (1 - c)^2$.
4. **LAN:** Availability is $1$ (always operational).

Multiplying the availabilities of these independent subsystems in series yields:
$$\text{Total Availability} = a(1 - (1 - b)^3)(1 - (1 - c)^2)$$
