# 2018A_FE_AM_75
![[2018A_FE_AM_Q75_p1.png]]

a) Agree to and participate in the affiliate program
b) Click the advertisement on the website
c) Pay a contingency fee in accordance with the sales of the product
d) Purchase the desired product
?
c) Pay a contingency fee in accordance with the sales of the product
### Explanation
In an affiliate marketing program model:
- **(1):** The website operator registers and agrees to participate in the affiliate program run by the EC site (advertiser) (choice a).
- **(2):** A web site viewer browsing the operator's site clicks on the product advertisement (choice b).
- **(3):** The viewer is redirected via the affiliate link to the product sales page.
- **(4):** The viewer completes a purchase of the desired product on the EC site (choice d).
- **(5):** The EC site (advertiser) pays a contingency (commission) fee to the website operator based on the sales generated (choice c).

Thus, step **(5)** corresponds to choice **c)**.
