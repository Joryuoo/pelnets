d) 260,000
### Explanation
To find the required sales revenue, we can perform a Cost-Volume-Profit (CVP) analysis:

1. **Calculate the Unit Contribution Margin:**
   Unit Contribution Margin = Selling Price - Variable Cost
   Unit Contribution Margin = $40 - $28 = $12 per unit

2. **Calculate the required number of units to sell:**
   Required Units = (Fixed Cost + Target Profit) / Unit Contribution Margin
   Required Units = ($42,000 + $36,000) / $12 = $78,000 / $12 = 6,500 units

3. **Calculate the Total Sales Revenue:**
   Total Sales Revenue = Required Units * Selling Price
   Total Sales Revenue = 6,500 units * $40 = $260,000
