# 2018S_FE_AM_67
Which of the following is an explanation of the digital divide?

a) It refers to an economical or social gap that occurs because of differences in abilities and opportunities when using computers and communication networks.
b) It refers to communications and broadcast services that can be used equally by any citizen with reasonable charges, without any difference between regions.
c) It refers to citizens participating directly in government policies and autonomous bodies through the use of the Internet.
d) It refers to the provision of all services from a single place for each event of the daily life of citizens or each field of corporate activity.
?
a) It refers to an economical or social gap that occurs because of differences in abilities and opportunities when using computers and communication networks.
### Explanation
The "digital divide" is a term that describes the uneven distribution of information and communication technologies (ICTs) in society. It refers to the social and economic inequality between those who have easy access to digital technology and those who do not, often based on geographical, demographic, or socioeconomic differences.
