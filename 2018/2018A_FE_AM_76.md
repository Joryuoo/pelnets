# 2018A_FE_AM_76
Which of the following is a part of total quality management?

a) Designing products and services that meet or exceed customers’ expectations
b) Focusing on the appointment of staff with long-term work experience in a similar environment
c) Prioritizing central decisions rather than empowerment for ensuring quality of products and services
d) Promoting the capability of each department to work independently in a competitive manner
?
a) Designing products and services that meet or exceed customers’ expectations
### Explanation
**Total Quality Management (TQM)** is an organization-wide management approach centered on quality, continuous improvement, and the participation of all members to achieve customer satisfaction. A core tenet of TQM is customer focus—designing and delivering products and services that satisfy and exceed customer expectations.

In contrast:
- **b)** TQM emphasizes comprehensive training and continuous education across the entire organization, not just hiring based on long past tenure.
- **c)** TQM relies heavily on employee empowerment and decentralized problem-solving rather than centralized control.
- **d)** TQM encourages cross-functional teamwork and total optimization across departments, rather than internal competition between silos.
