# 2018S_FE_AM_25
Which of the following is a process in PCM to capture analog values from audio signals at a regular interval?

a) Encoding
b) Reverse quantization
c) Quantization
d) Sampling
?
d) Sampling
### Explanation
PCM (Pulse Code Modulation) converts analog audio signals into digital data through three main consecutive steps:
1. **Sampling:** Capturing the continuous analog signal at regular discrete time intervals.
2. **Quantization:** Converting the sampled amplitude values into discrete levels.
3. **Encoding:** Converting the quantized discrete levels into binary digital data.

Therefore, the process of capturing analog values at a regular interval is called sampling.
