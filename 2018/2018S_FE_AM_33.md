# 2018S_FE_AM_33
Which of the following is a pair of IP addresses belonging to the same subnet, when the subnet mask is 255.255.255.240?

a) 192.168.1.14 and 192.168.1.17
b) 192.168.1.17 and 192.168.1.29
c) 192.168.1.29 and 192.168.1.33
d) 192.168.1.33 and 192.168.1.49
?
b) 192.168.1.17 and 192.168.1.29
### Explanation
A subnet mask of 255.255.255.240 (/28) means the last octet is divided into subnets of 16 addresses each (since 256 - 240 = 16). 
The subnets start at multiples of 16:
- Subnet 0: 0 to 15
- Subnet 1: 16 to 31
- Subnet 2: 32 to 47
- Subnet 3: 48 to 63

Let's evaluate the options:
- a) 14 is in Subnet 0 (0-15), 17 is in Subnet 1 (16-31). (Different subnets)
- b) 17 is in Subnet 1 (16-31), 29 is in Subnet 1 (16-31). (Same subnet)
- c) 29 is in Subnet 1 (16-31), 33 is in Subnet 2 (32-47). (Different subnets)
- d) 33 is in Subnet 2 (32-47), 49 is in Subnet 3 (48-63). (Different subnets)
