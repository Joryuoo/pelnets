### Explanation
MRP (Material Requirements Planning) is a production planning and inventory control system. To calculate the net requirements for materials, it is necessary to subtract the current inventory on hand and scheduled receipts from the gross requirements. Therefore, the "Inventory status", which includes the inventory amount, residual orders, and in-process quantity, is essential information needed to be inserted into step A.
