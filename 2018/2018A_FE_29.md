a) Projection and selection
### Explanation
After performing a natural join between relations $X$ and $Y$ on `FacultyCode`, the resulting relation contains attributes: $(\text{StudentNumber}, \text{Name}, \text{FacultyCode}, \text{FacultyName})$.

To produce relation $Z$:
1. **Selection ($\sigma$):** Filters rows where $\text{FacultyName} = \text{'Information'}$, keeping only records for StudentNumbers 2 and 4.
2. **Projection ($\pi$):** Chooses specific columns $(\text{FacultyName}, \text{StudentNumber}, \text{Name})$, omitting the `FacultyCode` column.

Therefore, the required relational algebra operations are **projection and selection**, corresponding to choice **a)**.
