c) 775
### Explanation
1. Determine the data recording rate per second:
   $$\text{Rate per second} = 11{,}000 \text{ samples/s} \times 8 \text{ bits/sample} = 11{,}000 \text{ bytes/s}$$

2. Determine the data recording rate per minute:
   $$\text{Rate per minute} = 11{,}000 \text{ bytes/s} \times 60 \text{ s/min} = 660{,}000 \text{ bytes/min}$$

3. Calculate the maximum recording time in minutes:
   $$\text{Total minutes} = \frac{512 \times 10^6 \text{ bytes}}{660{,}000 \text{ bytes/min}} \approx 775.76 \text{ minutes}$$

Rounding down to the nearest whole minute gives **775** minutes.
