# 2018A_FE_69
Which of the following is a leader strategy among the competitive strategies of a company?

a) Concentrating limited management resources in a specialized market into which other companies do not enter despite the presence of potential demand
b) Employing a differentiation strategy from various viewpoints such as products, services, sales promotion, and distribution channels, with the aim of seizing the market share
c) Employing an omnidirectional strategy targeting all market customers with the aim of maintaining or improving profit and reputation, and securing optimum market share
d) Suppressing the cost of development and advertising, and aiming for continued existence in the market by observing the strategies of target companies and simulating them promptly
?
c) Employing an omnidirectional strategy targeting all market customers with the aim of maintaining or improving profit and reputation, and securing optimum market share
### Explanation
Kotler’s competitive positioning identifies four strategic postures:
- **Leader (c):** Holds the largest market share; deploys an omnidirectional (full-line) strategy across all market segments to expand total demand and protect its dominant market position.
- **Challenger (b):** Competes directly or indirectly against the leader using targeted differentiation strategies to gain market share.
- **Follower (d):** Mimics the leader’s successful products rapidly with low R&D and marketing expenditures to remain profitable.
- **Nicher (a):** Focuses specialized resources on small, overlooked market niches with limited competition.
