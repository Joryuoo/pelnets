# 2018S_FE_AM_38
Which of the following is the name of an attack where manipulation is made to place a malicious website near the top of the results of a search website?

a) Cross-site scripting
b) DNS cache poisoning
c) SEO poisoning
d) Social engineering
?
c) SEO poisoning
### Explanation
SEO (Search Engine Optimization) poisoning is a technique where attackers use deceptive SEO tactics to rank malicious websites high in search engine results. When users search for popular terms and click on the top results, they are directed to a site that may distribute malware or conduct phishing.
