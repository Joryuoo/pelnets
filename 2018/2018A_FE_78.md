# 2018A_FE_78
There are several quality control tools that have been used primarily for manufacturing and inspection departments. Which of the following sets two corresponding types of data on the vertical and horizontal axes, after which the measured values are plotted?

a) Control chart
b) Histogram
c) Pareto chart
d) Scatter diagram
?
d) Scatter diagram
### Explanation
A **scatter diagram** (scatter plot) plots paired numerical data with one variable on the horizontal axis ($x$) and another on the vertical axis ($y$) to examine relationships, correlations, and trends between two sets of data.

Other QC tools:
- **a) Control chart:** Plots sample statistics over time against calculated upper and lower control limits to monitor process stability.
- **b) Histogram:** Shows the frequency distribution of continuous data across grouped intervals (bins).
- **c) Pareto chart:** Combines descending vertical bars of category frequencies with a cumulative percentage curve to highlight the "vital few" causes.
