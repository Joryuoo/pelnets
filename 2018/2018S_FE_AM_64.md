# 2018S_FE_AM_64
Which of the following is a characteristic of hosting service?

a) A usage method, such as a high-performance server of a service provider being exclusively used by a single user, is not supported.
b) In terms of operations management, all activities, including the monitoring of server operations, incident handling, etc. are addressed by users.
c) The utilization right of a server prepared by a service provider is lent out to users.
d) Users freely select and combine servers by providing their own servers to the service provider facility.
?
c) The utilization right of a server prepared by a service provider is lent out to users.
### Explanation
A hosting service is a type of service where a service provider provides the physical servers and infrastructure, and users rent or lease the right to use them. Choice A is incorrect because dedicated hosting allows exclusive use of a high-performance server. Choice B is incorrect as providers typically handle basic monitoring and incident response for the hardware. Choice D describes a colocation service, not a typical hosting service.
