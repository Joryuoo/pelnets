# 2018S_FE_AM_73
Which of the following is a management organization that creates a cross-functional team between project and functional organizations in order to flexibly respond to changes in the business environment?

a) Divisional organization
b) Line and staff organization
c) Matrix organization
d) Project organization
?
c) Matrix organization
### Explanation
A matrix organization combines two or more types of organizational structures, usually functional and project management structures. This setup creates cross-functional teams where employees report to both a functional manager (for their specific skill or department) and a project manager. This dual-reporting structure allows the organization to flexibly deploy resources and respond quickly to changes in the business environment.
