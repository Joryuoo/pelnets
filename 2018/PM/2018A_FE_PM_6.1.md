### Explanation
**Blank A:** The first loop initializes the total weight of each person (stored in array `T`) to `0` before starting to add the weights of their cakes. Thus, `T[I]` is initialized to `0`. The answer is `a`.

**Blank B:** The second loop calculates the total weight of the cakes belonging to each person. For each cake `I` (from `1` to `M`), it adds the weight of the cake `W[I]` to the total weight of the person who received it, `T[P[I]]`. Thus, the correct assignment is `T[P[I]] <- T[P[I]] + W[I]`. The answer is `c`.

**Blank C (C1 and C2):** Equation (I') states: `Tx - Ty > x - y > 0` AND `Tx - Ty > x - y`. Breaking this down into the code's variables where `Tx = T[P[I]]`, `Ty = T[P[J]]`, `x = W[I]`, and `y = W[J]`:
The conditions are:
1. `T[P[I]] > T[P[J]]` (i.e., `Tx > Ty`)
2. `W[I] > W[J]` (i.e., `x > y > 0`)
3. `(T[P[I]] - T[P[J]]) > (W[I] - W[J])` (i.e., `Tx - Ty > x - y`)
Since all these conditions must be met simultaneously for equation (I') to hold, both `C1` and `C2` must be logical `and` operators. Thus, the answer is `a`.
