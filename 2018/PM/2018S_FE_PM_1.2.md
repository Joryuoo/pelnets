### Explanation
During password verification at logon:
- **(2) Plaintext Password:** The user submits their UserID and plaintext password.
- **(1) Encryption Algorithm:** The system retrieves the plaintext salt from the password file using the UserID. The salt and the user-supplied plaintext password are fed into the modified DES **Encryption Algorithm**.
- **(3) Comparison:** A **Comparison** is performed between the resulting hash and the stored hashed password from the password file. If they match, access is granted.

Thus, (1) is Encryption Algorithm, (2) is Plaintext Password, and (3) is Comparison, matching option **d**.
