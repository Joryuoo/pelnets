### Explanation
**Blank A**: Represents the relationship between a `Member` and a `User`. Since a Member is a specific type of User (who has registered), this is an inheritance or **generalization** relationship, which is denoted by a solid line with a hollow arrow pointing from the child (`Member`) to the parent (`User`). Thus, the answer is `d`.

**Blank B**: Represents the relationship between making a reservation and checking available rooms. When a user makes a reservation, they may or may not need to check available rooms separately if they already know what they want, or checking rooms is an optional extension of the standard process flow depending on the diagram's explicit rules. In standard UML, `<<extend>>` indicates optional or conditional behavior. Thus, the answer is `a`.

**Blank C**: Represents the relationship between making a reservation and logging in. Since a user *must* log in before making a reservation (as per the prompt: "a member first logs in to the system"), the `Make Reservation` use case definitively incorporates the `Login` use case. This mandatory relationship is represented by `<<include>>`. Thus, the answer is `b`.
