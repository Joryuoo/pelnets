### Explanation

**Blank G:** `f` (`right side -> seven five eight minus`)
In `Tester.main`, since $n = -857$ is not greater than 0, the `if (n > 0)` block (which runs `LeftToRightIterator`) is skipped. Only `RightToLeftIterator` executes, printing `in words from right side ->`.

The words array for $-857$ is `["minus", "eight", "five", "seven"]`. Traversing this array from right to left (index 3 to 0) yields `"seven"`, `"five"`, `"eight"`, and `"minus"`, resulting in `right side -> seven five eight minus`.
