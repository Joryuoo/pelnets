### Explanation
- **b (The hashed password may be used as the primary key):** The 12-bit salt ensures that even if users choose the identical password, their hashed values differ because the salt values are assigned at different times. Consequently, each record contains a unique hashed password, allowing it to satisfy uniqueness and potentially serve as a primary key.
- **Incorrect options:**
  - Password hashing is a one-way irreversible process, so neither users nor administrators can retrieve or recover forgotten plaintext passwords (**a** and **e** are false).
  - UserID uniquely identifies each account and is indeed a candidate key (**f** is false).
