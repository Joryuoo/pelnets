### Explanation
- **A (authorized):** Authentication verifies the identity of an individual to determine whether they are **authorized** to access the system.
- **B (privileged user):** Users given elevated attributes and access rights to manage protected operating system functions and files are known as **privileged users** (e.g., administrators or superusers).
- **C ($2^{12}$):** The modified DES algorithm employs a 12-bit salt. A 12-bit value provides $2^{12}$ (4,096) possible permutations, thereby expanding the search space of possible password hashes by a factor of **$2^{12}$** and increasing resistance to brute-force and dictionary attacks.
