# 2018S_FE_PM_2.3

![[2018S_FE_PM_Q2_SQ3_p1.png]]
?
c

### Explanation
Given parameters: $T_m = 40\text{ ns}$, $T_h = 4\text{ ns}$, $E = 10\text{ ns}$.
- Hit access time: $T_{\text{hit}} = T_h + E = 4 + 10 = 14\text{ ns}$.
- Miss access time: $T_{\text{miss}} = T_m + T_h + E = 40 + 4 + 10 = 54\text{ ns}$.

Trace the sequence of 6 page references (capacity = 4 frames, initially empty):
1. **2:** Miss (loaded into cache)
2. **1:** Miss (loaded into cache)
3. **3:** Miss (loaded into cache)
4. **0:** Miss (loaded into cache; frames now full: {2, 1, 3, 0})
5. **2:** Hit (already in cache)
6. **1:** Hit (already in cache)

Result: 4 misses and 2 hits.
$$\text{Total Time} = (4 \times 54\text{ ns}) + (2 \times 14\text{ ns}) = 216 + 28 = 244\text{ ns}$$

Thus, the correct answer is **c** (244 ns).
