### Explanation
**Blank E:** The inner subquery creates a derived table (aliased as `LC`) that calculates the labor costs for *each individual staff member* working on a particular job. The labor cost for a single staff member is their hourly rate multiplied by their working hours (`WorkingHours * S.Rate`). Thus, the answer is `e`.

**Blank D:** The outer query aggregates the results from the `LC` derived table, grouping by `JobID`. To find the total labor costs for the entire job, we must sum up the individual `LaborCosts` calculated in the inner query for all staff members assigned to that job. We use the aggregate function `SUM(LC.LaborCosts)`. Thus, the answer is `b`.
