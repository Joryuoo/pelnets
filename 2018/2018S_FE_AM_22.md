# 2018S_FE_AM_22
![[2018S_FE_AM_Q22_p1.png]]

a) Exclusive logical sum (XOR) and Logical product (AND)
b) Logical product (AND) and Logical sum (OR)
c) Negative logical product (NAND) and Negative logical sum (NOR)
d) Negative logical sum (NOR) and Exclusive logical sum (XOR)
?
a) Exclusive logical sum (XOR) and Logical product (AND)
### Explanation
A half adder adds two 1-bit binary numbers `x` and `y`, and produces a sum `z` and a carry bit `c`.
The logic boolean equations for a half adder are:
- Sum (z) = x XOR y
- Carry (c) = x AND y

Therefore, gate A which produces the sum `z` must be an Exclusive OR (XOR) gate, while gate B which produces the carry `c` must be an AND gate.
