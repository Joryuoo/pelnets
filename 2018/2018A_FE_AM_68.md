# 2018A_FE_AM_68
In SWOT analysis, which of the following is generally positioned as a threat?

a) Entry of strong companies in the market
b) High growth rate of business domain
c) High production efficiency as compared with other competitors
d) Low market share
?
a) Entry of strong companies in the market
### Explanation
**SWOT analysis** evaluates internal and external strategic factors:
- **Threat (External, Unfavorable):** Environmental risks outside the organization's direct control, such as the **entry of strong competitors into the market** (choice a).
- **Opportunity (External, Favorable):** Positive market trends, such as a **high growth rate of the business domain** (choice b).
- **Strength (Internal, Favorable):** Core organizational competencies, such as **high production efficiency** (choice c).
- **Weakness (Internal, Unfavorable):** Internal deficiencies, such as **low market share** (choice d).
