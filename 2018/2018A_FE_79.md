# 2018A_FE_79
![[2018A_FE_AM_Q79_full.png]]

a) 3.0
b) 3.2
c) 3.5
d) 4.0
?
b) 3.2
### Explanation
The payback period is the time required for cumulative cash inflows to equal the initial investment of $100 million:

- **End of Year 1:** Cumulative cash flow = $\$20\text{M}$ (Remaining: $\$80\text{M}$)
- **End of Year 2:** Cumulative cash flow = $\$20\text{M} + \$30\text{M} = \$50\text{M}$ (Remaining: $\$50\text{M}$)
- **End of Year 3:** Cumulative cash flow = $\$50\text{M} + \$40\text{M} = \$90\text{M}$ (Remaining: $\$10\text{M}$)

At the end of Year 3, $\$10\text{M}$ remains to be recovered.
In Year 4, the expected cash inflow is $\$50\text{M}$. Assuming cash flow occurs evenly throughout the year:
$$\text{Fraction of Year 4} = \frac{\text{Unrecovered balance}}{\text{Year 4 cash flow}} = \frac{\$10\text{M}}{\$50\text{M}} = 0.2\text{ years}$$

$$\text{Payback Period} = 3 + 0.2 = \mathbf{3.2}\text{ years}$$

This corresponds to choice **b)**.
