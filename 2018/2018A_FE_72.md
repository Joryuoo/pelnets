# 2018A_FE_72

a) Purchasing samples and modifying them in-house
b) Outsourcing in batch
c) Modifying in-house assets
d) Creating new products in-house
?
a) Purchasing samples and modifying them in-house
### Explanation
Calculate the total cost (Purchase + Outsourcing + Labor) for each development method:
- Creation process: 6 months at $6,000/person-month
- Modification process: 3 months at $10,000/person-month
- Evaluation process: 2 months at $10,000/person-month

1. **Method a:**
   $$\text{Cost} = \$200,000 + (4 \times 3 \times \$10,000) + (1 \times 2 \times \$10,000) = \$200,000 + \$120,000 + \$20,000 = \mathbf{\$340,000}$$
2. **Method b:**
   $$\text{Cost} = \mathbf{\$350,000} \text{ (outsourcing)}$$
3. **Method c:**
   $$\text{Cost} = (10 \times 3 \times \$10,000) + (3 \times 2 \times \$10,000) = \$300,000 + \$60,000 = \mathbf{\$360,000}$$
4. **Method d:**
   $$\text{Cost} = (10 \times 6 \times \$6,000) + (2 \times 2 \times \$10,000) = \$360,000 + \$40,000 = \mathbf{\$400,000}$$

Method a has the lowest overall cost ($340,000).
