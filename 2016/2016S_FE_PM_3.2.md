### Explanation

**Subquestion 2:**
- **Blank C (d - NOT IN):** To find drivers who are available, we must exclude those who are already assigned to conflicting trips. Therefore, we check that the `DriverID` is `NOT IN` the list of drivers who have overlapping trips.
- **Blanks D (c) and E (d):** The customer wants a new booking from `2016-05-07` to `2016-05-09`. After finishing a trip, a driver takes the next day off. Therefore, the driver is effectively busy for this new trip from `2016-05-07` through `2016-05-10`. 
  An existing trip (from `t.StartDate` to `t.EndDate`, with a day off on `t.EndDate + 1`) conflicts with the new trip if their busy periods overlap. 
  The existing trip's busy period is `[t.StartDate, t.EndDate + 1]`. The overlap condition is:
  `t.StartDate <= '2016-05-10'` AND `t.EndDate + 1 >= '2016-05-07'` (which simplifies to `t.EndDate >= '2016-05-06'`).
  Translating these to strict inequalities as given in the choices:
  - `t.StartDate < '2016-05-11'` (Blank D is **c**)
  - `t.EndDate > '2016-05-05'` (Blank E is **d**)
- **Blank F (c):** We only consider conflicting trips that are actually scheduled or finished. We must ignore canceled trips, so we use `TripStatus <> 3`.
