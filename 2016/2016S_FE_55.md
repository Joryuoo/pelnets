# 2016S_FE_55
Which of the following is an action that is carried out during the work breakdown structure (WBS) creation process in project scope management?

a) Defining activities for creating deliverables
b) Estimating the man-hours of the work and then estimating costs
c) Ordering the work into a schedule
d) Subdividing the work hierarchically
?
d) Subdividing the work hierarchically
### Explanation
In project management (PMBOK Guide), the **Create WBS** process falls under **Project Scope Management**.
- **Subdividing the work hierarchically (d)**: The primary purpose of Create WBS is decomposition—subdividing project deliverables and project work into smaller, more manageable components hierarchically, down to the work package level.

Analysis of other choices:
- **Defining activities for creating deliverables (a)**: This is part of the **Define Activities** process under Project Schedule Management, where work packages are further decomposed into individual schedule activities.
- **Estimating the man-hours of the work and then estimating costs (b)**: Pertains to **Estimate Activity Durations** (Schedule Management) and **Estimate Costs** (Cost Management).
- **Ordering the work into a schedule (c)**: Pertains to **Sequence Activities** and **Develop Schedule** under Project Schedule Management.
