# 2016A_FE_69
Which of the following is an explanation of knowledge management?

a) Management centered on strengths that are a challenge for other companies to imitate, such as the enterprise’s own know-how and technology
b) Management innovation driven by performing comparative analysis with other successful companies
c) Management which improves the overall problem-solving capability by sharing knowledge that is scattered across the enterprise
d) Management which maintains a flat organization structure with minimal hierarchical layers to accelerate decision-making
?
c) Management which improves the overall problem-solving capability by sharing knowledge that is scattered across the enterprise
### Explanation
**Knowledge Management (KM)** is the systematic discipline of identifying, capturing, organizing, sharing, and leveraging the collective knowledge and experience within an enterprise. By converting individual, tacit knowledge (personal experience and intuition) into shared, explicit knowledge (documentation, best practices, repositories), the organization elevates its collective problem-solving capability, innovation, and operational performance.

* **a)** Describes **Core Competence Management**, which focuses on cultivating and protecting distinct, hard-to-imitate capabilities that provide competitive advantage.
* **b)** Describes **Benchmarking**, an improvement process that compares business processes and performance metrics to industry bests or best practices from other companies.
* **c)** **Correct.** This accurately defines the purpose and mechanism of **Knowledge Management**.
* **d)** Describes a **Flat (Horizontal) Organization**, which minimizes administrative layers to foster rapid communication and decision-making.
