# 2016A_FE_76
Which of the following can secure employment and create employment opportunities in an entire society by shortening the work hours per employee?

a) Cafeteria plan
b) Free agent system
c) Work sharing
d) Work-life balance
?
c) Work sharing
### Explanation
**Work sharing** (or job sharing) is an employment strategy where total work hours are distributed among a larger number of workers by reducing the standard working hours per employee. By lowering individual hours (and corresponding wages), organizations can avoid layoffs during economic recessions and expand employment opportunities to more people across society.

* **a) Cafeteria plan** is a flexible employee benefits scheme where employees receive a fixed budget or points to choose their preferred fringe benefits from a menu of options.
* **b) Free agent system** originates in professional sports and human resources, allowing experienced individuals to negotiate and transfer contracts independently to any organization.
* **d) Work-life balance** is a concept supporting an appropriate balance between an individual's career and personal/family life.
