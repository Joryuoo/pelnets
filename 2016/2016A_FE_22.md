c) $X = A \cdot \overline{B} + \overline{A} \cdot B$
### Explanation
Let's analyze the digital circuit step by step from left to right:

1. **Inverters (NOT Gates):**
   - The top inverter receives input $A$ and produces $\overline{A}$.
   - The bottom inverter receives input $B$ and produces $\overline{B}$.

2. **Middle OR Gate:**
   - Inputs are $\overline{A}$ and $\overline{B}$.
   - Output = $\overline{A} + \overline{B}$
   - *(Note: By De Morgan's law, $\overline{A} + \overline{B} = \overline{A \cdot B}$)*.

3. **Upper AND Gate:**
   - First input is taken directly from line $A$.
   - Second input is the output from the middle OR gate $(\overline{A} + \overline{B})$.
   - Output:
     $$A \cdot (\overline{A} + \overline{B}) = (A \cdot \overline{A}) + (A \cdot \overline{B}) = 0 + A \cdot \overline{B} = A \cdot \overline{B}$$

4. **Lower AND Gate:**
   - First input is the output from the middle OR gate $(\overline{A} + \overline{B})$.
   - Second input is taken directly from line $B$.
   - Output:
     $$B \cdot (\overline{A} + \overline{B}) = (B \cdot \overline{A}) + (B \cdot \overline{B}) = \overline{A} \cdot B + 0 = \overline{A} \cdot B$$

5. **Final OR Gate (Output $X$):**
   - Inputs are the outputs of the upper AND gate ($A \cdot \overline{B}$) and the lower AND gate ($\overline{A} \cdot B$).
   - Output $X$:
     $$X = A \cdot \overline{B} + \overline{A} \cdot B$$

This is the standard sum-of-products expression for the XOR (Exclusive OR) function, which corresponds to option **c)**.
