### Explanation
The **critical path** is the longest sequence of dependent activities from project start to finish, which determines the shortest possible project completion time.

From the table, the activities, durations (weeks), and their immediate predecessors are:
* **1: Collection requirements** (3 weeks) – None
* **2: Analyze process** (2 weeks) – Predecessor: 1
* **3: Analyze data** (2 weeks) – Predecessor: 2
* **4: Design Process** (6 weeks) – Predecessor: 2
* **5: Design data** (3 weeks) – Predecessor: 3
* **6: Design screens** (2 weeks) – Predecessors: 3, 4
* **7: Design reports** (4 weeks) – Predecessors: 4, 5
* **8: Programming** (5 weeks) – Predecessors: 6, 7
* **9: Test and Documentation** (7 weeks) – Predecessor: 7
* **10: Install** (2 weeks) – Predecessors: 8, 9

Now let's calculate the total duration for each of the given paths:
* **Path a) 1-2-3-5-7-9-10:**
  $$3 + 2 + 2 + 3 + 4 + 7 + 2 = 23 \text{ weeks}$$
* **Path b) 1-2-3-5-7-8-10:**
  $$3 + 2 + 2 + 3 + 4 + 5 + 2 = 21 \text{ weeks}$$
* **Path c) 1-2-4-7-9-10:**
  $$3 + 2 + 6 + 4 + 7 + 2 = 24 \text{ weeks}$$
* **Path d) 1-2-4-6-8-10:**
  $$3 + 2 + 6 + 2 + 5 + 2 = 20 \text{ weeks}$$

Since **Path c (1-2-4-7-9-10)** has the longest total duration of **24 weeks**, it is the **critical path**.
