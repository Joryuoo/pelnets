# 2016S_FE_43

Which of the following is an appropriate description of risk assessment?

a) As the purpose of risk assessment is to prevent a future loss, referencing of data used in the past risk assessment is avoided.
b) Risk assessment is implemented after a risk is identified and the budget for responding to the risk is decided according to the amount of loss.
c) The implementation of risk assessment is avoided before the response to all risks identified beforehand is complete.
d) The priority order of the response to risks is determined by the size of the risk based on the expected amount of loss and occurrence probability.
?
d) The priority order of the response to risks is determined by the size of the risk based on the expected amount of loss and occurrence probability.

### Explanation

**Risk assessment** (as standardized in ISO/IEC 27005 and ISO 31000) is the overall process encompassing:
1. **Risk Identification**: Finding, recognizing, and describing risks.
2. **Risk Analysis**: Understanding the nature, sources, and causes of risks, and estimating the risk level. Risk level is fundamentally determined by:
   $$\text{Risk Level} = \text{Occurrence Probability (Likelihood)} \times \text{Expected Loss (Impact)}$$
3. **Risk Evaluation**: Comparing the estimated risk levels against defined risk criteria to determine the significance of risks and establish the **priority order** of risk treatment.

Therefore, statement **d** correctly describes the purpose and mechanism of risk evaluation/assessment.

### Option Analysis
- **a)** Past risk assessments, historical incident logs, and vulnerability databases provide crucial empirical data for accurately estimating probability and impact. Referencing past data is highly encouraged, not avoided.
- **b)** The budget and treatment plans for responding to risks are decided *after* risk assessment has evaluated and prioritized the risks, not before.
- **c)** Risk assessment is an ongoing, periodic activity. New threats and vulnerabilities continuously arise, so risk assessments must not be halted simply because previous risk treatment is still underway.
- **d)** **Correct**. Risk magnitude is evaluated based on likelihood and impact, allowing organizations to prioritize mitigation for the most critical risks.
