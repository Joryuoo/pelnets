# 2016S_FE_56
![[2016S_FE_AM_Q56_p1.png]]

a) The project completion date is accelerated by 1 day.
b) The project completion date is delayed by 1 day.
c) The project completion date is delayed by 3 days.
d) The project completion date is neither accelerated nor delayed.
?
d) The project completion date is neither accelerated nor delayed.
### Explanation
Let us analyze the network paths and compare the original plan with the modified plan.

#### 1. Original Plan
Assume the project begins at Day 0:
- **Activity A**: starts at Day 0, duration = 20 days $\rightarrow$ completes on **Day 20**.
From the node following A, the project branches into three possible paths to the finish:
- **Path 1 ($A \rightarrow B \rightarrow F \rightarrow G$)**:
  $$\text{Duration} = 20 + 10 + 35 + 10 = 75 \text{ days}$$
- **Path 2 ($A \rightarrow B \rightarrow D \rightarrow E \rightarrow G$)**:
  $$\text{Duration} = 20 + 10 + 15 + 20 + 10 = 75 \text{ days}$$
- **Path 3 ($A \rightarrow C \rightarrow E \rightarrow G$)**:
  $$\text{Duration} = 20 + 30 + 20 + 10 = 80 \text{ days}$$

The critical path is **Path 3 ($A \rightarrow C \rightarrow E \rightarrow G$)**, so the original project duration is **80 days**.

---

#### 2. Modified Plan
Now calculate earliest completion dates with the specified changes:
1. **Activity A**: Completed on schedule as planned $\rightarrow$ finishes on **Day 20**.
2. **Activity B**: Completed on the **33rd day** after the start of the project $\rightarrow$ finishes on **Day 33**.
3. **Activity C**: Planned duration was 30 days, completed 2 days earlier $\rightarrow$ duration = 28 days.
   - Finishes at: $\text{Day } 20 + 28 = \text{\textbf{Day 48}}$.
4. **Activity D**: Planned duration was 15 days, completed 1 day later $\rightarrow$ duration = 16 days.
   - Starts after B finishes (Day 33).
   - Finishes at: $\text{Day } 33 + 16 = \text{\textbf{Day 49}}$.
5. **Node before Activity E**: Requires both C and D to complete:
   - Earliest start time for E = $\max(\text{Finish of C}, \text{Finish of D}) = \max(48, 49) = \text{\textbf{Day 49}}$.
6. **Activity E**: Planned duration was 20 days, completed 1 day later $\rightarrow$ duration = 21 days.
   - Finishes at: $\text{Day } 49 + 21 = \text{\textbf{Day 70}}$.
7. **Activity F**: Planned duration = 35 days, completed on schedule.
   - Starts after B finishes (Day 33).
   - Finishes at: $\text{Day } 33 + 35 = \text{\textbf{Day 68}}$.
8. **Node before Activity G**: Requires both F and E to complete:
   - Earliest start time for G = $\max(\text{Finish of F}, \text{Finish of E}) = \max(68, 70) = \text{\textbf{Day 70}}$.
9. **Activity G**: Planned duration = 10 days, completed on schedule.
   - Finishes at: $\text{Day } 70 + 10 = \text{\textbf{Day 80}}$.

---

#### Conclusion
- Original completion date: **Day 80**
- Modified completion date: **Day 80**
- Net impact: $80 - 80 = 0$ days.

Therefore, the project completion date is **neither accelerated nor delayed** (choice **d**).
