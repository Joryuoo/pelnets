# 2016S_FE_73

Which of the following is an explanation of an e-marketplace?

a) It is a method in which many sellers and buyers meet in a market set up on the Internet and trade directly without an intermediate distributor.
b) It is a method that aims for overall optimization from material procurement to product distribution by sharing information such as ordering, inventory, sales, and logistics between a company and its business partners.
c) It is a method that converts information concerning business transactions into a standardized format and electronically exchanges data such as estimation, ordering, shipment and delivery, and payment between companies.
d) It is a method that runs a sales promotion campaign on the Internet and then draws customers to a real store to encourage them to make purchases.
?
a) It is a method in which many sellers and buyers meet in a market set up on the Internet and trade directly without an intermediate distributor.

### Explanation

An **e-marketplace** (electronic marketplace) is a web-based platform that brings together multiple buyers and multiple sellers to conduct commercial trade over the Internet. It facilitates direct transactions between participating parties, matching supply and demand while cutting out unnecessary intermediary distribution channels and lowering transaction costs.

### Analysis of Incorrect Options:
- **b)** Optimizing the complete flow of materials, information, and products across trading partners from procurement to end distribution describes **Supply Chain Management (SCM)**.
- **c)** Converting business transaction documents into standardized electronic formats and exchanging them electronically between computers describes **Electronic Data Interchange (EDI)**.
- **d)** Conducting digital marketing campaigns online to attract customers into physical retail stores to make purchases describes **O2O (Online-to-Offline)** commerce.
