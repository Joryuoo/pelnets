# 2016S_FE_62

Among the items to be written into an audit report by a system auditor, which of the following deviates from the scope of work of an auditor?

a) Judging the urgency of improvements
b) Ordering improvements
c) Pointing out improvements
d) Recommending improvements
?
b) Ordering improvements

### Explanation

A **system auditor** functions in an independent, objective advisory role. The purpose of a system audit is to evaluate information systems, assess risks and internal controls, identify defects or weaknesses, and report the findings to executive management and the auditee.

- **Auditor's Scope of Work**:
  - Identifying and **pointing out deficiencies/improvements** (findings).
  - **Judging the urgency and severity** of the identified risks/improvements.
  - Formulating and **recommending corrective measures or advice** for improvement.
- **Why Ordering Improvements Deviates from Scope**:
  - Auditors **do not have executive, management, or operational authority** to order or command staff to implement changes.
  - Issuing directives or operational orders is the sole responsibility of the organization's executive management or relevant department managers.
  - If auditors had the authority to order improvements, their professional independence and objectivity for future audits would be compromised.
