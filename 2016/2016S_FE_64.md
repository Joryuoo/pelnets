# 2016S_FE_64

Which of the following is subject to system audits that evaluate and verify internal control related to IT?

a) The methods for ensuring accuracy in the processes of input and update of the database being done by the sales department
b) The process of creation of a mid-term business plan being led by the business planning department
c) The results of employee personnel evaluations being led by the personnel department
d) The status of production equipment review being conducted by the manufacturing department for the purpose of reducing defects
?
a) The methods for ensuring accuracy in the processes of input and update of the database being done by the sales department

### Explanation

**Internal control related to IT** consists of policies, procedures, and automated controls implemented to ensure that an organization's IT systems operate reliably, securely, and effectively in support of business objectives. It is broadly divided into:

1. **IT General Controls (ITGC)**: Controls that apply to all systems, components, processes, and data (e.g., access control, change management, disaster recovery).
2. **IT Application Controls (ITAC)**: Specific controls within software applications that ensure transactions are properly authorized, complete, accurate, and valid.
   - Ensuring the **accuracy, completeness, and validity of input and database updates** (such as validation checks, duplicate checks, audit logs, and authorization controls performed during sales data entry) is a fundamental application control.
   - Therefore, verifying the methods used to ensure accuracy in database updates is directly subject to system audits evaluating IT internal control.

### Analysis of Other Options:
- **b) Mid-term business plan creation**: This is part of strategic corporate governance and business management, not an IT internal control.
- **c) Employee personnel evaluations**: This is a human resources management function, not subject to IT system audits.
- **d) Production equipment review for defect reduction**: This is a physical manufacturing quality control (QC/QA) process, not an IT system internal control.
