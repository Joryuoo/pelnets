# 2016S_FE_24

![[2016S_FE_AM_Q24_full.png]]

a) $\overline{A \cdot B \cdot C} \cdot C \cdot D$
b) $(\overline{A} + \overline{B}) \cdot \overline{C} \cdot (C + D)$
c) $\overline{A \cdot B} + \overline{C} + D$
d) $\overline{A} \cdot \overline{B} \cdot \overline{C} \cdot D$
?
d) $\overline{A} \cdot \overline{B} \cdot \overline{C} \cdot D$

### Explanation

Let us determine the Boolean expression at each stage of the logic circuit:

1. **Top Gate (NOR Gate):**
   - Inputs: $A$ and $B$
   - Output: $\overline{A + B}$
   - By **De Morgan’s Law**:
     $$\overline{A + B} = \overline{A} \cdot \overline{B}$$

2. **Middle Gate (NOT Gate / Inverter):**
   - Input: $C$
   - Output: $\overline{C}$

3. **Bottom Gate (OR Gate):**
   - Inputs: $C$ and $D$
   - Output: $C + D$

4. **Final Gate (3-Input AND Gate):**
   - Combines the outputs of the three previous gates with logical AND ($\cdot$):
     $$\text{Output} = (\overline{A + B}) \cdot \overline{C} \cdot (C + D)$$

---

#### Simplifying the Expression:

First, distribute $\overline{C}$ into $(C + D)$:
$$\overline{C} \cdot (C + D) = (\overline{C} \cdot C) + (\overline{C} \cdot D)$$

By the Complement Law, $\overline{C} \cdot C = 0$:
$$= 0 + \overline{C} \cdot D = \overline{C} \cdot D$$

Now, substitute back into the overall output expression and apply De Morgan's Law to the top term:
$$\text{Output} = (\overline{A} \cdot \overline{B}) \cdot (\overline{C} \cdot D)$$
$$\text{Output} = \overline{A} \cdot \overline{B} \cdot \overline{C} \cdot D$$

This matches choice **d)** exactly.
