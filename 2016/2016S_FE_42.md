# 2016S_FE_42

When Mr. A, who has a digital certificate, uses an e-mail to place an order with store B, he uses his own private key and digitally signs the e-mail. Store B then uses Mr. A’s public key to confirm the signature. Which of the following can be achieved using this method? Here, only Mr. A can use his private key.

a) It can be confirmed that store B is permitted to sell products to Mr. A.
b) It can be confirmed that the order arriving at store B is an order from Mr. A.
c) The order details sent from Mr. A to store B can be prevented from being leaked to third parties.
d) The order sent from Mr. A can be made to arrive at store B.
?
b) It can be confirmed that the order arriving at store B is an order from Mr. A.

### Explanation

A **digital signature** is a mathematical scheme for demonstrating the authenticity of digital messages or documents.

#### How Digital Signatures Work
1. **Creation**: The sender (Mr. A) generates a hash (message digest) of the email and encrypts that hash using his own **private key**. This encrypted hash is the digital signature.
2. **Verification**: The recipient (Store B) decrypts the signature using Mr. A’s **public key** (obtained via Mr. A's digital certificate) to recover the original hash, and computes the hash of the received message independently. If both hashes match:
   - **Sender Authentication**: Since only Mr. A possesses his private key, the signature could only have been created by Mr. A.
   - **Data Integrity**: The content has not been modified or tampered with in transit.
   - **Non-repudiation**: Mr. A cannot later deny sending the order.

Therefore, Store B can confirm that the order arriving at Store B is truly from Mr. A (**choice b**).

### Option Analysis
- **a)** Digital signatures verify the identity of the signer and data integrity; they do not verify commercial permissions, licenses, or legal rights to sell products.
- **b)** **Correct**. As explained above, verifying the signature using Mr. A's public key confirms that the order originated from Mr. A.
- **c)** Digital signing by itself does **not** encrypt the message body. Anyone intercepting the email could read the order details. To ensure confidentiality (prevent leakage), the message would need to be encrypted using Store B's public key.
- **d)** A digital signature is an application-layer security mechanism and has no role in network delivery or routing guarantees.
