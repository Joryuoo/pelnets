# 2016A_FE_74
Which of the following is a role that CIO is required to perform?

a) Design and operation of an organization of legal compliance.
b) Planning and execution of the research and development policy of the company.
c) Planning and implementation of a financial strategy for business administration.
d) Promotion of use of IT services that maximizes business value.
?
d) Promotion of use of IT services that maximizes business value.
### Explanation
A **CIO (Chief Information Officer)** is the executive responsible for the overall strategy, governance, and management of information technology and computer systems within an enterprise. A key responsibility of the CIO is aligning IT strategies with overarching business objectives to ensure IT investments maximize business value and operational effectiveness.

* **a)** describes the responsibility of a **CCO (Chief Compliance Officer)** or Chief Legal Officer (CLO).
* **b)** describes the responsibility of a **CTO (Chief Technology Officer)** or Head of R&D.
* **c)** describes the responsibility of a **CFO (Chief Financial Officer)**.
