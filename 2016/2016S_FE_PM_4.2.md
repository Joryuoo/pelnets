### Explanation

**Subquestion 2:**
*   **Answer (d):** The "Lost Update problem" described in the scenario happens because workstations hold files open for a long time without communicating their changes to the server. If workstations **frequently close files after every small update and re-open the file**, their updates are committed to the server much sooner. This minimizes the time window during which concurrent changes from different workstations can overlap and overwrite each other, effectively reducing the risk of a lost update (even though it increases network traffic).
