# 2016S_FE_79

A company has planned to provide a product with a fixed cost of $128,000, a variable cost per unit of $48, and a selling price per unit of $80. Which of the following is the point of sales ($) to become break-even?

a) 240,000
b) 320,000
c) 480,000
d) 560,000
?
b) 320,000

### Explanation

The **break-even point (BEP)** is the level of sales where total revenues equal total costs (both fixed and variable), resulting in zero profit and zero loss.

### Method 1: Using the Contribution Margin Ratio (CMR)

1. **Unit Contribution Margin ($CM$)**:
   $$CM = \text{Selling Price per Unit} - \text{Variable Cost per Unit}$$
   $$CM = \$80 - \$48 = \$32$$

2. **Contribution Margin Ratio ($CMR$)**:
   $$CMR = \frac{CM}{\text{Selling Price per Unit}} = \frac{\$32}{\$80} = 0.40 \text{ (or } 40\%)$$

3. **Break-Even Point in Sales Dollars ($BEP_{\$}$)**:
   $$BEP_{\$} = \frac{\text{Fixed Cost}}{CMR} = \frac{\$128,000}{0.40} = \$320,000$$

---

### Method 2: Using Break-Even Volume in Units

1. **Break-Even Volume in Units ($Q_{BE}$)**:
   $$Q_{BE} = \frac{\text{Fixed Cost}}{CM} = \frac{\$128,000}{\$32} = 4,000 \text{ units}$$

2. **Break-Even Sales Revenue**:
   $$\text{Sales Revenue} = Q_{BE} \times \text{Selling Price} = 4,000 \times \$80 = \$320,000$$

Both methods yield **$320,000**, confirming that **b)** is the correct option.
