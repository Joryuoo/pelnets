### Explanation
The goal is to generate a report detailing the number of dogs and the total balance due grouped by township.
- **Blank F**: The `SELECT` list must include the count of dogs (`COUNT(DogID) AS NoOfDogs`), the sum of the balance dues (`SUM(BalanceDue) AS TotalAmount`), and the `Township` attribute (since it is used in the `GROUP BY` clause). This matches option a.
- **Blank G**: The query retrieves data spanning two tables: `OwnerTable` (for `Township`) and `PetTable` (for `DogID` and `BalanceDue`). An `INNER JOIN` is required to combine these tables on their shared attribute, `OwnerID`. This corresponds to `OwnerTable INNER JOIN PetTable ON OwnerTable.OwnerID = PetTable.OwnerID`, which is option d.
Therefore, the correct choices are a, d.
