# 2016A_FE_11
![[2016A_FE_AM_Q11_p1.png]]

a) 7
b) 8
c) 9
d) 10
?
c) 9
### Explanation
We are given:
- The data bus width is $2^n$ bits.
- The number of redundant bits required for ECC (Error Checking and Correction) is $n + 2$.
- The actual data bus width is $128$ bits.

#### Step 1: Solve for $n$
Express the data bus width as a power of 2:
$$2^n = 128$$
$$2^n = 2^7 \implies n = 7$$

#### Step 2: Calculate the number of redundant bits
Substitute $n = 7$ into the given formula $n + 2$:
$$\text{Redundant bits} = n + 2 = 7 + 2 = 9$$

Therefore, **9** redundant bits are required. The correct choice is **c) 9**.
