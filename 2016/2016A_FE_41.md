# 2016A_FE_41
Biometric authentication includes a method of authentication by extracting physical characteristics, and a method of authentication by extracting behavior characteristics. Which of the following has behavior characteristics?

a) Performing authentication by extracting characteristics from the branching angle of the branching point of a blood vessel, and the length between the branching points
b) Performing authentication by extracting characteristics from the speed of and pressure on a pen when a signature is written
c) Performing authentication by extracting characteristics of chaotic wrinkles spreading out from the pupil
d) Performing authentication by extracting the characteristic points called minutiae from the patterns formed by ridge lines
?
b) Performing authentication by extracting characteristics from the speed of and pressure on a pen when a signature is written
### Explanation
Biometric authentication methods verify an individual's identity based on two main categories:

1. **Behavioral Characteristics**: Characteristics based on measurable dynamic actions or behavior patterns performed by a human:
   * **Dynamic signature verification (Option b):** Analyzes the speed, rhythm, stroke sequence, acceleration, and pen pressure while writing a signature.
   * Other examples include keystroke dynamics (typing cadence) and gait analysis.

2. **Physical (Physiological) Characteristics**: Static anatomical features of the human body:
   * **Blood vessel / vein pattern recognition (Option a):** Analyzes the structure, branching angles, and lengths between vascular branching points in the hand or finger.
   * **Iris recognition (Option c):** Analyzes the complex, chaotic wrinkle patterns in the colored ring of tissue surrounding the pupil.
   * **Fingerprint recognition (Option d):** Analyzes minutiae points (ridge endings, bifurcations, dots) formed by friction ridge lines on the fingertip.

Therefore, **b** is the only method based on behavioral characteristics.
