# 2016A_FE_77
Which of the following is an explanation of an ABC analysis method?

a) A survey of the same panelists is repeatedly conducted. This data is used to understand trends in regions and changes in purchasing groups.
b) Products are arranged in order of high sales or high gross margins, and then products are divided into various levels by using the cumulative ratio. Following this, an increase in sales volume is planned for the products at the high levels.
c) Regions are divided into a grid with multiple sections, and detailed regional analysis is performed on the basis of various data, such as population and purchasing power.
d) The sales potential and the purchasing record of a product are analyzed by classifying and aggregating multiple items of survey data by factor.
?
b) Products are arranged in order of high sales or high gross margins, and then products are divided into various levels by using the cumulative ratio. Following this, an increase in sales volume is planned for the products at the high levels.
### Explanation
**ABC analysis** is an inventory and product management categorization technique based on the **Pareto Principle (80/20 rule)**:

1. Items are sorted in descending order according to a quantitative metric (e.g., annual sales value, gross margin, inventory value).
2. The cumulative percentage (cumulative ratio) is calculated.
3. Items are grouped into three categories:
   * **Group A (Vital Few):** Typically accounts for ~70–80% of total sales/value with only ~10–20% of the total number of items. These receive the highest priority and strict management.
   * **Group B (Moderate):** Accounts for ~15–20% of sales/value and ~30% of items.
   * **Group C (Trivial Many):** Accounts for ~5–10% of sales/value but constitutes ~50% of items. These require simpler, automated controls.

* **a)** describes a **panel survey / tracking study** (longitudinal consumer research).
* **c)** describes **mesh analysis** (grid-based geographic marketing analysis).
* **d)** describes **factor analysis / cross-tabulation** of survey data.
