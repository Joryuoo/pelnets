# 2016A_FE_62
When a workflow system is used for business improvement, which of the following is an appropriate effect that can be expected?

a) A standard protocol used for data exchange between a company and its customers can be provided.
b) Processing speed for office procedures from document submission to approval can be improved.
c) Services, such as a discount, can be provided according to the amount purchased by the customer.
d) Shipping and receiving of stocked products can be automated in a warehouse.
?
b) Processing speed for office procedures from document submission to approval can be improved.
### Explanation
A **workflow system** electronically defines, coordinates, and automates the flow of documents, tasks, and requests through predefined approval chains (such as purchase approvals, travel expense claims, or internal authorizations).

* **a)** Describes **EDI (Electronic Data Interchange)** or standard B2B communication protocols.
* **b)** **Correct.** By digitizing paper forms, routing documents automatically to the appropriate approvers, and eliminating physical delivery delays, processing speed from document submission to approval is significantly improved.
* **c)** Describes a loyalty program or sales promotion mechanism within a **CRM (Customer Relationship Management)** system.
* **d)** Describes a **WMS (Warehouse Management System)** or automated material handling system.
