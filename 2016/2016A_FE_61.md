# 2016A_FE_61
Which of the following is the purpose of BPM (Business Process Management) in the context of corporate activity?

a) Analysis and effective utilization of information resources.
b) Continual improvement of business processes.
c) Effective utilization of management resources.
d) Management and analysis of customer information.
?
b) Continual improvement of business processes.
### Explanation
**Business Process Management (BPM)** is a systematic management discipline that focuses on analyzing, designing, executing, monitoring, and continuously improving business processes to optimize organizational performance and agility. It commonly applies iterative PDCA (Plan-Do-Check-Act) cycles to adapt workflows to changing business environments.

* **a)** Describes **Information Resource Management (IRM)** or Business Intelligence (BI).
* **b)** **Correct.** The central goal of BPM is the **continual improvement of business processes**.
* **c)** Describes **Enterprise Resource Planning (ERP)**, which integrates and coordinates core business functions to effectively utilize enterprise resources (people, goods, money, information).
* **d)** Describes **Customer Relationship Management (CRM)**, which manages and analyzes customer interactions and customer data throughout the customer lifecycle.
