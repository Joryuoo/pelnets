# 2016S_FE_28

Which of the following is an explanation of texture mapping?

a) It is a process by which surfaces that should not be visible to the user are prevented from being rendered.
b) It renders a scene by calculating reflection, refraction, and transparency from the light source.
c) It renders shadows based on light sources and shapes of objects when a scene is realized.
d) It represents surface characteristics by pasting an image onto the surface of a three-dimensional object.
?
d) It represents surface characteristics by pasting an image onto the surface of a three-dimensional object.

### Explanation

**Texture mapping** is a computer graphics technique used in 3D rendering to add surface detail, color, patterns, and realistic material appearance (such as wood, brick, fabric, or marble) to a 3D polygonal model. 

Instead of modeling intricate geometric details with millions of additional polygons, a 2D digital image (the **texture**) is projected, wrapped, or "pasted" onto the surface polygons of the 3D model using $(u, v)$ texture coordinates. This significantly reduces computational complexity while drastically improving visual realism. Therefore, **d)** is the correct definition.

#### Analysis of Other Computer Graphics Concepts:
- **a) Hidden surface removal (or culling):** Algorithms (such as the Z-buffer algorithm, Painter's algorithm, or backface culling) that determine which surfaces and parts of objects are hidden from the viewer's line of sight and discard them from rendering.
- **b) Ray tracing:** A global illumination rendering algorithm that traces the paths of light rays as pixels in an image plane and simulates the effects of its encounters with virtual objects, accurately calculating reflections, refractions, and transparency.
- **c) Shadowing / Shadow mapping:** Techniques used to calculate and render realistic shadows cast onto objects based on light source positions and obstacle geometries.
