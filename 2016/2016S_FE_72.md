# 2016S_FE_72

Which of the following is an explanation of consumer generated media (CGM)?

a) It allows the customers of a company who want to join a mailing list to be registered to it and periodically delivers information via e-mail.
b) It enables the distribution of digital content that an individual reader creates, and it enables sharing of such content between users.
c) It integrates online shops offered by personal tenants, issues common loyalty points, and settles credit card payments on behalf of those tenants.
d) It offers functions for trading goods in an auction format and works as an intermediary between consumers in personal trading.
?
b) It enables the distribution of digital content that an individual reader creates, and it enables sharing of such content between users.

### Explanation

**Consumer Generated Media (CGM)**, also widely known as **User-Generated Content (UGC)**, refers to websites and online media platforms where content is created, published, and shared directly by individual consumers or users rather than by corporate publishers or professional content creators.

- Prominent examples of CGM include blogs, internet forums, social networking sites (SNS), video-sharing platforms, online review websites, and wikis.
- CGM allows users to express opinions, share experiences, provide word-of-mouth feedback on products, and interact with other users.

### Analysis of Incorrect Options:
- **a)** Registering users to a mailing list and delivering periodic updates via email describes an **email newsletter / opt-in email marketing** system.
- **c)** Integrating online shops with shared point systems and credit card settlement describes an **online shopping mall platform / multi-vendor e-commerce portal** (such as Rakuten or Amazon Marketplace).
- **d)** Providing auction functionality and intermediating consumer-to-consumer trades describes an **online C2C auction platform** (such as eBay or Yahoo! Auctions).
