# 2016S_FE_69

Which of the following is an appropriate explanation of the “Question mark” product in the product portfolio management (PPM) matrix?

a) It needs appropriate investments to maintain, although it has a large rate of market share and a high rate of market growth.
b) It needs minimal investments to harvest owing to a large rate of market share and a low rate of market growth.
c) It needs significant investments to nurture owing to a small rate of market share and a high rate of market growth.
d) It needs to be withdrawn owing to a small rate of market share and a low rate of market growth.
?
c) It needs significant investments to nurture owing to a small rate of market share and a high rate of market growth.

### Explanation

The **Product Portfolio Management (PPM)** matrix (also known as the **BCG Matrix** developed by the Boston Consulting Group) evaluates business units and products across two dimensions: **Market Growth Rate** (vertical axis) and **Relative Market Share** (horizontal axis).

| Quadrant | Market Growth Rate | Relative Market Share | Cash Flow & Strategy | Description |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Star** | **High** | **High** | Uses cash / reinvestment | Needs significant investment to maintain rapid growth and fend off competitors (Option a). |
| **Cash Cow** | **Low** | **High** | Generates high cash | Generates strong profits with low capital requirements; funds are harvested to finance Question Marks (Option b). |
| **Question Mark** (Problem Child) | **High** | **Low** | Consumes high cash | Operates in an attractive, fast-growing market, but holds low market share. Needs significant investments to nurture into a Star (Option c). |
| **Dog** | **Low** | **Low** | Low or negative cash | Generates little profit in a stagnant market. Prime candidate for divestiture, phase-out, or withdrawal (Option d). |

Because a **Question Mark** has a low market share in a rapidly expanding market, it consumes large amounts of cash. Significant capital investments are required to build market share and turn it into a **Star**; otherwise, it risks degenerating into a **Dog** as growth slows.

Therefore, **Option c** is the correct explanation.
