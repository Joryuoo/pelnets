# 2016S_FE_26

![[2016S_FE_AM_Q26_full.png]]

a) 0110000
b) 0000110
c) 1001111
d) 1111001
?
a) 0110000

### Explanation

#### 1. Common Anode Seven-Segment Display Operation
In a **common anode** configuration:
- The anodes of all individual segment LEDs are tied together to the positive supply voltage (+).
- To light up an LED segment (**ON**), its corresponding cathode input terminal must be connected to ground (**logic 0 / Low**).
- To keep an LED segment dark (**OFF**), its input terminal is connected to high voltage (**logic 1 / High**).

Therefore, for common anode:
- **ON = 0**
- **OFF = 1**

*(Note: In a common cathode display, the logic would be inverted: ON = 1, OFF = 0).*

---

#### 2. Determining Segment States for Character "E"
Looking at the diagram displaying the character "E":
- **Segment A:** Lit (**ON**) $\rightarrow 0$
- **Segment B:** Unlit (**OFF**) $\rightarrow 1$
- **Segment C:** Unlit (**OFF**) $\rightarrow 1$
- **Segment D:** Lit (**ON**) $\rightarrow 0$
- **Segment E:** Lit (**ON**) $\rightarrow 0$
- **Segment F:** Lit (**ON**) $\rightarrow 0$
- **Segment G:** Lit (**ON**) $\rightarrow 0$

---

#### 3. Arranging Bits from MSB to LSB
The question specifies that **A represents the Most Significant Bit (MSB)** and **G represents the Least Significant Bit (LSB)**:

$$\text{Bit sequence} = \text{A B C D E F G}$$

Substituting each segment value:
$$\text{Bit sequence} = 0\ 1\ 1\ 0\ 0\ 0\ 0$$

Thus, the required input is **0110000**, which matches **a)**.
