### Explanation
The goal is to update the `BalanceDue` for pet owners who reside in a township other than 'Wild Rose'.
- **Blank B**: The table containing the `BalanceDue` attribute to be updated is `PetTable`. Thus, B is `e`.
- **Blank C**: In the `WHERE` clause of the `UPDATE` statement, we need to filter records by `OwnerID` to match it against a subquery. Thus, C is `c`.
- **Blank D**: The subquery must return a list of `OwnerID` values to match the `IN` condition. Thus, D is `c`.
- **Blank E**: The subquery retrieves `OwnerID` based on the `Township` condition. The `Township` attribute exists in `OwnerTable`. Thus, E is `d`.
Therefore, the correct choices are e, c, c, d.
