# 2016A_FE_60
Which of the following is a check point for auditing control in relation to the accuracy of the processing logic of a spreadsheet?

a) A backup of the spreadsheet is stored.
b) The person who creates and uses the spreadsheet is the same.
c) The program of the spreadsheet is documented and verified.
d) The users of the spreadsheet are defined.
?
c) The program of the spreadsheet is documented and verified.
### Explanation
In **End-User Computing (EUC)** auditing and spreadsheet controls:

* **Accuracy of Processing Logic (Option c):**
  Spreadsheets often incorporate complex calculation formulas, lookup tables, and embedded scripts/macros (collectively referred to as the "program" or logic of the spreadsheet). To ensure that calculations are logically sound, mathematically correct, and properly aligned with business rules, auditors check whether the formulas and macros are **thoroughly documented, independently reviewed, and verified** against known test cases.

Analysis of other options:
* **a) Backups stored:** Relates to **availability** and data protection (disaster recovery / business continuity), ensuring data can be restored if corrupted or lost.
* **b) Creator and user being the same person:** This actually poses an internal control risk due to lack of **segregation of duties** and lack of independent peer review, making undetected errors or fraudulent adjustments more likely.
* **d) Defined users:** Relates to **authorization and access control** (confidentiality and governance), not the mathematical correctness of internal processing formulas.
