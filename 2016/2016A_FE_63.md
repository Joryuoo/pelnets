# 2016A_FE_63
![[2016A_FE_AM_Q63_full.png]]

a) 0.3
b) 0.5
c) 0.7
d) 1.0
?
c) 0.7
### Explanation
Let's break down the current working hours and calculate the required reduction step-by-step:

#### 1. Analyze Current Daily Operations
* **Total working hours:** $8.0\text{ hours}$
* **Number of customer visits:** $5\text{ visits}$
* **Customer visit time:** $5.0\text{ hours}$
  $$\text{Hours per visit} = \frac{5.0\text{ hours}}{5\text{ visits}} = 1.0\text{ hour/visit}$$
* **Office work:** $3.0\text{ hours}$
  * **Preparation time for customer visits:** $1.5\text{ hours}$
    $$\text{Preparation time per visit} = \frac{1.5\text{ hours}}{5\text{ visits}} = 0.3\text{ hours/visit}$$
  * **Other working time:** $1.5\text{ hours}$

---

#### 2. Apply the Effects of SFA and Target Operations
* **New preparation time per customer visit:**
  Reduced by $0.1\text{ hours}$:
  $$0.3 - 0.1 = 0.2\text{ hours/visit}$$
* **Target number of customer visits:** $6\text{ visits}$
* **Hours per visit remains unchanged:** $1.0\text{ hour/visit}$
  $$\text{New total customer visit time} = 6\text{ visits} \times 1.0\text{ hour/visit} = 6.0\text{ hours}$$
* **New total preparation time for 6 visits:**
  $$6\text{ visits} \times 0.2\text{ hours/visit} = 1.2\text{ hours}$$

---

#### 3. Determine New "Other Working Time"
* Total working hours must remain $8.0\text{ hours}$:
  $$\text{Total working hours} = \text{Customer visit time} + \text{Office work}$$
  $$8.0 = 6.0 + \text{Office work} \implies \text{Office work} = 2.0\text{ hours}$$
* Office work consists of preparation time and other working time:
  $$\text{Office work} = \text{Preparation time} + \text{Other working time}$$
  $$2.0 = 1.2 + \text{New Other working time}$$
  $$\text{New Other working time} = 2.0 - 1.2 = 0.8\text{ hours}$$

---

#### 4. Calculate the Reduction from "Other Working Time"
* **Original Other working time:** $1.5\text{ hours}$
* **New Other working time:** $0.8\text{ hours}$
* **Required reduction:**
  $$1.5 - 0.8 = 0.7\text{ hours}$$

Therefore, **0.7 hours** must be reduced from "Other working time".
