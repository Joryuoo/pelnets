# 2016A_FE_75
Among the measures that a company should adopt in order to fulfill social responsibility, which of the following is implemented in terms of the environment?

a) To develop an internal organization for the purpose of green purchasing.
b) To enhance the transparency of the management status of the company for shareholders.
c) To establish a system that supports volunteer activities of employees following disasters.
d) To setup an ethics helpline within the company.
?
a) To develop an internal organization for the purpose of green purchasing.
### Explanation
**Corporate Social Responsibility (CSR)** refers to a company's commitment to operate in an economically, socially, and environmentally sustainable manner while recognizing the interests of its stakeholders.

* **a) Green purchasing** involves prioritizing goods and services that have a minimal negative impact on the environment throughout their life cycle (e.g., energy-saving, recyclable, reduced packaging). This is an environmental measure.
* **b)** relates to **corporate governance** and investor relations (IR).
* **c)** relates to **philanthropic/community involvement** and disaster relief.
* **d)** relates to **business ethics and legal compliance** (whistleblower protection).
