# 2016S_FE_58
Which of the following is the most appropriate sentence describing the difference between intermediate COCOMO and COCOMO II?

a) Intermediate COCOMO consists of one overall model based on lines of code, whereas COCOMO II consists of three different models, depending on the available knowledge of product to be built.
b) Intermediate COCOMO estimates cost based on object point, whereas COCOMO II estimates cost based on function points.
c) Intermediate COCOMO has 11 cost drivers, whereas COCOMO II has 17 multiplicative cost drivers.
d) In intermediate COCOMO, exponent b varies between 1.01 and 1.26, depending on the variety of parameters of the model, whereas in COCOMO II, the value of b takes on three different values, depending on whether the mode of the product to be built is organic (b=1.05), semi-detached (b=1.12), or embedded (b=1.20).
?
a) Intermediate COCOMO consists of one overall model based on lines of code, whereas COCOMO II consists of three different models, depending on the available knowledge of product to be built.
### Explanation
- **Intermediate COCOMO (COCOMO 81)** uses a single overarching model based strictly on source lines of code (SLOC / KDSI) and 15 cost drivers across three fixed development modes (Organic, Semi-detached, Embedded).
- **COCOMO II** consists of **three different sub-models**, each suited to different stages of the software life cycle depending on the level of information and maturity of the project:
  1. **Application Composition Model**: Used in the early prototyping stage, based on **Object Points**.
  2. **Early Design Model**: Used in the architectural exploration stage before detailed requirements are defined, based on **Function Points** or SLOC with 7 cost drivers.
  3. **Post-Architecture Model**: Used during the actual development and maintenance phase, based on SLOC or Unadjusted Function Points with 17 cost drivers and 5 scale factors.

Analysis of other choices:
- **b) is incorrect**: Intermediate COCOMO is based on lines of code (SLOC/KDSI), not object points.
- **c) is incorrect**: Intermediate COCOMO uses 15 cost drivers (not 11), while COCOMO II Post-Architecture uses 17 cost drivers.
- **d) is incorrect**: The statements are inverted. In intermediate COCOMO, exponent $b$ takes fixed values based on the three modes (Organic: $1.05$, Semi-detached: $1.12$, Embedded: $1.20$). In COCOMO II, the scale exponent $B$ varies dynamically between $1.01$ and $1.26$ based on five scale factors.
