# 2016A_FE_PM_6.2

![[2016A_FE_PM_Q6_SQ2_p1.png]]

?

d, b, b

### Explanation

We simulate placing the items `{4, 8, 5, 7, 6, 1, 4, 2, 2, 1}` into bins of capacity `10`.

**NextFit() (Blank F):**
NextFit places the item in the *current* bin. If it doesn't fit, it closes that bin and starts a new one.
*   Item 1 (size 4): Goes to Bin 1. (Remaining: 6) -> Output: **1**
*   Item 2 (size 8): Doesn't fit in Bin 1. Goes to Bin 2. (Remaining: 2) -> Output: **2**
*   Item 3 (size 5): Doesn't fit in Bin 2. Goes to Bin 3. (Remaining: 5) -> Output: **3**
*   Item 4 (size 7): Doesn't fit in Bin 3. Goes to Bin 4. (Remaining: 3) -> Output: **4**
*   Item 5 (size 6): Doesn't fit in Bin 4. Goes to Bin 5. (Remaining: 4) -> Output: **5**
*   Item 6 (size 1): Fits in Bin 5. Goes to Bin 5. (Remaining: 3) -> Output: **5**
*   Item 7 (size 4): Doesn't fit in Bin 5. Goes to Bin 6. (Remaining: 6) -> Output: **6**
*   Item 8 (size 2): Fits in Bin 6. Goes to Bin 6. (Remaining: 4) -> Output: **6**
*   Item 9 (size 2): Fits in Bin 6. Goes to Bin 6. (Remaining: 2) -> Output: **6**
*   Item 10 (size 1): Fits in Bin 6. Goes to Bin 6. (Remaining: 1) -> Output: **6**
Sequence: `1 2 3 4 5 5 6 6 6 6` (Answer: **d**)

**FirstFit() (Blank G):**
FirstFit places the item in the *lowest numbered* open bin that can accommodate it.
*   Item 1 (4): Bin 1. (Bin 1 has 6 left) -> **1**
*   Item 2 (8): Bin 2. (Bin 2 has 2 left) -> **2**
*   Item 3 (5): Fits in Bin 1. (Bin 1 has 1 left) -> **1**
*   Item 4 (7): Bin 3. (Bin 3 has 3 left) -> **3**
*   Item 5 (6): Bin 4. (Bin 4 has 4 left) -> **4**
*   Item 6 (1): Fits in Bin 1. (Bin 1 has 0 left) -> **1**
*   Item 7 (4): Fits in Bin 4. (Bin 4 has 0 left) -> **4**
*   Item 8 (2): Fits in Bin 2. (Bin 2 has 0 left) -> **2**
*   Item 9 (2): Fits in Bin 3. (Bin 3 has 1 left) -> **3**
*   Item 10 (1): Fits in Bin 3. (Bin 3 has 0 left) -> **3**
Sequence: `1 2 1 3 4 1 4 2 3 3` (Answer: **b**)

**BestFit() (Blank H):**
BestFit places the item in the bin that leaves the *least remaining capacity*.
*   Item 1 (4): Bin 1. (Bin 1 has 6 left) -> **1**
*   Item 2 (8): Bin 2. (Bin 2 has 2 left) -> **2**
*   Item 3 (5): Fits in Bin 1 (leaves 1). Best is Bin 1. -> **1**
*   Item 4 (7): Bin 3. (Bin 3 has 3 left) -> **3**
*   Item 5 (6): Bin 4. (Bin 4 has 4 left) -> **4**
*   Item 6 (1): Fits in Bin 1 (leaves 0), Bin 2 (leaves 1), Bin 3 (leaves 2), Bin 4 (leaves 3). Best is Bin 1. -> **1**
*   Item 7 (4): Fits in Bin 4 (leaves 0). Best is Bin 4. -> **4**
*   Item 8 (2): Fits in Bin 2 (leaves 0), Bin 3 (leaves 1). Best is Bin 2. -> **2**
*   Item 9 (2): Fits in Bin 3 (leaves 1). Best is Bin 3. -> **3**
*   Item 10 (1): Fits in Bin 3 (leaves 0). Best is Bin 3. -> **3**
Sequence: `1 2 1 3 4 1 4 2 3 3` (Answer: **b**)
