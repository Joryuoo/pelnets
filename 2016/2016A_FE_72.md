# 2016A_FE_72
Which of the following is an example of the use of social media in business?

a) A company posts its business strategies regarding accommodating itself to market changes, in professional business magazines, thus aiming to improve their reputation as a trustworthy company.
b) A company sets up a site on the Internet for sharing opinions on matters such as the usage status of its products, which leads to improvement of the product.
c) The researchers of a company buy the latest professional magazines that are not sold within the country through online shopping, which contributes to the research and development activities.
d) The sales department frequently posts product articles in magazines that are circulated in large numbers, thus aiming at improving awareness of the product.
?
b) A company sets up a site on the Internet for sharing opinions on matters such as the usage status of its products, which leads to improvement of the product.
### Explanation
**Social media** in a business context refers to interactive web-based and mobile platforms that enable two-way communication, user-generated content, collaboration, and community dialogue between a company and its stakeholders (such as customers).

* **b)** Setting up an online community or forum where consumers can share feedback, experiences, and opinions about product usage facilitates two-way interaction and user-driven insights, which directly exemplifies social media usage.
* **a)** and **d)** represent traditional one-way mass media communication and public relations (PR) via print/trade magazines.
* **c)** represents e-commerce (online purchasing) of research materials, not social media engagement.
