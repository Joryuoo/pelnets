# 2016S_FE_44

Which of the following is a description of measures against information leakage?

a) A checksum is added to the data to be sent.
b) A copy of the data backup medium is stored at a remote location.
c) The contents of the hard disk of a notebook PC are encrypted.
d) The hard disk in which data are stored is mirrored.
?
c) The contents of the hard disk of a notebook PC are encrypted.

### Explanation

Information leakage is a violation of **confidentiality** in the CIA (Confidentiality, Integrity, Availability) triad. Measures against information leakage ensure that confidential data cannot be accessed, read, or exposed to unauthorized parties.

Portable devices such as notebook PCs and laptops carry a high risk of being lost or stolen. **Full disk encryption (FDE)** (e.g., BitLocker, FileVault) converts all data on the disk into ciphertext. Even if the laptop falls into an attacker's hands, the contents cannot be read without the proper decryption key or authentication credentials. Hence, disk encryption directly protects against information leakage.

### Option Analysis
- **a) A checksum is added to the data to be sent**: A checksum detects accidental bit alterations during transmission or storage. This is a measure to guarantee **integrity**, not confidentiality or leakage prevention.
- **b) A copy of the data backup medium is stored at a remote location**: Remote backups protect against disaster scenarios (e.g., fire, flood, earthquake) and ensure business continuity. This is a measure to maintain **availability**.
- **c) The contents of the hard disk of a notebook PC are encrypted**: **Correct**. Encryption prevents unauthorized parties from reading data from stolen or lost storage devices.
- **d) The hard disk in which data are stored is mirrored**: Disk mirroring (such as RAID 1) provides redundancy against hardware drive failure. This maintains system **availability**.
