# 2016A_FE_51
According to PMBOK, which of the following is an appropriate explanation of project definition?

a) It is a group of related projects, subprograms, and program activities managed in a coordinated way to obtain benefits not available from managing them individually.
b) It is a number of people who are gathered to undertake a common job with different tasks with the aim of finishing all the tasks timeously to satisfy their customers or stakeholders.
c) It is a temporary endeavor undertaken to create a unique product, service, or result.
d) It is the application of knowledge, skills, tools, and techniques to a program to meet the program requirements and to obtain benefits and control not available by managing projects individually.
?
c) It is a temporary endeavor undertaken to create a unique product, service, or result.
### Explanation
According to the Project Management Institute's **PMBOK Guide** (Project Management Body of Knowledge):

* **Project (Option c):** Defined as "a **temporary endeavor undertaken to create a unique product, service, or result**."
  * **Temporary** means that every project has a definite beginning and end.
  * **Unique** means that the deliverable (product, service, or result) is distinct from all other products or services.

Analysis of other options:
* **a)** Describes a **program**: A group of related projects, subprograms, and program activities managed in a coordinated way to obtain benefits not available from managing them individually.
* **b)** Describes a project team or working group.
* **d)** Describes **program management**: The application of knowledge, skills, tools, and techniques to a program to meet the program requirements and to obtain benefits and control not available by managing projects individually.
