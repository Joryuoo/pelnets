# 2016A_FE_54
According to PMBOK, which of the following is the project management knowledge area that includes the activities of make-or-buy decisions, development time and material contracts, and selection of sellers from bidders?

a) Project Communication Management
b) Project Cost Management
c) Project Integration Management
d) Project Procurement Management
?
d) Project Procurement Management
### Explanation
According to the **PMBOK Guide**, **Project Procurement Management** includes the processes necessary to purchase or acquire products, services, or results needed from outside the project team. Key components include:

* **Make-or-buy analysis/decisions:** Determining whether a particular product or service should be developed internally by the project team or procured from outside sellers.
* **Contract types:** Selecting appropriate contractual agreements, such as Fixed-Price, Cost-Reimbursable, or Time and Material (T&M) contracts.
* **Conducting procurements:** Obtaining seller responses, evaluating bids/proposals, selecting qualified sellers, and awarding contracts.

Analysis of other knowledge areas:
* **a) Project Communication Management:** Ensures timely and appropriate planning, collection, creation, distribution, storage, retrieval, management, control, monitoring, and ultimate disposition of project information.
* **b) Project Cost Management:** Involves planning, estimating, budgeting, financing, funding, managing, and controlling costs so that the project can be completed within the approved budget.
* **c) Project Integration Management:** Involves coordinating all elements of the project, including unifying and consolidating processes and activities from project initiation through closure.
