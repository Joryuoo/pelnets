# 2016S_FE_74

Which of the following is an explanation of a smart grid?

a) It is a system that aims at the utilization of recyclable energy, supply of stable electric power, and optimum demand adjustment through the integrated control of power generation and power consumption based on communication and information-processing technology.
b) It is a system that can provide ideal health advice to each individual by performing analysis based on similar case reports, such as the result of health examinations and medication use.
c) It is a system that can search for information on restaurants and entertainment, send and receive traffic information, and communicate the current position at the time of an emergency by using information-processing equipment installed in vehicles.
d) It is a system that supports varied work styles by enabling mutual communication between workers with varied working styles, such as home-based employees and senior workers.
?
a) It is a system that aims at the utilization of recyclable energy, supply of stable electric power, and optimum demand adjustment through the integrated control of power generation and power consumption based on communication and information-processing technology.

### Explanation

A **smart grid** is a modernized electrical distribution and transmission network that leverages digital communication, smart sensors, and advanced information-processing technology to connect both electricity producers and consumers.

Key features of a smart grid include:
- Two-way digital communication between utility providers and end customers (often utilizing smart meters).
- Real-time monitoring and automated control of power generation and consumption.
- Seamless integration of renewable and distributed energy resources (solar, wind, etc.).
- Demand-response management to balance peak loads, prevent power blackouts, and maintain a stable, resilient power supply.

### Analysis of Incorrect Options:
- **b)** Analyzing health examinations and medication history to deliver personalized health advice describes a **personalized healthcare / clinical decision support system (CDSS)** or health informatics application.
- **c)** Integrating vehicular information-processing devices for navigation, traffic updates, emergency location tracking, and infotainment describes **telematics** (in-vehicle telecommunications and informatics).
- **d)** Enabling flexible communication and collaborative work for remote/home-based staff describes **telework / remote collaboration tools** or groupware.
