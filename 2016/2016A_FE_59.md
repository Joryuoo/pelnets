# 2016A_FE_59
Which of the following is an appropriate description of the implementation structure of a system audit?

a) For an audit of the development progress in an information systems department, the audit team consists of members of the information systems department who are familiar with the details of the development.
b) In order to enable the person requesting the audit to issue improvement instructions on the basis of the audit report, the system auditor reports the audit results to the person requesting the audit.
c) When an audit of the information system is performed as a part of the business operations audit, the audit team is formed by the members of the user’s department.
d) When the system auditor accepts the help of an expert, the decisions concerning the range of support, methods, and audit results is the responsibility of the expert.
?
b) In order to enable the person requesting the audit to issue improvement instructions on the basis of the audit report, the system auditor reports the audit results to the person requesting the audit.
### Explanation
In system auditing standards and practice:

* **Reporting to Audit Requester (Option b):**
  A system audit is initiated by an **audit requester** (such as executive management, the Board of Directors, or the Audit Committee). The system auditor acts in an independent advisory capacity to evaluate IT controls and provide an objective audit report to the requester. Armed with this independent report, the requester holds the managerial authority to direct the auditee to implement corrective actions and improvements.

Why the other options are incorrect:
* **a) & c) Violation of Independence:**
  A fundamental ethical and professional principle of system auditing is **independence and objectivity**. Auditors must be organizationally independent of the entities they audit.
  * Information systems department personnel cannot audit their own department's development progress (**a**).
  * User department members cannot audit the business and IT systems for which they have operational involvement or interest (**c**).
* **d) Auditor Responsibility When Using Experts:**
  While an auditor may seek the assistance of external technical specialists (e.g., security penetration testers, database forensics experts), the **system auditor** retains ultimate professional responsibility for defining the scope, evaluating the findings, and forming the audit opinion.
