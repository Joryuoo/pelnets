# 2016A_FE_57
Among the structures and characteristics of a service desk organization, which of the following is a description of a local service desk?

a) By consolidating the service desks at a single site or limited locations, service staff can be deployed effectively and a large number of calls can be handled.
b) By deploying the service desk near the users, it is possible to provide services for users who have different languages and cultures or for VIPs by dedicated service staff.
c) By performing centralized and integrated management of all service staff including those at the dispersed locations, well-controlled services can be provided.
d) By using communication technology, services such as a single service desk can be provided, even if the service staff is dispersed across several regions and departments.
?
b) By deploying the service desk near the users, it is possible to provide services for users who have different languages and cultures or for VIPs by dedicated service staff.
### Explanation
In **ITIL Service Operation**, the service desk is the single point of contact between the service provider and users. It can be organized into different structural models:

* **Local Service Desk (Option b):**
  Physically situated close to or within the user community it supports (e.g., at a specific office, manufacturing plant, or regional facility). This location proximity makes it easier to support unique local languages, specialized business cultures, physical on-site assistance, or dedicated VIP support.

Analysis of other service desk organizational structures:
* **a)** Describes a **Centralized Service Desk**: Consolidating staff into a single physical location (or few regional hubs) to maximize staff utilization, achieve economies of scale, and efficiently handle high volumes of calls.
* **c)** Describes a centralized management model for distributed operations.
* **d)** Describes a **Virtual Service Desk**: Leveraging telecommunications and internet technologies so that physically dispersed staff across multiple regions or home offices appear and function as a single unified service desk to users.
