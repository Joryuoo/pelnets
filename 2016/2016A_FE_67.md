c) Internal: Strengths, Weaknesses | External: Opportunities, Threats
### Explanation
**SWOT Analysis** evaluates an organization by categorizing factors across two dimensions:
1. **Origin:** **Internal factors** (organizational attributes controllable by the company) vs. **External factors** (environmental conditions outside the company's direct control).
2. **Impact:** **Advantageous** (helpful to achieving objectives) vs. **Disadvantageous** (harmful to achieving objectives).

Let's map each cell from the problem:

| Origin | Impact | Example in Question | SWOT Classification |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Internal Factor** | **Advantage** | Holds a significant number of patents due to innovation promotion. | **Strengths (S)** |
| **Internal Factor** | **Disadvantage** | Cannot provide staff training due to low budget, resulting in inefficiency. | **Weaknesses (W)** |
| **External Factor** | **Advantage** | An international world contest will be held nearby next year. | **Opportunities (O)** |
| **External Factor** | **Disadvantage** | Heavy rainfall caused flooding in the surrounding area. | **Threats (T)** |

* **Internal:** Advantage = **Strengths**, Disadvantage = **Weaknesses**
* **External:** Advantage = **Opportunities**, Disadvantage = **Threats**

This combination corresponds to option **c**.
