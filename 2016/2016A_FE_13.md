# 2016A_FE_13
Which of the following is an appropriate description of the reliability of computer systems?

a) As the system configuration becomes more complicated, MTBF increases.
b) Availability of systems can be improved by increasing MTTR and MTBF.
c) Preventive maintenance of systems is performed to increase MTBF.
d) Remote maintenance of systems can increase MTTR and improve availability.
?
c) Preventive maintenance of systems is performed to increase MTBF.
### Explanation
Computer system reliability and serviceability are evaluated using the following key metrics:

- **MTBF (Mean Time Between Failures):** The average operating time between consecutive system failures. A higher MTBF indicates greater reliability.
- **MTTR (Mean Time To Repair):** The average time required to diagnose, repair, and restore a failed system to normal operation. A lower MTTR indicates higher maintainability.
- **Availability ($A$):** The proportion of time the system is operational and accessible:
  $$A = \frac{\text{MTBF}}{\text{MTBF} + \text{MTTR}}$$

---

#### Evaluation of Choices:

- **a) As the system configuration becomes more complicated, MTBF increases.**
  - *Incorrect.* As a system becomes more complex with more interconnected components, the probability of at least one component failing increases. Consequently, overall system MTBF decreases (reliability decreases).

- **b) Availability of systems can be improved by increasing MTTR and MTBF.**
  - *Incorrect.* Availability increases when MTBF increases (longer uptime) and MTTR **decreases** (shorter downtime). Increasing MTTR prolongs downtime, which lowers availability.

- **c) Preventive maintenance of systems is performed to increase MTBF.**
  - *Correct.* Preventive maintenance involves scheduled inspections, proactive hardware replacements, and software updates to fix defects before failures happen. By avoiding unexpected breakdowns, it extends continuous operating time and thus increases MTBF.

- **d) Remote maintenance of systems can increase MTTR and improve availability.**
  - *Incorrect.* Remote maintenance allows technicians to monitor, diagnose, and fix issues over the network without traveling on-site. This significantly **decreases** (shortens) MTTR, rather than increasing it.
