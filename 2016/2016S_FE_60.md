# 2016S_FE_60
Which of the following term indicates the amount of data transactions allowed to be lost after a downtime occurs?

a) Maximum Tolerable Outage (MTO)
b) Recovery Point Objective (RPO)
c) Recovery Time Objective (RTO)
d) Service Delivery Objective (SDO)
?
b) Recovery Point Objective (RPO)
### Explanation
In business continuity planning and disaster recovery:
- **Recovery Point Objective (RPO) (b)**: The maximum acceptable amount of data loss measured in time or transaction volume. It indicates the point in time to which systems and data must be recovered after an outage (e.g., an RPO of 2 hours means no more than 2 hours of data/transactions can be lost).

Analysis of other terms:
- **Maximum Tolerable Outage / Downtime (MTO / MTD) (a)**: The maximum duration of time that a business process can remain unavailable without causing irreparable damage or severe business impact.
- **Recovery Time Objective (RTO) (c)**: The target duration of time within which a business process, system, or application must be restored after a disruption (i.e., how long it takes to recover from downtime).
- **Service Delivery Objective (SDO) (d)**: The minimum acceptable level of service that must be delivered during an alternate operating mode while awaiting full system recovery.
