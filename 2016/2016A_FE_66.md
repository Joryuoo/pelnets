# 2016A_FE_66
![[2016A_FE_AM_Q66_p1.png]]

a) Betterment of the work process and improvement of work quality
b) Creation of business and establishment of competitive advantages
c) Reduction of system maintenance and management cost, and improvement of system performance
d) Visualization and sharing of knowledge
?
b) Creation of business and establishment of competitive advantages
### Explanation
IT investments are categorized based on their intended strategic and operational objectives:

* **A: Business efficiency improvement investment**
  * **KPI:** Adhering to the delivery date, required number of days for monthly closing of accounts.
  * **Purpose:** **a) Betterment of the work process and improvement of work quality** (aims to streamline daily operations and cut waste).

* **B: Information utilization investment**
  * **KPI:** Number of registered proposals, number of proposals to customers.
  * **Purpose:** **d) Visualization and sharing of knowledge** (aims to leverage organizational information and promote internal knowledge sharing).

* **C: Strategic investment**
  * **KPI:** ROI of new business, market share of a new product.
  * **Purpose:** **b) Creation of business and establishment of competitive advantages** (aims to enter new markets, launch innovative products, and outcompete rivals).

* **D: IT infrastructure investment**
  * **KPI:** Number of system failures, search response time.
  * **Purpose:** **c) Reduction of system maintenance and management cost, and improvement of system performance** (aims to ensure platform stability, reliability, and security).

Therefore, investment purpose **C** corresponds to **b**.
