# 2016S_FE_71

Which of the following is a price-setting method based on cost-plus pricing?

a) On the assumption of a discount for a customer, the price is set by adding a profit margin from the beginning.
b) On the basis of manufacturing cost and operating expenses, the price is set by incorporating a desired margin.
c) The maximum profit and minimum risk are considered based on price analysis, and the price is set step by step.
d) The price is set with an estimated competitive price on the market.
?
b) On the basis of manufacturing cost and operating expenses, the price is set by incorporating a desired margin.

### Explanation

**Cost-plus pricing** (also called markup pricing) is a pricing method where a fixed percentage (markup / profit margin) is added to the total product cost to arrive at the final selling price.

$$\text{Selling Price} = \text{Total Cost} + \text{Desired Profit Margin}$$

Where:
- **Total Cost** consists of direct manufacturing/production costs (materials, direct labor, manufacturing overhead) plus operating expenses (sales, general, and administrative expenses).
- A desired profit margin (markup) is incorporated on top of this total cost base.

### Analysis of Incorrect Options:
- **a)** Adding a profit margin from the beginning to absorb later discounts describes a high-low or discount-cushioned pricing tactic rather than the standard cost-plus pricing methodology.
- **c)** Analyzing market prices step-by-step to achieve maximum profit with minimum risk describes target-return pricing, dynamic pricing, or value-based pricing.
- **d)** Setting prices based on estimated market competition describes **competition-based pricing** (going-rate pricing or market-oriented pricing).
