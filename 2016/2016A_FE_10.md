# 2016A_FE_10
Which of the following is classified as an internal interrupt?

a) An interrupt because of the occurrence of a memory parity error
b) An interrupt because of the operation of division by zero (0)
c) An interrupt caused by a power failure, such as an instantaneous failure of the commercial power supply
d) An interrupt caused by the completion of I/O
?
b) An interrupt because of the operation of division by zero (0)
### Explanation
Interrupts are classified as internal or external based on their source:
*   **Internal interrupts (Exceptions/Traps):** These originate from within the CPU itself as a direct result of executing an instruction. An attempt to divide by zero is an arithmetic exception generated synchronously by the CPU during instruction execution.
*   **External interrupts:** Originate from outside the CPU execution flow, usually from hardware peripherals or timers.
    *   *Memory parity error* (Hardware error, external to the execution pipeline)
    *   *Power failure* (Hardware/System environment, external)
    *   *I/O completion* (Peripheral device signaling the CPU, external)
