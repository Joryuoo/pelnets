### Explanation

**Subquestion 1:**
- **Blank A (a):** The requirement is to find trips where the driver is actually working on a specific date (`:SpDate`). The rental period spans from `StartDate` to `EndDate`. Therefore, the specific date must fall within this range, which is correctly expressed using the `BETWEEN` operator: `:SpDate BETWEEN StartDate AND EndDate`.
- **Blank B (c):** We need to ensure the driver is or was actually working, meaning the trip was not canceled. Since `TripStatus` 1 is "Booked", 2 is "Finished", and 3 is "Canceled", the correct condition is `TripStatus <> 3` to exclude canceled trips.
