# 2016A_FE_3
When two unbiased dice are rolled one by one, what is the probability that either the first one is 2 or the sum of the two is less than 5?

a) 1/6
b) 2/9
c) 5/18
d) 1/3
?
c) 5/18
### Explanation
There are 36 possible outcomes when rolling two 6-sided dice (6 × 6 = 36).
Let $A$ be the event "the first die is 2".
Let $B$ be the event "the sum of the two dice is less than 5".

We need to find the probability of $A \cup B$, which is $P(A) + P(B) - P(A \cap B)$.

1.  **Event $A$ (first die is 2):**
    The outcomes are (2,1), (2,2), (2,3), (2,4), (2,5), (2,6).
    Total = 6 outcomes.

2.  **Event $B$ (sum < 5):**
    The possible outcomes are sums of 2, 3, or 4.
    - Sum = 2: (1,1)
    - Sum = 3: (1,2), (2,1)
    - Sum = 4: (1,3), (2,2), (3,1)
    Total = 6 outcomes.

3.  **Event $A \cap B$ (first die is 2 AND sum < 5):**
    The outcomes from $A$ that also have a sum less than 5 are (2,1) and (2,2).
    Total = 2 outcomes.

4.  **Calculate $A \cup B$:**
    Total outcomes = $6 + 6 - 2 = 10$.

The probability is $10 / 36$, which simplifies to $5 / 18$.
