# 2016S_FE_75

![[2016S_FE_AM_Q75_full.png]]

a) A
b) B
c) C
d) D
?
b) B

### Explanation

According to the **expected value principle**, the expected profit $E(X)$ for each product is calculated as the sum of each potential profit outcome multiplied by its respective probability of occurrence:

$$E(X) = \sum_{i} (\text{Profit}_i \times \text{Probability}_i)$$

Let us calculate the expected profit for each product:

- **Product A**:
  $$E(A) = (100,000 \times 0.75) + (20,000 \times 0.20) + (-40,000 \times 0.05)$$
  $$E(A) = 75,000 + 4,000 - 2,000 = \$77,000$$

- **Product B**:
  $$E(B) = (130,000 \times 0.60) + (30,000 \times 0.30) + (-20,000 \times 0.10)$$
  $$E(B) = 78,000 + 9,000 - 2,000 = \$85,000$$

- **Product C**:
  $$E(C) = (150,000 \times 0.50) + (40,000 \times 0.30) + (-20,000 \times 0.20)$$
  $$E(C) = 75,000 + 12,000 - 4,000 = \$83,000$$

- **Product D**:
  $$E(D) = (100,000 \times 0.70) + (10,000 \times 0.20) + (-50,000 \times 0.10)$$
  $$E(D) = 70,000 + 2,000 - 5,000 = \$67,000$$

### Summary of Expected Values:
- Product A: **\$77,000**
- Product B: **\$85,000** (Highest)
- Product C: **\$83,000**
- Product D: **\$67,000**

**Product B** yields the highest expected profit (\$85,000). Thus, **b)** is the correct choice.
