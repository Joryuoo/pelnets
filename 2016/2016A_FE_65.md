# 2016A_FE_65
Which of the following is an explanation of application architecture that configures enterprise architecture?

a) It systematically indicates the business processes and information flow necessary for the business strategy
b) It systematically indicates the content of data required for a business operation, the relationship between data, and the structure of data.
c) It systematically indicates the functions and configuration of a system supporting a business process.
d) It systematically indicates the technical components necessary for the development and the operation of the information system.
?
c) It systematically indicates the functions and configuration of a system supporting a business process.
### Explanation
**Enterprise Architecture (EA)** provides a comprehensive framework to align an organization's business strategies with its information technology infrastructure. It is typically divided into four hierarchical architectures:

1. **Business Architecture (BA):**
   * Systematically defines business strategies, organizational structure, business processes, and the flow of information required to achieve business goals. *(Described in option a)*

2. **Data Architecture (DA):**
   * Systematically defines the structure, relationships, contents, and standards of data required for business operations and decision-making (e.g., ER models, data dictionaries). *(Described in option b)*

3. **Application Architecture (AA):**
   * Systematically defines the individual application software systems, their functions, interactions, interfaces, and configurations that support business operations. *(Described in option c)*

4. **Technology Architecture (TA):**
   * Systematically defines the hardware, system software, networking protocols, security platforms, and technical infrastructure required to deploy and operate the applications. *(Described in option d)*

Therefore, **c** is the correct description of **Application Architecture**.
