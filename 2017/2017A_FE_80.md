### Explanation
In an Operating Characteristic (OC) curve for acceptance sampling:
* The **horizontal axis** represents the lot defective rate ($p$).
* The **vertical axis** represents the probability of lot acceptance ($L(p)$).
* **C** is the maximum number of defectives allowed for the lot to pass.

For a fixed sample size $n$, as the acceptance number $C$ increases, the probability of accepting the lot will strictly increase for any given defect rate $p > 0$.
Therefore, the curve for $C=2$ must be entirely above the curve for $C=1$, which in turn must be above the curve for $C=0$. 

Looking at the graphs, only option b shows the acceptance probability correctly ordered, with the highest curve corresponding to $C=2$ and the lowest corresponding to $C=0$.
