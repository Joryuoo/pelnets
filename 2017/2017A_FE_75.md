# 2017A_FE_75
![[2017A_FE_AM_Q75_full.png]]

a) 15

?
c) 25
### Explanation
Based on the Bill of Materials (BOM) structure:
1. Product A requires 2 units of B and 1 unit of C.
2. Component B requires 1 unit of C, 1 unit of D, and 1 unit of E.

To produce 10 units of A:
* Direct requirement of C for A: 1 unit/A × 10 A's = 10 units of C.
* Requirement of B for A: 2 units/A × 10 A's = 20 units of B.
* Indirect requirement of C (via B): 1 unit/B × 20 B's = 20 units of C.

Total requirement of C = 10 (direct) + 20 (indirect) = 30 units.
Since there are 5 units of C already in inventory:
Order quantity = Total requirement - Inventory = 30 - 5 = 25 units.
