# 2017S_FE_AM_56
According to the ISO/IEC 20000-1, which of the following is a service delivery process that the service provider shall agree the services to be delivered and shall agree a catalogue of services with the customer?

a) Capacity management
b) Information security management
c) Service continuity and availability management
d) Service level management
?
d) Service level management
### Explanation
Service Level Management (SLM) is the process responsible for negotiating, agreeing, and documenting appropriate IT service targets with the business, and then monitoring and producing reports on delivery against the agreed level of service. Agreeing on the services to be delivered and the service catalogue falls directly under this process.

- **Capacity management** focuses on ensuring IT infrastructure can meet current and future business performance needs.
- **Information security management** ensures the confidentiality, integrity, and availability of an organization's assets and information.
- **Service continuity and availability management** ensures services can recover from disasters and remain available to meet business needs.
