# 2017S_FE_AM_75
Which of the following is the procurement process that enables suppliers to bid against one another on the Internet to sell their products or services under the conditions specified by a general customer?

a) B to B
b) G to C
c) Reverse auction
d) Virtual mall
?
c) Reverse auction
### Explanation
In a reverse auction, the roles of buyer and seller are reversed. A single buyer requests a product or service, and multiple sellers compete to offer the lowest price or best terms.

- **a) B to B (Business to Business):** Commerce transactions between businesses, such as between a manufacturer and a wholesaler.
- **b) G to C (Government to Citizen):** Communication link between a government and private individuals.
- **d) Virtual mall:** An online website that aggregates various independent sellers in one platform.
