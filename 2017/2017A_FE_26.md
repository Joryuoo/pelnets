d) OrderDetails (OrderNumber, ProductNumber, Quantity, UnitSalesPrice)
### Explanation
During normalization to the Third Normal Form (3NF), we eliminate partial and transitive dependencies.
*   From dependencies (4) and (5), `{OrderNumber, ProductNumber} → Quantity` and `{OrderNumber, ProductNumber} → UnitSalesPrice`.
*   This means `Quantity` and `UnitSalesPrice` are fully functionally dependent on the composite primary key `{OrderNumber, ProductNumber}`.
*   Therefore, the `OrderDetails` relation must include `OrderNumber` and `ProductNumber` as its composite primary key, along with the attributes they functionally determine: `Quantity` and `UnitSalesPrice`.
