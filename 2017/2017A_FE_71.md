# 2017A_FE_71
The sales price of products is decided according to the different sales price setting methods. Which of the following approaches sets a high sales price by using the consumer psychology that high quality equals to high price?

a) Cost plus pricing
b) Differential Pricing
c) Marketing price following pricing
d) Prestige pricing
?
d) Prestige pricing
### Explanation
Prestige pricing is a strategy where prices are set higher than normal because lower prices might hurt the product's image or reputation. It relies on the consumer psychology that equates higher price with higher quality.
* **Cost plus pricing:** Adding a standard markup to the cost of the product.
* **Differential pricing:** Selling the same product to different customers at different prices.
