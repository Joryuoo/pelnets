### Explanation
Postfix notation (also known as reverse Polish notation) expressions are evaluated left-to-right using a stack:
1. Push numbers to the stack: `100`, `5`, `6`, `2`. Stack: [100, 5, 6, 2]
2. Encounter `+`: Pop `2` and `6`. Evaluate `6 + 2 = 8`. Push `8`. Stack: [100, 5, 8]
3. Encounter `*`: Pop `8` and `5`. Evaluate `5 * 8 = 40`. Push `40`. Stack: [100, 40]
4. Push `12` and `4`. Stack: [100, 40, 12, 4]
5. Encounter `/`: Pop `4` and `12`. Evaluate `12 / 4 = 3`. Push `3`. Stack: [100, 40, 3]
6. Encounter `-`: Pop `3` and `40`. Evaluate `40 - 3 = 37`. Push `37`. Stack: [100, 37]
7. Encounter `-`: Pop `37` and `100`. Evaluate `100 - 37 = 63`. Push `63`. Stack: [63]
8. Push `36`. Stack: [63, 36]
9. Encounter `-`: Pop `36` and `63`. Evaluate `63 - 36 = 27`. Push `27`. Stack: [27]

The final evaluated value remaining on the stack is 27.
