# 2017S_FE_61
When data sheets issued by a business operations department are entered in a system in an information system department, which of the following is an appropriate task executed by the information system department?

a) The data sheets are retained in the information system department until the due date for processing, and confirmation including a check of the number of sheets received is performed for all sheets collectively just before the due date.
b) To enable the business operations department to check the processing results of each data sheet, the processing results list is sent to the business operations department.
c) To prevent unauthorized use or leakage of confidential information, data sheets are disposed of immediately after completion of data entry.
d) When the data sheet contains an error, and only when the error is obvious, the data sheet is corrected and an entry is made at the judgment of only the person in charge of data entry.
?
b) To enable the business operations department to check the processing results of each data sheet, the processing results list is sent to the business operations department.
### Explanation
When entering data sheets, it is important to provide feedback on the processing results to the source department (business operations) so they can verify accuracy and handle any errors.
- **a)** is incorrect because accumulating sheets and checking them collectively just before the due date increases the risk of delayed processing and missed errors.
- **c)** is incorrect because data sheets should be retained according to document retention policies for audit and verification purposes, not disposed of immediately.
- **d)** is incorrect because the information system department should not alter data independently, even if the error seems obvious; changes should be confirmed with the business operations department.
