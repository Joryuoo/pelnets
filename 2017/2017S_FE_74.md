# 2017S_FE_AM_74
Which of the following performs processing according to steps (1) through (3)?

(1) Based on the types and quantities as well as the parts list of the product planned to be manufactured in a fixed period of time in the future, the required quantity of components is calculated.
(2) The net order quantity of each component is calculated from the amount of inventory that can be allocated.
(3) The ordering time of the components is determined while considering the manufacturing and procurement lead time.

a) CAD (Computer Aided Design)
b) CRP (Capacity Requirements Planning)
c) JIT (Just In Time)
d) MRP (Material Requirements Planning)
?
d) MRP (Material Requirements Planning)
### Explanation
Material Requirements Planning (MRP) is a production planning, scheduling, and inventory control system used to manage manufacturing processes. It ensures materials are available for production, products are available for delivery to customers, maintains lowest possible inventory levels, and plans manufacturing activities, delivery schedules and purchasing activities.

- **a) CAD:** Software used by architects, engineers, drafters, and artists to create precision drawings or technical illustrations.
- **b) CRP:** The process of determining the amount of labor and machine resources required to accomplish the tasks of production.
- **c) JIT:** An inventory strategy companies employ to increase efficiency and decrease waste by receiving goods only as they are needed in the production process.
