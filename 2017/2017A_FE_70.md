d) Customer value (value for customers) 
### Explanation
The 4Ps and 4Cs of the marketing mix correspond to each other (Seller's viewpoint vs. Buyer's viewpoint):

- **P**roduct $\rightarrow$ **C**ustomer Value (A)
- **P**romotion $\rightarrow$ **C**ommunication (D)
- **P**lace $\rightarrow$ **C**onvenience (C)
- **P**rice $\rightarrow$ **C**ost (B)

Blank A corresponds to Product, which translates to Customer Value (value for customers) from the buyer's perspective.
