# 2017S_FE_AM_79
Which of the following is a method of making decisions based on the discount rate at which the net present value of the payback amount and the investment amount are equal?

a) Internal Rate of Return method
b) Net Present Value method
c) Payback Period method
d) Return on Investment method
?
a) Internal Rate of Return method
### Explanation
The Internal Rate of Return (IRR) is a metric used in capital budgeting to estimate the profitability of potential investments. It is a discount rate that makes the Net Present Value (NPV) of all cash flows from a particular project equal to zero. In other words, it is the rate at which the present value of the payback amounts equals the initial investment amount.

- **b) Net Present Value (NPV):** Evaluates the difference between the present value of cash inflows and outflows using a predetermined discount rate.
- **c) Payback Period:** Calculates the time it takes for an investment to generate an amount of income or cash equal to the cost of the investment.
- **d) Return on Investment (ROI):** A ratio that calculates profitability by dividing net profit by total cost of investment.
