# 2017S_FE_69
Which of the following is a marketing technique to create demands from consumers by using advertisements that improve the brand image of a company or a product?

a) Direct marketing
b) Relationship marketing
c) Pull strategy
d) Push strategy
?
c) Pull strategy
### Explanation
A **Pull strategy** in marketing involves "pulling" consumer interest and demand toward a product through advertising, mass media, and brand-building. Consumers then seek out the product from retailers.
- **a) Direct marketing:** Reaching out directly to the consumer (e.g., via email, mail, or telemarketing) rather than through intermediaries.
- **b) Relationship marketing:** Focusing on long-term customer engagement and loyalty rather than short-term sales.
- **d) Push strategy:** "Pushing" products through the distribution channel using sales force and trade promotions to get wholesalers and retailers to stock the product.
