### Explanation
The question asks for a logic circuit that outputs `1` only when inputs `X` and `Y` have the same value. This describes an XNOR (Exclusive NOR) gate, also known as an Equivalence gate.

- An **XOR** gate outputs `1` when inputs are different.
- An **XNOR** gate is the inverse of XOR and outputs `1` when inputs are the same (`0,0` or `1,1`).

Looking at the choices provided in the image, choice (c) depicts the standard logic symbol for an XNOR gate (an XOR shape with an inversion bubble at the output).
