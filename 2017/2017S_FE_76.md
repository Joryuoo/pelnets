# 2017S_FE_AM_76
Which of the following is a management organization that has a characteristic of both project and functional organizations to flexibly respond to changes in the business environment?

a) Divisional organization
b) Hierarchical organization
c) Line and staff organization
d) Matrix organization
?
d) Matrix organization
### Explanation
A Matrix Organization combines both functional and project-based structures. Employees have dual reporting relationships - generally to both a functional manager (e.g., IT manager) and a product or project manager. This allows for flexible resource allocation and responsiveness to changes.

- **a) Divisional organization:** Structured around product lines, markets, or geographies.
- **b) Hierarchical organization:** Traditional top-down structure with clear levels of authority.
- **c) Line and staff organization:** Direct reporting relationships with staff specialists acting in advisory capacities.
