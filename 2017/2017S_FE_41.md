# 2017S_FE_41
Bob and Alice encrypt all of their communications with each other. Because of the high level of sensitivity of the information they are sharing, they have implemented a cryptographic mechanism that offers secrecy to their messages. However, the trade-off of this encryption is that it is impossible for Bob or Alice to be 100% sure that they are communicating with each other, nor can they be 100% certain of the integrity of the content of the messages. Which of the following types of encryption methods are Bob and Alice using?

a) AES (Advanced Encryption Standard)
b) DES (Data Encryption Standard)
c) One-time pad
d) Stream cipher
?
c) One-time pad
### Explanation
A **One-time pad** (OTP) is an encryption technique that provides perfect secrecy if the key is random, exactly as long as the plaintext, never reused, and kept completely secret. However, OTP itself does not provide message authentication or integrity checks; it only encrypts the data. An attacker could potentially alter the ciphertext (e.g., via a bit-flipping attack) and the receiver wouldn't know, nor does it inherently verify the identity of the sender without additional mechanisms like MACs or digital signatures. 

* **a) AES and b) DES:** These are block ciphers. While standard block ciphers also don't inherently provide integrity without specific modes of operation (like GCM), One-Time Pad is famously the textbook mechanism defined strictly by "perfect secrecy" but absolute lack of inherent integrity or authentication.
* **d) Stream cipher:** Similar to OTP in implementation (XORing a stream with plaintext), but uses a pseudo-random key stream rather than a truly random key, meaning it does not offer "perfect secrecy".
