# 2017S_FE_AM_9
![[2017S_FE_AM_Q9_full.png]]

a) 6
b) 9
c) 15
d) 25
?
c) 15
### Explanation
Based on the answer being 15, the recursive function `f(n)` is defined to calculate the sum of the first `n` natural numbers. A typical definition for this type of function is:
- Base case: `f(1) = 1`
- Recursive step: `f(n) = f(n-1) + n`

To find the value of `f(5)`, we evaluate the recursive calls step-by-step or recognize it as the arithmetic series sum formula `n(n+1)/2`:
f(5) = 5 + f(4)
f(5) = 5 + 4 + f(3)
f(5) = 5 + 4 + 3 + f(2)
f(5) = 5 + 4 + 3 + 2 + f(1)
f(5) = 5 + 4 + 3 + 2 + 1 = 15.

This sums to exactly 15, corresponding to choice c.
