# 2017S_FE_AM_59
Which of the following is an appropriate activity that is undertaken by a system auditor who audits access control?

a) Confirmation of the management status of access control concerning data
b) Creation and storage of a management table for access control concerning software
c) Establishment of a management policy for access control concerning the network
d) Implementation of operations for access control concerning hardware
?
a) Confirmation of the management status of access control concerning data
### Explanation
A system auditor's primary role is an independent, objective assurance and consulting activity designed to add value and improve an organization's operations. An auditor evaluates, reviews, and confirms the adequacy and effectiveness of controls. 

- **Choice a** is correct because "confirmation" and evaluation of the management status of access controls is a typical auditing activity.
- **Choices b, c, and d** describe operational or management tasks (creation, establishment, implementation). An auditor should not design, implement, or operate the systems or controls they are auditing to maintain independence and objectivity.
