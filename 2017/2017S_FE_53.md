# 2017S_FE_AM_53
According to PMBOK, which of the following is an appropriate explanation concerning the scope creep in project scope management?

a) A hierarchical decomposition of the total scope of work to be carried out by the project team to accomplish the project objectives and create the required deliverables.
b) Any change to the project scope. It almost always requires an adjustment to the project cost or schedule.
c) The sum of the products, services, and results to be provided as a project.
d) The uncontrolled expansion to product or project scope without adjustments to time, cost, and resources.
?
d) The uncontrolled expansion to product or project scope without adjustments to time, cost, and resources.
### Explanation
Scope creep refers to the uncontrolled expansion to product or project scope without adjustments to time, cost, and resources. It often occurs when new requirements are added without proper review and approval through the integrated change control process.

- **Choice a** describes the Work Breakdown Structure (WBS).
- **Choice b** describes a scope change, which is formally approved and includes adjustments to cost or schedule.
- **Choice c** defines the overall project scope.
