# 2017S_FE_AM_1
For an 8-bit binary number, which of the following sets the middle 4 bits to 1s while inverting the remaining bits?

a) Performing a bitwise AND operation with 00111100
b) Performing a bitwise NAND operation with 11000011
c) Performing a bitwise OR operation with 11000011
d) Performing a bitwise XOR operation with 00111100
?
b) Performing a bitwise NAND operation with 11000011
### Explanation
The NAND operation is the negation of the AND operation (`x NAND y = NOT (x AND y)`). Let's evaluate its effect on a bit `x` based on the bit `y` from a mask:
- When the mask bit is `0`: `x NAND 0 = NOT (x AND 0) = NOT (0) = 1`. This effectively sets the bit to `1`.
- When the mask bit is `1`: `x NAND 1 = NOT (x AND 1) = NOT (x)`. This effectively inverts the bit.

The question requires setting the middle 4 bits to `1` (which requires `0`s in the mask) and inverting the remaining 4 outer bits (which requires `1`s in the mask).
Therefore, the correct mask must have `0`s in the middle 4 bits and `1`s in the outer bits, which corresponds to the binary sequence `11000011`.
Performing a bitwise NAND operation with `11000011` achieves exactly this outcome.
