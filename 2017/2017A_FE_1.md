# 2017A_FE_1
What is the octal representation of a hexadecimal fraction F1B0.C?

a) 170660.6
b) 361540.14
c) 743300.6
d) 5213052.3
?
a) 170660.6
### Explanation
To convert a hexadecimal fraction to octal, we first convert it to binary:
* `F` = `1111`
* `1` = `0001`
* `B` = `1011`
* `0` = `0000`
* `.C` = `.1100`

So, `F1B0.C` in binary is `1111000110110000.1100`.
To convert binary to octal, group the bits into 3s from the binary point, padding with zeros at the ends if necessary:
* Integer part: `001 111 000 110 110 000` = `170660`
* Fractional part: `.110` = `.6`

Combining them, we get `170660.6`. Therefore, the correct answer is **a**.
