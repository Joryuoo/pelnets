# 2017A_FE_AM_57
Which of the following device is used for protecting power supply circuits, communication devices, or other equipment from the overvoltage and the over current generated from the overvoltage?

a) AVR
b) CVCF
c) SPD
d) UPS
?
c) SPD
### Explanation
An **SPD (Surge Protective Device)** is used to protect electrical installations and communication devices from voltage spikes or overvoltage by limiting transient voltages and diverting surge currents.

* **AVR (Automatic Voltage Regulator):** A device that maintains a constant voltage level automatically, but is not specifically for surge overvoltage protection.
* **CVCF (Constant Voltage Constant Frequency):** A power supply that provides stable voltage and frequency, often forming part of a UPS system.
* **UPS (Uninterruptible Power Supply):** Provides emergency power to a load when the input power source fails. While some UPS devices include surge protection, an SPD's primary and specific purpose is dealing with overvoltage/surges.
