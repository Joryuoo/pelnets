# 2017A_FE_AM_58
Which of the following is an appropriate precaution to be observed when monitoring the use of the capacity and performance of an online system in the capacity management process of IT service management?

a) Incidents concerning capacity and performance are recorded.
b) Multiple measurement items such as the response time and CPU utilization are monitored on a regular basis.
c) Only the maximum value of performance data during a fixed period is focused upon, and deviation from the management limit is checked.
d) Since the measurement of performance during online hours causes a decline in service level, measurement is performed during offline hours.
?
b) Multiple measurement items such as the response time and CPU utilization are monitored on a regular basis.
### Explanation
In **capacity management**, it is essential to monitor multiple aspects of the system (such as CPU, memory, storage utilization, and response times) continuously or on a regular basis to identify trends, predict future needs, and ensure performance meets service levels.

* **Choice A** refers more to the Incident Management process, although capacity-related incidents do feed into capacity management.
* **Choice C** is incorrect because focusing *only* on maximum values ignores averages, trends, and normal operational baselines which are critical for capacity planning.
* **Choice D** is incorrect because performance must be monitored during online hours (peak usage) to truly understand the capacity constraints and user experience.
