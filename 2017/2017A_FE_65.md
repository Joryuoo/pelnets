# 2017A_FE_AM_65
![[2017A_FE_AM_Q65_full.png]]

a) It is necessary for A and B to make the same amount of annual profit. 
b) It is necessary for A to make twice the annual profit than that of B. 
c) It is necessary for B to make 1.5 times the annual profit than that of A. 
d) It is necessary for B to make twice the annual profit that of A. 
?
d) It is necessary for B to make twice the annual profit that of A. 
### Explanation
To calculate the minimum amount of annual profit necessary to recover the investment within the payout period, divide the total amount of investment by the payout period.

- **Device A:** $900,000 \text{ yen} \div 3 \text{ years} = 300,000 \text{ yen/year}$
- **Device B:** $3,000,000 \text{ yen} \div 5 \text{ years} = 600,000 \text{ yen/year}$

Comparing the two: $600,000$ (B) is exactly twice the amount of $300,000$ (A). Therefore, it is necessary for B to make twice the annual profit that of A.
