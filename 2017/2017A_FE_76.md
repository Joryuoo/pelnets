# 2017A_FE_76
To secure transparency of business administration, which of the following is a concept that supervises and monitors a company’s activities from the perspective of questions such as for whom the company runs its business, what the structure of the top management is like, and whether the organization has self-improvement capabilities?

a) Core competence
b) Corporate governance
c) Corporate identity
d) Stakeholder analysis
?
b) Corporate governance
### Explanation
**Corporate governance** is the system of rules, practices, and processes by which a company is directed and controlled. It involves balancing the interests of a company's many stakeholders and ensuring transparency, accountability, and ethical business administration.
