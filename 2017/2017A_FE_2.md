# 2017A_FE_2
When two unbiased coins are tossed and an unbiased six-sided die is rolled at the same time, what is the probability that at least one coin shows heads and the die shows an even number?

a) 1/4
b) 3/8
c) 1/2
d) 3/4
?
b) 3/8
### Explanation
This problem asks for the probability of two independent events occurring together:
1.  **Event A (at least one coin shows heads):**
    The possible outcomes of tossing two coins are HH, HT, TH, TT. The outcomes with at least one head are HH, HT, and TH. 
    Probability of Event A = 3/4
2.  **Event B (die shows an even number):**
    The possible outcomes of rolling a six-sided die are 1, 2, 3, 4, 5, 6. The even numbers are 2, 4, and 6. 
    Probability of Event B = 3/6 = 1/2

Since tossing coins and rolling a die are independent events, we multiply their probabilities to find the probability of both occurring:
`P(A and B) = P(A) * P(B) = (3/4) * (1/2) = 3/8`
Therefore, the correct answer is **b**.
