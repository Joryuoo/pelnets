Objective function 2000x + 800y  minimize
?
b) Conditions  8x + 10y ≥ 60,   6x + 3y ≤ 40,    x ≥ 0,   y ≥ 0
Objective function 2000x + 800y  minimize
### Explanation
Let $x$ be the number of TV ads and $y$ be the number of radio ads.
Total exposure in the city from TV and radio ads = $8x + 10y$.
Condition: "at least 60 points in the city" means $8x + 10y \ge 60$.

Total exposure outside the city from TV and radio ads = $6x + 3y$.
Condition: "at most 40 points outside the city" means $6x + 3y \le 40$.

Total cost to be minimized = $2000x + 800y$.

Thus, choice **b** correctly reflects the constraints:
$8x + 10y \ge 60$
$6x + 3y \le 40$
