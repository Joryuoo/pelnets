# 2017S_FE_AM_57
According to ITIL, which of the following is an activity or process in the Service Transition?

a) Assessing the IT recovery options of the new IT infrastructure considering the business risks and cost to the project
b) Classifying events once it is detected and communicating specific information to corresponding groups as an immediate action
c) Defining the reliability and service life of the IT infrastructure considering projected growth in the service
d) Implementing multiple strategies such as web seminars, newsletters, and visualization in transferring knowledge
?
d) Implementing multiple strategies such as web seminars, newsletters, and visualization in transferring knowledge
### Explanation
In the ITIL framework, **Service Transition** involves building, testing, and deploying new or changed services into production. A key process within Service Transition is **Knowledge Management**, which involves gathering, analyzing, storing, and sharing knowledge and information within an organization to improve efficiency. Transferring knowledge via web seminars, newsletters, and visualization fits directly into this process.

- **Choice a** relates to IT Service Continuity Management (part of Service Design).
- **Choice b** relates to Event Management (part of Service Operation).
- **Choice c** relates to Capacity and Availability Management (part of Service Design).
