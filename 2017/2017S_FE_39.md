# 2017S_FE_AM_39
An attacker calls a corporate help desk, masquerading as an employee who has lost their password. The help desk staff resets the password to the company default "password1." The attacker then uses this password to access the company network and information in the server. What is this type of attack called?

a) Buffer overflow attack
b) Denial-of-Service attack
c) Shoulder surfing
d) Social engineering
?
d) Social engineering
### Explanation
**Social engineering** involves manipulating people into divulging confidential information or performing actions that compromise security. In this scenario, the attacker manipulated the help desk staff over the phone (a common social engineering technique called pretexting or vishing).

- **a) Buffer overflow attack**: Exploiting a software vulnerability by writing more data to a buffer than it can hold.
- **b) Denial-of-Service attack**: Flooding a system with traffic to make it unavailable to legitimate users.
- **c) Shoulder surfing**: Physically looking over someone's shoulder to steal sensitive information like a password or PIN.
