# 2017A_FE_AM_43
Which of the following is an appropriate term for an organized and highly skilled team, whose mission is to continuously monitor and improve the organization’s security posture while preventing, detecting, analyzing, and responding to cyber security incidents with the aid of both technology and well-defined processes and procedures?

a) Cybersecurity
b) Incident Management Center (IMC)
c) Network Operations Center (NOC)
d) Security Operations Center (SOC)
?
d) Security Operations Center (SOC)
### Explanation
A **Security Operations Center (SOC)** is a centralized function within an organization employing people, processes, and technology to continuously monitor and improve an organization's security posture while preventing, detecting, analyzing, and responding to cybersecurity incidents.

* **Cybersecurity** is the practice itself, not the term for the organized team or center.
* **Network Operations Center (NOC)** focuses on monitoring and managing the overall health, performance, and availability of an organization's IT network, rather than specifically focusing on security threats.
