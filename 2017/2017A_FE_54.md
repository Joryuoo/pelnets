# 2017A_FE_AM_54
Company A is going to launch a new software product and the sales team then has to demonstrate it to the customers. There is a risk that the sales team does not understand the product and cannot provide good demonstrations. As a consequence, the sales team will make fewer sales and there will be less revenue for the company. So, the management decided to provide good training for the sales team. Which of the following is the best method to manage the risk?

a) Risk acceptance
b) Risk exploitation
c) Risk mitigation
d) Risk transfer
?
c) Risk mitigation
### Explanation
**Risk mitigation** (or reduction) is the strategy of taking proactive steps to reduce the probability of occurrence or the impact of a negative risk. Providing training to the sales team reduces the likelihood that they will give poor demonstrations, effectively mitigating the risk.

* **Risk acceptance:** Acknowledging the risk but taking no proactive action to avoid it.
* **Risk exploitation:** An aggressive strategy for positive risks (opportunities) to ensure they happen.
* **Risk transfer:** Shifting the impact of a risk to a third party (e.g., buying insurance or outsourcing).
