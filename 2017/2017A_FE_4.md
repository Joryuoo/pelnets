# 2017A_FE_4
Analog audio data is being sampled and converted to 8-bit digital audio data using the PCM method, then transmitted in 64,000 bits per second. What is the sampling interval in microseconds?

a) 15.6
b) 46.8
c) 125
d) 128
?
c) 125
### Explanation
We are given:
*   Transmission rate = 64,000 bits per second (bps)
*   Sample size = 8 bits per sample

First, find the **sampling rate** (samples per second) by dividing the transmission rate by the sample size:
*   `64,000 bits/second ÷ 8 bits/sample = 8,000 samples/second` (This is 8 kHz)

Next, find the **sampling interval**, which is the time between each sample (the reciprocal of the sampling rate):
*   `1 ÷ 8,000 seconds = 0.000125 seconds`

Convert this to microseconds by multiplying by 1,000,000:
*   `0.000125 × 1,000,000 = 125 microseconds`

Therefore, the correct answer is **c**.
