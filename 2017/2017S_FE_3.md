# 2017S_FE_AM_3
![[2017S_FE_AM_Q3_full.png]]
?
a) 1/26
### Explanation
There are 16 cards in total, numbered 1 to 16. Out of these, exactly half (8 cards) are even numbers (2, 4, 6, 8, 10, 12, 14, 16).
We want to find the probability of choosing 4 even cards out of the 16 cards when 4 cards are drawn randomly at the same time.

First, calculate the total number of ways to choose any 4 cards out of 16 (the denominator):
C(16, 4) = (16 × 15 × 14 × 13) / (4 × 3 × 2 × 1) = 1820.

Next, calculate the number of ways to choose 4 even cards out of the 8 available even cards (the numerator):
C(8, 4) = (8 × 7 × 6 × 5) / (4 × 3 × 2 × 1) = 70.

The probability is the ratio of these combinations:
P = 70 / 1820 = 7 / 182 = 1 / 26.

Therefore, the correct answer is a.
