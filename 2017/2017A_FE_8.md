# 2017A_FE_8
There are two boxes; the first box contains two green balls and three red balls; the second box contains three green balls and two red balls. A boy randomly selects a box from the two, and then randomly picks a ball from that box. If the ball is red, what is the probability that he selected the first box?

a) 3/20
b) 3/10
c) 1/2
d) 3/5
?
d) 3/5
### Explanation
We use **Bayes' Theorem** to find the probability of picking the first box given that the ball drawn is red. Let $B_1$ be Box 1, $B_2$ be Box 2, and $R$ be picking a red ball.

1. **Calculate the prior probabilities:**
   * $P(B_1) = 1/2$
   * $P(B_2) = 1/2$

2. **Calculate the conditional probabilities of picking a red ball:**
   * $P(R | B_1) = 3/5$ (3 red balls out of 5 total in Box 1)
   * $P(R | B_2) = 2/5$ (2 red balls out of 5 total in Box 2)

3. **Find the total probability of picking a red ball:**
   * $P(R) = P(B_1) \cdot P(R | B_1) + P(B_2) \cdot P(R | B_2)$
   * $P(R) = (1/2 \cdot 3/5) + (1/2 \cdot 2/5) = 3/10 + 2/10 = 5/10 = 1/2$

4. **Apply Bayes' Theorem to find $P(B_1 | R)$:**
   * $P(B_1 | R) = \frac{P(B_1) \cdot P(R | B_1)}{P(R)}$
   * $P(B_1 | R) = \frac{3/10}{1/2} = \frac{3}{10} \cdot 2 = \frac{6}{10} = \frac{3}{5}$

*(Note: While some distributed answer keys might contain typos for this question, the mathematically correct probability is 3/5).*
