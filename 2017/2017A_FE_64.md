# 2017A_FE_AM_64
Which of the following is a purpose of Business Process Management (BPM) in corporate activities? 

a) Analysis and effective use of information resources 
b) Conservation and analysis of customer information 
c) Continuous improvement of business processes 
d) Effective use of management resources 
?
c) Continuous improvement of business processes 
### Explanation
Business Process Management (BPM) focuses on continuously analyzing, improving, and optimizing a company's business processes. It involves a cyclical approach (such as PDCA) to enhance efficiency, effectiveness, and adaptability to changing environments.

- Choice a describes Information Resource Management.
- Choice b describes Customer Relationship Management (CRM).
- Choice d describes Enterprise Resource Planning (ERP).
