# 2017S_FE_AM_51
According to the Capability Maturity Model Integration, which is the final stage and has the highest level of maturity?

a) Auditable
b) Custom
c) Optimized
d) Quantitative
?
c) Optimized
### Explanation
In the Capability Maturity Model Integration (CMMI), the maturity levels are:
1. Initial: Unpredictable, poorly controlled, and reactive.
2. Managed: Processes characterized for projects and is often reactive.
3. Defined: Processes characterized for the organization and is proactive.
4. Quantitatively Managed: Processes measured and controlled.
5. Optimizing (Optimized): Focus on process improvement.

Therefore, "Optimized" is the final stage with the highest level of maturity.
- **Auditable** and **Custom** are not valid CMMI maturity levels.
- **Quantitative** corresponds to Level 4, not the final level.
