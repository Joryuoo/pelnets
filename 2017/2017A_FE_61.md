# 2017A_FE_AM_61
Which of the following situation is judged as appropriate when conducting an audit on a Business Continuity Plan (BCP)? 

a) A BCP having the same standard level is applied to all business operations without any prioritization. 
b) An emergency contact list of employees is created and updated to the latest version. 
c) During normal times, the BCP is not disclosed to employees. 
d) The important documents are stored collectively at one place without making copies. 
?
b) An emergency contact list of employees is created and updated to the latest version. 
### Explanation
A Business Continuity Plan (BCP) is a document that outlines how a business will continue operating during an unplanned disruption in service. 

- Creating and keeping the emergency contact list of employees updated ensures that personnel can be quickly reached during an emergency. This is a critical and appropriate measure.
- Choice a is incorrect because business operations should be prioritized based on criticality to ensure resources are focused on the most vital functions first.
- Choice c is incorrect because a BCP must be disclosed to employees so they are aware of their roles and procedures during an emergency.
- Choice d is incorrect because important documents should be backed up and stored in multiple locations (e.g., offsite) to prevent total loss in case of a disaster at one location.
