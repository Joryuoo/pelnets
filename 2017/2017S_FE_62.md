# 2017S_FE_62
Which of the following is an appropriate feasibility evaluation when checking whether the lifetime benefits of the proposed information system is greater than its lifetime costs?

a) Economic feasibility
b) Operational feasibility
c) Scheduling feasibility
d) Technical feasibility
?
a) Economic feasibility
### Explanation
**Economic feasibility** refers to analyzing the cost-effectiveness of a project, specifically comparing its expected lifetime benefits against its lifetime costs to ensure financial viability.
- **b) Operational feasibility:** Assesses whether the system will be used effectively and solve the business problems.
- **c) Scheduling feasibility:** Evaluates if the project can be completed within the required timeframe.
- **d) Technical feasibility:** Determines if the organization has the necessary technology, resources, and expertise to develop and support the proposed system.
