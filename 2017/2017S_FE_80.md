# 2017S_FE_AM_80
Which of the following is not protected by “copyright” that is based on the World Trade Organization’s Agreement on Trade-Related Aspects of Intellectual Property Rights (TRIPS)?

a) Database
b) Free software publicly available on the Internet
c) Programming language and its conventions
d) Software operation manual
?
c) Programming language and its conventions
### Explanation
Copyright protects the expression of an idea, not the idea itself. Under TRIPS and international copyright laws:
- **Programming languages, syntax, algorithms, and conventions** are considered ideas, concepts, or systems, and are not protected by copyright.
- **a) Database:** Protected under copyright as a compilation of data if there is creativity in selection and arrangement.
- **b) Free software:** Still protected by copyright, even if it is distributed freely under open-source licenses.
- **d) Software operation manual:** Protected as a written literary work.
