# 2017A_FE_77
Which of the following items should be put under “Liabilities and Stockholder’s Equity” in a balance sheet?

a) Cash
b) Common stock
c) Fixed assets
d) Inventories
?
b) Common stock
### Explanation
A balance sheet is divided into two main sections: Assets, and Liabilities & Stockholder's Equity. The accounting equation states that Assets = Liabilities + Stockholder's Equity.
* **Common stock** represents ownership equity and is placed under "Stockholder's Equity".
* **Cash**, **Fixed assets**, and **Inventories** are all considered "Assets" and placed on the other side of the balance sheet.
