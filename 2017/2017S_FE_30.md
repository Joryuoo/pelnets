# 2017S_FE_30
Among the phases of database design, which of the following phases involves the creation of an ER diagram?

a) Conceptual design of databases phase
b) Distributed design of databases phase
c) Logical design of databases phase
d) Physical design of databases phase
?
a) Conceptual design of databases phase
### Explanation
An Entity-Relationship (ER) diagram is created during the **conceptual design phase**. This phase focuses on mapping business concepts, entities, and their relationships at a high level without concern for the specific Relational Database Management System (RDBMS) being used.

- **Logical design phase**: The ER model is mapped to a relational schema (defining tables, primary keys, foreign keys) specific to an RDBMS type (e.g., relational, object-oriented).
- **Physical design phase**: Focuses on performance and storage details such as indexes, partitioning, and exact data types.
