### Explanation

The input string is `"Apple apple red apple green apple"`.
Let's trace the `analyse` and `setCount` functions for each word in this sequence.

1. **"Apple"**
   - It's a new word (currently `wordCount` is 0).
   - Added to `word[0]`. `frequency` becomes 1.
   - `wordCount` becomes 1. 
   - `highestFreq` is set to 1.
   - Statement `β` (copying string) executes: **1st time**.

2. **"apple"**
   - It's a new word (case-sensitive, so "Apple" is different from "apple").
   - Added to `word[1]`. `frequency` becomes 1.
   - `wordCount` becomes 2.
   - Statement `β` executes: **2nd time**.

3. **"red"**
   - It's a new word.
   - Added to `word[2]`. `frequency` becomes 1.
   - `wordCount` becomes 3.
   - Statement `β` executes: **3rd time**.

4. **"apple"**
   - This word already exists at index 1.
   - `word[1].frequency` increments from 1 to 2.
   - `highestFreq` is currently 1. Since `2 > 1`, `highestFreq` is updated to 2.
   - Statement `α` (updating `highestFreq`) executes: **1st time**.

5. **"green"**
   - It's a new word.
   - Added to `word[3]`. `frequency` becomes 1.
   - `wordCount` becomes 4.
   - Statement `β` executes: **4th time**.

6. **"apple"**
   - This word already exists at index 1.
   - `word[1].frequency` increments from 2 to 3.
   - `highestFreq` is currently 2. Since `3 > 2`, `highestFreq` is updated to 3.
   - Statement `α` executes: **2nd time**.

Therefore:
- **Blank F (Statement α):** Executes 2 times in total. The correct answer is **b) 2**.
- **Blank G (Statement β):** Executes 4 times in total. The correct answer is **d) 4**.
