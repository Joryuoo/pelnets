### Explanation

**Semaphore Operations Overview:**
- `wait(S)`: Decrements `S` by 1. If `S < 0`, the process is blocked (placed in the wait queue).
- `signal(S)`: Increments `S` by 1. If `S <= 0`, one blocked process is unblocked (moved from the wait queue to the ready queue).

**Step-by-Step Trace for Figures 5 to 7:**

1. **Figure 5 (Initial State):**
   - **Running Process:** P3
   - **Semaphore (S):** 0
   - **Wait Queue:** Empty
   - **Ready Queue:** R, P1, P2 (with R at the front)

2. **Figure 6 (After P3 executes `wait(S)`):**
   - P3 executes `wait(S)`. The value of `S` is decremented by 1 (`0 - 1 = -1`).
     - **Blank B is `S = -1` (choice a)**.
   - Because `S < 0` (`-1 < 0`), P3 is blocked and placed into the wait queue.
     - **Blank A is `P3` (choice c)**.
   - Process R is picked up from the front of the ready queue to run. The remaining processes in the ready queue are P1 and P2.
     - **Blank C is `P2 P1` (choice d)**.

3. **Figure 7 (After R executes `signal(S)`):**
   - R executes `signal(S)` at the end of its execution. The value of `S` is incremented by 1 (`-1 + 1 = 0`).
   - Because `S <= 0` (`0 <= 0`), one blocked process is unblocked. P3 is removed from the wait queue and placed at the back of the ready queue.
   - Process P1 (which was at the front of the ready queue) is picked up to run.
   - The processes now in the ready queue are P2 (which was already there) and P3 (which was just unblocked). 
     - **Blank D is `P3 P2` (choice e)**.
