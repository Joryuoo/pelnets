### Explanation
**Blank A:**
The variable `I` represents the current length of the rod being evaluated, which iterates from `1` to `N`. `J` represents the length of the first piece being cut off to solve the subproblem of size `I`. Since a piece cannot be longer than the rod itself, `J` can range from `1` up to `I`. Thus, `J ≤ I`. The correct answer is **a**.

**Blank B:**
The algorithm calculates the revenue of cutting a piece of size `J`. The total revenue is the price of that piece (`P[J]`) plus the optimal revenue for the remaining rod length. The remaining length is `I - J`. The optimal revenue for this remaining length is retrieved from `R[I - J]`. Thus, the expression is `P[J] + R[I - J]`. The correct answer is **b**.

**Blank C:**
The `displayPiece()` subprogram loops to print the sizes of the pieces for the optimal solution. It prints `S[N]`, which holds the optimal size of the first piece to cut from a rod of length `N`. After cutting this piece off, the length of the rod is reduced by `S[N]`. Therefore, the new length `N` is updated to `N - S[N]`. The correct answer is **b**.
