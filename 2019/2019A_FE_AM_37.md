# 2019A_FE_AM_37
Which of the following is an attack that threatens the “integrity” of information?

a) A DoS attack by which the system is set to an overloaded state
b) Falsification of a web page
c) Tapping of communication content
d) Unauthorized copying of data saved in a system
?
b) Falsification of a web page
### Explanation
In information security, the CIA triad stands for Confidentiality, Integrity, and Availability:
* **Integrity** is the assurance that information remains accurate and unaltered by unauthorized parties. **Falsification of a web page** alters data, thereby violating integrity.
* **Confidentiality** prevents unauthorized access to data. **Tapping communication content** and **unauthorized copying of data** are threats to confidentiality.
* **Availability** ensures data and services are accessible to authorized users. A **DoS (Denial of Service) attack** is a threat to availability.
