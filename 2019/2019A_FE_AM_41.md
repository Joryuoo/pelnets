# 2019A_FE_AM_41
In ISO/IEC 27000:2018 (Information Security Management Systems – Overview and Vocabulary), which of the following is defined as the “property that an entity is what it claims to be”?

a) Accountability
b) Authenticity
c) Nonrepudiation
d) Reliability
?
b) Authenticity
### Explanation
In ISO/IEC 27000:2018, **authenticity** is defined as the "property that an entity is what it claims to be." It ensures that a subject or object is indeed who or what it claims to be.
- **Accountability** ensures actions of an entity can be traced uniquely to that entity.
- **Nonrepudiation** proves that an event or action took place, preventing the subjects from denying their involvement.
- **Reliability** is the property of consistent intended behavior and results.
