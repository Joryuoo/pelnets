# 2019A_FE_AM_16
There exists a device with an MTBF of forty-five (45) hours and an MTTR of five (5) hours. What is the availability of a system that connects two (2) such devices in series?

a) 0.81
b) 0.90
c) 0.95
d) 0.99
?
a) 0.81
### Explanation
First, we find the availability ($A$) of a single device.
Availability $A$ = MTBF / (MTBF + MTTR)
$A = 45 / (45 + 5) = 45 / 50 = 0.9$

For a system composed of two devices connected in **series**, the entire system functions only if *both* devices function.
The availability of the series system is the product of the individual availabilities:
$A_{system} = A_{device1} \times A_{device2}$
$A_{system} = 0.9 \times 0.9 = 0.81$

Therefore, the availability of the system is 0.81.
