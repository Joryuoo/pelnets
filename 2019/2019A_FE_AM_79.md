# 2019A_FE_AM_79
Which of the following inventory valuation methods calculates the inventory unit price by dividing the total purchase amount by the total quantity of items purchased at the end of the accounting period?

a) First-in- first-out method
b) Last-in- first-out method
c) Moving average method
d) Periodic average method
?
d) Periodic average method
### Explanation
The periodic average method (also known as the weighted average cost method) calculates the unit price by dividing the total cost of goods available for sale by the total number of units available for sale at the end of an accounting period. 
- In contrast, the moving average method re-computes the average unit price immediately after every new purchase during the period.
- The First-in-first-out (FIFO) and Last-in-first-out (LIFO) methods do not use an overall average unit price for ending inventory valuation but base costs on the chronological order of purchases.
