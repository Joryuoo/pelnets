# 2019A_FE_AM_26
Which of the following is the most appropriate explanation of augmented reality (AR)?

a) By appealing to the five senses through equipment, such as a head-mounted display, a person can experience locations or worlds that do not actually exist as if they were real.
b) By constructing a 3D virtual space that is natural to humans, and changing the virtual space in accordance with a person’s movement, he or she can feel as if he or she is in that place.
c) By seamlessly interweaving a computer-generated information with the physical world, the viewer can perceive it as an immersive aspect of the real environment.
d) By viewing a video recorded in the past, the viewer can feel as if he or she were in that era and at that location.
?
c) By seamlessly interweaving a computer-generated information with the physical world, the viewer can perceive it as an immersive aspect of the real environment.
### Explanation
Augmented Reality (AR) enhances the real world by overlaying or interweaving digital content (such as computer-generated images or information) onto the user's view of their physical surroundings in real-time.

a) Describes Virtual Reality (VR), which completely replaces the real world with a simulated environment.
b) Describes another aspect of Virtual Reality (VR) or virtual environments.
d) Simply describes watching a historical or recorded video.
