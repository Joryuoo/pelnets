# 2019A_FE_AM_33
Which of the following is the maximum theoretical data rate (in Mbps) achievable in the IEEE 802.11n network?

a) 11
b) 54
c) 248
d) 600
?
d) 600
### Explanation
IEEE 802.11 wireless network standards have different maximum data rates:
* **802.11b:** Up to 11 Mbps
* **802.11a / 802.11g:** Up to 54 Mbps
* **802.11n (Wi-Fi 4):** Up to 600 Mbps using MIMO (Multiple-Input Multiple-Output) technology with 4 spatial streams.
* **802.11ac (Wi-Fi 5):** Can reach over 1 Gbps (up to ~6.93 Gbps theoretically).

Thus, the maximum theoretical data rate for 802.11n is 600 Mbps.
