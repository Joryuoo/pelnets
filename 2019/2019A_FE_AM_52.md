d) 102
### Explanation
First, we calculate the number of person-days required to create all the screens based on their size and complexity:
- Small and Low: 30 screens × 0.4 person-days/screen = 12 person-days
- Medium and Medium: 40 screens × 0.9 person-days/screen = 36 person-days
- Large and Medium: 20 screens × 1.0 person-days/screen = 20 person-days
- Large and High: 10 screens × 1.2 person-days/screen = 12 person-days
Total person-days for creation = 12 + 36 + 20 + 12 = 80 person-days.

Next, we add the 5 person-days for review and revision:
80 + 5 = 85 person-days.

Finally, we calculate the person-days for project management, which is 20% of the total person-days (including creation, review, revision, and project management itself).
Let T be the total person-days.
0.20 × (85) = 17 person-days for project management.
Total person-days = 85 + 17 = 102 person-days.
