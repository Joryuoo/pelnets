# 2019A_FE_AM_70
Which of the following is a technique for separating company activities into core activities and support activities for each business function, and analyzing which activities generate the profit derived from the products and services provided to customers?

a) 3C analysis
b) Five forces analysis
c) SWOT analysis
d) Value chain analysis
?
d) Value chain analysis
### Explanation
Value chain analysis is a strategy tool used to analyze internal firm activities. Its goal is to recognize, which activities are the most valuable (i.e. are the source of cost or differentiation advantage) to the firm and which ones could be improved to provide competitive advantage. It divides activities into primary (core) activities (like inbound logistics, operations, outbound logistics, marketing & sales, service) and support activities (like procurement, technology development, human resource management, firm infrastructure).
- 3C analysis focuses on Company, Competitors, and Customer.
- Five forces analysis evaluates industry attractiveness based on five competitive forces.
- SWOT analysis evaluates Strengths, Weaknesses, Opportunities, and Threats.
