# 2019A_FE_AM_63
Concerning computerization costs, which of the following is the cost that indicates the running cost?

a) The consulting cost of information systems strategy planning
b) The cost of system engineering for performance requirements definition
c) The installation cost of software packages
d) The maintenance cost of information-processing equipment, such as a server
?
d) The maintenance cost of information-processing equipment, such as a server
### Explanation
Running costs (also known as operational costs or OPEX) are the ongoing, day-to-day expenses incurred during the regular operation of a business or system.
- The maintenance cost of information-processing equipment like a server is an ongoing expense required to keep the system operational, hence it is a running cost.
- Options a), b), and c) are initial costs (capital expenditures or CAPEX) incurred during the planning and implementation phases.
