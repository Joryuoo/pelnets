# 2019S_FE_AM_75

Which of the following is a major role of a CIO? 

**a) The CIO creates a plan for optimizing the effect of investment on the information resources of the entire company to support the business strategy when a computerization strategy is established.** 

**b) The CIO gives advice to the information system department by auditing whether the information system is functioning appropriately with respect to the corporate activities.** 

**c) The CIO receives queries about the information system, reports on the problems, and gives specific instructions to the department in charge so that the information system of the entire company can be managed optimally.** 

**d) The CIO understands the status of the information system development and operation and specifically instructs improvement points so that the information system of the entire company functions optimally.**
?
**a) The CIO creates a plan for optimizing the effect of investment on the information resources of the entire company to support the business strategy when a computerization strategy is established.** 
## **Explanation:**

The **CIO (Chief Information Officer)** is a senior executive responsible for the management, implementation, and usability of information and computer technologies. Their primary role is to align the company's IT strategy with its business goals, ensuring that technology investments yield maximum value for the organization.

Therefore, creating a plan to optimize the effect of investment on information resources to support the business strategy is a major role of the CIO.

### Overview of Other Options

- **b) The CIO gives advice... by auditing whether the information system is functioning appropriately...**  
    This describes the role of an Information Systems Auditor.
    
- **c) The CIO receives queries... and gives specific instructions to the department in charge...**  
    This describes the role of a Help Desk or IT Support Manager.
    
- **d) The CIO understands the status of the information system development... and specifically instructs improvement points...**  
    This describes the role of an IT Project Manager or Operations Manager.
