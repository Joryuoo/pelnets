# 2019A_FE_AM_45
Which of the following is the malware that is used to gain unauthorized privileged access, to hide its own presence, and to perform malicious activities in a computer system?

a) Botnet
b) Ransomware
c) Rootkit
d) Security hole
?
c) Rootkit
### Explanation
A **rootkit** is a collection of malicious software designed to enable access to a computer or an area of its software that is not otherwise allowed (for example, to an unauthorized user) and often masks its existence or the existence of other software.
- **Botnet:** A network of compromised computers controlled remotely to perform distributed tasks like DDoS attacks.
- **Ransomware:** Malware that encrypts the victim's files and demands payment for the decryption key.
- **Security hole:** A vulnerability or flaw in a system, not a type of malware.
