### Explanation
In First-Come-First-Serve (FCFS) scheduling, processes are executed in the order of their arrival.

1. **Process P1** arrives at time 0. The CPU is idle, so P1 starts executing immediately.
   - P1 Waiting Time: 0
   - P1 Completion Time: 0 + 6 = 6

2. **Process P2** arrives at time 2. It must wait until P1 finishes at time 6.
   - P2 Waiting Time: 6 (start time) - 2 (arrival time) = 4
   - P2 Completion Time: 6 + 4 = 10

3. **Process P3** arrives at time 4. It must wait until P2 finishes at time 10.
   - P3 Waiting Time: 10 (start time) - 4 (arrival time) = 6
   - P3 Completion Time: 10 + 9 = 19

4. **Process P4** arrives at time 5. It must wait until P3 finishes at time 19.
   - P4 Waiting Time: 19 (start time) - 5 (arrival time) = 14
   - P4 Completion Time: 19 + 7 = 26

Thus, the waiting times are 0, 4, 6, and 14 for P1, P2, P3, and P4 respectively.
