# 2019A_FE_AM_11
Which of the following is the average cycles per instruction (CPI) of a computer that can execute 1 billion instructions per second with a clock rate of 2.4 GHz?

a) 0.04
b) 0.12
c) 2.4
d) 25
?
c) 2.4
### Explanation
Given:
- Instructions per second = 1 billion ($10^9$) instructions/sec
- Clock rate = 2.4 GHz = $2.4 \times 10^9$ cycles/sec

CPI (Cycles Per Instruction) is calculated by dividing the total number of cycles per second by the total number of instructions per second:
CPI = (Clock rate) / (Instructions per second)
CPI = ($2.4 \times 10^9$) / ($10^9$) = 2.4

Therefore, the average CPI is 2.4.
