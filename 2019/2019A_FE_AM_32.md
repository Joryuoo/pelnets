# 2019A_FE_AM_32
Which of the following is the device that operates only at the physical layer of the OSI model?

a) Bridge
b) Repeater
c) Router
d) Switch
?
b) Repeater
### Explanation
In the OSI model, network devices operate at different layers:
* **Repeater / Hub (Layer 1 - Physical Layer):** Simply amplifies or regenerates the electrical or optical signals to extend transmission distance.
* **Bridge / Switch (Layer 2 - Data Link Layer):** Forwards data frames based on MAC addresses.
* **Router (Layer 3 - Network Layer):** Forwards packets between different networks based on IP addresses.

Therefore, a repeater is the device that operates only at the physical layer.
