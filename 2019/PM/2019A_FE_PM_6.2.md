**Subquestion 2**

Let's trace the execution of the graph representing tasks and dependencies shown in Figure 1.

The initial in-degrees for each vertex (number of incoming edges) are:
*   Task 1: 1 (from 2)
*   Task 2: 2 (from 3 and 5)
*   Task 3: 0
*   Task 4: 2 (from 2 and 5)
*   Task 5: 1 (from 3)

**Finding C and D:**
The line pointed out by `/* α */` (`insert_queue(i);`) is executed during the initialization phase when a node has an in-degree of 0. Since only Task 3 has an initial in-degree of 0, `/* α */` is executed exactly **1 time**. Therefore, **C** is **a) 1**.

The line pointed out by `/* β */` (`adj[vertex][i]  0;`) is executed every time an edge is removed from the graph. Because there are a total of 6 directed edges in Figure 1, `/* β */` is executed exactly **6 times**. Therefore, **D** is **f) 6**.

**Finding E:**
Let's trace the queue operations with the `FOR (i  1; i ≤ N; i  i + 1)` loop:
1.  **Queue: [3]**. Pop 3. `schedule` = [3].
    *   Edges from 3: 3->2, 3->5.
    *   `indeg[2]` becomes 1. `indeg[5]` becomes 0. Insert 5.
2.  **Queue: [5]**. Pop 5. `schedule` = [3, 5].
    *   Edges from 5: 5->2, 5->4.
    *   `indeg[2]` becomes 0. Insert 2. `indeg[4]` becomes 1.
3.  **Queue: [2]**. Pop 2. `schedule` = [3, 5, 2].
    *   Edges from 2: 2->1, 2->4.
    *   `indeg[1]` becomes 0. Insert 1. `indeg[4]` becomes 0. Insert 4.
4.  **Queue: [1, 4]**. Pop 1. `schedule` = [3, 5, 2, 1].
    *   No outgoing edges.
5.  **Queue: [4]**. Pop 4. `schedule` = [3, 5, 2, 1, 4].
    *   No outgoing edges.
    
The order of tasks is 3 5 2 1 4. Therefore, **E** is **d) 3 5 2 1 4**.

**Finding F:**
If we replace the loop with `FOR (i  N; i ≥ 1; i  i - 1)`, the order in which adjacent nodes are processed is reversed (from N down to 1).
1.  **Queue: [3]**. Pop 3. `schedule` = [3].
    *   Edges from 3 (checked 5 to 1): 3->5, then 3->2.
    *   `indeg[5]` becomes 0. Insert 5. `indeg[2]` becomes 1.
2.  **Queue: [5]**. Pop 5. `schedule` = [3, 5].
    *   Edges from 5 (checked 5 to 1): 5->4, then 5->2.
    *   `indeg[4]` becomes 1. `indeg[2]` becomes 0. Insert 2.
3.  **Queue: [2]**. Pop 2. `schedule` = [3, 5, 2].
    *   Edges from 2 (checked 5 to 1): 2->4, then 2->1.
    *   `indeg[4]` becomes 0. Insert 4. `indeg[1]` becomes 0. Insert 1.
4.  **Queue: [4, 1]**. Pop 4. `schedule` = [3, 5, 2, 4].
    *   No outgoing edges.
5.  **Queue: [1]**. Pop 1. `schedule` = [3, 5, 2, 4, 1].
    *   No outgoing edges.

The new order of tasks is 3 5 2 4 1. Therefore, **F** is **e) 3 5 2 4 1**.
