**Subquestion 2 (Decision Table)**

In a decision table, the conditions determine the actions to be taken. A hyphen (`-`) means the condition is "don't care" (irrelevant) for that specific column's outcome.

**Blank A (OPS of 95 or higher condition):**
This condition is only relevant for determining the **Distinctive Graduate Award**. According to the criteria, this award requires:
1. Conduct rating of at least "Good" (Y)
2. No score of lower than 85 (Y)
3. Currently in grade 12 (Y)
If any of these prerequisite conditions are false (N), then the student is already disqualified from the Distinctive Graduate Award, making the "OPS of 95 or higher" condition irrelevant (`-`). Thus, the corresponding values for Blank A must match the evaluation of these prerequisites, making **Option e** the correct sequence.

**Blank B (Give Excellence in Academics Award action):**
The criteria for the **Excellence in Academics Award** are:
1. Conduct rating of at least "Good" (Y)
2. No score of lower than 85 (Y)
3. Average academic score of 95 or higher (Y)
This award does not depend on Grade 12 status, OPS, or ECP. Whenever the three required conditions are met (Y), the action should be executed (X). By tracing the columns where these three conditions are True, we can map the `X` and `-` values for Blank B. **Option b** correctly represents the execution pattern for this award across the remaining columns.
