### Explanation
The two-step registration process requires the user to first enter their email address. The system then sends an email with a unique, one-time URL to that address. The user must access this URL to complete their registration. 

This mechanism acts as an email verification step. If the applicant enters an incorrect email address or another person's email address, they will not receive the URL, thus preventing them from completing the registration process. Therefore, the main objective is to prevent the registration of incorrect or unauthorized email addresses.
