### Explanation
To find students who got As in all courses they completed:
SQL1 looks for students where there `NOT EXISTS` any course they completed (in GradeReport) that does not have an 'A' grade. 
Blank A is `NOT EXISTS` (c) and Blank B is `Grade != 'A'` (a).
This double-negation translates to: "There does not exist a grade for this student that is not an 'A'".
