### Explanation
**Subquestion 1 (Distinctive Graduate Award Criteria)**

The criteria for the **Distinctive Graduate Award** includes:
- attain a final conduct rating of at least "Good"
- the student should have no score of lower than 85 in any academic subjects
- should attain an overall performance score (OPS) of 95 or higher
- only applicable to graduating (currently at grade 12) students

The criteria for the **Good Conduct Award** is simply to "attain a final conduct rating of at least 'Good'".

Therefore, any student who meets the stringent criteria for the Distinctive Graduate Award will automatically meet the criteria for the Good Conduct Award (Option D).

* Option A is incorrect because the OPS formula allows for an average academic score slightly lower than 95 if their ECScore is high enough to compensate.
* Option B is incorrect because they do not necessarily need the *highest* ECP in their grade level.
* Option C is incorrect because the Excellence in Academics Award strictly requires an average academic score of 95 or higher, which is not strictly required for the Distinctive Graduate Award depending on their ECScore.
