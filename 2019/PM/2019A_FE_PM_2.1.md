### Explanation

**Logical Expressions and Karnaugh Maps:**
The problem asks us to simplify boolean logical expressions for a 4-bit input (I3, I2, I1, I0) by analyzing their Karnaugh maps. A Karnaugh map simplifies logic by grouping adjacent 1s (horizontally or vertically, including wraparound edges).

**Blank A:**
Expression Pr-1 represents the prime numbers (Pr) before full simplification. The set of prime numbers from 0 to 15 is 2, 3, 5, 7, 11, and 13.
In binary: 2 (0010), 3 (0011), 5 (0101), 7 (0111), 11 (1011), 13 (1101).
Looking at the terms provided, Blank A represents one of the minterms that has not been grouped yet. Based on the options and the binary representations, it corresponds to the unsimplified term for 11 (1011), which matches answer **b**.

**Blanks B, C, and D:**
These blanks deal with simplifying the groups of adjacent 1s in the Karnaugh map for Pr.
- Grouping 2 (0010) and 3 (0011) eliminates I0, leaving I3¯ • I2¯ • I1.
- Grouping 3 (0011) and 7 (0111) eliminates I2, leaving I3¯ • I1 • I0.
- Grouping 5 (0101) and 13 (1101) eliminates I3, leaving I2 • I1¯ • I0.
- Grouping 3 (0011) and 11 (1011) eliminates I3, leaving I2¯ • I1 • I0.
By applying K-map simplification (grouping pairs of adjacent 1s), the correct simplified terms match the choices **h**, **g**, and **a**.

**Blank E:**
The function Mp outputs 1 when the 4-bit number is a multiple of 3.
Applicable numbers: 0 (0000), 3 (0011), 6 (0110), 9 (1001), 12 (1100), 15 (1111).
When plotted on a 4-variable Karnaugh map, we check for adjacencies (cells that differ by exactly one bit):
- 0 (0000) differs by two bits from 3 (0011), 6 (0110), 9 (1001), 12 (1100).
- 15 (1111) differs by two bits from 3 (0011), 6 (0110), 9 (1001), 12 (1100).
Since none of these values differ by exactly one bit from any other valid multiple of 3, there are absolutely **no** adjacent 1s in the Karnaugh map for Mp. Thus, the correct answer for E is **a (no)**.
