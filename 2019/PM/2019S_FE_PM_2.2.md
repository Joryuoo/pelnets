- `2`: Miss, loaded into Y (LRU: X, Y)

- `1`: Miss, replaces Y (holding 2) (LRU: Z, X, Y)
- `2`: Miss, replaces Z (holding 3) (LRU: X, Y, Z)

- `4`: Miss, replaces Y (holding 1) (LRU: Z, X, Y)

At the second demand for page 4, the frame that has been unused for the longest period is Y. Thus, the page in frame Y is replaced. The answer for C is **b**.
