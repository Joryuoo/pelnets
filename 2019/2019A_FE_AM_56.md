# 2019A_FE_AM_56
IT services are provided under the following conditions in the SLA. What is the maximum number of hours of downtime in a month that can satisfy the SLA?

[Conditions of the SLA]
The number of business days per month is 30.
The service hours are from 7 AM to 11 PM on a business day.
The required availability is 99% or more.
Other required time, such as maintenance time, can be ignored.

a) 1.2
b) 3.0
c) 4.8
d) 7.2
?
c) 4.8
### Explanation
First, calculate the total service hours in a month:
- Service hours per day: from 7 AM to 11 PM = 16 hours/day.
- Total business days per month = 30 days.
- Total service hours per month = 16 hours/day × 30 days = 480 hours.

The required availability is 99% or more, meaning the maximum allowed downtime is 1% (100% - 99%) of the total service hours.
- Maximum downtime = 480 hours × 1% = 480 × 0.01 = 4.8 hours.
