# 2015A_FE_43
Which of the following technology is the most suitable to divide the whole company network into the networks by department?

a) DMZ (DeMilitarized Zone)
b) NAT (Network Address Translation)
c) VLAN (Virtual Local Area Network)
d) VPN (Virtual Private Network)
?
c) VLAN (Virtual Local Area Network)
### Explanation
A VLAN (Virtual Local Area Network) allows a single physical network switch to be logically partitioned into multiple independent broadcast domains. This makes it the ideal technology to divide a company-wide physical network into logical networks separated by department without needing separate physical wiring.
- a) DMZ is a subnet that separates an internal local area network from other untrusted networks (like the Internet).
- b) NAT translates private IP addresses to public IP addresses (and vice versa).
- d) VPN creates a secure, encrypted connection over a less secure network like the public Internet.
