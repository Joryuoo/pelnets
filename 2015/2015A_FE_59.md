# 2015A_FE_59
According to ISO/IEC 20000-1, which of the following is the appropriate processes that contain change management, configuration management, and release and deployment management?

a) Control processes
b) Relationship processes
c) Resolution processes
d) Service delivery processes
?
a) Control processes
### Explanation
In ISO/IEC 20000-1, the Control Processes group includes Change Management, Configuration Management, and Release and Deployment Management. These processes are responsible for controlling and managing changes and assets within the IT environment.
- **Relationship processes** include Business Relationship Management and Supplier Management.
- **Resolution processes** include Incident and Problem Management.
- **Service delivery processes** include Service Level, Availability, Capacity, and Continuity Management.
