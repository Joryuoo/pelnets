# 2015May_FE_1
![[2015May_FE_AM_Q1_p4.png]]

a) 00010011, 11101100
b) 00010011, 11101101
c) 11101100, 11101100
d) 11101100, 11101101
?
d) 11101100, 11101101
### Explanation
To find the binary representations of –19 in 8-bit format:
1. Convert the positive decimal number 19 into 8-bit binary: 19 = 16 + 2 + 1, so the binary is `00010011`.
2. **One's complement**: Invert all the bits of the positive binary representation. `00010011` becomes `11101100`.
3. **Two's complement**: Add 1 to the one's complement. `11101100` + 1 = `11101101`.
Therefore, the one's complement is `11101100` and the two's complement is `11101101`. This matches choice d).
