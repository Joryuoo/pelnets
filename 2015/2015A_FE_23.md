c) 1,500

### Explanation
To find the electrical energy consumed, we calculate the Power and multiply it by the Operating Time.
* **Power:** Power (W) = Voltage (V) × Current (A) = 100 V × 10 A = 1,000 W.
* **Operating Time:** The device operates from 0 to 60 minutes and from 90 to 120 minutes. 
  Total operating time = 60 mins + 30 mins = 90 minutes. 
  Converting minutes to hours: 90 / 60 = 1.5 hours.
* **Energy Consumption:** Energy (Wh) = Power (W) × Time (h) = 1,000 W × 1.5 h = 1,500 Wh.

Therefore, the electrical energy consumed is 1,500 Wh.
