# 2015May_FE_17
There exist the same two devices, each with an MTBF of 45 hours and an MTTR of 5 hours.  What is the availability of a system that connects these two devices in series? 

a) 0.81 
b) 0.90 
c) 0.95 
d) 0.99 
?
a) 0.81
### Explanation
The availability ($A$) of a single device is calculated using Mean Time Between Failures (MTBF) and Mean Time To Repair (MTTR):
$A = \frac{\text{MTBF}}{\text{MTBF} + \text{MTTR}}$

For each device:
$A = \frac{45}{45 + 5} = \frac{45}{50} = 0.90$

When devices are connected in a series configuration, all components must function for the system to work. Therefore, the overall system availability is the product of the individual availabilities:
$A_{\text{series}} = A_1 \times A_2 = 0.90 \times 0.90 = 0.81$
