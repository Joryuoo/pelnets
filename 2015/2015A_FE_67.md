# 2015A_FE_67
Which of the following is a technique that is used to identify and evaluate the internal and external factors that are helpful or harmful to the objectives of business organizations or projects?

a) Five forces analysis
b) PPM analysis
c) SWOT analysis
d) Value chain analysis
?
c) SWOT analysis
### Explanation
**SWOT Analysis** identifies and evaluates four key aspects of an organization or project:
1. **S**trengths (Internal, Helpful)
2. **W**eaknesses (Internal, Harmful)
3. **O**pportunities (External, Helpful)
4. **T**hreats (External, Harmful)

* **a) Five forces analysis:** Assesses the competitive intensity and attractiveness of an industry.
* **b) PPM (Project Portfolio Management) analysis:** Evaluates and balances a company's mix of products or projects.
* **d) Value chain analysis:** Identifies which internal business activities create value and competitive advantage.
