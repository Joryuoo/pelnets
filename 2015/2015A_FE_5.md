# 2015A_FE_5
What is the value of the following expression in Reverse Polish Notation?

9 2 1 + - 3 *

a) 0
b) 4
c) 10
d) 18
?
d) 18
### Explanation
Reverse Polish Notation (RPN) is evaluated using a stack. We process the expression from left to right:

1. Push 9
2. Push 2
3. Push 1
4. Encounter `+`: Pop 1 and 2, calculate $2 + 1 = 3$, Push 3
5. Encounter `-`: Pop 3 and 9, calculate $9 - 3 = 6$, Push 6
6. Push 3
7. Encounter `*`: Pop 3 and 6, calculate $6 \times 3 = 18$, Push 18

The final value is 18.
