# 2015May_FE_3
![[2015May_FE_AM_Q3_full.png]]

a) 1/18
b) 8/121
c) 4/55
d) 2/27
?
c) 4/55
### Explanation
There are initially 4 green apples and 8 red apples, making a total of 12 apples.
The probability of picking:
1. **First apple (Green)**: 4 green / 12 total = 4/12 = 1/3
2. **Second apple (Green)**: 3 green remaining / 11 total remaining = 3/11
3. **Third apple (Red)**: 8 red remaining / 10 total remaining = 8/10 = 4/5

Multiply the probabilities: (1/3) * (3/11) * (4/5) = 4 / 55.
This matches choice c).
