# 2015May_FE_75
When the relationship between the list price of a product and the demand for the product is defined as a linear expression, which of the following is an appropriate numerical value to be inserted into blank A below?

(1) When the list price is set to 30 dollars, demand is 0 units.
(2) When the list price is set to 10 dollars, demand is 60,000 units.
(3) When the list price is set to 15 dollars, demand is A units.

a) 30,000
b) 35,000
c) 40,000
d) 45,000
?
d) 45,000
### Explanation
Let $P$ be the list price in dollars, and $D$ be the demand in units. We are given two points: $(P_1, D_1) = (30, 0)$ and $(P_2, D_2) = (10, 60000)$.
Since the relationship is linear, we can find the equation of the line.

The slope (change in demand per dollar) is:
$$m = \frac{60000 - 0}{10 - 30} = \frac{60000}{-20} = -3000$$

So, for every dollar the price decreases, the demand increases by 3,000 units.
The linear equation is:
$$D - 0 = -3000(P - 30)$$
$$D = -3000(P - 30)$$

When the list price is $P = 15$ dollars:
$$D = -3000(15 - 30) = -3000(-15) = 45000$$

Thus, the demand is 45,000 units.
