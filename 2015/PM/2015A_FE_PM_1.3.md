### Explanation

To find the final size of the transmitted ASCII stream, we must trace the operations performed on the email message according to the scenario:

1. **Initial Message Size:** The original email message size is 9 kB.
2. **Compression:** The text specifies that the ZIP algorithm has a compression ratio of approximately 2.0. 
   - Size after compression = 9 kB / 2.0 = **4.5 kB**.
3. **Radix-64 Conversion:** To accommodate email systems that only permit ASCII text, the binary stream is converted using radix-64. The scenario states that this maps three 8-bit codes into four ASCII characters, expanding the length by 33% (a multiplier of 4/3).
   - Final transmitted size = 4.5 kB * (4 / 3) = **6 kB**.

**Incorrect Choices:**
- **a) 4.5 kB:** Incorrect. This is the size after compression, forgetting the radix-64 expansion step.
- **c) 9 kB:** Incorrect. This is the original uncompressed message size.
- **d) 12 kB:** Incorrect. This would be the result if the original 9 kB message was converted to radix-64 without ever being compressed (9 * 4/3 = 12).
