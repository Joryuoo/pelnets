# 2015May_FE_38
In the context of information security defined by ISO/IEC 27001, which of the following is the property of safeguarding the accuracy and completeness of information assets?

a) Authentication
b) Availability
c) Confidentiality
d) Integrity
?
d) Integrity
### Explanation
In the CIA triad of information security (Confidentiality, Integrity, Availability):
*   **Integrity** is the property of safeguarding the accuracy and completeness of information and processing methods. It ensures data has not been altered, tampered with, or corrupted in an unauthorized manner.

Other properties:
*   **Confidentiality:** Ensuring that information is not made available or disclosed to unauthorized individuals, entities, or processes.
*   **Availability:** Ensuring that authorized users have access to information and associated assets when required.
*   **Authentication:** The provision of assurance that a claimed characteristic of an entity (like user identity) is correct.
