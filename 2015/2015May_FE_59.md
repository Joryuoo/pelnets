### Explanation
A **declining metered rate method** means that as the usage amount increases, the unit rate of the charge decreases. This results in a graph that continues to go up as usage increases, but the slope becomes flatter (less steep) over time, reflecting the lower cost per unit at higher usage levels.

Option **c** correctly shows an upward-sloping curve where the rate of increase tapers off.
