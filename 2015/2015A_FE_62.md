# 2015A_FE_62
Which of the following is an appropriate description concerning the role of a system auditor?

a) It is to assure the preparation and operations regarding the control of the information system or to give advice on these, from a standpoint of professional standpoint independent from the audit target.
b) It is to evaluate the performance of the information system and to issue an audit working paper to the system users.
c) It is to instruct the testing staff to improve defects found during the comprehensive test for the information system.
d) It is to test if processes are performed according to the plan and then to approve the release.
?
a) It is to assure the preparation and operations regarding the control of the information system or to give advice on these, from a standpoint of professional standpoint independent from the audit target.
### Explanation
A system auditor operates as an independent professional who evaluates and provides assurance or advice regarding the controls, security, and efficiency of an information system.

* **b)** Audit working papers are internal documents prepared by the auditor to record the audit procedures performed and evidence obtained. They are not issued to system users.
* **c)** Instructing testing staff to improve defects is the role of a test manager or quality assurance lead, not an auditor.
* **d)** Approving the release based on test processes is the responsibility of the system owner, project manager, or release manager.
