# 2015A_FE_71
Among the different business systems supported by technology, which of the following analyzes routes of distribution, tracking and load tendency?

a) LMS
b) OMS
c) TMS
d) WMS
?
c) TMS
### Explanation
**TMS (Transportation Management System)** is a subset of supply chain management concerning transportation operations. It analyzes distribution routes, tracking, and load tendency to optimize the physical movement of goods.

Brief overview of the other choices:
* **LMS (Learning Management System):** A software application for the administration, documentation, and delivery of educational courses or training programs.
* **OMS (Order Management System):** A system used to track customer orders, inventory, and fulfillment.
* **WMS (Warehouse Management System):** A software application designed to support and optimize warehouse functionality and distribution center management.
