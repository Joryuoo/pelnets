### Explanation
The given condition is an implication: $P \implies Q$
Where $P$ = "it rains" and $Q$ = "the street will be wet".

- **Inference 1**: "The street is not wet (not $Q$), and therefore, it did not rain (not $P$)."
  This is the contrapositive of the condition ($\neg Q \implies \neg P$), which is logically equivalent to $P \implies Q$. Therefore, Inference 1 is **Valid**.

- **Inference 2**: "It did not rain (not $P$), and therefore, the street is not wet (not $Q$)."
  This is the inverse of the condition ($\neg P \implies \neg Q$), which is NOT logically equivalent to the original condition. The street could be wet for other reasons (like a sprinkler). Therefore, Inference 2 is **Not valid**.

This matches choice c).
