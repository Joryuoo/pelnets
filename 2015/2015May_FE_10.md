d) 13.4
### Explanation
There are 86 coins in total. We need to select 60 coins to maximize the total value. 
To maximize the value, we should pick the coins with the highest denominations first until we reach 60 coins:

1. **1-dollar**: 1 coin (Value: 100 cents) -> 1 coin total, 59 left.
2. **50-cent**: 10 coins (Value: 500 cents) -> 11 coins total, 49 left.
3. **20-cent**: 25 coins (Value: 500 cents) -> 36 coins total, 24 left.
4. **10-cent**: We need 24 more coins out of the 30 available.
   - 24 coins * 10 cents = 240 cents.
   - Total coins is now 60.

Now, calculate the total value:
- 100 cents (1-dollar)
- 500 cents (50-cents)
- 500 cents (20-cents)
- 240 cents (10-cents)
- **Total**: 100 + 500 + 500 + 240 = 1340 cents.

Since 100 cents = 1 dollar, the total value is $1340 / 100 = 13.4$ dollars.
