# 2015May_FE_80
Which of the following is not protected by "copyright" that is based on the WTO’s Agreement on Trade-Related Aspects of Intellectual Property Rights (TRIPS)?

a) Database
b) Free software publicly available on the Internet
c) Programming language and its conventions
d) Software operation manual
?
c) Programming language and its conventions
### Explanation
Copyright protects the *expression* of ideas, not the ideas, procedures, methods of operation, or mathematical concepts themselves.
According to the WTO TRIPS agreement and standard copyright laws, a programming language (its syntax and conventions) cannot be copyrighted, as it is considered a system or method of operation.

* **a)** Databases are protected if their selection or arrangement constitutes an intellectual creation.
* **b)** Free software is still software and is protected by copyright (the author simply grants broad usage rights via a license).
* **d)** Software operation manuals are literary works and are clearly protected by copyright.
