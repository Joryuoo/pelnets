a) A, B, D 
b) A, C, D  
c) B, E 
d) D, E  
?
c) B, E
### Explanation
We have a knapsack with a maximum volume of 7 units, and we want to maximize the total price.
Let's evaluate the total volume and total price for each given choice:

- **a) A, B, D**
  - Volume: 3 (A) + 2 (B) + 1 (D) = 6
  - Price: 4 (A) + 3 (B) + 2 (D) = 9
- **b) A, C, D**
  - Volume: 3 (A) + 3 (C) + 1 (D) = 7
  - Price: 4 (A) + 5 (C) + 2 (D) = 11
- **c) B, E**
  - Volume: 2 (B) + 5 (E) = 7
  - Price: 3 (B) + 9 (E) = 12
- **d) D, E**
  - Volume: 1 (D) + 5 (E) = 6
  - Price: 2 (D) + 9 (E) = 11

The maximum total price is 12, which is achieved by packing items B and E.
