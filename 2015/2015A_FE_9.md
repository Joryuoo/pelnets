# 2015A_FE_9
![[2015A_FE_AM_Q9_full.png]]

a) (x + y) → m
b) (x + y) / 2 → m
c) (x - y) / 2 → m
d) (y - x) / 2 → m
?
b) (x + y) / 2 → m
### Explanation
The flowchart describes the Binary Search algorithm. 
- `x` and `y` represent the lower and upper bounds of the search space, initialized to 1 and n.
- In each iteration, the midpoint `m` is calculated as the average of `x` and `y`.
- The correct formula for the midpoint is `(x + y) / 2`.
- The algorithm checks if `k` is equal to `A(m)`. If it is less, the upper bound `y` becomes `m - 1`. If greater, the lower bound `x` becomes `m + 1`.
