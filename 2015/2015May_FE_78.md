# 2015May_FE_78
Which of the following is the form of organization that has the characteristics (1) through (4) described below?

(1) The organization is formed by assembling necessary experts from all departments to achieve strategic objectives.
(2) The organization is a strategic organization that responds to changes in the environment, and is independent of functional departments.
(3) The organization has the flexibility to dissolve if the expected objectives are achieved.
(4) A task force team belongs to this form of organization.

a) Divisional organization
b) Line and staff organization
c) Matrix organization
d) Project organization
?
d) Project organization
### Explanation
A project organization is a temporary structure created to achieve specific goals or objectives. Experts from various departments are brought together to work on a strategic task (like a task force), and once the project is completed, the team is dissolved.

* **a)** A divisional organization groups functions into divisions based on products, markets, or geographies.
* **b)** A line and staff organization combines direct chain-of-command (line) with advisory experts (staff).
* **c)** A matrix organization is a permanent structure where employees have dual reporting relationships (e.g., to a functional manager and a product manager), unlike the temporary nature of a project organization.
