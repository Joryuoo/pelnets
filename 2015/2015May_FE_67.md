# 2015May_FE_67
According to "competitive strategy" proposed by Philip Kotler, which of the following is the company that has a small market share, but has the strategic aim of increasing income and acquiring a unique position by focusing management resources on particular products and services?

a) Market challenger
b) Market follower
c) Market leader
d) Market nicher
?
d) Market nicher
### Explanation
According to Philip Kotler's competitive strategies, firms can be classified by their market roles:
1. **Market Leader**: The firm with the largest market share, which leads in price changes, product introductions, and promotional intensity.
2. **Market Challenger**: A runner-up firm that aggressively tries to expand its market share by attacking the leader and other competitors.
3. **Market Follower**: A runner-up firm that seeks to maintain its market share without rocking the boat, often imitating or adapting the leader's products.
4. **Market Nicher**: A firm that serves small, specialized market segments not being served by larger firms, aiming for high margins rather than high volume. 

The question perfectly describes a **Market Nicher**.
