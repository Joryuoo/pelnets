# 2015May_FE_77
![[2015May_FE_AM_Q77_full.png]]

a) 6,250
b) 30,000
c) 31,500
d) 33,000
?
d) 33,000
### Explanation
To maximize profit given constrained assembly time, we need to calculate the profit per minute of assembly time for each product, and prioritize the highest ones.

| Product | Profit/Unit | Time/Unit (min) | Profit/Minute | Limit (units) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| X | $18 | 6 | $3.00 / min | 1000 |
| Y | $25 | 10 | $2.50 / min | 900 |
| Z | $30 | 15 | $2.00 / min | 500 |

Product X gives the highest return per minute, followed by Y, then Z.
Total available assembly time is 200 hours/month = 12,000 minutes.

1. **Produce Product X up to its limit:**
   1,000 units × 6 min/unit = 6,000 minutes used.
   Profit from X = 1,000 × $18 = $18,000.
   Remaining time = 12,000 - 6,000 = 6,000 minutes.

2. **Produce Product Y with remaining time:**
   The limit is 900 units, which would require 9,000 minutes. We only have 6,000 minutes left.
   We can produce: 6,000 min / 10 min/unit = 600 units of Y.
   Profit from Y = 600 × $25 = $15,000.
   Remaining time = 0.

Total maximum possible profit = $18,000 + $15,000 = $33,000.
