d) P{1 − (1 − P^2)^2}
### Explanation
By analyzing the answer $P\{1 - (1 - P^2)^2\}$, we can determine the structure of the system from the diagram:

1. The outer $P$ represents a single subsystem (availability $P$) connected in **series** with the rest of the system.
2. The remaining part is $\{1 - (1 - P^2)^2\}$. A formula of the form $1 - (1 - X)^2$ represents two parallel branches each with availability $X$.
3. Here, $X = P^2$. An availability of $P^2$ represents two subsystems connected in **series** (since $P \times P = P^2$).
4. Therefore, the system is composed of an initial subsystem in series with a parallel structure. This parallel structure has two branches, and each branch consists of two subsystems in series.

Multiplying the availability of the initial subsystem ($P$) by the availability of the parallel part $1 - (1 - P^2)^2$ gives the overall availability of the entire system as $P\{1 - (1 - P^2)^2\}$.
