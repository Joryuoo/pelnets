# 2015A_FE_57
An IT service is provided under the conditions described below. In order to meet the SLA, which of the following is the maximum allowable downtime (in hours) per month during service hours? Here, the number of business days in a month is 30, and there is no planed service outage for a reason of maintenance or something similar during the service hours.

[Conditions of the SLA]
- Service hours: 8 a.m. to 10 p.m. on business days
- Availability: 99.5% or more

a) 0.3
b) 2.1
c) 3.0
d) 3.6
?
b) 2.1
### Explanation
First, calculate the total service hours in a month:
- Service hours per day: 8 a.m. to 10 p.m. is 14 hours.
- Number of business days: 30 days.
- Total service hours per month = 14 hours/day × 30 days = 420 hours.

Next, calculate the maximum allowable downtime based on the 99.5% availability requirement:
- Required uptime = 99.5%
- Allowable downtime percentage = 0.5% (or 0.005)
- Maximum allowable downtime = 420 hours × 0.005 = 2.1 hours.
