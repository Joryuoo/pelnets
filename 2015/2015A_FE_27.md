# 2015A_FE_27
![[2015A_FE_AM_Q27_full.png]]

a) A (1..1), B (1..*), C (1..*), D (1..1)
b) A (1..1), B (1..*), C (1..1), D (1..*)
c) A (1..1), B (0..*), C (0..*), D (1..1)
d) A (1..1), B (0..*), C (1..1), D (0..*)
?
b) 

### Explanation
By analyzing the relationships described in the prompt:
1. **Continent to Country**: "Each continent has at least one country" implies that the multiplicity on the Country side (B) is `1..*`. "There is no cross-continental country" means every country belongs to exactly one continent, so the multiplicity on the Continent side (A) is `1..1`.
2. **Country to City**: "Each country has at least one city" implies the multiplicity on the City side (D) is `1..*`. A city intrinsically resides within a single country, so the multiplicity on the Country side (C) is `1..1`.

Therefore, the multiplicities are: A is `1..1`, B is `1..*`, C is `1..1`, and D is `1..*`.
