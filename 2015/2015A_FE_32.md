# 2015A_FE_32
In OSI basic reference model, what devices are used for segmentation or coupling of Physical layer, Data Link layer and Network layer respectively? 

a) Bridge, Repeater, Router 
b) Bridge, Router, Repeater 
c) Repeater, Bridge, Router 
d) Repeater, Router, Bridge 
?
c) Repeater, Bridge, Router 
### Explanation
In the OSI reference model, network interconnection devices operate at specific layers:

- **Repeater:** Operates at the **Physical layer** (Layer 1). It amplifies and retransmits signals to extend the network length.
- **Bridge:** Operates at the **Data Link layer** (Layer 2). It forwards frames based on MAC addresses.
- **Router:** Operates at the **Network layer** (Layer 3). It routes packets between different networks based on IP addresses.
