# 2015May_FE_69
Which of the following is the business management technique that is used to develop specific targets and measures to implement a planned strategy, in consideration of the appropriate mutual relationships among four perspectives (i.e., financial, customer, internal business processes, and learning and growth)?

a) Balanced score card
b) Growth matrix
c) Product portfolio management
d) SWOT analysis
?
a) Balanced score card
### Explanation
The Balanced Scorecard (BSC) is a strategic management performance metric used to identify and improve various internal business functions and their resulting external outcomes. It measures performance across four key perspectives:
1. Financial
2. Customer
3. Internal Business Processes
4. Learning and Growth

* **b) is incorrect** because the Growth Matrix (e.g., Ansoff Matrix) deals with market penetration, market development, product development, and diversification.
* **c) is incorrect** because Product Portfolio Management (e.g., BCG Matrix) analyzes product lines based on market growth rate and relative market share.
* **d) is incorrect** because SWOT analysis assesses Strengths, Weaknesses, Opportunities, and Threats.
