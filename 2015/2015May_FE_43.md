# 2015May_FE_43
Which of the following is the security attack that is used for an illegal attempt to manipulate the people of an organization into divulging the password and confidential information under the pretext of an emergency?  

a) Password cracking 
b) Social engineering  
c) Springboard attack  
d) Trojan horse  
?
b) Social engineering
### Explanation
**Social engineering** involves manipulating people into giving up confidential information. Attackers exploit human psychology, trust, or fear (like a fake emergency) to bypass security rather than using technical hacking methods.

* **a) Password cracking** involves technical methods (like brute force or dictionary attacks) to discover a password.
* **c) Springboard attack** uses a compromised system to launch attacks against other systems, hiding the attacker's true origin.
* **d) Trojan horse** is malicious software disguised as legitimate software.
