# 2015A_FE_68
Which of the following is a technique for classifying company activities into primary activities and support activities, and analyzing which activities generate the profit of the products or services provided to customers?

a) 3C analysis
b) Five forces analysis
c) SWOT analysis
d) Value chain analysis
?
d) Value chain analysis
### Explanation
**Value Chain Analysis**, developed by Michael Porter, categorizes a firm's processes into **primary activities** (inbound logistics, operations, outbound logistics, marketing and sales, service) and **support activities** (procurement, human resource management, technological development, infrastructure). It is used to analyze cost behaviors and identify potential sources of competitive advantage or profit generation.

* **a) 3C analysis:** Evaluates the Company, Competitor, and Customer.
* **b) Five forces analysis:** Assesses the competitive environment of an industry.
* **c) SWOT analysis:** Evaluates internal strengths/weaknesses and external opportunities/threats.
