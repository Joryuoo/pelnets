c) Average cost of producing 2 units: 30, Marginal cost of producing 5th unit: 10
### Explanation
**1. Average cost of producing 2 units:**
The average cost is the total cost divided by the number of units.
From the table, the total cost for 2 units is 60.
`Average Cost = 60 / 2 = 30`

**2. Marginal cost of producing the 5th unit:**
The marginal cost is the increase in total cost resulting from producing one additional unit.
From the table:
* Total cost for 4 units = 80
* Total cost for 5 units = 90
`Marginal Cost = Total Cost (5 units) - Total Cost (4 units) = 90 - 80 = 10`
