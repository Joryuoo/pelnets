# 2015A_FE_1
What is the sum of two binary fractions 1.0101 and 1.0111 expressed in decimal?

a) 2.5
b) 2.75
c) 2.875
d) 2.9375
?
b) 2.75
### Explanation
First, perform the binary addition:
```text
  1.0101
+ 1.0111
--------
 10.1100
```
Then, convert the binary sum `10.1100` to decimal:
* $1 \times 2^1 = 2$
* $0 \times 2^0 = 0$
* $1 \times 2^{-1} = 0.5$
* $1 \times 2^{-2} = 0.25$

Sum = $2 + 0.5 + 0.25 = 2.75$.
The correct answer is b.
