### Explanation
In an arrow diagram (PERT/CPM chart), reducing the entire project time requires shortening the critical path. To shorten the total project duration by two days, you must reduce activities along the critical path(s) by a total of two days, ensuring that no other path becomes longer than the new shortened duration. Shortening activities B and D by one day each successfully reduces the overall critical path by two days.
