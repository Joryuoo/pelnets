d) Philippines, Vietnam, Mongolia, Thailand, Bangladesh, Myanmar, Malaysia
### Explanation
A binary search tree is built such that for any node, elements in its left subtree are alphabetically smaller, and elements in its right subtree are alphabetically larger. 
When building a BST by inserting elements one by one, the first element becomes the root.
To evaluate the choices, look at the first element (the root) and check if the resulting BST structure holds based on the description:
- The question states Mongolia comes before Philippines (Mongolia < Philippines), and Myanmar comes before Philippines but after Mongolia (Mongolia < Myanmar < Philippines).
- In choice d), the root is **Philippines**. 
  - **Vietnam** is > Philippines (right child).
  - **Mongolia** is < Philippines (left child).
  - **Thailand** is > Philippines but < Vietnam (left child of Vietnam).
  - **Bangladesh** is < Philippines, < Mongolia (left child of Mongolia).
  - **Myanmar** is < Philippines, > Mongolia (right child of Mongolia).
  - **Malaysia** is < Philippines, < Mongolia, > Bangladesh (right child of Bangladesh).

This perfectly satisfies a standard binary search tree structure based on alphabetical order.
