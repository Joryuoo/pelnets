# 2015A_FE_54
![[2015A_FE_AM_Q54_full.png]]

a) 80
b) 85
c) 101
d) 102
?
d) 102
### Explanation
First, calculate the person-days required for the screens:
- 30 small screens of low complexity: 30 × 0.4 = 12 person-days
- 40 medium screens of medium complexity: 40 × 0.9 = 36 person-days
- 20 large screens of medium complexity: 20 × 1.0 = 20 person-days
- 10 large screens of high complexity: 10 × 1.2 = 12 person-days

Total person-days for screens = 12 + 36 + 20 + 12 = 80 person-days.

Next, add the review time:
Total including review = 80 + 5 = 85 person-days.

Finally, account for project management (20% of the total person-days for screens and review):
Management = 0.20 × 85 = 17 person-days.

Total person-days required = 85 + 17 = 102 person-days.
