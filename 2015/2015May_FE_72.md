b) Notice of receipt of product
### Explanation
In an escrow service, the third party (Escrow service company) holds the payment until the transaction is successfully completed.
The sequence of transactions is:
1. Agreement of sale between buyer and seller.
2. Payment for purchase by the buyer to the escrow service.
3. Notice of deposit of payment (from escrow to seller).
4. Shipment of product (from seller to buyer).
5. Notice of receipt of product (from buyer to escrow).
6. Transfer of payment (from escrow to seller).

Since (4) is the shipment of the product from the seller to the buyer, (5) must be the buyer notifying the escrow service that they have received the product.
