# 2015A_FE_50
Which of the following is the purpose of a stress test?

a) To compare performance with other computers by measuring the execution time of a standard program
b) To confirm the operation at the limits of processing capability that is required for a system
c) To confirm that there are no unintended effects on the program functions that have no relation to correction or change in the program
d) To evaluate the usability of a system by having actual users use the system
?
b) To confirm the operation at the limits of processing capability that is required for a system
### Explanation
A stress test pushes the system beyond its normal operational limits to determine its robustness, stability, and error handling capabilities under extreme workloads or resource exhaustion. The goal is to ensure the system doesn't crash or fail catastrophically when pushed to or past its limits.
- a) describes a Benchmark test.
- c) describes Regression testing.
- d) describes Usability testing or User Acceptance Testing (UAT).
