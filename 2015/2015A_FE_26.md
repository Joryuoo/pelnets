# 2015A_FE_26
Among the descriptions concerning database models, which of the following is an appropriate explanation of the network model?

a) It consists of two or more data files located at different sites on a computer network.
b) It presents information in two-dimensional tables with rows and columns.
c) Its records are organized into a tree structure.
d) Its records can have multiple parent records and multiple child records.
?
d) Its records can have multiple parent records and multiple child records.

### Explanation
The **network model** is a database model that allows a flexible way of representing objects and their relationships by structuring records as a graph, rather than a strict tree. Consequently, a record can have multiple parent and child records.
* **a)** refers to a **distributed database**, where data is spread across different nodes on a network.
* **b)** refers to the **relational model**, which utilizes tables with rows and columns.
* **c)** refers to the **hierarchical model**, where records form a strict tree structure (one parent per child record).
