# 2015May_FE_2
![[2015May_FE_AM_Q2_full.png]]

a) 101.11  
b) 101.1000011  
c) 101.10(0011)  
d) 101.100(0011) 
?
d) 101.100(0011)
### Explanation
To convert the decimal 5.525 to a binary fraction:
1. **Integer part**: 5 in binary is `101`.
2. **Fractional part**: 0.525. Multiply by 2 repeatedly:
   - 0.525 * 2 = 1.05  (digit 1)
   - 0.05 * 2 = 0.10   (digit 0)
   - 0.10 * 2 = 0.20   (digit 0)
   - 0.20 * 2 = 0.40   (digit 0)
   - 0.40 * 2 = 0.80   (digit 0)
   - 0.80 * 2 = 1.60   (digit 1)
   - 0.60 * 2 = 1.20   (digit 1)
   - 0.20 * 2 = 0.40   (digit 0, repeats from here)

The fractional part is `100001100110011...` which is `.100(0011)`.
Combining both parts gives `101.100(0011)`, which matches choice d).
