# 2015A_FE_25
In the process of creating animation, which of the following is used to incorporate the natural movement of objects, humans and animals, by using sensors and video cameras?

a) Key frame method
b) Morphing
c) Motion capture
d) Pixel shader
?
c) Motion capture

### Explanation
**Motion capture** is the technique of recording the movement of physical objects or people using sensors and video cameras to animate digital character models naturally in 2D or 3D computer animation.
* **a) Key frame method** involves animators defining starting and ending points (key frames) for a smooth transition, which software interpolates.
* **b) Morphing** is a special visual effect that seamlessly transforms one image or shape into another.
* **d) Pixel shader** is a graphics rendering function that calculates effects on a per-pixel basis to alter lighting and color.
