### Explanation
In UML (Unified Modeling Language), a class diagram typically represents a class using a rectangle divided into three compartments:
1. **Top compartment:** Class name
2. **Middle compartment:** Attributes (properties or fields)
3. **Bottom compartment:** Operations (methods or functions)

The blank indicated by the question points to the top compartment, which holds the **Class name**.
