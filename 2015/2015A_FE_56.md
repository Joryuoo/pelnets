# 2015A_FE_56
![[2015A_FE_AM_Q56_full.png]]

a) 18
b) 24
c) 30
d) 32
?
a) 18
### Explanation
First, calculate the unadjusted function point count by multiplying each function's count by its weighting coefficient and summing them up:
- External input: 1 × 4 = 4
- External output: 2 × 5 = 10
- Internal logical file: 1 × 10 = 10
- External interface file: 0 × 7 = 0
- External inquiry: 0 × 4 = 0

Unadjusted function point total = 4 + 10 + 10 = 24.

Next, multiply by the correction coefficient of complexity (0.75):
Function point value = 24 × 0.75 = 18.
