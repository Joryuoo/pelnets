# 2024A_FE-A_17

Q17. Audio signals are recorded using 8-bit samples at a sampling rate of 11,000 times per second. When a flash memory of $512 \times 10^6$ bytes is used, what is the maximum recording time of such data in minutes?

a) 77
b) 96
c) 775
d) 969

?
c) 775

### Explanation

To find the maximum recording time, we need to calculate the amount of storage consumed per second, and then divide the total available memory by this consumption rate.

**1. Calculate the Data Rate (Storage per second)**
- **Sample size:** The audio uses 8-bit samples. Since $8 \text{ bits} = 1 \text{ byte}$, each sample takes exactly $1 \text{ byte}$.
- **Sampling rate:** The audio is sampled at $11,000 \text{ times per second}$.
- **Data per second:** 
  $$\text{Data Rate} = 11,000 \text{ samples/sec} \times 1 \text{ byte/sample} = 11,000 \text{ bytes/sec}$$

**2. Determine the Total Memory Capacity**
- Total available flash memory $= 512 \times 10^6 \text{ bytes} = 512,000,000 \text{ bytes}$.

**3. Calculate the Maximum Recording Time**
To find the recording time in **seconds**, divide the total capacity by the data rate:
$$\text{Time (seconds)} = \frac{512,000,000 \text{ bytes}}{11,000 \text{ bytes/sec}} \approx 46,545.45 \text{ seconds}$$

Now, convert this time into **minutes** by dividing by 60:
$$\text{Time (minutes)} = \frac{46,545.45}{60} \approx 775.75 \text{ minutes}$$

The approximate value is **775 minutes**, corresponding to option **c**.
