### Explanation

This pseudocode calculates the **Hamming Distance** between two strings `s1` and `s2`. For strings of different lengths, the length difference is also added to the distance.

#### Step-by-Step Analysis:
1. **Finding Minimum Length (Blank A)**: The distance is initially calculated up to the length of the shorter string. `minLength` is set to `length1` by default. If `length2` is shorter, it must be updated. Thus, **Blank A** checks if `length2 < length1`.
2. **Character Comparison (Blank B)**: Hamming distance counts the number of *differing* characters. Inside the `for` loop, the `distance` is incremented by 1 when the characters do not match. Therefore, **Blank B** must be `is not equal to`.
3. **Adding Remaining Length (Blank C)**: After comparing the overlapping prefix, the remaining unmatched characters must be added to the distance. The `remainingLength` is calculated as the absolute difference between `length1` and `length2`. The final distance adds this difference. Thus, **Blank C** is `distance + remainingLength`.
