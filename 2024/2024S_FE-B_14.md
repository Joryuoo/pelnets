# 2024S_FE-B_14

![[2024S_FE-B_Q14_full.png]]
?
i) 0.9

### Explanation
The function `calcSim(v1, v2)` calculates the **Cosine Similarity** between two vectors, $v_1$ and $v_2$.

```mermaid
flowchart TD
    A[Start Similarity Calc] --> B[Initialize sxx=0, syy=0, sxy=0]
    B --> C[Loop i from 1 to length]
    C --> D{Get v1[i] as x, v2[i] as y}
    D --> E[sxx = sxx + x^2]
    E --> F[syy = syy + y^2]
    F --> G[sxy = sxy + x*y]
    G --> C
    C -- End Loop --> H[Return sxy / sqrt[sxx * syy]]
```

Given:
- $v_1 = \{2, 2, 1, 0, 4\}$
- $v_2 = \{3, 1, 1, 1, 2\}$

The program computes three sums iteratively for $i = 1$ to $5$:
- `sxx` accumulates $\sum (v_1[i])^2$
- `syy` accumulates $\sum (v_2[i])^2$
- `sxy` accumulates $\sum (v_1[i] \times v_2[i])$

Let's trace the values step-by-step:

| `i` | `v1[i]` | `v2[i]` | `x * x` | `y * y` | `x * y` | `sxx` | `syy` | `sxy` |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | 2 | 3 | 4 | 9 | 6 | 4 | 9 | 6 |
| 2 | 2 | 1 | 4 | 1 | 2 | 8 | 10 | 8 |
| 3 | 1 | 1 | 1 | 1 | 1 | 9 | 11 | 9 |
| 4 | 0 | 1 | 0 | 1 | 0 | 9 | 12 | 9 |
| 5 | 4 | 2 | 16 | 4 | 8 | 25 | 16 | 17 |

After the loop:
- `sxx` = $25$
- `syy` = $16$
- `sxy` = $17$

The return value is:
$$ \frac{sxy}{\sqrt{sxx \times syy}} = \frac{17}{\sqrt{25 \times 16}} = \frac{17}{\sqrt{400}} = \frac{17}{20} = 0.85 $$

The return value is rounded to the first decimal place.
$0.85$ rounded to the first decimal place is **$0.9$**.

Therefore, the correct answer is **i) 0.9**.
