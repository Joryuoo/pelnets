### Explanation

The geometric mean of a set of $n$ numbers $a_1, a_2, ..., a_n$ is defined as the $n$-th root of their product.

$$
\text{Geometric Mean} = \sqrt[n]{a_1 \times a_2 \times \dots \times a_n}
$$

Mathematically, calculating the $n$-th root of a number is equivalent to raising that number to the power of $\frac{1}{n}$.

$$
\sqrt[n]{x} = x^{\frac{1}{n}}
$$

In the given pseudo-code:
1. The `for` loop iterates through the array and calculates the total product of all its elements, storing it in the variable `product`.
2. The function `pow(x, y)` raises `x` to the power of `y` ($x^y$).
3. To calculate the geometric mean, we must raise the `product` to the power of $\frac{1}{n}$.

Therefore, the `pow` function should be called as:
`pow(product, 1 ÷ n)`

This means Blank A is `product` and Blank B is `1 ÷ n`. This corresponds to option **b**.
