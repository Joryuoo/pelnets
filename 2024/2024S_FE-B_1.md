- **50 – 79**: P (Pass)
- **0 – 49**: F (Fail)

1. To return `"D"`, the score must be at least 80. Thus, the condition `if (score ___A___ 80)` requires the operator `≥` (greater than or equal to).

3. If the score is less than 50, it falls into the `else` block, which handles the remaining case (0 - 49). Here, we should return `"F"`, so `___C___` must be `"F"`.

Therefore, the correct combination is:
- **A**: `≥`
- **B**: `"P"`
- **C**: `"F"`
