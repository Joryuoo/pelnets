d) `((age ≤ 10) or (age ≥ 60)) or isMember`

### Explanation
The problem specifies that the discount ticket price ($10) is applied if the passenger meets **any** of the following conditions:
1. Aged 10 and under (`age ≤ 10`)
2. Aged 60 and over (`age ≥ 60`)
3. Is a registered member (`isMember = true`)

We can express the age conditions using a logical **OR**: `(age ≤ 10) or (age ≥ 60)`.
Since being a registered member also qualifies a person for the discount regardless of age, we combine the age condition with the membership condition using another logical **OR**. 

Thus, the full condition for returning 10 is:
`((age ≤ 10) or (age ≥ 60)) or isMember`

Let's verify with the other choices:
- a) `and isMember`: This would require the person to be BOTH in the age range AND a member.
- b) `((age ≤ 10) and (age ≥ 60))`: This age condition is impossible since a person cannot be simultaneously $\le 10$ and $\ge 60$.
- c) `and isMember`: Similar to a, requires membership on top of the age condition.

Choice **d** is the only one that correctly represents the "any of these conditions" logic.
