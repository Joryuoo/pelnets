# 2024A_FE-B_2

![[2024A_FE-B_Q2_full.png]]
?
g) `prod_id mod 10 = 3`, `integer part of (pur_prod ÷ 3)`

### Explanation

We need to implement two logic checks:
1. Determine if the last digit of the product ID (`prod_id`) is a 3.
2. Calculate the number of coupons received given the number of purchased products (`pur_prod`), where every 3 products yield 1 coupon.

**1. Finding the Last Digit (Blank A)**
To extract the last digit of an integer in base 10, we can find the remainder when the number is divided by 10. The modulo operator (`mod`) gives us this remainder.
$$ \text{Last Digit} = \text{prod\_id} \pmod{10} $$
Therefore, to check if the last digit is 3, the correct condition is:
`prod_id mod 10 = 3`

**2. Calculating the Number of Coupons (Blank B)**
The customer receives 1 coupon for every 3 products. This is a classic integer division problem. For example:
- $3$ products $\rightarrow 1$ coupon
- $5$ products $\rightarrow 1$ coupon
- $6$ products $\rightarrow 2$ coupons

This calculation corresponds to dividing `pur_prod` by 3 and taking the integer part (floor) of the result:
`integer part of (pur_prod ÷ 3)`

Combining these two gives option **g**.

| `pur_prod` | `pur_prod ÷ 3` | `integer part` (Coupons) |
|------------|----------------|--------------------------|
| 2          | $0.66...$      | 0                        |
| 3          | $1.0$          | 1                        |
| 4          | $1.33...$      | 1                        |
| 6          | $2.0$          | 2                        |
