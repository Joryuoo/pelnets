### Explanation

This incident involves a ransomware attack targeting a corporate network. To strengthen measures against future attacks, we must analyze the vulnerabilities exploited during this incident based on the provided text.

#### Analysis of Vulnerabilities:
- **(7) OS Vulnerability**: The ransomware "succeeded only in compromising the shared PC, which had neglected to apply OS security patches." Therefore, to prevent future compromises at the OS level, **Prevention (Blank A)** must include **OS updates**. (SPAM filters and virus definitions failed or were bypassed, but the direct cause of lateral movement success was the unpatched OS).
- **(4) Data Loss**: "Employee X's PC was not backed up, and important data could not be restored." Since the shared PC was backed up (3) and its data could be restored, regular backups are proven effective for recovery. Thus, **Data Protection (Blank B)** is **Data backup** (encryption was explicitly stated in (9) as "not effective against the ransomware attack").

#### Security Incident Summary
| Category | Identified Weakness | Recommended Measure |
