# 2024A_FE-A_58

![[2024A_FE-A_Q58_full.png]]

c) 70

### Explanation
This is a **Linear Programming** problem. We need to maximize the profit given the constraints on raw materials.

**1. Define Variables**
Let $x$ be the number of units of Product X.
Let $y$ be the number of units of Product Y.

**2. Formulate the Objective Function**
Profit per unit of X = $\$1$
Profit per unit of Y = $\$1.5$
Total Profit ($P$) to maximize:
$$P = 1x + 1.5y$$

**3. Set up Constraints**
From the table, the raw material constraints are:
*   Material A: $2x + 1y \leq 100$
*   Material B: $1x + 2y \leq 80$
*   Non-negativity: $x \geq 0$, $y \geq 0$

**4. Find the Feasible Vertices**
We find the intersection points of the constraint boundary lines:
1.  **Intersection of Material A and B lines:**
    $$2x + y = 100 \implies y = 100 - 2x$$
    Substitute into B's equation:
    $$x + 2(100 - 2x) = 80$$
    $$x + 200 - 4x = 80$$
    $$-3x = -120 \implies x = 40$$
    $$y = 100 - 2(40) = 20$$
    Vertex: $(40, 20)$
2.  **Y-intercept of Material B (where $x=0$):**
    $$1(0) + 2y = 80 \implies y = 40$$
    Vertex: $(0, 40)$
3.  **X-intercept of Material A (where $y=0$):**
    $$2x + 1(0) = 100 \implies x = 50$$
    Vertex: $(50, 0)$
4.  **Origin:**
    Vertex: $(0, 0)$

**5. Evaluate Profit at Each Vertex**
| Vertex $(x, y)$ | Profit Formula $P = x + 1.5y$ | Calculated Profit |
|---|---|---|
| $(0, 0)$ | $0 + 1.5(0)$ | $\$0$ |
| $(50, 0)$ | $50 + 1.5(0)$ | $\$50$ |
| $(0, 40)$ | $0 + 1.5(40)$ | $\$60$ |
| **$(40, 20)$** | **$40 + 1.5(20) = 40 + 30$** | **$\$70$** (Maximum) |

The maximum profit achievable is **$\$70$**.
