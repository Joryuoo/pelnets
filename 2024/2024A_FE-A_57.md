?
b) The correlation coefficient between x and y is negative.

### Explanation
The provided scatter plot shows the relationship between two variables, $x$ and $y$. As the value of $x$ increases (moves to the right), the value of $y$ tends to decrease (moves downward). 

This visual pattern indicates a **negative linear correlation**.

| Correlation Type | Scatter Plot Pattern | Correlation Coefficient ($r$) |
|---|---|---|
| Positive Correlation | Points slope upwards from left to right | $0 < r \leq 1$ |
| **Negative Correlation** | **Points slope downwards from left to right** | $-1 \leq r < 0$ |
| No Correlation | Points are randomly scattered with no clear trend | $r \approx 0$ |

Since the plotted points in the image form a downward-sloping cluster, the correlation coefficient between $x$ and $y$ must be negative.
