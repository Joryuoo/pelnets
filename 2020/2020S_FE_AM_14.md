# 2020S_FE_AM_14
A RAID 6 server is configured with six hard disk drives, and each drive’s capacity is one TB. What is the maximum total data storage capacity in TB? Here, spare disks are not used.

a) 3
b) 4
c) 5
d) 6
?
b) 4
### Explanation
RAID 6 requires a minimum of four disks and uses block-level striping with double distributed parity. It can withstand the failure of two drives simultaneously. 
The usable capacity for a RAID 6 array of $N$ disks is given by the formula $(N - 2) \times C$, where $C$ is the capacity of one drive. 
Given $N = 6$ disks and $C = 1 \text{ TB}$, the usable capacity is $(6 - 2) \times 1 \text{ TB} = 4 \text{ TB}$.
