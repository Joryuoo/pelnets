# 2020S_FE_AM_55
In ITIL, the reduction in the index value expressing maintainability is cited as an example of a key performance index (KPI) in availability management. Which of the following corresponds to such an index?

a) Mean time between failures
b) Mean time between service incidents
c) Mean time to restore service
d) Number of interruptions in a certain period of time
?
c) Mean time to restore service
### Explanation
In ITIL availability management, maintainability is often measured by the Mean Time To Restore Service (MTRS) or Mean Time To Repair (MTTR). A reduction in this index implies that it takes less time to restore a service after a failure, indicating improved maintainability. 
Mean Time Between Failures (MTBF) and Mean Time Between Service Incidents (MTBSI) are measures of reliability, while the number of interruptions measures service continuity/availability directly.
