# 2020S_FE_AM_1
![[2020S_FE_AM_Q1_full.png]]

a) 0111 1111, 1111 1110
b) 0111 1111, 0111 1110
c) 1000 0000, 0111 0000
d) 1111 1111, 1111 1111
?
b) 0111 1111, 0111 1110
### Explanation
In 2's complement format, an overflow occurs when the sum of two positive numbers yields a negative result, or the sum of two negative numbers yields a positive result.
* **a)** `0111 1111` (+127) + `1111 1110` (-2) = `0111 1101` (+125). No overflow.
* **b)** `0111 1111` (+127) + `0111 1110` (+126) = `1111 1101` (-3). Overflow occurs since adding two positive numbers resulted in a sign bit of `1` (which means negative).
* **c)** `1000 0000` (-128) + `0111 0000` (+112) = `1111 0000` (-16). No overflow.
* **d)** `1111 1111` (-1) + `1111 1111` (-1) = `1111 1110` (-2). No overflow.
