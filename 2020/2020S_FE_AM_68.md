# 2020S_FE_AM_68
Which of the following is an external factor related to company's products, identified by SWOT analysis?

a) Strengths in product functions
b) Strengths in sales power
c) Threats by new entrants
d) Weaknesses in product quality
?
c) Threats by new entrants
### Explanation
SWOT analysis assesses Strengths, Weaknesses, Opportunities, and Threats.
- **Strengths** and **Weaknesses** are internal factors specific to the organization (like product functions, sales power, and product quality).
- **Opportunities** and **Threats** are external factors present in the market. Threats by new entrants represent an external factor affecting the company's competitive environment.
