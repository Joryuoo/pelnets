# 2020S_FE_AM_58
Among situations that are identified as results of performing an ISMS internal audit on the basis of ISO/IEC 27001:2013 (Information security management systems—Requirements), which of the following is a situation that the auditor should record in the audit report as a finding?

a) After a risk assessment was performed, risk acceptance criteria were determined.
b) An incident of erroneous disposal of personal information was reported to a competent authority with specified procedures.
c) Spyware was detected by a malware scan, and it was disinfected.
d) The use of a USB memory was permitted in accordance with defined procedures.
?
a) After a risk assessment was performed, risk acceptance criteria were determined.
### Explanation
In ISO/IEC 27001:2013, the organization must establish and maintain risk acceptance criteria *prior* to conducting a risk assessment, not after. Determining risk acceptance criteria after performing a risk assessment compromises the objectivity of the risk evaluation process. Therefore, this non-conformity should be recorded as an audit finding. 
The other options represent appropriate security practices (incident reporting, malware removal, and controlled USB usage).
