# 2020A_FE_AM_80
Which of the following is an organization that manages the standardization of technologies used on the Internet, and develops the technical specifications as an RFC?

a) ANSI
b) IEEE
c) IETF
d) NIST
?
c) IETF
### Explanation
The Internet Engineering Task Force (IETF) is the premier internet standards body, developing open standards through open processes. It publishes RFCs (Request for Comments) that detail the technical and organizational notes about the Internet.
