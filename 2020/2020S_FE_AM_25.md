c) 775
### Explanation
* **Sampling rate:** 11,000 samples per second
* **Sample size:** 8 bits (which equals 1 byte)
* **Data rate:** 11,000 samples/sec * 1 byte/sample = 11,000 bytes/second
* **Memory capacity:** 512 * 10^6 bytes

To find the maximum recording time in seconds:
Time in seconds = (512 * 10^6) / 11,000 = 46,545.45 seconds

Convert seconds to minutes:
Time in minutes = 46,545.45 / 60 = 775.75 minutes.
This is approximately 775 minutes.
