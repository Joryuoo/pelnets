### Explanation
**Blank A:** The SQL statement requires us to filter by date (between January 1, 2020 and March 31, 2020) and then output the customer ID, name, and exam count of customers who took the exam three times or more.
- We need `AND ExamDate BETWEEN '2020-01-01' AND '2020-03-31'` to filter the dates.
- Since we are grouping by customer and selecting aggregates (`COUNT(*)`), we must include a `GROUP BY Customer.CustomerID, Customer.Name` clause. 
- To filter groups with 3 or more exams, we use the `HAVING` clause: `HAVING COUNT(*) >= 3`.
- Therefore, option **c** provides the correct combination of filtering the date, grouping the results, and filtering the aggregated count.
