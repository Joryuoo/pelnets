### Explanation

According to the security policy enforced by R1:
*   "No traffic initiated from the [B1] should be allowed into the [B2] or [B3]." Since the Internet is an untrusted public network, traffic from it should not be allowed into the organization's networks. Thus, **B1 is the Internet**.
*   "Hosts in the [B2] are allowed to initiate any type of traffic..." The internal network users need broad access and are fully trusted. Thus, **B2 is the internal network**.
*   "Hosts in the [B3] are allowed to initiate only web traffic (HTTP or HTTPS) to the [B1]." Guest users on the meeting network should only be allowed basic web access to the Internet. Thus, **B3 is the meeting network**.

Matching these assignments gives us **B1 = Internet**, **B2 = internal network**, and **B3 = meeting network**, corresponding to choice **c**.
