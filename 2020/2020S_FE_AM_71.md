# 2020S_FE_AM_71
Which of the following is an appropriate explanation of the technology S curve?

a) It represents the diffusion process of a new technology. It shows that the growth rate of the penetration rate gradually decreases as products applying the technology penetrate the market, by using a frequency distribution curve.
b) It represents the maturing process of technology. It shows that during the time until the new technology is actually spread, the degree of awareness changes as time passes.
c) It represents the process of technological progress. It shows that technology advances slowly at the beginning and then advances rapidly, and thereafter, slowly stagnates.
d) It represents the relationship between production volume and unit cost. It shows the course of reduction in unit cost in accordance with an increase in cumulative production volume.
?
c) It represents the process of technological progress. It shows that technology advances slowly at the beginning and then advances rapidly, and thereafter, slowly stagnates.
### Explanation
The **technology S curve** describes the evolution of a technology's performance or capability over time. 
* At the beginning (introduction phase), progress is slow as the technology is being developed and early challenges are addressed.
* Once breakthroughs are made, it enters a phase of rapid growth (growth phase) where significant improvements are achieved quickly.
* Eventually, the technology approaches its physical or theoretical limits, and the rate of progress slows down (maturity phase), creating an "S" shape.
