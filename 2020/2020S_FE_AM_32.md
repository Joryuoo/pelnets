# 2020S_FE_AM_32
![[2020S_FE_AM_Q32_full.png]]

a) 0.13
b) 0.26
c) 0.35
d) 0.52
?
b) 0.26
### Explanation
To calculate the total delay for data sent from location A to arrive at location B via a geostationary satellite:

1. **Uplink Propagation Time (A to Satellite):**
   Distance = 37,500 km = 37,500,000 meters.
   Speed of radio waves = $3 \times 10^8$ m/s = 300,000,000 m/s.
   Time = Distance / Speed = 37,500,000 / 300,000,000 = 0.125 seconds = 125 milliseconds.

2. **Satellite Relay Delay:**
   The relay at the satellite causes a delay of 10 milliseconds = 0.010 seconds.

3. **Downlink Propagation Time (Satellite to B):**
   Distance = 37,500 km, so time = 0.125 seconds = 125 milliseconds.

**Total Delay:**
Total Delay = Uplink + Relay + Downlink
Total Delay = 0.125 s + 0.010 s + 0.125 s = 0.26 seconds.

Therefore, the correct answer is 0.26 seconds.
