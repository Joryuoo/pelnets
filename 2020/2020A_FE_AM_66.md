# 2020A_FE_AM_66
According to the “competitive strategy” proposed by Philip Kotler, which of the following describes a company that has no high market share but utilizes quantitatively large management resources? 

a) Market challenger 
b) Market follower 
c) Market leader 
d) Market nicher
?
a) Market challenger
### Explanation
Philip Kotler classified companies in a market based on market share and competitive position into four types:
- Market Leader: The company with the largest market share.
- Market Challenger: The runner-up company aiming to become the leader. While it doesn't have the top market share, it utilizes significant management resources to aggressively challenge the market leader.
- Market Follower: A company that maintains a lower share and chooses not to challenge the leader, minimizing risks and following the leader's pricing or product strategies.
- Market Nicher: A company targeting a small, specialized segment of the market that larger firms overlook.

Since the company has a low market share but abundant (quantitatively large) management resources, it describes a Market challenger.
