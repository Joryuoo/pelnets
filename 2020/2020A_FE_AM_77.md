# 2020A_FE_AM_77
Which of the following is a method of education and training in which trainees engage in simulated business activities, interact with other people to develop experiences, and try out different strategies?

a) Case study
b) Debate
c) In-basket
d) Roll-playing
?
d) Roll-playing
### Explanation
Role-playing (sometimes spelled roll-playing in translated materials) is a training technique in which employees act out simulated business scenarios, interacting with others to develop soft skills, experience, and test different strategies in a safe environment.
