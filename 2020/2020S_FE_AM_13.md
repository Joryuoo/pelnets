# 2020S_FE_AM_13
As a backup system configuration, which of the following is an appropriate description of a hot site?

a) A shared site is prepared, and at the time of the occurrence of a failure, the backed-up data and program are loaded to recover the system, and business operations are resumed.
b) A site is operated as a standby site, the data and programs are updated at all times via a network, and the business operation is resumed immediately when a failure occurs.
c) A spare site is secured beforehand, and at the time of the occurrence of a failure, the required hardware and the backed-up data and program are loaded to recover the system, and the business operation is resumed.
d) Hardware is prepared at a spare site, and the periodically backed-up data and program are loaded and stored. At the time of occurrence of a failure, the system is restored by making use of these stored entities, and the business operation is resumed.
?
b) A site is operated as a standby site, the data and programs are updated at all times via a network, and the business operation is resumed immediately when a failure occurs.
### Explanation
A hot site is a fully functional backup site that already has hardware, software, and real-time or near-real-time data mirroring the primary site. In the event of a failure, it allows for immediate resumption of business operations.
- Option a refers to a shared standby site.
- Option c refers to a cold site.
- Option d refers to a warm site.
