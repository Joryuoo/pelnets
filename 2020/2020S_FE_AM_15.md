# 2020S_FE_AM_15
The life cycle of a computer system can be classified from the viewpoint of failure into the three periods of early failure period, random failure period, and wear-out failure period. Which of the following is the most appropriate description of the measures in the early failure period?

a) Implementing prior replacement of components
b) Implementing scheduled maintenance and condition-based maintenance
c) Performing age-based maintenance on the basis of the cumulative operating time of the items that make up the system
d) Strengthening the design review and failure analysis in order to reduce errors in the design and manufacturing
?
d) Strengthening the design review and failure analysis in order to reduce errors in the design and manufacturing
### Explanation
The early failure period (also known as infant mortality) is characterized by a decreasing failure rate over time. It is typically caused by manufacturing defects, poor quality control, or design errors. The best measure is to eliminate these causes before or shortly after deployment by strengthening design reviews, failure analysis, and rigorous testing (e.g., burn-in testing). Options a, b, and c are more appropriate for mitigating failures during the wear-out failure period.
