### Explanation
The Exclusive-OR (XOR) operation evaluates to true if and only if exactly one of the inputs is true. In boolean algebra, the XOR of A and B can be written as `(A AND NOT B) OR (NOT A AND B)`. It is also logically equivalent to `(A OR B) AND NOT (A AND B)`. In the given notation, this matches choice b, where the second term represents the inverse of the logical AND of A and B.
