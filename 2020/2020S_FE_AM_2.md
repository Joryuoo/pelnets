# 2020S_FE_AM_2
What is the probability of getting at least two heads when a fair coin is flipped five times?

a) 0.78125
b) 0.8125
c) 0.84375
d) 0.875
?
b) 0.8125
### Explanation
The total number of possible outcomes when flipping a coin 5 times is $2^5 = 32$.
The probability of getting at least two heads is equal to $1 - P(\text{0 heads}) - P(\text{1 head})$.
* Probability of 0 heads: There is only 1 outcome (all tails), so $P(0) = \frac{1}{32}$.
* Probability of 1 head: There are $\binom{5}{1} = 5$ outcomes (each having one head), so $P(1) = \frac{5}{32}$.

Thus, the probability of at least two heads is:
$1 - (\frac{1}{32} + \frac{5}{32}) = 1 - \frac{6}{32} = \frac{26}{32} = \frac{13}{16} = 0.8125$.
