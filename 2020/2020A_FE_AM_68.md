# 2020A_FE_AM_68
Which of the following is mostly tacit knowledge? 

a) A list of countries and their capitals 
b) An inventory of assets 
c) Contents of a washing machine manual 
d) How to ride a bicycle
?
d) How to ride a bicycle
### Explanation
Tacit knowledge is knowledge that is difficult to transfer to another person by means of writing it down or verbalizing it. It involves skills, ideas, and experiences that are highly personal and usually gained through practice.
- Choice d, "How to ride a bicycle", is a classic example of tacit knowledge because it relies on physical coordination and experience that is very hard to write in a manual.
- Choices a, b, and c are all examples of explicit knowledge, which can be easily articulated, codified, and stored in documents or databases.
