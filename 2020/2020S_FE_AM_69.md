# 2020S_FE_AM_69
Heskett classified customers into four categories and explained the importance of improving customer satisfaction. Which of the following types of customers is dissatisfied and, if given a chance, would encourage others to switch as well?

a) Captive
b) Mercenary
c) Propagandist
d) Terrorist
?
d) Terrorist
### Explanation
In Heskett's model of customer loyalty, customers are categorized as:
- **Apostles/Propagandists:** Highly satisfied and very loyal.
- **Mercenaries:** Satisfied but not loyal (price sensitive and prone to switch).
- **Hostages/Captives:** Dissatisfied but loyal (stuck due to lack of alternatives or high switching costs).
- **Terrorists:** Highly dissatisfied and actively spread negative word-of-mouth, discouraging others and switching if possible.
