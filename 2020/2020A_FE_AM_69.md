# 2020A_FE_AM_69
Which of the following is appropriate as a description of Customer Relationship Management (CRM) software? 

a) It mainly provides online tools for sales, customer service, and marketing. 
b) It mainly provides partner relationship management. 
c) It only supports direct-marketing campaigns.  
d) It provides information and tools to make call center, help desk, and customer support staff more efficient mainly by phone. 
?
a) It mainly provides online tools for sales, customer service, and marketing.
### Explanation
Customer Relationship Management (CRM) software is designed to manage a company's interactions with current and potential customers across multiple channels.
- Choice a accurately describes the core modules of CRM systems, which typically integrate sales automation, customer service/support, and marketing automation into a single platform.
- Choice b describes Partner Relationship Management (PRM).
- Choice c is incorrect because CRM goes beyond just direct marketing.
- Choice d restricts the definition only to call center or help desk functionality (often termed just "customer support" or "CTI"), which is merely a subset of what a full CRM does.
