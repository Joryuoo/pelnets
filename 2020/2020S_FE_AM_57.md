# 2020S_FE_AM_57
Which of the following is an effective means of protecting an information system from the damage from over-voltage that occurs because of a lightning strike?

a) Connecting the communication cable and the computer through a surge protection device (SPD)
b) Digitalizing the control circuit of electric power facilities
c) Installing a private power generator
d) Splitting the communication wire into two (2) systems that have different paths
?
a) Connecting the communication cable and the computer through a surge protection device (SPD)
### Explanation
A lightning strike can cause a sudden surge or over-voltage in electrical and communication lines. A Surge Protection Device (SPD) is specifically designed to protect electronic equipment from such voltage spikes by diverting the excess voltage to the ground.
