# 2020A_FE_AM_75
In a company, which of the following is the main role of the manufacturing department?

a) Attracting, developing, and sustaining the labor force and maintaining employee records
b) Managing financial assets and maintaining financial records
c) Producing products and services
d) Selling products and services
?
c) Producing products and services
### Explanation
The manufacturing (or production) department is responsible for transforming raw materials or components into finished goods or services.
- a) describes Human Resources.
- b) describes Finance/Accounting.
- d) describes Sales/Marketing.
