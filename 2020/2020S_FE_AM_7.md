# 2020S_FE_AM_7
Every year that is exactly divisible by four is a leap year, except for years that are exactly divisible by 100, but these centurial years are leap years if they are exactly divisible by 400. For example, the years 1700, 1800, and 1900 are not leap years, but the years 1600 and 2000 are. How many leap years are there between 1895 and 2021?

a) 30
b) 31
c) 32
d) 33
?
b) 31
### Explanation
First, find all the years exactly divisible by 4 between 1895 and 2021.
The first year divisible by 4 is 1896, and the last is 2020.
The number of years in this sequence is $\frac{2020 - 1896}{4} + 1 = 32$.

Next, identify the centurial years (divisible by 100) within this range and apply the leap year rules:
* 1900 is divisible by 100 but not by 400, so it is **not** a leap year.
* 2000 is divisible by 100 and by 400, so it **is** a leap year.

Since 1900 was included in the initial count of 32 but is not a leap year, we must subtract it:
Total leap years = $32 - 1 = 31$.
