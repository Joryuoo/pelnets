# 2020S_FE_AM_10
Which of the following is classified as an internal interrupt?

a) An interrupt due to an abnormal power condition, such as a momentary loss of the commercial power supply
b) An interrupt due to having performed division by zero
c) An interrupt due to the completion of input or output
d) An interrupt due to the occurrence of a memory parity error
?
b) An interrupt due to having performed division by zero
### Explanation
Interrupts are classified as internal or external based on their source:
* **Internal Interrupts (Exceptions):** Caused by the CPU's execution of an instruction. Examples include division by zero, overflow, invalid opcode, and page faults.
* **External Interrupts:** Caused by hardware outside the CPU. Examples include I/O completion, timer expiration, power failures, or hardware errors like a memory parity error.

Therefore, division by zero (b) is an internal interrupt.
