# 2020A_FE_AM_67
Which of the following is an appropriate explanation of telemarketing? 

a) Direct marketing that includes calling people at home and asking for donations, opinions, or purchases. 
b) Direct marketing that sells products and services and builds customer relationships over the internet. 
c) Direct marketing that sends offers, announcements, reminders, or other items to a person at a particular address. 
d) Direct marketing that sends sales alerts, links to website updates, appointments, or delivery reminders to large groups of people.
?
a) Direct marketing that includes calling people at home and asking for donations, opinions, or purchases.
### Explanation
Telemarketing refers specifically to direct marketing utilizing the telephone.
- Choice a correctly describes telemarketing (marketing via telephone calls).
- Choice b describes internet or digital marketing.
- Choice c describes direct mail.
- Choice d describes email marketing or SMS marketing.
