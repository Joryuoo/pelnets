# 2020A_FE_AM_78
![[2020A_FE_AM_Q78_full.png]]

a) Final acquisition cost method (Final purchase cost method)
b) First-in first-out method
c) Moving average method
d) Periodic average method
?
c) Moving average method
### Explanation
The moving average method calculates the new average unit cost of inventory each time a new purchase is made. The formula sums the cost of the existing inventory and the newly received goods, then divides it by the total quantity of the existing and newly received goods.
