c) 190
### Explanation
Jobs are executed sequentially based on priority by a single CPU, and printing starts right after a job finishes execution on the CPU, using a single printer via spooling.
Job CPU execution times: A=50, B=30, C=20, D=40
Job print times: A=50, B=40, C=30, D=20

Let's track the start and end times for CPU and Printer for each job:
- **Job A**:
  - CPU starts at 0, ends at 50.
  - Printer starts at 50, ends at 50 + 50 = 100.
- **Job B**:
  - CPU starts at 50 (after A finishes CPU), ends at 50 + 30 = 80.
  - Printer starts at 100 (after A finishes printing), ends at 100 + 40 = 140.
- **Job C**:
  - CPU starts at 80 (after B finishes CPU), ends at 80 + 20 = 100.
  - Printer starts at 140 (after B finishes printing), ends at 140 + 30 = 170.
- **Job D**:
  - CPU starts at 100 (after C finishes CPU), ends at 100 + 40 = 140.
  - Printer starts at 170 (after C finishes printing), ends at 170 + 20 = 190.

The total time until all printing is completed is 190 seconds.
