a) Current net profit
b) Current net profit before taxes
c) Extraordinary profit
d) Ordinary profit
?
d) Ordinary profit
### Explanation
In Japanese corporate accounting, Ordinary Profit (Keijo rieki) is calculated by adding Non-operating revenue and subtracting Non-operating expense from the Operating profit. It reflects the normal profitability of the company's continuous business activities, including financial activities like interest income or expenses.
