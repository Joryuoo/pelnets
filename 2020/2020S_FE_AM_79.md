# 2020S_FE_AM_79
An investor purchased property A for $500,000. Two years later, the investor sold the property for $1,000,000. Which of the following is the ROI of this investment (%)?

a) 50
b) 100
c) 150
d) 200
?
b) 100
### Explanation
**Return on Investment (ROI)** is calculated as:
`ROI (%) = (Net Profit / Cost of Investment) * 100`

1. Cost of Investment = $500,000
2. Revenue from Sale = $1,000,000
3. Net Profit = Revenue - Cost of Investment = $1,000,000 - $500,000 = $500,000

ROI = ($500,000 / $500,000) * 100 = 1 * 100 = 100%
