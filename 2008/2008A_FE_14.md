### Explanation
A factorial function $f(n)$ calculates $n!$, which is the product of all positive integers less than or equal to $n$. 

It can be defined recursively as follows:
- **Recursive step (Box A):** For $n > 1$, $n! = n \times (n-1)!$. In terms of the function, $f(n) = n \times f(n-1)$.
- **Base case (Box B):** For $n = 0$, $0!$ is defined as 1. So, $f(0) = 1$.

Matching these to the choices, Box A should be $n \times f(n-1)$ and Box B should be $1$. This corresponds to option c.
