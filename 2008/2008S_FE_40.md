### Explanation
A Data Flow Diagram (DFD) or Context Diagram illustrates the flow of information between a system and external entities. 

In a typical ordering and fulfillment system:
1.  When goods are shipped to a customer, a **Delivery invoice** (DF1) is typically sent along with or prior to the physical goods.
2.  To request payment for the delivered goods, a **Payment bill** (or invoice for payment) (DF2) is sent to the customer.
3.  Internal or warehouse operations involve checking or updating the **In-stock inventory** (DF3).

Based on the standard flow of these business processes, combination b) correctly aligns the logical data flows to their respective roles in the omitted context diagram.
