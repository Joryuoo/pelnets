# 2008A_FE_36

Q36. Which of the following is the term that refers to collecting a large volume of data through a variety of corporate activities, organizing, integrating, and saving the data for specific purposes, and using it for decision-making support, for example?

a) Data administration
b) Data dictionary
c) Data mapping
d) Data warehouse
?
d) Data warehouse

### Explanation
### Explanation
A **Data warehouse** is a centralized repository of integrated data from one or more disparate sources within an enterprise. It is specifically designed to organize, store, and analyze large volumes of historical data to support business intelligence (BI) activities, such as reporting, data mining, and decision-making.

* **a) Data administration:** Refers to the management and maintenance of databases (e.g., DBA roles).
* **b) Data dictionary:** A centralized repository of information about data (metadata), such as its meaning, relationships to other data, origin, and usage.
* **c) Data mapping:** The process of matching fields from one database to another, often used during data migration or integration, but not the repository itself.
