# 2008A_FE_7

There are 3 white balls and 3 red balls in a box. What is the probability that the two balls taken out at random from the box are one white ball and one red ball?

a) 1/5
b) 1/3
c) 1/2
d) 3/5
?
d) 3/5

### Explanation
The total number of balls is 6. We want to find the probability of drawing one white and one red ball.
- Total number of ways to draw 2 balls from 6 is ${6 \choose 2} = \frac{6 \times 5}{2} = 15$.
- Number of ways to draw 1 white ball from 3 is ${3 \choose 1} = 3$.
- Number of ways to draw 1 red ball from 3 is ${3 \choose 1} = 3$.
- The number of favorable outcomes is $3 \times 3 = 9$.

The probability is $\frac{9}{15} = \frac{3}{5}$.
