# 2008A_FE_74

### Explanation
Let the fixed cost per period be $F$ and the variable cost per product unit be $v$.
From the table, we have two equations based on the manufacturing cost formula (Total Cost = Fixed Cost + Variable Cost * Quantity):
For Period 1: $F + 1000v = 5000$ (Eq. 1)
For Period 2: $F + 1500v = 6000$ (Eq. 2)

Subtracting Eq. 1 from Eq. 2:
$1500v - 1000v = 6000 - 5000$
$500v = 1000$
$v = 2$ (The variable cost per unit is $2)

Substitute $v = 2$ back into Eq. 1:
$F + 1000(2) = 5000$
$F + 2000 = 5000$
$F = 3000$

The fixed cost to produce the planned products during a single period is $3,000.
