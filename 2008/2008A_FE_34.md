# 2008A_FE_34

Q34. Which of the following helps improve availability?

a) Doubling both MTBF and MTTR
b) Doubling MTBF and reducing MTTR to half
c) Reducing both MTBF and MTTR to half
d) Reducing MTBF to half and doubling MTTR
?
b) Doubling MTBF and reducing MTTR to half

### Explanation
### Explanation
Availability represents the proportion of time a system is in a functioning condition. It is mathematically defined as:

$Availability = \frac{MTBF}{MTBF + MTTR}$

Where:
* **MTBF (Mean Time Between Failures):** The average time the system operates without failing (uptime). A higher MTBF is better.
* **MTTR (Mean Time To Repair):** The average time required to repair a failed system (downtime). A lower MTTR is better.

To increase Availability, you must increase the numerator (MTBF) and/or decrease the repair time (MTTR). 
Choice **b)**, which doubles MTBF (more uptime) and reduces MTTR to half (less downtime), clearly maximizes the Availability fraction compared to the other options.
