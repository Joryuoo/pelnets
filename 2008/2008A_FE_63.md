The SQL statement selects `mName`, `mTypeCode`, and `mCompany` from tables `movie` (aliased as M) and `movietype` (aliased as MT) where `M.mTypeID = MT.mTypeID` AND `MT.mTypeName = 'Action'`.

1. Looking at `movietype`, the row with `mTypeName = 'Action'` has `mTypeID = '01'` and `mTypeCode = 'AC'`.
2. Looking at `movie`, the rows with `mTypeID = '01'` are `M01` (Beowulf, Paramount Pictures) and `M04` (Hitman, 20th Century Fox).
3. Therefore, the result will extract `mName`, `mTypeCode` ('AC'), and `mCompany` for Beowulf and Hitman.
