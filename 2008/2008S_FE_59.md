# 2008S_FE_59

Q59. Which of the following is an appropriate purpose of installing a firewall in a computer 
network?  
 
a) To authenticate internal users  
b) To block and permit data traffic  
c) To protect a computer from computer viruses  
d) To verify company policies
?
b) To block and permit data traffic

### Explanation
### Explanation
A firewall is a network security device or software that monitors and controls incoming and outgoing network traffic based on predetermined security rules. Its primary purpose is to block unauthorized traffic and permit authorized data traffic, acting as a barrier between a trusted internal network and untrusted external networks.
- **Authenticating internal users** is typically done by directory services (like Active Directory) or RADIUS/TACACS servers.
- **Protecting from viruses** is the job of antivirus/anti-malware software.
- **Verifying company policies** is a broader compliance function.
