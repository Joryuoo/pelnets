# 2008S_FE_50

Q50. Which of the following can make MTBF longer in system maintenance?

a) To disperse a single-location maintenance center to various locations
b) To do preventive maintenance
c) To do provisional maintenance where a failure has occurred
d) To do remote maintenance
?
b) To do preventive maintenance

### Explanation
MTBF (Mean Time Between Failures) is a metric of reliability that measures the average time a system operates before failing. To make it longer, you need to reduce the occurrence of failures. **Preventive maintenance** proactively services or replaces components before they break, thereby preventing failures and extending MTBF.
- The other choices (dispersing centers, provisional maintenance, remote maintenance) are strategies designed to reduce MTTR (Mean Time To Repair) by fixing the system faster once a failure has already occurred.
