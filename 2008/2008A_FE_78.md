# 2008A_FE_78

Q78. A certain company has installed an online computer for the purpose of improving its inventory control system. When the company anticipates sales of 15,000 units per year, ordering cost of $60 per order, and annual holding cost of $20 per unit, what is the optimal order quantity (in units) per order? Here, the fixed order quantity model can be applied.

a) 300
b) 500
c) 1,500
d) 3,000
?
a) 300

### Explanation
The fixed order quantity model (also known as the Economic Order Quantity or EOQ model) formula is:
$EOQ = \sqrt{\frac{2DS}{H}}$
Where:
- $D$ = Annual demand quantity = 15,000 units
- $S$ = Fixed cost per order (ordering cost) = $60
- $H$ = Annual holding cost per unit = $20

Plugging in the values:
$EOQ = \sqrt{\frac{2 \times 15000 \times 60}{20}}$
$EOQ = \sqrt{\frac{1800000}{20}}$
$EOQ = \sqrt{90000} = 300$

The optimal order quantity is 300 units.
