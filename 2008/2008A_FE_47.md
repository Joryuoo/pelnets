### Explanation
The condition is `Condition 1 OR (Condition 2 AND Condition 3)`.

Decision-condition coverage (also known as condition/decision coverage) requires that every condition in a decision takes on all possible outcomes at least once, and the decision itself takes on all possible outcomes at least once.

Let's evaluate the given test cases:
- **(1) C1=True, C2=False, C3=False:** `True OR (False AND False)` -> `True OR False` -> **Decision is True**.
- **(2) C1=False, C2=True, C3=True:** `False OR (True AND True)` -> `False OR True` -> **Decision is True**.

Notice that in both existing test cases, the overall decision evaluates to True. For branch/decision coverage, we need the overall decision to evaluate to False at least once. To make `C1 OR (C2 AND C3)` evaluate to False, we need:
- `C1` must be False.
- `(C2 AND C3)` must be False (meaning either C2 is False, C3 is False, or both are False).

Looking at choice a): `C1=False, C2=False, C3=True`.
`False OR (False AND True)` -> `False OR False` -> **Decision is False**.
This adds a test case where the decision is False, fulfilling the coverage criteria.
