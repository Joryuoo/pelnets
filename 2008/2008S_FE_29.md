### Explanation
Based on the Gantt chart provided, the OS uses a 30 ms Round-Robin slice.

1. **Time 0-10:** Process A executes on CPU (10 ms) then requests I/O.
2. **Time 10-40:** Process B takes its first CPU slice (30 ms of its 60 ms requirement).
3. **Time 40-70:** Since A is still busy with its 60 ms I/O (which finishes at time 70), B is scheduled again. Process B gets another CPU slice (30 ms), completing its 60 ms CPU requirement, then requests I/O.
4. **Time 70:** Process A finishes I/O and becomes ready.
5. **Time 70-80:** Process A executes on CPU (10 ms) then requests its second I/O.
6. **Time 80-130:** Process A is busy with I/O (finishing at time 140). Process B is busy with I/O (finishing at time 130). The CPU is idle.
7. **Time 130-160:** Process B finishes I/O and executes its final CPU burst (30 ms). Process B is now complete.
8. **Time 140:** Process A finishes I/O and enters the ready queue.
9. **Time 160-170:** Process A executes its final CPU burst (10 ms). Process A is now complete.

Both processes finish exactly at **170 ms**.
