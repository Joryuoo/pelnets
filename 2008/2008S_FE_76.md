# 2008S_FE_76

Q76. The seven traditional, fundamental quality control tools consist of Check sheet, Pareto chart, Stratification, Scatter Diagram, Histogram, and Control Chart.  What is the last (or 7th) one?  

a) Affinity diagram 
b) Cause and effect diagram  
c) Relation diagram 
d) Tree diagram
?
b) Cause and effect diagram

### Explanation
### Explanation
The Seven Basic Tools of Quality are a fixed set of graphical techniques identified as being most helpful in troubleshooting issues related to quality. They are: 
1. Check sheet
2. Pareto chart
3. Stratification (sometimes substituted with Flowchart)
4. Scatter diagram
5. Histogram
6. Control chart
7. Cause-and-effect diagram (also known as an Ishikawa or fishbone diagram)

Options a), c), and d) are part of the Seven *New* Management and Planning Tools.
