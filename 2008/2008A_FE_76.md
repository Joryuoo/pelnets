# 2008A_FE_76

![[2008A_FE_AM_Q76_full.png]]
?
c) 5

### Explanation
The maximin strategy involves making a decision that maximizes the minimum possible payoff (the "best of the worst" outcome).
Let's find the minimum payoff for Company A for each of its strategies:
- Strategy a1: The payoffs for A are -15 (if B chooses b1) and 20 (if B chooses b2). The minimum payoff is -15.
- Strategy a2: The payoffs for A are 5 (if B chooses b1) and 0 (if B chooses b2). The minimum payoff is 0.
So Company A's maximin choice is **Strategy a2** (guarantees at least 0).

Now let's find the minimum payoff for Company B for each of its strategies:
- Strategy b1: The payoffs for B are 15 (if A chooses a1) and -5 (if A chooses a2). The minimum payoff is -5.
- Strategy b2: The payoffs for B are -20 (if A chooses a1) and 0 (if A chooses a2). The minimum payoff is -20.
So Company B's maximin choice is **Strategy b1** (guarantees at least -5).

If both companies play their maximin strategies, Company A plays a2 and Company B plays b1.
The resulting payoff cell is (a2, b1), which gives a profit of 5 for Company A and -5 for Company B.
Therefore, the profit of Company A is 5.
