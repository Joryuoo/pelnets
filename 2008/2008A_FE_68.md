# 2008A_FE_68

Which of the following is an appropriate action to be taken when a computer virus is found?

a) First, the computer is powered off, because the virus program may be resident in memory.
b) First, the infected computer is disconnected from the relevant network, because other computers may be infected via the network.
c) If a worm that infects a wide area in a short period of time is found while an on-line business system is running, the virus countermeasure is taken without stopping the system.
d) The reproducibility of virus activities is confirmed for the purpose of identifying the virus name by special characteristics activated at the time of infection.
?
b) First, the infected computer is disconnected from the relevant network, because other computers may be infected via the network.

### Explanation
### Explanation
When a computer virus is detected, the immediate first step should be to disconnect the infected computer from the network (e.g., unplug the LAN cable or disable Wi-Fi) to prevent the malware from propagating to other systems.
* **a)** Powering off the computer immediately may destroy volatile memory evidence useful for later forensic analysis.
* **c)** Worms require prompt containment; attempting to fix it without isolating the system allows it to continue spreading.
* **d)** Reproducing virus activities poses severe risks and shouldn't be done as an immediate response.
