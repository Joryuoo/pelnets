# 2008S_FE_2

In what radix does the following equation hold?

131 - 45 = 53

a) 6
b) 7
c) 8
d) 9
?
b) 7

### Explanation
### Explanation
Let the unknown radix be `r`. We can set up an algebraic equation by converting each number to base 10:
- $131_r = 1 \cdot r^2 + 3 \cdot r + 1$
- $45_r = 4 \cdot r + 5$
- $53_r = 5 \cdot r + 3$

Substitute these into the given equation:
$(r^2 + 3r + 1) - (4r + 5) = 5r + 3$
$r^2 - r - 4 = 5r + 3$
$r^2 - 6r - 7 = 0$

Factoring the quadratic equation gives:
$(r - 7)(r + 1) = 0$

The solutions are `r = 7` and `r = -1`. Since a radix must be a positive integer greater than any digit used in the numbers (the largest digit here is 5), the radix must be 7.
