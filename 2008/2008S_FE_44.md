### Explanation
Reliability growth curves (like the Gompertz curve) are often used to evaluate software testing progress by plotting the cumulative number of bugs detected over time. When the curve reaches a saturation level (flattens out), it indicates that most bugs have been found, making the software's quality stable and reliable. Since the graph for Subsystem A shows it reaching this saturation level compared to Subsystem B, Subsystem A is considered to have superior and more stable quality.
