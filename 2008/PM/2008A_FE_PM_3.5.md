For a Sliding Window protocol with a window size $W = 5$, the sender transmits 5 frames consecutively, and the receiver sends a single ACK after receiving all 5 frames.

* **Time to transmit user's data:** $5 \text{ frames} \times 10.0 \text{ ms} = 50.0$ ms.
* **Total transmission time:** This includes the time to transmit all 5 frames, the propagation delay of the last frame, the transmission time of the ACK, and the propagation delay of the ACK.
  * Time to transmit 5 full frames = $5 \times 10.1 \text{ ms} = 50.5$ ms.
  * Propagation delay of the last bit = $20.0$ ms.
  * ACK transmission time = $0.1$ ms.
  * ACK propagation delay = $20.0$ ms.
* **Total Time** = $50.5 + 20.0 + 0.1 + 20.0 = 90.6$ ms.

**Efficiency** = $50.0 \text{ ms} / 90.6 \text{ ms} \approx 0.5518$, which matches $0.55$.
