The private key `d` must satisfy the equation: `d × e = 1 mod φ(n)`.
1. From our previous calculations, `e = 3` and `φ(n) = 4`.
2. We need a number `d` such that `(d × 3)` divided by `4` leaves a remainder of `1`.

Let's test the options:
- **a) 3:** `3 × 3 = 9`. `9 mod 4 = 1`. (Correct)
- **b) 4:** `4 × 3 = 12`. `12 mod 4 = 0`. (Incorrect)
- **c) 5:** `5 × 3 = 15`. `15 mod 4 = 3`. (Incorrect)
- **d) 6:** `6 × 3 = 18`. `18 mod 4 = 2`. (Incorrect)

Therefore, the private key `d` is `3`. Choice (a) is the correct answer.
