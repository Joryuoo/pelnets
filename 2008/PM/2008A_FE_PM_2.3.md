- The SQL query selects `Teacher_id` and the sum of `Units` from the `Course_Table`.
- First, the `WHERE Class_id <> '1-A'` clause filters out any records where `Class_id` is '1-A'. Removing these leaves the following valid rows:
  - F-0201 (MAT-01, 2-C, 3 units)
  - F-0250 (BIO, 2-B, 4 units)
  - F-0201 (MAT-01, 2-B, 3 units)
  - F-0250 (CHEM, 2-C, 4 units)
  - F-0145 (ECON, 2-B, 2 units)
  - F-0250 (PHYSICS, 2-C, 4 units)
  - F-0201 (MAT-02, 2-B, 3 units)
  - F-0125 (WHIST, 2-B, 2 units)
- Next, `GROUP BY Teacher_id` groups the remaining records by the teacher, and `HAVING COUNT(Teacher_id) > 2` filters groups that have 2 or fewer records.
  - F-0201 has 3 records. (Sum of Units = 3 + 3 + 3 = 9)
  - F-0250 has 3 records. (Sum of Units = 4 + 4 + 4 = 12)
  - F-0145 has 1 record. (Excluded)
  - F-0125 has 1 record. (Excluded)
- The resulting output is F-0201 with a sum of 9, and F-0250 with a sum of 12, which matches choice **c**.
