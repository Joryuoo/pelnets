### Explanation
- The question asks for the primary key of the `Course` table assuming it is in 3rd Normal Form.
- A primary key uniquely identifies each record in a table.
- The scenario states: "Identical courses are distinguished by the section id. One teacher and one class are assigned for each course and section."
- This means that a combination of `Course_id` and `Section_id` uniquely identifies the class and teacher assigned. Thus, `Course_id` and `Section_id` together serve as the composite primary key. Choice **b** is correct.
- Choice **a** (`Course_id`) is insufficient since a course can have multiple sections, meaning `Course_id` alone does not uniquely identify a row.
- Choices **c** and **d** include `Class_id` and `Teacher_id`. Since these are determined by the primary key (`Course_id` and `Section_id`), they are non-key attributes and not part of the primary key themselves.
