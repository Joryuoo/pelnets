### Explanation
The problem defines efficiency as the ratio of the time to transmit the user's data to the total transmission time.

* **Time to transmit user's data:** The user data is 10,000 bits. Its transmission time = $10,000 \text{ bits} / 1,000,000 \text{ bits/s} = 0.01 \text{ seconds} = 10.0$ ms.
* **Total transmission time:** $50.2$ ms (calculated in Subquestion 3.3).

**Efficiency** = $10.0 \text{ ms} / 50.2 \text{ ms} \approx 0.1992$, which matches $0.199$.
