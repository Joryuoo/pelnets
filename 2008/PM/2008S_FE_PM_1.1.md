To determine which activities are most important to emphasize in order to meet the project schedule, we need to identify the **critical path**. The critical path is the longest sequence of dependent activities and determines the minimum duration of the overall project. Any delay in an activity on the critical path will delay the entire project.

By analyzing the table of activities, their predecessors, and durations, we can identify two main paths through the project:
*   **Path 1:** Activity 1 -> 4 -> 5 -> 6 -> 7 -> 10
    *   Duration: 7 + 5 + 18 + 5 + 5 + 5 = **45 days**
*   **Path 2:** Activity 1 -> 2 -> 3 -> 8 -> 9 -> 10
    *   Duration: 7 + 5 + 5 + 10 + 7 + 5 = **39 days**

Since Path 1 takes the longest time (45 days), it is the critical path. Therefore, the activities that require the most emphasis to meet the schedule are **1, 4, 5, 6, 7, and 10**.

*   **a, b, d** are incorrect because they include activities from the non-critical path (such as 2, 3, 8, or 9), which have slack time and are not as critical to strictly monitoring the total project duration.
