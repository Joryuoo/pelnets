In Flowchart 1, the standard scoring logic evaluates whether the examinee's answer matches the correct answer exactly (`ExamAns = CorAns`).
- **True branch:** Full marks are awarded (`CA[a][5] → Marks`).
- **False branch:** Typically, an incorrect answer results in 0 marks. 

However, for questions with "two-answer selections", an examinee can receive partial credit (Marks / 2) if they get one of the two answers correct. This means even if `ExamAns = CorAns` is False (because they didn't get both right), we still need to check for partial credit. Thus, the "Processing two-answer selections subroutine" must be placed at **β** to intercept the False branch, evaluate for partial marks, and assign the correct score before proceeding to node B.
