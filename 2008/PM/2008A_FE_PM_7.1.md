### Explanation
- In Program 1, `int i = 5/k;` where `k=0` results in a division by zero, which throws an `ArithmeticException`.
- The `catch (ArithmeticException e)` block catches this exception and executes `System.out.println("1");`, printing **1**.
- A `finally` block in Java always executes regardless of whether an exception was caught or not. Thus, `System.out.println("4");` executes next, printing **4**.
- Since the exception was handled and there is no `return` statement in that specific `catch` block (unlike the `RuntimeException` block), normal execution resumes after the `try-catch-finally` construct, executing `System.out.println("5");` and printing **5**.
- Therefore, the output is **1**, **4**, and **5**, in that order.
