### Explanation
In Java, an `abstract` class is not required to implement abstract methods inherited from its parent class. Because `RegisteredMotorVehicle` is declared as an `abstract` class (Blank C), the compiler allows it to leave `getAnnualFee()` unimplemented. The responsibility of implementing `getAnnualFee()` is simply passed down to the first concrete subclass in the hierarchy, which in this case is `Truck`.

- **a**, **b**, and **c** are incorrect because they provide fictitious reasons that do not align with standard Java language rules regarding abstract classes and inheritance.
