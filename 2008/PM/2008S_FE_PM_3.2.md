### Explanation
Using the public key `(n, e) = (10, 3)` found in Subquestion 1, we can encrypt the message `M`. 
1. The message Alice wants to send is `M = 7`.
2. The encryption formula given is `C = M^e mod n`.
3. Substituting the values: `C = 7^3 mod 10`.
4. First, calculate `7^3 = 343`.
5. Then, `343 mod 10 = 3` (the remainder when 343 is divided by 10 is 3).

Therefore, the cipher text `C` is `3`. Choice (b) is the correct answer.
