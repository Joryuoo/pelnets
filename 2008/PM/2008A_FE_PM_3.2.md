### Explanation
The propagation delay is the time it takes for a signal to travel from the sender to the receiver. It is determined by the distance and the speed of propagation.

* **Distance:** $6 \times 10^6$ meters.
* **Speed of Signal:** $3 \times 10^8$ meters per second.
* **Propagation Delay:** $(6 \times 10^6 \text{ m}) / (3 \times 10^8 \text{ m/s}) = 2 \times 10^{-2} \text{ seconds}$, which is $0.02$ seconds, or $20.0$ milliseconds.
