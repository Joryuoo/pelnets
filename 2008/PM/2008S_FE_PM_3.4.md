The encryption formula is `C = M^e mod n`. 
1. In this scenario, `n = 10`. 
2. The modulo operation (`mod 10`) always results in a remainder ranging from `0` to `n - 1`. 
3. Therefore, the possible values for the cipher text `C` can only be integers from `0` to `9`.
4. Since `10` is greater than or equal to `n = 10`, it is mathematically impossible for the remainder of any division by `10` to be `10`.

Therefore, `10` cannot be a cipher text. Choice (d) is the correct answer.
