### Explanation
The transmission time is calculated by dividing the total size of the frame by the link data rate. 

* **Frame Size:** The user data is 10,000 bits and the header is 100 bits, making the total frame size 10,100 bits.
* **Link Data Rate:** 1 Mbit/s, which is $1 \times 10^6$ bits per second.
* **Transmission Time:** $10,100 \text{ bits} / 1,000,000 \text{ bits/s} = 0.0101 \text{ seconds}$, which is exactly $10.1$ milliseconds.
