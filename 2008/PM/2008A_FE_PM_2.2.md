- **Blank A:** The `CREATE VIEW` statement specifies the columns `TS_name`, `TS_day`, `TS_start`, `TS_end`, `TS_course_name`, and `TS_section`. Therefore, the `SELECT` clause (Blank A) must extract matching columns in the exact same order from the respective tables. `TT.Teacher_name`, `ST.Day`, `ST.Start_time`, `ST.End_time`, `CT.Course_name`, and `ST.Section_id` strictly matches this requirement. This corresponds to choice **b**.
- **Blank B:** This is the `WHERE` clause responsible for joining the three tables (`Schedule_Table ST`, `Course_Table CT`, `Teacher_Table TT`).
  - To properly join `ST` and `CT`, we need to match both `Course_id` and `Section_id` since identical courses are distinguished by the section ID (`ST.Course_id = CT.Course_id AND ST.Section_id = CT.Section_id`).
  - To join `CT` and `TT`, we must match the teacher ID (`CT.Teacher_id = TT.Teacher_id`).
  - Combining these conditions gives choice **e**.
