### Explanation
In a k-ary tree, every node has at most `k` children. At height `h`, the maximum possible number of nodes occurs in a full (or complete) k-ary tree.

- At level 0 (the root), there is 1 node ($k^0$).
- At level 1, there are at most $k$ nodes ($k^1$).
- At level 2, there are at most $k^2$ nodes.
- Following this pattern, at level `h`, there are at most $k^h$ nodes.

Therefore, the upper bound for the number of leaves in a k-ary tree of height `h` is $k^h$, which matches option b.
