a) A → C → E → G

### Explanation
The critical path is the longest sequence of dependent activities and determines the minimum time required to complete the entire project. Let's trace the possible paths from start to finish and calculate their durations based on the table:

Activity E requires both B and C to be completed. So E can only start when the longer of its preceding paths (B or A→C) is finished.
- Time to finish B = 5 days.
- Time to finish A → C = 4 + 5 = 9 days.
Thus, E cannot start until day 9.
From E, the path goes to G.
Total time for the path ending in G: 9 (start of E) + 5 (E) + 5 (G) = 19 days.
The sequence determining this 19-day duration is A → C → E → G.

Path A → D → F takes 4 + 6 + 4 = 14 days.
So the longest duration (critical path) is 19 days, which corresponds to the sequence A → C → E → G.
