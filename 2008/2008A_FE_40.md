# 2008A_FE_40

Q40. Based on the concept of object orientation, which of the following can be commonly regarded as a subclass of the class “automobile”?

a) Engine
b) Serial number
c) Tire
d) Truck
?
d) Truck

### Explanation
### Explanation
In object-oriented programming, a **subclass** (or child class) has an **"is-a"** relationship with its superclass (or parent class). It inherits attributes and behaviors from the parent class while optionally adding its own specific characteristics.

* A **Truck** "is an" automobile, making it a valid subclass.
* **Engine** and **Tire** are parts of an automobile. This represents a **"has-a"** relationship (composition or aggregation), not a subclass.
* **Serial number** is an attribute or property of an automobile, not a subclass.
