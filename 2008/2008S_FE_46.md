### Explanation
To find the minimum time to complete all activities, we determine the expected time ($T_e$) for each activity and find the critical path.

$T_e = (x + 4y + z) / 6$
- $T_e(a) = (2 + 16 + 6) / 6 = 24 / 6 = 4.00$
- $T_e(b) = (3 + 20 + 9) / 6 = 32 / 6 = 5.33$
- $T_e(c) = (4 + 20 + 7) / 6 = 31 / 6 = 5.17$
- $T_e(d) = (4 + 24 + 10) / 6 = 38 / 6 = 6.33$
- $T_e(e) = (4 + 20 + 7) / 6 = 31 / 6 = 5.17$
- $T_e(f) = (3 + 16 + 8) / 6 = 27 / 6 = 4.50$
- $T_e(g) = (3 + 20 + 8) / 6 = 31 / 6 = 5.17$

Now calculate early finish times:
- **a** ends at $4.00$
- **b** ends at $5.33$
- **c** (depends on a) ends at $4.00 + 5.17 = 9.17$
- **d** (depends on a) ends at $4.00 + 6.33 = 10.33$
- **e** (depends on b and c) starts at $\max(5.33, 9.17) = 9.17$, ends at $9.17 + 5.17 = 14.34$
- **f** (depends on d) starts at $10.33$, ends at $10.33 + 4.50 = 14.83$
- **g** (depends on e) starts at $14.34$, ends at $14.34 + 5.17 = 19.51$

The project completes when all activities finish, which is $\max(14.83, 19.51) = 19.51$ days. The critical path is a -> c -> e -> g.
