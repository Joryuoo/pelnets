### Explanation
The CPU relies on several units to execute instructions. 
- **Fetch**: The instruction is retrieved from **Storage** (Main Memory) into the CPU's instruction register.
- **Decode**: The **Control** unit interprets the fetched instruction to determine what operation needs to be performed and signals other units (like the Processing/ALU) accordingly.
