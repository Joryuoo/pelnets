# 2008A_FE_38

Q38. CASE tools can be classified on the basis of the applicable development processes and phases. Which of the following classifications includes support functions for requirements analysis?

a) Downstream
b) Maintenance
c) Testing
d) Upstream
?
d) Upstream

### Explanation
### Explanation
CASE (Computer-Aided Software Engineering) tools are typically classified based on the phases of the software development life cycle they support:

* **Upper CASE (Upstream):** Tools used in the early stages of the life cycle, such as planning, requirements analysis, and system design.
* **Lower CASE (Downstream):** Tools used in the later stages, such as coding (implementation), testing, and maintenance.

Since requirements analysis occurs at the beginning of the development process, it is supported by Upstream (Upper CASE) tools.
