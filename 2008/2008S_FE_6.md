All choices share the same first 9 digits: `0 0 7 1 3 6 1 2 8`.
Let's apply the weights from 10 down to 2:
- `0 * 10 = 0`
- `0 * 9 = 0`
- `7 * 8 = 56`
- `1 * 7 = 7`
- `3 * 6 = 18`
- `6 * 5 = 30`
- `1 * 4 = 4`
- `2 * 3 = 6`
- `8 * 2 = 16`

Sum of products = 0 + 0 + 56 + 7 + 18 + 30 + 4 + 6 + 16 = 137.

The check validation rule is: `(Sum + Check Digit) modulo 11 = 0`.
Let's find `137 modulo 11`:
`11 * 12 = 132`, so `137 - 132 = 5`.
This means we need a check digit `C` such that `(5 + C)` is a multiple of 11. The only single-digit number that works is `C = 6` (since 5 + 6 = 11).

Therefore, the correct check digit is 6, making the valid ISBN `0071361286`.
