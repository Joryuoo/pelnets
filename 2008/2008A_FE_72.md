# 2008A_FE_72

Q72. Which of the following is an appropriate description concerning financial indexes?

a) Capital ratio signifies the ratio of equity capital to fixed assets. The larger the value of this ratio, the greater the financial soundness.
b) Current ratio means the ratio of current assets to current liabilities. The smaller the value of this ratio, the higher the financial stability.
c) Fixed ratio means the ratio of fixed assets to fixed liabilities. The smaller the value of this ratio, the higher the financial stability.
d) Total capital profit ratio denotes the ratio of profits to gross capital. The larger the value of this ratio, the higher the profitability.
?
d) Total capital profit ratio denotes the ratio of profits to gross capital. The larger the value of this ratio, the higher the profitability.

### Explanation
Financial indexes evaluate a company's financial health:
- a) Capital ratio (or equity ratio) is the ratio of equity capital to total assets, not fixed assets. A larger value generally means greater financial soundness.
- b) Current ratio is the ratio of current assets to current liabilities. The *larger* the value, the higher the short-term financial stability (liquidity).
- c) Fixed ratio is the ratio of fixed assets to equity capital, not fixed liabilities. It measures how much of the fixed assets are financed by equity. A *smaller* value indicates higher stability.
- d) Total capital profit ratio (Return on Assets or Return on Capital) is the ratio of profits to gross (total) capital. The larger the value, the higher the profitability. This is the correct description.
