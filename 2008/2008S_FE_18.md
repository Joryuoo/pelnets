### Explanation
By tracing the logic circuit from the inputs A and B to the outputs W, X, Y, and Z:
- **W** is the output of an AND gate receiving A and B directly. So, W is 1 when A=1 and B=1.
- **X** receives A and the inverted value of B. So, X is 1 when A=1 and B=0.
- **Y** receives the inverted value of A, and B directly. So, Y is 1 when A=0 and B=1.
- **Z** receives the inverted values of both A and B. So, Z is 1 when A=0 and B=0.

This circuit corresponds to a 2-to-4 line decoder. Its truth table matches choice b), where each unique combination of A and B sets exactly one specific output to 1.
