# 2008A_FE_54

Which of the following terms can be applicable to an explanation below?

“The sender sends a single datagram; the routers are responsible for making copies and sending them to a group of interested receivers.”

a) Anycast
b) Broadcast
c) Multicast
d) Unicast
?
c) Multicast

### Explanation
Multicast is a networking method where a single sender transmits data to a specific group of interested receivers. Routers in the network duplicate the packets only when necessary to reach all group members, efficiently saving bandwidth.

* **a) Anycast:** Routes datagrams to a single member (usually the nearest one) of a group of potential receivers that share the same destination address.
* **b) Broadcast:** Sends data to all possible receivers on a local network segment.
* **d) Unicast:** A one-to-one transmission from a single sender to a single receiver.
