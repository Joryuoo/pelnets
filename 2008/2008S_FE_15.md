### Explanation
In a post-order traversal of a binary tree, the nodes are recursively visited in the order:
1. Traverse the Left Subtree.
2. Traverse the Right Subtree.
3. Visit the Root.

Applying this traversal to the given tree:
- We process the bottom-most leaves first before their parents, progressing from left to right.
- We finally visit the root node of the entire tree last.
- The correct resulting sequence of visited nodes matches choice b.
