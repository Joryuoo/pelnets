### Explanation
The FIFO (First In First Out) method assumes that the oldest inventory items are sold first.
The total number of watches sold in January is 120.
We fulfill this order of 120 watches from the oldest available stock:
1. From beginning inventory (Jan 1): 30 units at $20 each = $600
2. From Jan 10 purchase: 60 units at $21 each = $1,260
3. From Jan 16 purchase: The remaining 30 units (120 - 30 - 60) at $22 each = $660

Total purchase cost of the 120 watches sold = $600 + $1,260 + $660 = $2,520.
