# 2008A_FE_67

Which of the following is an advantageous effect of encrypting e-mails?

a) It is possible to prevent denial-of-service attacks against e-mails.
b) It is possible to prevent encryption keys from being lost.
c) It is possible to prevent the contents of e-mails from leaking out.
d) It is possible to protect transmission records on mail servers from being falsified.
?
c) It is possible to prevent the contents of e-mails from leaking out.

### Explanation
### Explanation
Encrypting emails ensures confidentiality. Even if an email is intercepted by an unauthorized third party while traversing the network, the contents remain unreadable without the decryption key, thus preventing information leakage.
* **a)** Encryption does not stop DoS attacks.
* **b)** Encryption does not manage or backup keys.
* **d)** Transmission logs on servers are not protected from falsification merely by encrypting the email body (digital signatures would be used for verifying integrity).
