# 2008A_FE_70

When LAN analyzers are used for finding out the causes of network failures, which of the following is a point to keep in mind?

a) As a provision against the occurrence of failures, it is necessary to inform all network users of the storage places and directions for use of LAN analyzers.
b) LAN cables must be temporarily disconnected during measurement. Therefore, it is necessary to inform users of the measurement dates in advance.
c) Packets originally intended to be transmitted to their destinations are destroyed during measurement. Therefore, it is necessary to limit the use of computers other than those to be measured.
d) Some analyzers are capable of displaying packets that pass through networks. Therefore, it is necessary to be careful not to use them in different ways such as wiretapping.
?
d) Some analyzers are capable of displaying packets that pass through networks. Therefore, it is necessary to be careful not to use them in different ways such as wiretapping.

### Explanation
### Explanation
A LAN analyzer (or packet sniffer) captures and decodes packets traversing the network. Because it can view the contents of unencrypted packets (including application data, passwords, etc.), it poses a security and privacy risk. Care must be taken to ensure it is not misused for unauthorized wiretapping or eavesdropping.
* **a)** LAN analyzers are diagnostic tools for network administrators, not regular users.
* **b)** A LAN analyzer can be used passively (e.g., via port mirroring) and does not inherently require disconnecting cables.
* **c)** LAN analyzers generally operate passively (promiscuous mode) and do not drop or destroy packets.
