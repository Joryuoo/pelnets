# 2008S_FE_39

Q39. Which of the following characteristics can be found in well-designed modules with a high degree of independence?

a) High coupling and high cohesion
b) High coupling and low cohesion
c) Low coupling and high cohesion
d) Low coupling and low cohesion
?
c) Low coupling and high cohesion

### Explanation
In software engineering, modular design aims to divide a system into independent, interchangeable modules. A well-designed module is characterized by:

1.  **Low Coupling:** This means modules have minimal dependencies on each other. Changes in one module are less likely to impact others, promoting independence and reusability.
2.  **High Cohesion:** This means the elements within a single module are strongly related and focused on a single, well-defined task or purpose.

Therefore, a high degree of independence is achieved with **low coupling and high cohesion**.
