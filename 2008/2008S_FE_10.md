# 2008S_FE_10

A coin is tossed three times. What is the probability that it lands on heads exactly one time?

a) 0.125
b) 0.25
c) 0.333
d) 0.375
?
d) 0.375

### Explanation
### Explanation
When tossing a coin 3 times, there are a total of $2^3 = 8$ possible outcomes (since each toss has 2 possibilities: Heads or Tails).

The complete sample space of outcomes is:
- HHH
- HHT
- HTH
- HTT  (Exactly 1 head)
- THH
- THT  (Exactly 1 head)
- TTH  (Exactly 1 head)
- TTT

There are exactly 3 outcomes where the coin lands on heads exactly one time: HTT, THT, and TTH.
The probability is the number of favorable outcomes divided by the total number of outcomes:
$3 / 8 = 0.375$.
