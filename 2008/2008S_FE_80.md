# 2008S_FE_80

Q80. There are goods whose unit purchase prices are gradually rising.  There was an inventory of these goods at the end of the last accounting period, and the goods were carried into and out of the warehouse several times during the current period.  Which of the following valuation methods produces the lowest valuation of the inventory at the end of the current period?  

a) Average cost method 
b) First-in first-out method  
c) Last-in first-out method 
d) Moving average method
?
c) Last-in first-out method

### Explanation
### Explanation
Under an inflationary environment where unit purchase prices are gradually rising:
- **Last-in, First-out (LIFO):** Assumes the most recently acquired (and thus most expensive) items are sold first. This leaves the oldest, cheapest goods in the ending inventory, resulting in the **lowest** inventory valuation.
- **First-in, First-out (FIFO):** Assumes the oldest (cheapest) goods are sold first, leaving the newest, most expensive goods in the ending inventory, yielding the highest inventory valuation.
- **Average methods** (Moving/Weighted Average) will yield a valuation somewhere between LIFO and FIFO.
