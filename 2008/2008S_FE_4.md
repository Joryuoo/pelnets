# 2008S_FE_4

In a certain program, in order to stay in a loop, variable X must be in the range from 1 through 10 (X >= 1 AND X <= 10). In this program, which of the following is the condition of exiting the loop? Here, "AND" and "OR" are the logical product and logical sum operations respectively. In addition, comparison operators <, <=, >, and >= are less than, less than or equal to, greater than, and greater than or equal to, respectively.

a) X < 1 AND X > 10
b) X >= 1 OR X <= 10
c) X <= 1 OR X >= 10
d) X < 1 OR X > 10
?
d) X < 1 OR X > 10

### Explanation
### Explanation
The condition to stay in the loop is: `X >= 1 AND X <= 10`.

To find the condition for exiting the loop, we must find the logical negation of the stay condition by applying De Morgan's laws:
`NOT (X >= 1 AND X <= 10)`

According to De Morgan's laws, the negation of a conjunction (`AND`) is the disjunction (`OR`) of the negations:
`NOT (X >= 1) OR NOT (X <= 10)`

Next, apply the negation to the comparison operators:
- The negation of `X >= 1` is `X < 1`.
- The negation of `X <= 10` is `X > 10`.

This results in the expression: `X < 1 OR X > 10`.
