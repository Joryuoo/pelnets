# 2008A_FE_1

Which of the following decimal fractions becomes a finite fraction when converted to an octal number?

a) 0.3
b) 0.4
c) 0.5
d) 0.8
?
c) 0.5

### Explanation
A decimal fraction becomes a finite octal fraction if its denominator in lowest terms is a power of 2 (since the base is 8, which is $2^3$).
- $0.3 = \frac{3}{10}$ (denominator has prime factor 5)
- $0.4 = \frac{2}{5}$ (denominator 5)
- $0.5 = \frac{1}{2}$ (denominator 2, which is a power of 2). In octal, 0.5 is exactly 0.4.
- $0.8 = \frac{4}{5}$ (denominator 5)

Thus, only 0.5 can be expressed as a finite fraction in octal.
