### Explanation
To convert a complete binary tree into a max heap, we apply the heapify algorithm. A max heap must satisfy the property that every parent node's value is greater than or equal to the values of its children.

By examining the given choices, choice b) perfectly satisfies the max heap property for all nodes:
- The root is 16, which is greater than its children (14, 10).
- Node 14 is greater than its children (8, 7).
- Node 10 is greater than its children (2, 9).
- Node 8 is greater than its children (3, 1).
- Node 7 is greater than its child (4).

Other choices violate the max heap property (e.g., a parent having a value smaller than one of its children).
