# 2008A_FE_57

Which of the following protocols is used for synchronizing the clocks in multiple nodes on the Internet?

a) NNTP
b) NTP
c) SMTP
d) SNMP
?
b) NTP

### Explanation
NTP (Network Time Protocol) is the networking protocol used for clock synchronization between computer systems over packet-switched, variable-latency data networks.

* **a) NNTP (Network News Transfer Protocol):** Used for transporting Usenet news articles.
* **c) SMTP (Simple Mail Transfer Protocol):** Used for sending email.
* **d) SNMP (Simple Network Management Protocol):** Used for collecting and organizing information about managed devices on IP networks.
