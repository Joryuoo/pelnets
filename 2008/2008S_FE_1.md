To convert the hexadecimal fraction `2A.4C` to a sum of powers of 2, convert each hex digit to its 4-bit binary equivalent:
- `2` = `0010`
- `A` = `1010`
- `4` = `0100`
- `C` = `1100`

Thus, `2A.4C` in hexadecimal is `00101010.01001100` in binary. 
Expressing this binary number as a sum of powers of 2 based on the position of each `1` bit (where the bit to the left of the binary point is $2^0$):
- $1 \times 2^5$ (for the 32s place)
- $1 \times 2^3$ (for the 8s place)
- $1 \times 2^1$ (for the 2s place)
- $1 \times 2^{-2}$ (for the 0.25 place)
- $1 \times 2^{-5}$ (for the 0.03125 place)
- $1 \times 2^{-6}$ (for the 0.015625 place)

This gives $2^5 + 2^3 + 2^1 + 2^{-2} + 2^{-5} + 2^{-6}$.
