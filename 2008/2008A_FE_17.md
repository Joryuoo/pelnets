### Explanation
An Exclusive-NOR (XNOR) gate produces an output of 1 when both inputs are the same (both 0s or both 1s) and an output of 0 when the inputs are different.

- **AND gate:** Output is 1 only when both inputs are 1.
- **Exclusive-OR (XOR) gate:** Output is 1 only when inputs are different.
- **NOR gate:** Output is 1 only when both inputs are 0.

Based on typical timing chart problems of this nature, the chart likely shows the output going high when inputs A and B match, which corresponds to the Exclusive-NOR logical function.
