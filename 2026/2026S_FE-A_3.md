# 2026S_FE-A_3

Q3. Which of the following is the ratio of the mean arrival rate to the mean service rate in queuing theory? 

a) Utilization
b) Average inter-arrival time 
c) Average service time
d) Average retention number
?
a) Utilization

### Explanation
In queuing theory, **Utilization** (often denoted by $\rho$) represents the proportion of the system's resources that are busy. It is calculated as the ratio of the mean arrival rate ($\lambda$) to the mean service rate ($\mu$).

Formula:
$$ \rho = \frac{\lambda}{\mu} $$

- **Mean arrival rate ($\lambda$)**: The average number of customers arriving per unit of time.
- **Mean service rate ($\mu$)**: The average number of customers served per unit of time by a single server.

If $\rho < 1$, the system is stable. If $\rho \ge 1$, the queue will grow indefinitely over time.
