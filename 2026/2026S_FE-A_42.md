# 2026S_FE-A_42

The functional size of a new system was estimated to be 500 function points (FPs). Apart from development, the project requires 10 person-months in total for installation of the system and education of developers. Additionally project management requires 10% of the total person-months of development, installation, and education. How many person-months are required in total for this project? Here, the productivity in development is 10 FPs per person-month. 

a) 51
b) 60
c) 65
d) 66 
?
d) 66

### Explanation
To calculate the total person-months (PM) required for the project, we must sum the effort across all activities: development, installation and education, and project management.

1. **Calculate Development Effort:**
   The total functional size is $500 \text{ FPs}$.
   Productivity is $10 \text{ FPs/PM}$.
   $$ \text{Development PM} = \frac{500 \text{ FPs}}{10 \text{ FPs/PM}} = 50 \text{ PM} $$

2. **Add Installation and Education:**
   This requires a fixed $10 \text{ PM}$.
   $$ \text{Subtotal (Dev + Install/Ed)} = 50 \text{ PM} + 10 \text{ PM} = 60 \text{ PM} $$

3. **Calculate Project Management Effort:**
   Project management requires $10\%$ of the total person-months of development, installation, and education.
   $$ \text{Project Management PM} = 60 \text{ PM} \times 0.10 = 6 \text{ PM} $$

4. **Calculate Total Project Effort:**
   $$ \text{Total PM} = 60 \text{ PM} + 6 \text{ PM} = 66 \text{ PM} $$
