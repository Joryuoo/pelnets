# 2026S_FE-A_57

Which of the following can secure employment and create employment opportunities across an entire society by shortening work hours per employee?

a) Cafeteria plan
b) Free-agent system
c) Work sharing
d) Work-life balance
?
c) Work sharing

### Explanation
**Work sharing** (or job sharing) is an employment arrangement where the workload and working hours of one full-time position are divided among two or more employees. On a macroeconomic scale, reducing the standard working hours per employee allows available work to be distributed among more people, thereby reducing unemployment and securing employment opportunities.

- **Option a** (Cafeteria plan) is a flexible benefits plan that allows employees to choose from a menu of benefits.
- **Option b** (Free-agent system) refers to workers acting as independent contractors or freelancers.
- **Option d** (Work-life balance) refers to the balance between an individual's professional work and personal life, but it isn't a direct systemic strategy for creating widespread employment opportunities.
