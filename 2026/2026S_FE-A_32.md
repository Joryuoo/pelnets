# 2026S_FE-A_32

Company A, a mobile game developer, is developing a new version of its game with a social media plugin that allows users to post their achievements on social media platforms. However, during beta testing, some users point out that posts from the game can bypass their privacy settings, making them visible to the public. Company A understands the risk and decides not to include the social media plugin in their new version of the game. Which of the following is the risk management strategy that Company A is choosing?

a) Risk acceptance
b) Risk avoidance
c) Risk mitigation
d) Risk sharing
?
b) Risk avoidance

### Explanation
Risk management strategies are categorized into four main types:

| Strategy | Description | Example in context |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Risk Avoidance** | Eliminating the risk completely by withdrawing from or not becoming involved in the activity that allows the risk to exist. | Removing the social media plugin entirely to eliminate the privacy risk. |
| **Risk Mitigation (Reduction)** | Taking steps to reduce the likelihood or impact of the risk. | Fixing the plugin's code to respect privacy settings. |
| **Risk Sharing (Transfer)** | Shifting the impact of a risk to a third party. | Purchasing insurance or outsourcing the plugin development to a firm with liability coverage. |
| **Risk Acceptance** | Acknowledging the risk and deciding to tolerate it without taking action, usually because the impact is low or cost of mitigation is too high. | Releasing the game with the flawed plugin and dealing with user complaints. |

Since Company A decided *not to include* the plugin, they completely removed the source of the risk. This is the definition of **risk avoidance**.
