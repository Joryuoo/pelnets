# 2026S_FE-A_50

A company expands into new markets by allowing independent business owners to operate under its brand name and quality standards including store management obligations. These local business owners pay royalties for using the brand name and getting business support from the company while investing in and managing their own stores. From a marketing strategy perspective, which of the following describes this arrangement? 

a) Direct distribution 
b) Franchising 
c) Licensing 
d) Wholesaling 
?
b) Franchising

### Explanation
The arrangement described is a classic **Franchising** model.

| Strategy | Description |
|---|---|
| **Direct distribution** | The company sells its products directly to the consumer without intermediaries (e.g., opening its own company-owned stores). |
| **Franchising** | A business model where an independent party (franchisee) pays fees and royalties to operate a business using the franchisor's brand, operational model, and support systems. |
| **Licensing** | Granting permission to another entity to use intellectual property (e.g., a logo or character) on their own products, usually without strict management obligations over their business operations. |
| **Wholesaling** | Selling goods in large quantities to be retailed by others. |

Because the independent owners must adhere to "quality standards including store management obligations" and receive "business support," this goes beyond simple licensing and represents **franchising**.
