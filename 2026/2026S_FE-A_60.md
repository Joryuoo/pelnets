# 2026S_FE-A_60

In developing new products that are expected to be employed by government agencies of WTO member countries, which of the following are the appropriate standards to be applied?

a) ANSI standards
b) BSI standards
c) FCC standards
d) ISO standards
?
d) ISO standards

### Explanation
The World Trade Organization (WTO) Agreement on Technical Barriers to Trade (TBT) encourages member countries to base their technical regulations and standards on international standards to prevent unnecessary obstacles to international trade.

- **Option d** (ISO standards) refers to the International Organization for Standardization, which produces international standards recognized and adopted globally. These are the appropriate standards for products targeting WTO member countries.
- **Option a** (ANSI) is a national standard for the United States.
- **Option b** (BSI) is a national standard for the United Kingdom.
- **Option c** (FCC) regulations apply to telecommunications and electronics in the United States.
