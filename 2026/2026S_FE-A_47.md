# 2026S_FE-A_47

Which of the following is a method of training in which trainees handle multiple simulated tasks within a set time limit, such as editing a piece of written correspondence, creating purchase orders, and completing travel expense reports? 

a) Case study 
b) Debate 
c) In-basket 
d) Role-playing
?
c) In-basket

### Explanation
The **In-basket** method is a training and assessment technique where participants are given a simulated set of tasks (traditionally placed in an "in-basket" on their desk, like emails, memos, requests, and reports). They must prioritize, make decisions, and process these items within a strict time limit. This tests their time management, decision-making, and administrative skills.

| Training Method | Description |
|---|---|
| **Case study** | Trainees analyze a detailed real or simulated scenario to identify problems and propose solutions. |
| **Debate** | Participants argue for or against a specific proposition to develop critical thinking and communication skills. |
| **In-basket** | Participants process a backlog of typical administrative tasks (memos, forms) under time pressure. |
| **Role-playing** | Trainees act out scenarios as specific characters (e.g., handling a customer complaint) to practice interpersonal skills. |
