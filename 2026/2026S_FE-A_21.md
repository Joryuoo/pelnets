### Explanation
To determine the candidate keys for the relation schema $A = (P, Q, R, S, T, Y)$, we need to find the attribute closures based on the given functional dependencies:
- $P \rightarrow Q$
- $QR \rightarrow S$
- $T \rightarrow R$
- $S \rightarrow P$

**Step 1: Identify essential attributes**
Attributes that do not appear on the right side of any functional dependency must be part of every candidate key.
- $T$ and $Y$ are never on the right side.
- Therefore, any candidate key must include at least $T$ and $Y$.

**Step 2: Test closures of combinations with TY**
Let's test the closure of $TY$:
- $(TY)^+ = TY$ (No dependencies can be applied)

Since $TY$ alone cannot determine all attributes, we combine it with other attributes ($P$, $Q$, $S$, $R$) and test their closures.

| Attribute Set | Closure Steps | Resulting Closure | Is Candidate Key? |
| ------------- | ------------- | ----------------- | ----------------- |
| **PTY** | $P \rightarrow Q \Rightarrow PQTY$ <br> $T \rightarrow R \Rightarrow PQRTY$ <br> $QR \rightarrow S \Rightarrow PQRSTY$ | $P, Q, R, S, T, Y$ | **Yes** |
| **QTY** | $T \rightarrow R \Rightarrow QRTY$ <br> $QR \rightarrow S \Rightarrow QRSTY$ <br> $S \rightarrow P \Rightarrow PQRSTY$ | $P, Q, R, S, T, Y$ | **Yes** |
| **STY** | $S \rightarrow P \Rightarrow PSTY$ <br> $P \rightarrow Q \Rightarrow PQSTY$ <br> $T \rightarrow R \Rightarrow PQRSTY$ | $P, Q, R, S, T, Y$ | **Yes** |
| **RTY** | $RTY$ (No dependencies can be applied to add new attributes) | $R, T, Y$ | **No** |

Thus, the candidate keys are **PTY, QTY, and STY**.
