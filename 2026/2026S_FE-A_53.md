# 2026S_FE-A_53

Which of the following is an explanation of a smart grid?

a) A system that aims to utilize recyclable energy, supply stable electric power, and optimise demand adjustment through integrated control of power generation and power consumption on the basis of communication and information-processing technology
b) A system that can provide ideal health advice to each individual by performing analysis on the basis of similar case reports such as the results of health examinations and medication use
c) A system that can search for information on restaurants and entertainment, send and receive traffic information, and communicate the current position at the time of an emergency by using information-processing equipment installed in vehicles
d) A system that supports varied work styles by enabling mutual communication between workers with varied working styles, such as home-based employees and senior workers
?
a) A system that aims to utilize recyclable energy, supply stable electric power, and optimise demand adjustment through integrated control of power generation and power consumption on the basis of communication and information-processing technology

### Explanation
A **Smart Grid** is a modernized electrical grid that uses analog or digital information and communications technology to gather and act on information (such as behaviors of suppliers and consumers) in an automated fashion. This improves the efficiency, reliability, economics, and sustainability of the production and distribution of electricity.

- **Option a** correctly describes the smart grid's use of IT and communication technology to optimize power generation, utilize renewable (recyclable) energy, and balance supply/demand.
- **Option b** describes a medical expert system or AI health advisor.
- **Option c** describes an Intelligent Transport System (ITS) or telematics system in vehicles.
- **Option d** describes telecommuting or unified communications for remote work.
