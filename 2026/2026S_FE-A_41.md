# 2026S_FE-A_41

In project management, which of the following is the subject group with Purpose 1 and Purpose 2? 

Purpose 1: To clarify the objectives, deliverables, requirements, and boundaries of the project 
Purpose 2: To maximize positive impact and minimize negative impact to the project occurring as a result of the changes in project objectives and deliverables 

a) Communication
b) Procurement 
c) Risk
d) Scope 
?
d) Scope

### Explanation
In project management, **Scope Management** encompasses the processes required to ensure that the project includes all the work required, and only the work required, to complete the project successfully.

- **Purpose 1** describes defining the scope: clarifying objectives, deliverables, requirements, and the boundaries (what is in and out of the project).
- **Purpose 2** describes scope control/change management: handling changes to the project's scope to maximize positive impacts and minimize negative ones (preventing scope creep).

| Knowledge Area | Description |
|---|---|
| **Communication** | Managing the flow of project information, ensuring timely generation, distribution, and storage of project information. |
| **Procurement** | Purchasing or acquiring products, services, or results needed from outside the project team. |
| **Risk** | Identifying, analyzing, and responding to project risks to maximize positive events and minimize negative ones. |
| **Scope** | Defining and controlling what is and is not included in the project. |
