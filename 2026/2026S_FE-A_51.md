# 2026S_FE-A_51

Which of the following is an example of the strategic target and key performance indicator from the viewpoint of internal business processes in a Balanced Scorecard (BSC)?

a) Since establishing a continuing relationship with the main customers is the target, the number of complaints is the indicator.
b) Since improvement in manufacturing productivity of the product is the target, a reduction in the number of days in manufacturing is the indicator.
c) Since improvement in product development capability is the target, time for classroom training on product development areas is the indicator.
d) Since sustainable growth is the target, backlog of orders is the indicator.
?
b) Since improvement in manufacturing productivity of the product is the target, a reduction in the number of days in manufacturing is the indicator.

### Explanation
The Balanced Scorecard (BSC) is a strategic management tool that measures organizational performance from four perspectives:
1. **Financial**: Profitability, revenue growth, cost reduction (e.g., choice d).
2. **Customer**: Customer satisfaction, retention, market share (e.g., choice a).
3. **Internal Business Processes**: Efficiency, quality, productivity (e.g., choice b).
4. **Learning and Growth**: Employee training, corporate culture, innovation (e.g., choice c).

Choice b) focuses on "manufacturing productivity" and "number of days in manufacturing," which are direct measures of how well internal processes are operating. Therefore, it is the correct answer for the internal business processes perspective.
