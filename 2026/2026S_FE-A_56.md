# 2026S_FE-A_56

Which of the following is part of total quality management?

a) Designing products and services that meet or exceed customers’ expectations
b) Focusing on the appointment of staff with long-term work experience in a similar environment
c) Prioritizing central decisions rather than empowerment to ensure the quality of products and services
d) Promoting the capability of each department to work independently in a competitive manner
?
a) Designing products and services that meet or exceed customers’ expectations

### Explanation
**Total Quality Management (TQM)** is a comprehensive, organization-wide approach to continuous improvement of products, services, and processes. Its core principle is customer focus, aiming to meet or exceed customer expectations.

- **Option a** aligns perfectly with TQM's primary goal: customer satisfaction and continuous improvement.
- **Option b** focuses on hiring practices rather than the systemic quality improvement framework of TQM.
- **Option c** contradicts TQM, which actually promotes employee empowerment and decentralization so that everyone is responsible for quality.
- **Option d** contradicts TQM, as TQM emphasizes cross-functional teamwork and collaboration rather than competitive, siloed departmental work.
