### Explanation
The flowchart illustrates the standard procurement process for an information system:

1. **Step A:** The orderer indicates the purpose of systemization and business details, requesting information from vendors. This is a **Request for Information (RFI)**.
2. **Step B:** The orderer specifies the target system and procurement conditions, requesting the submission of a proposal document. This is a **Request for Proposal (RFP)**.
3. **Step C:** The orderer evaluates the proposals and capabilities of the vendors to select a provider. This is **Vendor Selection**.
4. **Step D:** The orderer and selected vendor confirm roles and responsibilities in a formal document. This is **Concluding a Contract**.

Since blank **B** asks for the step involving the request for a proposal document, the correct answer is **RFP**.
