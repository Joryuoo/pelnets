# 2026S_FE-A_29

In the context of information security defined by ISO/IEC 27000, which of the following is the property of accuracy and completeness of information assets? 

a) Authentication 
b) Availability 
c) Confidentiality 
d) Integrity
?
d) Integrity

### Explanation
In information security (as defined by ISO/IEC 27000), the core principles are often represented by the **CIA Triad** (Confidentiality, Integrity, and Availability).

Here are the definitions of the properties:
* **Confidentiality:** The property that information is not made available or disclosed to unauthorized individuals, entities, or processes. It ensures privacy and secrecy.
* **Integrity:** The property of safeguarding the **accuracy and completeness** of assets. It ensures that information has not been modified or destroyed in an unauthorized or accidental manner.
* **Availability:** The property of being accessible and usable upon demand by an authorized entity. It ensures that systems and data are there when users need them.

Looking at the options:
* **a) Authentication:** The process of verifying the identity of a user, process, or device.
* **b) Availability:** Relates to accessibility, not accuracy.
* **c) Confidentiality:** Relates to unauthorized access, not completeness.
* **d) Integrity:** Matches the definition of accuracy and completeness perfectly.
