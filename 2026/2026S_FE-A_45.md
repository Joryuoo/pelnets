# 2026S_FE-A_45

In an information security audit, which of the following is a check item for confirming availability?

a) Appropriate performance of error checking for data entry 
b) Database encryption 
c) Managing and maintaining the level of SLA that defines downtime 
d) Prohibiting the removal of external storage media from the company without permission 
?
c) Managing and maintaining the level of SLA that defines downtime

### Explanation
In information security, the CIA triad consists of Confidentiality, Integrity, and Availability:

1. **Confidentiality:** Ensuring information is accessed only by authorized entities.
2. **Integrity:** Ensuring information is accurate and unmodified.
3. **Availability:** Ensuring information and systems are accessible to authorized users when needed.

Let's evaluate the choices:
- **a) Error checking for data entry:** Ensures data is correct and valid, which targets **Integrity**.
- **b) Database encryption:** Protects data from unauthorized viewing, which targets **Confidentiality**.
- **c) Managing SLA that defines downtime:** Downtime directly impacts whether a system is accessible. Minimizing downtime ensures **Availability**.
- **d) Prohibiting removal of external storage:** Prevents unauthorized data exfiltration or tampering, targeting **Confidentiality** and **Integrity**.
