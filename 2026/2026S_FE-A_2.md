# 2026S_FE-A_2

Q2. What is the decimal equivalent value of the octal number 1234.2? 

a) 27.0
b) 668.25
c) 1542.5
d) 5346.0
?
b) 668.25

### Explanation
To convert the octal number $1234.2_8$ to a decimal number, we expand it using powers of 8:

| Octal Digit | $1$ | $2$ | $3$ | $4$ | . | $2$ |
|-------------|-----|-----|-----|-----|---|-----|
| Position    | $3$ | $2$ | $1$ | $0$ | . | $-1$|
| Weight      | $8^3$| $8^2$| $8^1$| $8^0$| . | $8^{-1}$|
| Value       | $512$| $64$| $8$  | $1$  | . | $0.125$|

Calculation:
$1 \times 8^3 + 2 \times 8^2 + 3 \times 8^1 + 4 \times 8^0 + 2 \times 8^{-1}$
$= 1 \times 512 + 2 \times 64 + 3 \times 8 + 4 \times 1 + 2 \times 0.125$
$= 512 + 128 + 24 + 4 + 0.25$
$= 668.25$

This matches option (b).
