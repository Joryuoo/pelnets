### Explanation
We need to determine the number of units to sell to achieve a target profit of $42,000 using break-even analysis principles.

**Given Data:**
- Selling price per unit ($P$) = $17
- Fixed Costs ($FC$): General ($130,000) + Interest ($10,000) = $140,000
- Variable Cost per unit ($VC$): General ($3) + Selling ($4) = $7
- Target Profit ($\pi$) = $42,000

**Formula:**
$$ \text{Target Sales Units} = \frac{FC + \pi}{P - VC} $$

**Calculation:**
1. **Contribution Margin per unit:** 
   $P - VC = 17 - 7 = 10$ dollars per unit.
2. **Total Required Contribution:** 
   $FC + \pi = 140,000 + 42,000 = 182,000$ dollars.
3. **Required Units:** 
   $\frac{182,000}{10} = 18,200$ units.

Thus, the company needs to sell 18,200 units to achieve the target profit.
