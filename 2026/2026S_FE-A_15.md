### Explanation

To find the average waiting time using Round Robin scheduling with a time quantum $q = 5$ ms, we can simulate the execution of the processes. 
All processes arrive at $t=0$ in the order P1, P2, P3, P4.

Let's trace the execution step-by-step:

| Time | Process Scheduled | CPU Time Used | Remaining CPU Time | Action/Wait |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| $0 \rightarrow 5$ | P1 | $5$ ms (finishes) | $0$ | P1 completes. |
| $5 \rightarrow 10$ | P2 | $5$ ms | $13 - 5 = 8$ | P2 is preempted, put at back of queue. |
| $10 \rightarrow 14$ | P3 | $4$ ms (finishes) | $0$ | P3 completes before time slice ends. |
| $14 \rightarrow 19$ | P4 | $5$ ms | $8 - 5 = 3$ | P4 is preempted, put at back of queue. |
| $19 \rightarrow 24$ | P2 | $5$ ms | $8 - 5 = 3$ | P2 is preempted, put at back of queue. |
| $24 \rightarrow 27$ | P4 | $3$ ms (finishes) | $0$ | P4 completes. |
| $27 \rightarrow 30$ | P2 | $3$ ms (finishes) | $0$ | P2 completes. |

Now, we calculate the total **waiting time** for each process. Waiting time is the time spent in the ready queue.
It can also be calculated as: $\text{Turnaround Time} - \text{Execution Time}$ 
(Where $\text{Turnaround Time} = \text{Completion Time} - \text{Arrival Time}$)

*   **P1**: Completes at $5$. Wait Time $= 5 - 5 = 0$.
*   **P2**: Completes at $30$. Wait Time $= 30 - 13 = 17$.
*   **P3**: Completes at $14$. Wait Time $= 14 - 4 = 10$.
*   **P4**: Completes at $27$. Wait Time $= 27 - 8 = 19$.

**Average Waiting Time:**
$\text{Average} = \frac{0 + 17 + 10 + 19}{4} = \frac{46}{4} = 11.5 \text{ ms}$
