# 2026S_FE-A_27

In telecommunication, which of the following is a standard for wireless broadband communication for mobile devices and data terminals? 

a) IEEE 802.11 
b) LTE 
c) NFC 
d) UWB
?
b) LTE

### Explanation
**LTE (Long-Term Evolution)** is a widely used telecommunications standard for high-speed wireless broadband communication, primarily designed for mobile devices (like smartphones) and data terminals. It is commonly marketed as **4G LTE** and provides significantly faster data rates and lower latency compared to its predecessors (3G/UMTS).

Let's evaluate all the choices:
* **a) IEEE 802.11:** This is the standard for **Wireless Local Area Networks (WLANs)**, commonly known as **Wi-Fi**. It is used for short-to-medium-range local networking, not large-scale mobile cellular broadband.
* **b) LTE:** The correct answer. It is the global standard for cellular mobile broadband.
* **c) NFC (Near Field Communication):** A short-range wireless connectivity standard that uses magnetic field induction to enable communication between devices when they're touched together, or brought within a few centimeters of each other (e.g., contactless payments).
* **d) UWB (Ultra-Wideband):** A radio technology used for short-range, high-bandwidth communications at very low energy levels over a wide spectrum (often used for precise indoor positioning and fast local data transfer).
