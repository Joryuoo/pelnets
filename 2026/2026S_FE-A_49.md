c) Synergy effect

### Explanation
When a merger or acquisition results in a combined entity that is more valuable or capable than the sum of its independent parts, this is known as a **Synergy effect** (often expressed as $1 + 1 = 3$). 

In this scenario:
- **Revenue Synergy:** Cross-selling tools to the newly acquired customer base.
- **Product Synergy:** Integrating algorithms to enhance existing functionality.
- **Cost Synergy:** Cutting redundant office leases to save operational costs.

| Term | Explanation |
|---|---|
| **Horizontal integration** | Merging with or acquiring a competitor in the same industry at the same stage of production. |
| **Market penetration** | A growth strategy focused on selling existing products to existing markets. |
| **Synergy effect** | The creation of additional value through the successful integration of two companies. |
| **Vertical integration** | Expanding business operations into different steps on the same production path (e.g., a manufacturer buying a supplier). |
