a) 73940

### Explanation

We are given:
- Original Data: $7394$
- Weight: $1234$
- Base number: $11$

We will follow the procedure step-by-step:

**Step 1: Calculate the product sum**
For each digit in the data, multiply it by the weight at the corresponding position, then sum all products:

| Position | Data Digit | Weight | Product |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1st | $7$ | $1$ | $7 \times 1 = 7$ |
| 2nd | $3$ | $2$ | $3 \times 2 = 6$ |
| 3rd | $9$ | $3$ | $9 \times 3 = 27$ |
| 4th | $4$ | $4$ | $4 \times 4 = 16$ |

Total Sum $= 7 + 6 + 27 + 16 = 56$

**Step 2: Calculate the remainder**
Divide the total sum by the base number ($11$) and find the remainder.
$56 \div 11 = 5 \text{ with a remainder of } 1$
(Because $11 \times 5 = 55$, and $56 - 55 = 1$)

**Step 3: Subtract remainder from base to find the check digit**
Subtract the remainder ($1$) from the base number ($11$):
$11 - 1 = 10$

The rule states: "let the **last digit** of the result be the check digit".
The last digit of $10$ is $0$. Therefore, the check digit is $0$.

When the check digit ($0$) is appended to the original data ($7394$), the final result is **$73940$**.
