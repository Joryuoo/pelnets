# 2026S_FE-A_40

According to the Capability Maturity Model Integration, which is the final stage and has the highest level of maturity?

a) Defined
b) Managed
c) Optimizing
d) Quantitatively managed
?
c) Optimizing

### Explanation
The **Capability Maturity Model Integration (CMMI)** is a process level improvement training and appraisal program. It defines five maturity levels for processes:

| Level | Name | Description |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **1** | Initial | Processes are unpredictable, poorly controlled, and reactive. |
| **2** | Managed | Processes are planned, documented, performed, monitored, and controlled at the project level. |
| **3** | Defined | Processes are well characterized and understood, and are described in standards, procedures, tools, and methods for the organization. |
| **4** | Quantitatively Managed | Processes are controlled using statistical and other quantitative techniques. |
| **5** | Optimizing | Process performance is continually improved through both incremental and innovative technological improvements. |

The final stage and highest level of maturity (Level 5) is **Optimizing**.
