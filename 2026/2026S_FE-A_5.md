# 2026S_FE-A_5

Q5. Which of the following is the postfix notation for the expression `a+( b×c )+( d×e )`?

a) a+bc×de+×
b) abc×+de+×
c) abc×+de×+
d) abc+×de×+
?
c) abc×+de×+

### Explanation
To convert the infix expression `a + (b × c) + (d × e)` into postfix notation:

1. Follow the order of operations, starting with expressions inside parentheses:
   - `(b × c)` becomes `bc×`
   - `(d × e)` becomes `de×`
2. Substitute these back into the expression:
   `a + bc× + de×`
3. Operations with the same precedence (like `+` and `+`) are evaluated from left to right. First, convert `a + bc×`:
   - `abc×+`
4. Now, the expression is `(abc×+) + de×`. Convert this remaining addition:
   - `abc×+de×+`

Thus, the final postfix notation is `abc×+de×+`.

This corresponds to option (c).
