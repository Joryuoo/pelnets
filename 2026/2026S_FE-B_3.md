### Explanation
The problem requires computing the Manhattan distance between two vectors $S$ and $T$, which is given by the mathematical formula:
$$ \sum_{i=1}^{n} |S_i - T_i| $$

In the pseudo-code:
- `p` acts as the accumulator for the sum, initially set to `0`.
- The `for` loop iterates through each index `i` from $1$ to $n$.
- At each step, we must calculate the absolute difference between the $i$-th elements of $S$ and $T$, which is `abs(S[i] - T[i])`, and add it to `p`.

Therefore, the missing statement inside the loop is `p <- p + abs(S[i] - T[i])`, making **b** the correct choice.
