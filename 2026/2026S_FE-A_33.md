# 2026S_FE-A_33

Which of the following is an appropriate explanation of CSIRT?

a) A generic term for an organization that is established within a company, organization, or government agency, receiving reports on information security incidents for investigation and response
b) A generic term for people or an organization whose aim is to achieve a religious or political goal by utilizing IT
c) An organization that creates technical documents concerning the Internet and investigates standardization issues
d) An organization that defines the IP-address allocation policy, operating and monitoring DNS root servers and coordinating DNS management on a global scale
?
a) A generic term for an organization that is established within a company, organization, or government agency, receiving reports on information security incidents for investigation and response

### Explanation
**CSIRT (Computer Security Incident Response Team)** is a concrete organizational entity (i.e., one or more staff) that is assigned the responsibility to coordinate and support the response to a computer security event or incident.

- **Option (a)** is the correct definition for CSIRT. They handle incident reports, conduct investigations, and coordinate responses to cyber threats.
- **Option (b)** describes hacktivists or cyber terrorists.
- **Option (c)** describes organizations like the IETF (Internet Engineering Task Force).
- **Option (d)** describes ICANN (Internet Corporation for Assigned Names and Numbers).
