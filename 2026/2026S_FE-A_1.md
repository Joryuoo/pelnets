# 2026S_FE-A_1

Q1. Which of the following is the prefix notation for the infix expression ( (a−b)/( c+d))^ e .
Here, the symbols “+,” “−,” “/,” and “^” represent addition, subtraction, division, and exponentiation operators, respectively. 

a) ^ e /+dc−ba
b) ^−ab/+cde
c) ^ /−ab+cde
d) ^ e /−ab+cd
?
c) ^ /−ab+cde

### Explanation
To convert the infix expression `((a - b) / (c + d)) ^ e` to prefix notation:

1. Identify the operators and their operands following the order of operations. The expression can be viewed as an exponentiation operation where the base is `((a - b) / (c + d))` and the exponent is `e`.
2. Convert the innermost expressions first:
   - `a - b` becomes `-ab`
   - `c + d` becomes `+cd`
3. Substitute these back into the expression: `( -ab / +cd ) ^ e`
4. Convert the division: `/ -ab +cd`
5. Finally, convert the exponentiation with the base `/ -ab +cd` and exponent `e`: `^ / -ab +cd e`

So the final prefix notation is `^ / -ab +cd e` or without spaces `^/-ab+cde`.

This matches option (c).
